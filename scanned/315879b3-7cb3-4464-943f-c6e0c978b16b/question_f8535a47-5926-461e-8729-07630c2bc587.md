Target: `IntentPayloadBuilder.setVerifyingContract` combined with `buildAndSign`. Attacker input: a caller (via integrator-forwarded `routeConfig`) triggers `.setVerifyingContract('wrong-contract.near')` before `.build()`. Call sequence: `buildWithSalt` sets `verifying_contract: this.verifyingContract` unconditionally to whatever was last set, overriding the constructor default of `this.envConfig.contractID`; `buildAndSign` signs this payload via `signer.signIntent(payload)`. Equality broken: verifying_contract in the signed `MultiPayload` != `envConfig.contractID` established at construction time in `IntentPayloadBuilderConfig`. Impact: Critical - a payload signed for a contract other than the intents contract associated with the configured environment can be replayed against, or rejected by, an unintended contract with no recovery path for the signature.
