### Title
Unprivileged `dissolve` burns unsolicited/stray balance sent to a crowdloan pot account - (File: polkadot/runtime/common/src/crowdloan/mod.rs)

### Summary
The `dissolve` extrinsic is gated only on `fund.raised.is_zero()` and either the depositor calling it or the end date passing, not on the actual free balance held by the fund's pot account. Because `dissolve` unconditionally calls `CurrencyOf::<T>::make_free_balance_be(&pot, Zero::zero())`, any balance sitting in the pot — including balance from an unsolicited third-party transfer unrelated to `fund.raised` — is burned when dissolve is invoked.

### Finding Description
`dissolve` fetches `fund.raised` from the `Funds` storage item and checks only that value, not the actual on-chain free balance of `pot = Self::fund_account_id(fund.fund_index)`: [1](#0-0) 

There is no read of `CurrencyOf::<T>::free_balance(&pot)` before burning. Instead, dissolve directly does: [2](#0-1) 

`make_free_balance_be(&pot, Zero::zero())` forcibly sets the pot's free balance to zero regardless of what that balance actually is, burning (reducing total issuance by) whatever amount is present — including funds that arrived via unsolicited transfer from any account that knows the deterministic `PalletId`-derived pot address (a `Sub-account` computed from `T::PalletId::get().into_sub_account_truncating(index)`), which is public/derivable by anyone.

The path is fully reachable by any signed account:
1. Attacker (or anyone) transfers stray tokens (e.g., via `balances::transfer`) to the computed pot account of any fund — this is a normal, unprivileged, permissionless transfer.
2. Once `fund.raised` is zero (either because contributions were never made, or after all contributors have been refunded via `withdraw`), any signed account satisfying `who == fund.depositor || now >= fund.end` can call `dissolve`.
3. `dissolve` burns the entire pot balance, including the stray/unsolicited funds, with no check that the burned amount corresponds only to protocol-owned residual dust.

The `debug_assert!` at line 571 checking the child trie is empty is a debug-only assertion (compiled out in release builds) and does not check or protect the pot's currency balance at all — it only checks the contribution trie is empty, unrelated to arbitrary stray transfers into the pot's `AccountId`.

### Impact Explanation
Any account that sends tokens directly to a crowdloan fund's pot address (whether accidentally or as a "donation") will have those tokens permanently destroyed once the fund's `raised` counter reaches zero and dissolve is called by the depositor or after the end block — an action reachable by an ordinary signed account with no governance step. This matches the scoped impact: permanent, unprivileged-triggerable burn of a third party's tokens that were sent to the pot but never became part of `fund.raised` (i.e., not backed contributions subject to the crowdloan's built-in refund flow).

### Likelihood Explanation
Feasibility is high: pot account addresses are deterministically derivable off-chain by anyone from `PalletId` and `fund_index` (`Self::fund_account_id`), so an attacker can transfer to a known/soon-to-exist crowdloan pot at any time. The precondition (`fund.raised == 0`) is trivially reachable either immediately after fund creation (no contributions yet) or after normal contributor refunds via `withdraw` complete. Dissolve is callable by the depositor at will, or by anyone after `fund.end`, making the burn trigger reachable without waiting on privileged action. This is directly analogous to the griefing scenario already acknowledged in the codebase's own test `withdraw_cannot_be_griefed`, which demonstrates unsolicited transfers into the pot are a recognized attack surface for this pallet, but that protection was applied to `withdraw`'s accounting, not to `dissolve`'s unconditional burn.

### Recommendation
Before burning, read the pot's actual free balance and only "sweep"/burn amounts that are dust below the existential deposit or otherwise provably protocol-owned; alternatively, transfer any residual pot balance back to the fund depositor (or to a designated treasury) rather than burning it with `make_free_balance_be`. At minimum, replace the unconditional `make_free_balance_be(&pot, Zero::zero())` with logic that first checks whether the free balance exceeds what is expected (e.g., `>` existential deposit or `> 0` beyond known-refunded contributions) and routes any excess to the depositor instead of destroying it.

### Proof of Concept
Rust integration test in `polkadot/runtime/common/src/crowdloan/tests.rs`:
1. Create a fund via `Crowdloan::create`.
2. Have a contributor `A` contribute, then have the fund fail/end so `A` withdraws via `Crowdloan::withdraw`, driving `fund.raised` to zero.
3. Have an unrelated account `B` (attacker) call `Balances::transfer` to send `STRAY_AMOUNT` tokens directly to `Crowdloan::fund_account_id(fund_index)`.
4. Record `Balances::total_issuance()` before dissolve.
5. Call `Crowdloan::dissolve(RuntimeOrigin::signed(depositor_or_anyone_after_end), para_id)`.
6. Assert `Balances::total_issuance()` after dissolve equals `total_issuance_before - STRAY_AMOUNT` (plus any pre-existing dust), proving `B`'s tokens were burned.
7. Assert the call succeeded from a plain signed origin with no governance/root involvement, confirming unprivileged reachability.
