Preconditions: `emporiumMessage` values are chosen by the off-chain relay/wallet and are not scoped per-signer on-chain; `usedMessages` mapping is a flat `uint256 => bool` with no owner binding. Equality broken: (message IDs marked used) == (message IDs whose true owner cancelled them) fails since `usedMessages` has no signer scoping. Scoped impact: High, permanent freezing of the victim's in-flight funds/ops tied to that `emporiumMessage` (the underlying UTXOs become unspendable via that specific proof/message pairing).
