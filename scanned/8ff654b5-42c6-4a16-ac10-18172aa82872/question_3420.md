# Q3420: redeem_rewards breaks lamport conservation (mod.rs)

## Question
Can an unprivileged attacker entering through a transaction that lands in a block and is replayed by every node on the cluster reach `redeem_rewards` in `runtime/src/inflation_rewards/mod.rs` with a denominator that the attacker can drive to zero or one, and make the lamports `redeem_rewards` removes differ from the lamports it credits, so that the invariant "Sum of lamports before and after the operation is equal, except for the explicit fee/rent burn." breaks and the result is Loss of Funds?

## Target
- File/function: `runtime/src/inflation_rewards/mod.rs` -> `redeem_rewards()` (around line 35)
- Entrypoint: a transaction that lands in a block and is replayed by every node on the cluster
- Attacker controls: a denominator that the attacker can drive to zero or one
- Exploit idea: Drive `redeem_rewards` so the lamports it removes and the lamports it adds differ, minting or destroying value outside the inflation schedule.
- Invariant to test: Sum of lamports before and after the operation is equal, except for the explicit fee/rent burn.
- Expected Immunefi impact: Loss of Funds - theft or creation of lamports/tokens without the owner's signature (6,250-25,000 SOL)
- Fast validation: Property test around `redeem_rewards`: assert `sum_lamports_before == sum_lamports_after + burned` over randomized inputs.
