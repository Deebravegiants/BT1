# Q3284: is-healthy via liquidate-multi: make the per-user ledger and the vault aggregate disagree 

## Question
Does `liquidate-multi` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1593) let an unprivileged attacker who controls which borrowers are placed early versus late in the batch reach `is-healthy` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:656) in a state where it make the per-user ledger and the vault aggregate disagree by a repeatable amount?
