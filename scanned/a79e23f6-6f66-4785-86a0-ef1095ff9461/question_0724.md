# Q0724: total-assets-preview via redeem: destroy value through a truncation the opposite operation 

## Question
Does `redeem` (mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:797) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the vault's available liquidity relative to the redemption reach `total-assets-preview` (mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:341) in a state where it destroy value through a truncation the opposite operation does not restore? Given that it re-derives a FORWARD index inside calls that have already accrued, the invariant that tokens held by .v0-market-vault equal the sum of its `collateral` map for that asset breaks and the result is direct theft of another user's collateral.

- Invariant to test: tokens held by .v0-market-vault equal the sum of its `collateral` map for that asset
- Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - direct theft of another user's collateral
