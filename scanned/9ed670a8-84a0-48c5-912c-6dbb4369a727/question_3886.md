# Q3886: calc-liq-debt-repay-real via liquidate: leave a residue that no reconciliation pass ever inspects

## Question

- Exploit idea: `calc-liq-debt-repay-real` re-derives debt from capped collateral by dividing by `(+ BPS liq-penalty)`. Reach it through `liquidate` and leave a residue that no reconciliation pass ever inspects.

- Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - direct theft of another user's collateral
