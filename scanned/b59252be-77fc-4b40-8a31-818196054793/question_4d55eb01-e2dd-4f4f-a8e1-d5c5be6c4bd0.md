Preconditions: attacker has an open healthy position with collateral; asset has accrued interest since last update (time has passed causing `next-index` > cached `index`). Call sequence: attacker calls `borrow` with amount A; market caches index via `accrue-and-cache`; market calls `vault-system-borrow` which triggers vault's `accrue`, updating `index` to a higher value before computing `scaled-amount`; market then computes `scaled-debt-added` from the OLDER cached index. Identity that breaks: `scaled-debt-added * cached-index == scaled-amount * live-index == amount` should hold but `scaled-debt-added != scaled-amount` for the same `amount`, breaking DEBT AGREEMENT (`debt`-map-times-`index` == vault's `total-debt`). Scoped impact: Critical, protocol insolvency - the vault's `total-borrowed`/`principal-scaled` and the market's per-user `debt` map diverge, permanently corrupting the debt ledger and potentially allowing under-collateralized debt to be recorded lower than what the vault actually lent, socializing the shortfall to depositors.
