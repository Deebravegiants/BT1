### Title
Nomination pools reward-pool accounting lets a member capture rewards earned before they joined by front-running a delayed `payout_stakers` call - (File: `substrate/frame/nomination-pools/src/lib.rs`)

### Summary
`pallet-nomination-pools` distributes pooled staking rewards using a lazily-updated, points-based `RewardPool` counter that is recomputed against the *current* `bonded_pool.points` whenever it is touched (`join`, `bond_extra`, `unbond`, `claim_payout`). Because the underlying `pallet-staking` reward for an era is fixed by the pool's stash exposure *before* the era starts, but `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` (which actually moves that era's reward balance into the pool's reward account) can be called by anyone at any later time within `HistoryDepth`, a user can join a pool after an era's exposure has already been locked in but before that era's reward is paid into the reward account, and then claim a pro-rata share of that reward once it lands - despite having contributed zero stake/exposure to earning it. This is the same reward-timing arbitrage pattern described in the Liquid Ron `LiquidProxy.sol` report, mapped onto FRAME's nomination-pools reward accounting.

### Finding Description
In `Pallet::join` (`substrate/frame/nomination-pools/src/lib.rs:2120-2174`), `RewardPool::update_records` is invoked with the bonded pool's **old** points before the new member's points are added, and the new member's `last_recorded_reward_counter` is initialized to that just-committed value: [1](#0-0) 

This correctly prevents a joiner from claiming a slice of rewards that are *already sitting* in the reward account at join time.

However, `RewardPool::current_reward_counter` (`substrate/frame/nomination-pools/src/lib.rs:1450-1511`) computes `current_payout_balance` from the reward account's *current* balance and splits `new_pending_rewards` using whatever `bonded_points` is passed in **at the time the function is called**: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

There is no per-era or per-deposit-time attribution: any balance that arrives in the reward account after a member joins - regardless of which era's stake exposure generated it - is split among **whoever currently holds points**, including a member who joined after the era's exposure was already fixed.

Critically, `unbond` claims this pro-rata reward **immediately**, before the principal-unlock period even begins: [4](#0-3) 

and `do_reward_payout` transfers the computed reward to the member's free balance synchronously: [5](#0-4) 

Meanwhile, `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` in `pallet-staking` (which actually credits the pool's reward destination account with an era's earnings) is fixed at era-start exposure but is **permissionless and can be called by anyone, at any later time** within `HistoryDepth`: [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 

So an attacker can:
1. Wait/observe that a pool has one or more eras of exposure-locked-but-not-yet-`payout_stakers`'d rewards (a common situation since nominators/keepers batch these calls, or `HistoryDepth` gives a multi-day window).
2. Call `join` (or `bond_extra`) to acquire points in the pool - contributing zero exposure to the already-fixed past era(s), since staking exposure for those eras was snapshotted before the attacker joined.
3. Wait for (or itself trigger, since anyone can call it) `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` for that back era, which transfers the era's reward into the pool's reward account.
4. Immediately call `unbond` (or `claim_payout`), which recomputes `current_reward_counter` using **current** `bonded_pool.points` (which now include the attacker) and pays the attacker a pro-rata share of that freshly-arrived balance.
5. Wait only `bonding_duration` to reclaim the principal via `withdraw_unbonded` - the profitable part (the reward) was already extracted at step 4, with no lock.

This mirrors the Liquid Ron finding exactly: the "reward pot" size is fixed independently of the newcomer's capital (in Liquid Ron by the RON staking contract's daily-lowest-balance rule; here by `pallet-staking`'s era-exposure snapshot), yet the pooling/vault layer's share/points accounting lets a last-second depositor claim a slice of it.

### Impact Explanation
Existing long-term pool members are diluted every time a new member joins between an era's exposure lock-in and the corresponding `payout_stakers` call, and that new member can extract their unearned share immediately via `unbond`/`claim_payout` without bearing the bonding/slashing risk other members bore for that era. Because rewards do not "expire" and can be claimed for any era within `HistoryDepth` (which can span many days), the exploitable window is large and recurring every time the pool has backlogged, un-harvested era rewards - a very common operational state since `payout_stakers` is often called by third-party incentivized "harvester" bots on a schedule rather than instantly per era.

### Likelihood Explanation
All actions involved - `join`, `bond_extra`, `unbond`, `claim_payout`, and `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` - are permissionless extrinsics callable by any unprivileged account. No trusted role, mocked origin, or privileged path is required. The only requirement is observability of on-chain state (whether a pool has un-harvested era rewards pending, which is public storage), which is trivial for any monitoring bot. Likelihood is High for well-funded, actively-monitored public pools.

### Recommendation
Introduce a delay/attribution mechanism analogous to what was applied to Liquid Ron: either (a) snapshot reward eligibility so that newly joined points only start accruing from the *next* `update_records` call rather than any point already-locked-but-uncredited era exposure, (b) require `payout_stakers` to be called (or reward accounting to be settled) as part of `join`/`bond_extra` so incoming rewards from prior eras cannot be diluted by newcomers, or (c) add a minimum bonding/vesting period before newly joined points become eligible for the pro-rata share of rewards materializing from eras predating the join.

### Proof of Concept
1. Pool P has 1000 existing points from long-term members; era `N` ends with the pool's stash exposure fixed at that 1000-point-equivalent stake. `payout_stakers(P_stash, N)` has not yet been called.
2. Attacker calls `Pools::join(origin, 1000, P)` - `reward_pool.update_records` runs with the OLD (1000) points first, so the attacker's `last_recorded_reward_counter` starts at the current value; attacker now owns 1000 of 2000 total points, i.e., 50%.
3. Anyone calls `Staking::payout_stakers(origin, P_stash, N)`, crediting, say, 1000 tokens into P's reward account - this amount was determined solely by the pre-attacker 1000-point exposure of era `N`.
4. Attacker calls `Pools::unbond(origin, attacker, attacker, 1000)`. Inside `unbond`, `reward_pool.update_records(P, 2000, commission)` runs with CURRENT total points (2000, including attacker), computing `current_reward_counter` from the 1000 newly-arrived tokens split over 2000 points; `do_reward_payout` pays the attacker ~500 tokens immediately (`T::Currency::transfer` in `substrate/frame/nomination-pools/src/lib.rs:3556-3563`), despite the attacker having contributed 0 exposure to era `N`.
5. Attacker waits `bonding_duration` and calls `withdraw_unbonded` to recover the original 1000-token principal, having captured 500 tokens of rewards earned entirely by other members before the attacker joined.
