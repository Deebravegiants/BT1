# Q1900: price-resolve via borrow: count one deposit as backing for two simultaneous claims

## Question
Does `borrow` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1238) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the future mask produced by the new debt bit reach `price-resolve` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:373) in a state where it count one deposit as backing for two simultaneous claims?
