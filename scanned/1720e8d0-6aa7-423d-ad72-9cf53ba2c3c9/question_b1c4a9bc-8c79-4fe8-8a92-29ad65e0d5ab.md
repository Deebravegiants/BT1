Attacker input: nonces array containing a mix of a versioned nonce (long real deadline) and a legacy/non-versioned nonce (decode throws, caught, falls back to DEFAULT_DEADLINE_MS). Impact: Critical, an intent the caller explicitly tried to invalidate can still execute and move funds, with no recovery.
