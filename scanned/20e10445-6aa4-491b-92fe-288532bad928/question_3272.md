# Q3272: resolve-ststx via call-ststx-ratio: make the per-user ledger and the vault aggregate disagree 

## Question
Does `call-ststx-ratio` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1015) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the block and transaction position at which the external ratio is fetched reach `resolve-ststx` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:339) in a state where it make the per-user ledger and the vault aggregate disagree by a repeatable amount? - Invariant to test: the sum over users of the market-vault `debt` map times `index` equals the vault's `total-debt`
- Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - protocol insolvency through uncollateralised debt
