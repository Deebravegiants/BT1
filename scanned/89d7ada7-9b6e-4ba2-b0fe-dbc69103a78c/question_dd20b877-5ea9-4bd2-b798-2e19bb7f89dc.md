Identity that breaks: ROUNDING DIRECTION - each repay should round the reduction against the borrower (i.e. `principal-reduction` and `principal-repaid` should round down, leaving a strictly-non-negative residual owed by the borrower), but repeated rounding-down of `capped-reduction` relative to `updated-scaled-principal` across N tiny calls can leave `principal-scaled` never reaching exactly zero even after `capped-amount` sums to the full `debt`, i.e. `sum(capped-amount over calls) == original debt` but `principal-scaled` after last call `!= 0`, permanently stranding phantom debt that continues to accrue `total-assets` interest with no possible full repayment. Scoped impact: permanent freezing of the excess share value / protocol insolvency for that residual.
