Preconditions: no userChainType assertion is even performed for this branch (unlike SOLANA/STELLAR_SEP53), and no check that userInfo.userAddress matches signature.signatureData.address. Call sequence: prepareSwapSignedData(tonConnectSig_from_attacker, {userAddress: victimTonHexId, userChainType:'ton'}) -> returns public_key derived from victimTonHexId while signature/address in the payload come from the attacker's TonConnect proof. Equality broken: signer_id/public_key claimed in the payload must equal the key that produced the attached signature; here they can diverge entirely with no SDK-side rejection. Impact: Critical - a MultiPayload can be built naming one TON account as public_key while the cryptographic proof was produced by a different account, letting an attacker misattribute an intent to a victim's identity.
