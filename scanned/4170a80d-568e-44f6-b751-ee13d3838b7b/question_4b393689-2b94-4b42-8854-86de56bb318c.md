Target: IntentExecuter.signAndSendIntent (packages/intents-sdk/src/intents/intent-executer-impl/intent-executer.ts) composing with IntentSignerViem.signIntent. Attacker input: intentParams.signer_id supplied as a victim NEAR/EVM account id, combined with an attacker-controlled intentSigner instance. Preconditions: signAndSendIntent spreads ...intentParams into defaultIntentPayloadFactory and ultimately into intentSigner.signIntent(intentPayload) without validating intentPayload.signer_id against the configured signer's own identity anywhere in the executer. Call sequence: signAndSendIntent -> defaultIntentPayloadFactory(salt,{verifying_contract, signer_id: victimId, ...}) -> optional mergeIntentPayloads -> this.intentSigner.signIntent(intentPayload) (attacker's key signs a payload naming victimId as signer_id) -> intentRelayer.publishIntent(...) broadcasts it. Scoped impact: Critical — intent manipulation attempting to move funds the caller did not authorise, if any downstream logic (fee estimation, cached balances, integrator UI) treats a successfully-relayed ticket as proof the named signer authorized the intent before the contract's rejection is observed; also a status/hash misreport risk if the caller reports 'submitted for victimId' optimistically.
