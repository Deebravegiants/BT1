### Title
Supply-index floor resurrects wiped revenue shares and drains later deposits - (File: contracts/pool/src/interest.rs)

### Summary
Bad-debt socialization clamps a fully wiped supply index upward to `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW` without burning or discounting the surviving supply/revenue shares. After `clean_bad_debt`, collateral shares are reclassified as revenue, while debt shares are burned and the debt loss is applied by lowering the supply index. If that loss wipes out all supplied value, the index floor leaves those revenue shares with a positive claim. New supplier cash then makes that stale claim payable, causing the market to become undercollateralized and allowing protocol revenue to be paid from user deposits. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

### Finding Description
`clean_bad_debt` builds deposit-side and borrow-side seize entries for all remaining account positions, sends them to the pool, and deletes the account/NFT. [4](#0-3)  For deposit entries, `Cache::absorb_supply_as_revenue` adds the seized scaled supply to `revenue` without reducing `supplied`. For borrow entries, `seize::apply` converts the unpaid debt to an asset-denominated RAY amount, calls `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`, and burns the debt shares. [5](#0-4) 

`apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` caps the loss at total supplied value and computes the remaining index multiplicatively, but then applies `new_supply_index.max(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)`. [6](#0-5)  When the write-off consumes the complete supplied value, the economically correct remaining claim is zero; clamping instead restores every still-recorded supply share, including revenue shares created from the cleaned account's collateral, to `index / SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW` of its former claim.

Revenue withdrawal then evaluates `revenue * supply_index` using the floored index and pays up to the pool's recorded cash to the pool owner. [7](#0-6) [8](#0-7)  The revenue path checks utilization and only rejects nonzero debt with zero shares; it does not require `cash + debt >= supply claims`. [9](#0-8) [10](#0-9)  Consequently, a fresh deposit after a complete wipeout creates cash that the resurrected revenue claim can consume.

The same stale-claim pattern is demonstrated by the repository's raw-cache tests: after `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` clamps a complete wipeout to the floor, a fresh deposit is immediately owed both its own claim and the old stranded claim, and withdrawal of the stale shares drains exactly the fresh deposit. [11](#0-10) [12](#0-11) 

### Impact Explanation
An unprivileged caller can trigger `clean_bad_debt` on an eligible insolvent account. The cleanup can leave a positive revenue claim even though the market's entire supplied value was consumed. Once any later supplier deposits into that market, `claim_revenue` can transfer that new cash as protocol revenue. The market then owes the new supplier more than its remaining cash, so later withdrawals or recapitalization must cover the shortfall. This is theft of user funds and protocol insolvency caused by resurrecting an economically deleted share claim.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is Medium. The setup requires bad debt large enough to wipe out the market's supplied value after the residual collateral is converted to revenue, but `clean_bad_debt` is permissionless once the insolvency and dust conditions hold. [13](#0-12)  No oracle manipulation, privileged key, reentrancy, or malformed token is needed: the faulty index floor and stale revenue shares are internal accounting behavior. Exploitation completes when a victim supplies fresh liquidity and revenue is claimed.

### Recommendation
Do not preserve share claims across a complete supply-index wipeout. When `remaining == 0`, zero or burn all supply and revenue shares, or set the supply index to zero and make every share-conversion path handle a zero index safely. More generally, bad-debt cleanup should update `supplied`, `revenue`, and position claims atomically so `floor(supplied * supply_index) <= cash + ceil(borrowed * borrow_index)` after the write-down. Add an invariant and integration test covering a complete wipeout followed by a new deposit and revenue claim.

### Proof of Concept
1. Establish a market with a supply position and an insolvent borrow position eligible for `clean_bad_debt`, where the unpaid debt equals or exceeds total supplied value.
2. Call controller `clean_bad_debt(caller, account_id)`. The deposit legs call `absorb_supply_as_revenue`, while the borrow leg calls `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` and burns the debt.
3. Because the loss consumes all supplied value, `remaining` is zero, but line 88 sets the index to `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW` rather than eliminating the claims. The preexisting supply shares now represent a positive claim again.
4. A different user calls controller `supply` and deposits fresh tokens into the same market.
5. Call `claim_revenue` for that market. `burn_claimable_revenue` calculates the stale revenue claim with the floored index, caps it only by cash, burns revenue shares, and transfers the fresh deposit to the pool owner.
6. The fresh supplier's claim remains recorded, but the transferred cash is no longer available to back it, producing a market shortfall.

```rust
pub(crate) fn apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(cache: &mut Cache, bad_debt: Ray) {
    let total_supplied_value = cache.supplied().mul(cache.env(), cache.supply_index());

    if total_supplied_value == Ray::ZERO {
        return;
    }

    let capped = bad_debt.min(total_supplied_value);
    let remaining = total_supplied_value.checked_sub(cache.env(), capped);

    let reduction_factor = remaining.div_floor(cache.env(), total_supplied_value);
    let new_supply_index = cache
        .supply_index()
        .mul_floor(cache.env(), reduction_factor);

    cache.set_supply_index(new_supply_index.max(Ray::from(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)));
```

```rust
/// Panics with `PoolInsolvent` if the market has a positive backing shortfall.
///
/// Backing = cash + ceiled debt value; claims = floored supply value.
pub(crate) fn require_backed_market(env: &Env, cache: &Cache) {
    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        backing_shortfall(cache) == 0,
        CollateralError::PoolInsolvent
    );
}

/// Asset units by which supplier claims exceed cash + debt (0 if solvent).
pub(crate) fn backing_shortfall(cache: &Cache) -> i128 {
    let supplied_claim = cache.unscale_supply_floor(cache.supplied());
    let outstanding_debt = cache.unscale_borrow_ceil(cache.borrowed());
    let backing = cache.cash().saturating_add(outstanding_debt);
    supplied_claim.saturating_sub(backing).max(0)
}

/// Panics with `PoolInsolvent` if supplied is zero while borrowed debt is non-zero.
pub(crate) fn require_supply_for_debt(env: &Env, cache: &Cache) {
    if cache.supplied() == Ray::ZERO && cache.borrowed() != Ray::ZERO {
        panic_with_error!(env, CollateralError::PoolInsolvent);
    }
}
```

```rust
/// Authorizes permissionless dust-gated cleanup outside flash loans.
pub(crate) fn process_clean_bad_debt(env: &Env, caller: &Address, account_id: u64) {
    caller.require_auth();
    validation::require_not_flash_loaning(env);
    clean_bad_debt_standalone(env, account_id);
}

/// Admission condition for bad-debt socialization.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq)]
enum BadDebtGate {
    /// Permissionless: insolvent *and* collateral at or below the dust threshold.
    DustCapped,
    /// Owner-only: insolvent alone, with no cap on the collateral left behind.
    InsolventOnly,
}

/// Requires open debt and the selected insolvency gate, then cleans up the account.
fn socialize_bad_debt(env: &Env, account_id: u64, gate: BadDebtGate) {
    let mut cache = Context::new(env);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        !account.borrow_positions.is_empty(),
        CollateralError::DebtPositionNotFound
    );

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    let admits = match gate {
        BadDebtGate::DustCapped => {
            is_socializable_bad_debt(totals.total_debt, totals.total_collateral)
        }
        BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly => totals.total_debt > totals.total_collateral,
    };
    assert_with_error!(env, admits, CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt);

    bad_debt::execute_bad_debt_cleanup(env, &mut cache, account_id, &account, &totals);
```
