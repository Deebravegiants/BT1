### Title
Dust supply front-run permanently blocks `clean_bad_debt` on insolvent accounts - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs)

### Summary
`clean_bad_debt` is permissionless but gated by `is_socializable_bad_debt`, which requires collateral at or below a fixed 5 USD dust threshold. Because anyone may `supply` into an account's *existing* supply slot (INV-AUTH-03), an unprivileged address can donate a dust amount of the victim's collateral asset to push `total_collateral` just above the threshold, making every `clean_bad_debt` call revert with `CannotCleanBadDebt`. This mirrors the reported bug class: a cheap permissionless balance injection flips a revert condition on a maintenance operation, repeatable indefinitely.

### Finding Description
In `contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs`, `socialize_bad_debt` computes `AccountRiskTotals` and admits only when `BadDebtGate::DustCapped` passes: `is_socializable_bad_debt(total_debt, total_collateral)` = `debt > collateral && collateral <= BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` (5 USD in WAD). Otherwise it reverts `CannotCleanBadDebt` (mod.rs:229-235).

Per `scripts/permissionless_entrypoints.txt` and INV-AUTH-03, `controller::supply` lets any caller add funds to a foreign account in a hub asset the account already supplies — the attacker needs no authority over the victim account. So when an insolvent account's remaining collateral sits at, say, $4.90, the attacker supplies $0.20 of that same collateral asset; collateral becomes $5.10, the gate fails, and cleanup reverts. Each time price or liquidation drifts collateral back under $5, the attacker tops it up again for cents. The donated collateral is not recoverable by the attacker (it becomes protocol revenue on eventual cleanup), but the recurring cost is bounded near $5 per cycle.

### Impact Explanation
Permissionless bad-debt socialization can be delayed indefinitely. While blocked, the insolvent account persists: debt keeps accruing interest, utilization stays pinned at 100%, and suppliers of the debt market cannot withdraw — a temporary freezing of funds with the loss unrealized and growing. The only fallback is the owner-gated `force_socialize_bad_debt`, which requires a Sensitive-tier governance timelock — exactly the "delay or prevent the change" outcome of the original report. Severity is bounded: ordinary liquidation still runs, and the griefing costs the attacker real (if small) funds each cycle, so this is a Medium.

### Likelihood Explanation
Low-to-moderate cost, reachable by any address with a dust balance of the victim's collateral asset. A motivated party (e.g., a borrower whose stuck debt keeps borrow rates elevated for competitors, or a supplier who benefits from an unwritten-down supply index) can automate the top-up whenever a `clean_bad_debt` simulation would succeed. On Stellar, transactions are not publicly front-runnable pre-execution, but the attacker does not need ordering — they only need the collateral above the threshold whenever a cleaner calls, which a simple keeper polling `get_total_collateral_usd` achieves.

### Recommendation
Decouple the dust gate from attacker-injectable collateral: e.g., evaluate `total_collateral` excluding third-party top-ups, snapshot collateral at liquidation time, or let `clean_bad_debt` accept dust-cap violations by seizing the excess as revenue rather than reverting (analogous to the report's "automatically withdraw instead of reverting"). Alternatively, restrict third-party `supply` on accounts whose health factor is below 1.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bob supplies 30 units of collateral `C` and borrows 12 units of `B`; oracle crashes `C` so the account is insolvent with collateral ≈ $4.90 (dust gate open).
2. Attacker calls `supply(caller=attacker, account_id=bob, assets=[(hub, C, dust)])` — permitted because Bob already holds a `C` supply slot — raising collateral to $5.10.
3. Any `clean_bad_debt` call now reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt` (#114) since `collateral > BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD`.
4. Attacker repeats the dust top-up whenever collateral drifts back under $5; cleanup stays blocked until governance executes `force_socialize_bad_debt`.

Note: I could not verify whether `remove_asset_from_spoke`/spoke deprecation includes an analogous "nonzero balance blocks removal" check; `remove_spoke` explicitly performs no empty-spoke check (`config/spoke.rs:36-38`), which weakens the asset-removal variant of this class.
