### Title
`minUsdcAmount()` floor on `Zap._sellInternal` can permanently DOS a small holder's sell after the curve/LT price drops - ([File: packages/contracts/src/Zap.sol])

### Summary
`Zap._sellInternal` enforces a fixed USD floor (`minUsdcAmount()`, sourced from BounceTech's `minTransactionSize()`) on the *proceeds* of a sell, computed only after the trade already executed against the bonding-curve/AMM. Because a user can only sell the tokens they actually hold, and any partial sell of a shrinking position is worth even less, a holder whose full position's sale proceeds fall below the floor (e.g., after a large sell/curve-price drop or an LT `exchangeRate` decline) can never successfully sell that position through `Zap.sell` again unless they acquire more tokens to push the proceeds back above the floor. This mirrors the reported `MIN_NOTIONAL_WEI` bug class: a global minimum-notional check applied after price movement can strand smaller holders below the floor with no per-position accommodation.

### Finding Description
`_sellInternal` pulls the user's tokens, executes the curve or post-graduation swap to obtain `ltReceived`, and only then checks the USD value of the proceeds against the floor: [1](#0-0) 

```
address lt = bonding_.ltOf(tokenAddress);
IERC20(tokenAddress).safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), tokenAmount);
uint256 ltReceived = bonding_.isGraduated(tokenAddress)
    ? _sellOnUniswapV2(tokenAddress, lt, tokenAmount)
    : _sellOnCurve(tokenAddress, tokenAmount);
uint256 grossUsdcEstimate = (ltReceived * IBounceLeveragedToken(lt).exchangeRate()) / 1e18;
if (grossUsdcEstimate / 1e12 < minUsdcAmount()) revert BelowMinAmount();
```

`minUsdcAmount()` reads a single, position-agnostic BounceTech floor (default $10, live-tracked from `GlobalStorage.minTransactionSize()`): [2](#0-1) 

If the bonding-curve token price (via `Router._computeSell`'s constant-product math) or the paired LT's `exchangeRate()` drops — for example after a large seller drains the curve, or the LT's underlying asset depreciates — a smaller holder's entire token balance can be worth less than the floor. Since the check is on the *proceeds of the whole sell*, and the user cannot sell more tokens than they own, no smaller or larger slice of their existing position can clear the floor: selling less yields strictly less USD value, selling their full balance is already the maximum achievable proceeds. The transaction reverts with `BelowMinAmount`, and — critically — there is no analog to the buy-side "floor-bump" mechanism that `_executeBuy` uses to guarantee a mint always clears the LT's floor (`_executeBuy`'s `baseToConvert < floor` branch bumps the mint up to the floor). No such bump exists for sells because there is no way to conjure additional token supply for the user to sell.

The only two ways out for the stranded holder are:
1. Acquire additional tokens of the same launch (via `Zap.buy`) to enlarge the position until its sale proceeds clear the floor, or
2. Wait for `exchangeRate()`/curve price to recover, or for the curve to graduate (`canGraduate` triggers a graduation exit instead of a sell) — but graduation requires price to *rise*, the opposite of the condition that caused the freeze.

This is functionally identical to the reported bug class: a fixed minimum-notional check enforced without any per-position floor-bump on the sell side, so a price-drop event by any other trader can retroactively strand a smaller holder's existing position, forcing them to inject more capital simply to be able to exit.

### Impact Explanation
Medium: affected holders don't lose their tokens outright (they remain in the wallet), but they cannot redeem/exit their position through the only user-facing sell path (`Zap.sell`) unless they commit additional capital or wait for the market to move back in their favor — a real, if conditional, freezing of funds. Post-graduation sells route through HyperSwap V2 and face the same estimate-then-floor-check ordering, so the same class of freeze applies there too.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium: it requires a price-affecting event (large sell dragging down the curve reserve, or LT `exchangeRate()` depreciation) that leaves a smaller holder's position value below the ~$10 floor. This is realistic on thinly-traded, newly-launched curves where whale activity or LT price swings are common, matching the likelihood characterization in the original report.

### Recommendation
Either (a) disable/relax the `minUsdcAmount()` floor check specifically for sells (as the original report recommends), since it exists primarily to keep BounceTech's LT `mint`/`redeem` calls above their internal floor and not to protect trade quality; or (b) if the floor must be kept for `redeem()` compatibility, allow the sell to partially proceed by internally sizing up the redeemed LT (mirroring the buy-side floor-bump) rather than reverting the whole sell when the user's proceeds are below the floor, refunding the same way `_executeBuy` refunds LT/USDC overshoot.

### Proof of Concept
1. Token launches on the curve; trader A buys a small amount of `player x` tokens such that, at the current curve price/LT rate, their position is worth just above `minUsdcAmount()` (10 USDC).
2. A large trader sells a big position via `Zap.sell`, moving the curve's `reserveToken`/`reserveAsset` (per `Router._computeSell`) so the marginal token price drops, and/or the paired LT's `exchangeRate()` declines.
3. Trader A calls `Zap.sell(tokenAddress, theirFullBalance, 0)`. `_sellOnCurve` executes and returns `ltReceived`; `grossUsdcEstimate = ltReceived * exchangeRate() / 1e18` now normalizes to below `minUsdcAmount()` (per `Zap.sol:444-445`).
4. The call reverts with `BelowMinAmount()`. Since trader A cannot sell more tokens than they hold, and any smaller slice would be worth even less, they cannot ever clear this check for that position without buying additional tokens to grow the position (extra capital) or waiting for price to recover — both of which are outside their control and may never happen if the curve keeps declining.
