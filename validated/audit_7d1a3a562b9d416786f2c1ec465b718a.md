### Title
Unprivileged borrower can disable liquidation by draining an Aquarius LP collateral oracle below its minimum value - ([File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs](contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs))

### Summary
A borrower can hold a small Aquarius LP-token supply position as a secondary collateral leg, then withdraw liquidity they control from that pool until the pool's value falls below `min_pool_value_wad`; the price aggregator then returns `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity`, and every valuation-dependent liquidation or bad-debt cleanup for that account aborts before debt can be reduced. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

### Finding Description
`Controller::liquidate` builds a plan through `build_liquidation_plan`, which calls `calculate_account_risk_totals` before checking `health_factor < Wad::ONE`. [2](#0-1)  `calculate_account_risk_totals` loads prices and indexes for every supply and debt key in the account, so one unreadable collateral price aborts the entire valuation rather than merely omitting that leg. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4)  The controller calls the aggregator's strict `prices` endpoint, and that endpoint panics whenever any requested key cannot resolve to a usable feed. [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6)  An Aquarius LP source reads live reserves and total shares from the pool, computes the LP price and resulting pool value, and fails with `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity` when that value is below the configured minimum. [8](#0-7) 

### Impact Explanation
The borrower can keep an account that becomes unhealthy after the LP oracle outage permanently shielded from `liquidate`, because liquidation cannot even complete its risk-total calculation while the LP leg's price is unavailable. [2](#0-1)  The same account-wide valuation is also required by permissionless `clean_bad_debt`, so the unavailable LP price blocks residual-debt socialization as well. [3](#0-2)  This leaves borrowed principal outstanding and prevents suppliers from recovering it through liquidation while the attacker keeps the pool below the configured value floor; restoring sufficient Aquarius liquidity ends the outage. [1](#0-0) 

### Likelihood Explanation
The attacker only needs an ordinary borrowing account whose collateral includes a listed Aquarius LP token plus enough other collateral to establish debt, then performs an ordinary withdrawal from an Aquarius pool where their own share is large enough to push value below `min_pool_value_wad`. [9](#0-8) [10](#0-9)  `supply` allows an owner-created Normal account and updates its collateral positions without requiring the account to remain borrowable at that stage, while later market movement can make the debt unhealthy. [9](#0-8) [11](#0-10)  The feasibility depends on LP concentration and the configured minimum pool value, but neither the controller call nor the Aquarius withdrawal requires a privileged role.

### Recommendation
Do not let one auxiliary collateral leg's market-condition oracle failure abort the entire liquidation path: skip or zero-value a collateral leg whose oracle reports a non-configuration failure while retaining its stored risk contribution conservatively, and keep debt prices mandatory. [12](#0-11)  Alternatively, configure Aquarius LP sources with a sufficiently strict `min_pool_value_wad` relative to expected attacker-controlled liquidity, and avoid admitting pools where a single LP holder can cross that threshold. [1](#0-0) 

### Proof of Concept
1. Register an Aquarius LP token as collateral whose oracle uses `PriceSource::AquariusLp` or `PriceSource::AquariusStableLp` with a nonzero `min_pool_value_wad`. [13](#0-12) [1](#0-0) 
2. An unprivileged borrower calls `supply` to create a Normal account with enough ordinary collateral to borrow and adds a nonzero position in the Aquarius LP token. [9](#0-8) 
3. The borrower calls `borrow` and obtains debt while the account is healthy. [14](#0-13) 
4. From an LP position controlled by the same borrower, withdraw Aquarius liquidity until `fair_lp_price_wad * total_shares / share_unit` is below `min_pool_value_wad`. [15](#0-14) 
5. Change another collateral price or let interest accrue until the account would otherwise have `health_factor < Wad::ONE`. [2](#0-1) 
6. Any liquidator calls `liquidate` with a valid debt payment; `calculate_account_risk_totals` requests the LP token's strict price along with all other account assets, the aggregator panics with `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity`, and no debt is repaid or collateral seized. [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) [1](#0-0) 
7. A caller attempting `clean_bad_debt` reaches the same account-wide price load before the socialization gate, so that recovery path also reverts while the LP value remains below the floor. [3](#0-2)
