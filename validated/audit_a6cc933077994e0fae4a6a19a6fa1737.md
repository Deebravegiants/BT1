### Title
Borrower front-runs `liquidate` by supplying a dust collateral leg on an asset with a stale oracle, bricking `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` with `PriceFeedStale` - ([File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/plan.rs])

### Summary
The Symmio finding is "the victim can front-run the liquidation call with a cheap state change so the liquidation reverts". XOXNO Lending has the same shape: `liquidate` is permissionless and computes risk on-chain, but `build_liquidation_plan` calls `risk::calculate_account_risk_totals` over **every** supply position and `calculate_seized_collateral` prices **every** collateral leg via `cache.cached_price`. The hard-price path in the price aggregator reverts on a stale feed (`PriceFeedStale`). Meanwhile `supply` accepts deposits even when the asset's feed is stale (it uses the permissive price fallback). So the account owner — or anyone, since third-party `supply` may top up assets the account already holds — can plant a dust-sized collateral position in an asset whose feed is currently stale, and every subsequent `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` call on that account reverts until the feed recovers.

### Finding Description
- `build_liquidation_plan` calls `risk::calculate_account_risk_totals` over all `account.supply_positions`/`borrow_positions`, which prices each leg strictly via `Context::cached_price` → price-aggregator `prices` (the hard path that reverts on stale data; `tests/.../surface.rs` `audit_hard_price_reverts_stale_while_status_soft_flags` proves `try_prices` errors).
- `calculate_seized_collateral` again calls `cache.cached_price(&hub_asset.asset)` for every supply position (`contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs:383-385`), so a single unpriceable leg reverts the whole plan regardless of seize mode or offered payments.
- `supply` accepts a position in an asset with a stale feed: `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_liquidate_and_clean_stale_leg.rs:26-30` shows `try_supply(borrower, "WBTC", 0.001)` succeeding while WBTC's feed is stale.
- The same test then shows `try_liquidate` and `try_clean_bad_debt_by_id` both reverting with `PRICE_FEED_STALE` (lines 36-40), recovering only when the feed is refreshed (lines 42-51).
- A dust supply (e.g., `0.001` WBTC) adds negligible collateral value, so it does not move the health factor back above 1 — the account stays economically liquidatable but technically unliquidatable.

Attack path (single unprivileged address):
1. Borrower holds an account approaching HF < 1.
2. Some asset listed in the borrower's spoke has a feed that lapses (any `max_stale_seconds` window expiry — e.g., a market leg with a 5-minute budget, or a keeper feed that misses an update).
3. Borrower calls `supply(caller=borrower, account_id, spoke_id, [(hub_asset_stale, dust_amount)])` — succeeds because supply uses the permissive price path.
4. Every `liquidate(liquidator, account_id, …)` now reverts inside `build_liquidation_plan` at the price read; `clean_bad_debt` reverts too (`INV-LIQ-04` requires "valid required prices").
5. The borrower repeats/re-supplies whenever convenient, or rides out the stale window until prices move back in their favor — the same profit profile as the Symmio nonce-griefing report.

Because `clean_bad_debt` is also blocked, the account cannot even be socialized while the leg is unpriceable; interest keeps accruing, deepening bad debt.

### Impact Explanation
The borrower deterministically prevents their own liquidation during any stale-feed window for the cost of a dust deposit, with no nonce/signature assumption — pure on-chain state. If a feed dies permanently (deprecated feed id, adapter outage beyond `max_stale_seconds`), the account is permanently unliquidatable and uncleanable: collateral is frozen and bad debt accrues to the pool → temporary freezing of funds escalating to protocol insolvency. This matches the source report's impact (victim picks the profitable outcome by dodging liquidation).

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable by any borrower with a `supply` call. The prerequisite — one listed asset in the spoke with a currently-stale feed — occurs naturally whenever any feed misses its staleness window (windows can be as short as seconds/minutes for market legs; `MAX_PRICE_STALE_SECONDS` caps at 26h at the asset level). The borrower does not control the feed, but can opportunistically exploit any stale window, and during extended outages can repeatedly re-plant the leg. Cost is dust collateral plus tx fees.

### Recommendation
Make liquidation resilient to unpriceable collateral legs, e.g.:
- In `calculate_account_risk_totals`/`calculate_seized_collateral`, treat a collateral leg whose price is unavailable as zero-value (skip it for `total_collateral`/`weighted_collateral` and drop its seizure leg) rather than reverting — a stale-priced collateral can only lower HF, never raise it, so skipping is conservative. Debt legs must still hard-fail on stale prices.
- Alternatively, gate `supply` on a strict (non-stale) price so unpriceable legs cannot be planted, and/or let `clean_bad_debt` exclude collateral legs with unreadable prices from the dust-threshold valuation.

### Proof of Concept
The repo's own harness test demonstrates the full mechanism end-to-end: `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_liquidate_and_clean_stale_leg.rs`. It seeds a borrower with USDC collateral and ETH debt, sets WBTC's feed 3,600 s old, successfully `supply`s `0.001` WBTC (permissive supply), drops USDC's price to make the account liquidatable, then shows both `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` reverting with `PRICE_FEED_STALE` — recovery only after the WBTC feed is refreshed. The revert path is `build_liquidation_plan` → `risk::calculate_account_risk_totals`/`calculate_seized_collateral` → `Context::cached_price` → price-aggregator hard `prices`, which reverts on any stale leg (`plan.rs:34-44`, `math.rs:383-385`).
