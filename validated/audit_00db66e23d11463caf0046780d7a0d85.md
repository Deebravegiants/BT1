### Title
Collateral price crashes that occur while a spoke asset is `paused` still resolve against locked depositors — the position is liquidatable while the borrower cannot exit or top up the paused leg - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/plan.rs)

### Summary
The referenced bug class is: an adverse event that occurs during a period when users are locked in (the Y2K "deposit period") is nevertheless counted when the position/epoch is resolved afterward. In XOXNO Lending the analogous structure is the per-listing `paused` flag: pausing a spoke asset blocks all entries and exits for that asset, but it does not block liquidation seizure of that asset, and the health factor is always resolved against current oracle prices. A collateral depeg/crash that happens while the asset is paused is fully counted at liquidation time even though affected borrowers were denied the ability to withdraw or rebalance that leg during the pause.

### Finding Description
Each spoke asset carries three independent flags; `paused` blocks entries and exits, `frozen` blocks only entries, and `no_seize` is the only flag that stops a seizure leg [1](#0-0) . In `build_liquidation_plan`, the repayment legs are checked with `FreezePolicy::AllowOnExit` (rejects `paused`), but the collateral seizure legs are checked with `FreezePolicy::SeizureLeg`, which rejects only `no_seize` [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) . Liquidation eligibility is purely `health_factor < Wad::ONE` computed from current cached prices and indexes [4](#0-3) . Global pause likewise leaves `liquidate` callable [5](#0-4) .

So for an account whose collateral asset is `paused` while its debt asset remains active:

1. The borrower cannot `withdraw` the paused collateral, `supply` more of it, or `swap_collateral`/`repay_debt_with_collateral` out of it — every exit/entry path on that leg reverts with `SpokeAssetPaused` [6](#0-5) .
2. The collateral's price then falls (a depeg during the "deposit period").
3. Any unprivileged address calls `liquidate(liquidator, account_id, debt_payments, seize_mode)`; repayment legs on the still-active debt asset pass `AllowOnExit`, and the paused collateral leg passes `SeizureLeg` because only `no_seize` is checked. The crash that occurred during the lock is fully priced into the seizure.

### Impact Explanation
Theft of user funds: borrowers lose collateral to a liquidation bonus on a price move they were structurally prevented from reacting to — the exact harm of the Y2K report, where deposits locked during the deposit period are resolved by a depeg that began inside it. The borrower retains one defense (repaying debt on the still-active debt market), which is why this is Medium rather than High, but a user whose only available defense requires holding the debt asset off-platform is still forced to realize the loss or be liquidated.

### Likelihood Explanation
Guardian `set_spoke_asset_flags` is an immediate, unprivileged-window administrative action that is expected to be used in emergencies — precisely the conditions under which sharp price moves occur. Once set, relaxation requires the timelocked `relax_spoke_asset_flags` flow [7](#0-6) , so the locked window can be long enough for a material price move. No guardian misbehavior is required for the user-side harm.

### Recommendation
Treat `paused` on a collateral leg as also blocking its seizure leg (extend `FreezePolicy::SeizureLeg` to reject `paused`, or have `build_liquidation_plan` skip/revert on paused collateral legs), matching the invariant that users locked out of an asset should not have lock-period price action resolved against them. Alternatively, automatically set `no_seize` whenever `paused` is set and require both to be relaxed through the timelock.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice supplies `COL` (spoke 1) and borrows `DEBT` at HF ≈ 1.05.
2. Guardian calls `set_spoke_asset_flags(spoke_id, col_hub_asset, paused=true, ...)`. Alice's `withdraw`/`supply`/`swap_collateral` on `COL` now revert with `SpokeAssetPaused` (#315); clearing the flag requires the timelocked relax path.
3. `COL` depegs 30% while paused. `calculate_account_risk_totals` reprices the leg; HF < 1.
4. Any liquidator calls `liquidate` offering `DEBT` payments: the repayment-leg flag check passes (`DEBT` not paused) at plan.rs:24-32, and the `COL` seizure leg passes `SeizureLeg` since `no_seize` is unset at plan.rs:81-89. Alice's collateral is seized at a bonus for a price move that occurred entirely during her forced lock.
