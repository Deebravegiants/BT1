### Title
Residual supply shares survive a bad-debt index wipeout and can drain later deposits - (File: contracts/pool/src/ops/seize.rs)

### Summary
Borrow-side seizure writes bad debt into the supply index and then burns the debt, while deposit-side seizure merely reclassifies shares as revenue. [1](#0-0)  When the write-down reaches `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR`, existing scaled supply shares are not removed and retain a positive claim even when market cash is zero. [2](#0-1) 

### Finding Description
`execute_bad_debt_cleanup` submits all remaining deposit and borrow positions to the pool for seizure. [3](#0-2)  For a debt position, `ops::seize::apply` calls `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` and burns the borrower's debt shares. [4](#0-3) 

The write-down clamps the supply index at a floor rather than retiring all supply shares. [5](#0-4)  Consequently, a market can have zero debt and zero cash while old suppliers still possess withdrawable scaled positions. [6](#0-5) 

If a new user deposits into that market, the pool mints shares at the floored index and credits the fresh cash. [7](#0-6)  An old residual supplier can then withdraw against that cash, leaving the new depositor's recorded claim greater than the remaining reserves. [8](#0-7) 

### Impact Explanation
This creates a permanent undercollateralized market after a subsequent deposit: old claims are paid from cash backing new suppliers, causing theft of user funds and protocol insolvency in the affected market. [9](#0-8)  The surviving claim can extract the full fresh deposit even though the wiped market previously had no reserves. [10](#0-9) 

### Likelihood Explanation
An unprivileged caller can reach the cleanup path through `clean_bad_debt` once an insolvent account's residual collateral is at or below the dust threshold. [11](#0-10)  A severe collateral-price decline or sufficiently large accrued debt can make bad debt consume the debt market's supply value and drive the supply index to its floor. [12](#0-11)  The exploit then only requires an old supplier to remain and a later depositor to add cash.

### Recommendation
When bad debt reaches the supply-index floor or otherwise exhausts market backing, retire or tombstone all remaining external supply and revenue shares instead of leaving withdrawable scaled balances. Alternatively, make deposits and withdrawals check total external supply claims against cash plus collectible debt, and reject reopening a wiped market until all residual claims have been settled or burned.

### Proof of Concept
1. A supplier deposits `1,000` units into a market, receiving `1,000 * RAY` scaled supply shares.
2. A borrower accumulates `1,000 * RAY` scaled debt and becomes insolvent with only dust collateral.
3. An unprivileged caller invokes `clean_bad_debt`; the pool burns the debt and clamps the supply index to `RAY / 1_000`, leaving the supplier's scaled shares alive while cash remains zero. [13](#0-12) 
4. A new depositor supplies the residual amount owed to the old supplier; the pool mints corresponding shares at the floored index and credits the deposit as cash. [7](#0-6) 
5. The old supplier withdraws all residual shares, receives the fresh deposit, and leaves the new depositor's claim greater than the remaining cash. [8](#0-7)

/// Authorizes permissionless dust-gated cleanup outside flash loans.
pub(crate) fn process_clean_bad_debt(env: &Env, caller: &Address, account_id: u64) {
    caller.require_auth();
    validation::require_not_flash_loaning(env);
    clean_bad_debt_standalone(env, account_id);
}

/// Admission condition for bad-debt socialization.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq)]
enum BadDebtGate {
    /// Permissionless: insolvent *and* collateral at or below the dust threshold.
    DustCapped,
    /// Owner-only: insolvent alone, with no cap on the collateral left behind.
    InsolventOnly,
}

/// Requires open debt and the selected insolvency gate, then cleans up the account.
fn socialize_bad_debt(env: &Env, account_id: u64, gate: BadDebtGate) {
    let mut cache = Context::new(env);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        !account.borrow_positions.is_empty(),
        CollateralError::DebtPositionNotFound
    );

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    let admits = match gate {
        BadDebtGate::DustCapped => {
            is_socializable_bad_debt(totals.total_debt, totals.total_collateral)
        }
        BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly => totals.total_debt > totals.total_collateral,
    };
    assert_with_error!(env, admits, CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt);

    bad_debt::execute_bad_debt_cleanup(env, &mut cache, account_id, &account, &totals);
