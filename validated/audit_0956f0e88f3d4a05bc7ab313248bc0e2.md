### No vulnerability found for this question.

`write-feeds` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:149) and its fold helper `write-feed` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:129) only relay attacker-supplied Pyth update buffers to the external `pyth-oracle-v4` contract's `verify-and-update-price-feeds` and propagate a `(response bool uint)` success/failure accumulator via `try!` in `borrow` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1244). [1](#0-0) 

Tracing every read/write in this fold: it never touches `assets`, `principal-scaled`, `total-borrowed`, `index`/`lindex`, the zft supply, the `collateral`/`debt` maps, or any real token balances — it has no arithmetic at all (no `mul-div-up`/`mul-div-down`, no rounding), it just gates whether `borrow` proceeds by returning `(ok true)` or an error. [2](#0-1) 

The actual per-user ledger/vault-aggregate state changes in `borrow` happen afterward, in `vault-system-borrow` (token payout) and `contract-call? .v0-market-vault debt-add-scaled` (scaled-debt bookkeeping), both entirely independent of the price-feed buffers or the fold's accumulator value. [3](#0-2) 

Since `write-feeds`/`write-feed` produce no numeric divergence and have no round-up/round-down behavior whatsoever — they are a pure pass-through oracle-update gate — there is no identity to break here, and the claimed exploit path (folding attacker buffers to desynchronize per-user debt/collateral ledgers from vault aggregates) does not exist in this code.
