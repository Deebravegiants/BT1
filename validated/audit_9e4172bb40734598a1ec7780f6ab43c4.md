### Title
Unguarded subtraction after decimal-normalization round-trip can panic and permanently DoS `Withdraw` - (File: `program/src/processor.rs`)

### Summary
`process_withdraw` (and the analogous PnL-adjustment code in `calc_take_pnl`/`process_withdrawpnl`) computes `target_orders.calc_pnl_x`/`calc_pnl_y` by subtracting a freshly re-normalized withdrawal amount from a value (`x1`) that was already decimal-normalized and PnL-adjusted through a different rounding path. The two rounding paths (raw-token PnL subtraction vs. normalized-unit PnL subtraction) are not guaranteed to agree bit-for-bit, so the final `checked_sub(...).unwrap()` calls can underflow and panic, aborting the transaction. This mirrors the Karak `_transferToSlashStore()` rounding-underflow bug class: an unguarded subtraction between two values that are supposed to be mathematically equal but can diverge by rounding, causing a revert/DoS of a core, unprivileged user-facing operation.

### Finding Description
In `process_withdraw`: [1](#0-0) 
`total_pc_without_take_pnl`/`total_coin_without_take_pnl` are computed from raw vault balances minus `need_take_pnl_*` (raw token units), then normalized into `x1`/`y1` via `Calculator::normalize_decimal_v2` (fixed decimal-`sys_decimal_value` units). [2](#0-1) 
`calc_take_pnl` computes `delta_x`/`delta_y` **directly in normalized units** (via `x1.checked_sub(x2)`, scaled by `pnl_numerator/pnl_denominator`), while simultaneously computing `pc_pnl_amount`/`coin_pnl_amount` in **raw token units** by round-tripping through `restore_decimal` and truncating with `.as_u64()`: [3](#0-2) 
These two derived quantities (`delta_x` in normalized space and `pc_pnl_amount` subtracted from `total_pc_without_take_pnl` in raw space) are not algebraically identical due to independent floor/ceil truncations at each decimal conversion step.

Later, `pc_amount`/`coin_amount` (the LP's proportional share of the post-PnL raw totals, floor-rounded) are re-normalized and subtracted from the **pre-PnL** normalized value `x1`, then `delta_x` is subtracted again: [4](#0-3) 
This assumes `x1 - normalize(pc_amount) - delta_x >= 0` always holds, i.e. that `normalize(pc_amount) <= x1 - delta_x` exactly matches `normalize(total_pc_without_take_pnl_after_pnl_take)`. Because `delta_x` and the raw-space PnL deduction diverge by rounding (analogous to the ERC4626 `+1` rounding drift in the Karak report), there exist reachable states where `normalize_decimal_v2(pc_amount) + delta_x > x1`, causing `checked_sub(...).unwrap()` to panic.

### Impact Explanation
A panic from `.unwrap()` on an underflowing `checked_sub` aborts the transaction with a Rust panic rather than a graceful `AmmError`. If the AMM state drifts into a condition where this subtraction underflows for a given pool, **every subsequent `Withdraw` (and `WithdrawPnl`) call against that pool state reverts**, since `target_orders.calc_pnl_x`/`calc_pnl_y` are recomputed the same way on every call and the discrepancy does not self-correct (it can persist or grow across swaps/deposits which also feed `calc_take_pnl`). This permanently freezes LP funds in the pool — LPs cannot redeem their LP tokens for underlying `coin`/`pc` — matching the "permanent freezing of user or LP funds" impact bar for this bug class.

### Likelihood Explanation
The trigger requires accumulated decimal-normalization rounding drift between the raw-unit PnL bookkeeping (`need_take_pnl_pc/coin`, restored via `restore_decimal`) and the normalized-unit PnL bookkeeping (`calc_pnl_x/y`, updated via `delta_x/delta_y`) across repeated swaps, deposits, and withdrawals — this is directly analogous to the Karak scenario, where donations/dust transfers and repeated operations accumulate small rounding differences until an underflow is reached. Pools with mismatched `pc_decimals`/`coin_decimals` and non-trivial `sys_decimal_value` conversions (i.e., most real pools, since `normalize_decimal_v2`/`restore_decimal` always do a decimal-scaling division) are susceptible over time, especially for long-lived, actively-traded pools with many small transactions that repeatedly invoke `calc_take_pnl`.

### Recommendation
Replace the unguarded `checked_sub(...).unwrap()` chains for `target_orders.calc_pnl_x`/`calc_pnl_y` (in `process_withdraw`, `process_withdraw2`, and the pnl-adjustment path in `process_withdrawpnl`) with saturating subtraction or explicit `if a > b { a - b } else { 0 }` guards, and derive `pc_amount`/`coin_amount`/`delta_x`/`delta_y` from a single, consistent unit space (either always raw-token units or always normalized units) to eliminate the double-rounding divergence, rather than mixing `restore_decimal`-derived raw amounts with directly-computed normalized deltas.

### Proof of Concept
A full deterministic PoC requires simulating many `SwapBaseIn`/`SwapBaseOut`/`Deposit`/`Withdraw` calls (each invoking `calc_take_pnl` and its `restore_decimal`/`normalize_decimal_v2` round-trips) to accumulate rounding drift between `delta_x`/`delta_y` (normalized-space PnL) and the raw-space `need_take_pnl_pc/coin` deduction, until `normalize_decimal_v2(pc_amount) + delta_x > x1` at `process_withdraw`'s final subtraction. This exact numeric path (choice of decimals, fee ratios, and sequence of swap/deposit amounts) needs to be constructed and validated against the actual `Calculator` arithmetic in a test harness (e.g. extending `program/src/processor.rs`'s existing `test_calc_pnl_precision` test at lines 3090–3212) to confirm a concrete underflow-triggering sequence; this was not executed as part of this analysis.
