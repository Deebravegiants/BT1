### Title
Accrual overflows `scaled_to_original` before the borrow-index cap engages, permanently freezing a whale-scale market - (File: common/src/rates/simulate.rs)

### Summary
The crash-by-crafted-input class of CVE-2018-12016 maps onto XOXNO Lending as an unchecked `i128` overflow inside the mandatory interest-accrual step. Every state-changing entrypoint accrues first (`interest::global_sync`), and `accrue_step` computes `borrowed_original = scaled_to_original(borrowed, borrow_index)` and `supplied_original = scaled_to_original(supplied, supply_index)` *before* the `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` cap can stop index growth. On a market whose scaled principal is large enough (whale supply of a high-decimals token at sustained high utilization), `scaled * index` eventually exceeds `i128::MAX` and `Ray::mul` panics with `GenericError::MathOverflow` while `borrow_index` is still below its `1e36` cap. From that moment every call that touches the market — `supply`, `withdraw`, `borrow`, `repay`, `liquidate`, `clean_bad_debt`, `flash_loan`, `update_indexes` — panics in `global_sync` before any recovery path can run.

### Finding Description
`accrue_step` (common/src/rates/simulate.rs:60-62) unscales both books each chunk via `scaled_to_original`, which is `scaled.mul(env, index)` (common/src/rates/scaling.rs:14-16), an overflow-panicking `mul_div` (common/src/math/fp_core.rs). The only guard on growth is `update_borrow_index`'s clamp to `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` (common/src/rates/index.rs:13-19), applied *after* the unscale. The panic condition is `borrowed_scaled * borrow_index > i128::MAX * RAY`-ish, i.e. the *value* ceiling is hit well before the *index* ceiling once scaled principal exceeds ~`i128::MAX / MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY ≈ 1.7e2` — trivially true for any real market, so the cliff is reached when `borrow_index` grows to `i128::MAX·RAY / borrowed_scaled`. The harness test `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` (tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:321-361) demonstrates exactly this: after a few years at 98% utilization, `try_update_indexes_for` fails with `MATH_OVERFLOW` while `borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`, and subsequent `withdraw` and `repay` fail identically. `global_sync` (contracts/pool/src/interest.rs:20-33) runs unconditionally at the head of every verb, so the panic is a permanent, unrecoverable market freeze — there is no entrypoint that skips accrual to let users exit or repay.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing of funds: all supplier principal and all unclaimed yield in the affected market become unreachable forever, borrower collateral held against that market's debt can never be withdrawn or liquidated through the affected book, and bad debt cannot be cleaned or socialized. The contract is unable to operate for that market, meeting the "permanent freezing of funds / contract unable to operate" acceptance bar.

### Likelihood Explanation
A single unprivileged address can set up the precondition using only `supply` and `borrow`: deposit a very large principal of a high-decimals listed asset and borrow it to sustained high utilization (the test uses 1e9 · 10^18 units at 98% utilization on the XLM curve). No privilege is needed; the only cost is capital and time, since accrual is driven by ledger timestamps anyone advances via `update_indexes`. The freeze then traps *all* users of that market, not just the attacker. Likelihood is bounded by the capital required and by how close configured caps let one address push utilization, hence Medium rather than High.

### Recommendation
Make the value unscale saturate or bound-check before panicking in the accrual path: e.g. have `accrue_step` use a saturating `scaled * index` for the utilization inputs (utilization is already capped at RAY in `calculate_borrow_rate`, so saturation is lossless), and/or enforce the `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`/`MAX_SUPPLY_INDEX_RAY` caps *before* unscaling so a capped index pins all downstream products. Additionally gate `global_sync` so that once `borrow_index == MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` accrual short-circuits, keeping repay/withdraw/liquidate reachable.

### Proof of Concept
Existing executable reproduction: `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:321-361` — `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap`. It (1) supplies `1e9 · 10^18` of an 18-decimal asset, (2) borrows 98% of it, (3) advances ledger time year-by-year calling `try_update_indexes_for(&["BIG18"])`, (4) observes `MATH_OVERFLOW` while `borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` (the cap never engages), and (5) confirms `try_withdraw_raw` and `try_repay` fail with the same `MATH_OVERFLOW`, proving the market is permanently frozen.
