### Title
Price-context manipulation defeats the HF >= 1.05 restamp gate, forcing a tightened liquidation tuple onto a victim account - ([File: contracts/controller/src/risk/params.rs])

### Summary
`update_account_threshold` is callable by any authenticated address for any `account_ids`, and `sync_account_thresholds`/`apply_gated_liquidation_params` stamp a listing's newly tightened `liquidation_threshold`/`liquidation_bonus`/`liquidation_fees` tuple onto a victim's existing supply position whenever the *hypothetical* health factor computed with the new LT clears `THRESHOLD_UPDATE_MIN_HF_RAW` (1.05). That gate is the only protection preventing a permissionless caller from tightening a borrower's liquidation terms; it is evaluated against `Context`-cached strict prices fetched at call time. An attacker who can move a market price leg inside the aggregator's configured tolerance/sanity band (e.g., by trading on the Aquarius pool backing an LP-priced leg — an allowed capability) can transiently inflate the victim's collateral valuation just enough to pass the gate, restamp the adverse tuple, and then let the price revert. The account becomes liquidatable sooner than under its stamped vintage, and the attacker liquidates it for the bonus — analogous to CVE-2023-2454, where an attacker-influenced resolution context (`search_path`/schema element) defeats a protection that assumed the privileged operation ran under a fixed environment.

### Finding Description
- `contracts/controller/src/risk/params.rs:124-144` — `update_account_threshold(env, caller, has_risks, account_ids)` requires only `require_authorized_caller` (any signer); it iterates arbitrary `account_ids` with `RiskRefreshScope::FullTuple` when `has_risks = true`. No owner-or-delegate check binds the target accounts.
- `params.rs:68-93` — `apply_gated_liquidation_params` applies the adverse tuple whenever `clears_min_hf` returns true, i.e., hypothetical HF (with the *new* LT) >= 1.05.
- `params.rs:103-119` — `clears_min_hf` calls `calculate_account_risk_totals`, which values collateral with `cache.cached_price(asset)` — prices fetched once per `Context` from the price aggregator (`context.rs:142-151`).
- The price aggregator accepts a blended midpoint of two legs within `OracleTolerance` and `min/max_sanity_price_wad` (see `contracts/price-aggregator/README.md`, three gates). A DEX-derived leg (Aquarius constant-product or stable LP fair value) moves with the attacker's own trade; as long as the manipulated leg stays inside tolerance and the sanity band, `prices` returns the blended price, so the controller's gate sees a collateral value the attacker influenced.
- A third-party dust `supply` to the victim's account reaches the same gate via `merge_supply_leg` -> `refresh_supply_risk_params` (`docs/reference/runbooks/liqvid-listing-params.md:384-386`), giving a second, cheaper trigger that also allows the attacker to add collateral to push HF over the floor if needed.

### Impact Explanation
The stamped LT/bonus/fee tuple is what liquidation actually uses (`get_account_bonus_params`, `calculate_account_risk_totals`). Forcing the tightened tuple onto an account whose true HF would not clear the gate causes premature liquidatability: once the manipulated price reverts, the victim's HF is below 1 under the new LT while it would still be >= 1 under the old stamp. The attacker then liquidates and captures the (also restamped, possibly raised) bonus — theft of user collateral. The protection (HF >= 1.05 buffer) exists precisely to prevent griefing of borderline accounts; it is defeated because the "environment" it measures is attacker-influenced.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires (a) governance tightening LT/bonus/fees on a listing (a normal, recurring operation — the runbook describes exactly this flow), (b) a victim account near the 1.05 gate, and (c) the tightened asset or a co-collateral asset priced with a DEX-based leg whose tolerance band admits the needed move (tolerances are on the order of a few percent). All capabilities — calling `update_account_threshold` with any account id, dust-supplying to a foreign account, and trading on Aquarius/Soroswap — are permissionless. Severity: Medium — bounded by tolerance width and requires a config-change precondition plus a marginal victim.

### Recommendation
Evaluate the restamp gate against a manipulation-resistant bound rather than the blended spot: e.g., use the lower leg price (`price_spread` low) for collateral valuation inside `clears_min_hf`, or require a time-averaged/TWAP price for keeper-triggered restamps. Alternatively, restrict `update_account_threshold` (and the `merge_supply_leg`/`merge_withdraw_leg` restamp on third-party supplies) to the account owner or delegate for the `FullTuple` scope, or make the gate asymmetric so only the account owner can accept an adverse tuple while HF is within some larger margin of the floor.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance executes `EditAssetInSpoke` lowering `liquidation_threshold` on USDC (e.g., 8000 -> 7000 bps) and/or raising the bonus, as in `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/third_party_supply_and_risk_restamp.rs`.
2. Victim ALICE holds USDC collateral and ETH debt with HF(new LT) ≈ 1.03 at honest prices — below the 1.05 gate, so the adverse tuple should be held.
3. Attacker buys USDC (or the priced asset) on the Aquarius pool feeding one oracle leg, moving the leg price up by just under the configured `upper_ratio_bps` tolerance and inside the sanity band; the blended WAD price rises ~tolerance/2, lifting hypothetical HF above 1.05.
4. Attacker calls `update_account_threshold(attacker, true, [alice_account_id])` (or dust `supply` 1 stroop of USDC to Alice's account). `apply_gated_liquidation_params` stamps LT 7000 (and the higher bonus/fees) onto Alice's position.
5. Attacker unwinds the trade; the oracle leg reverts to the honest price. Alice's HF under LT 7000 falls below 1 while it would still exceed 1 under her original LT 8000 stamp.
6. Attacker calls `liquidate` and seizes collateral at the (increased) bonus.
