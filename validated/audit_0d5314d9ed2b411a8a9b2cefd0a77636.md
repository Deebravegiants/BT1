### Title
Spot-reserve manipulation inflates Aquarius LP collateral price used for borrows and liquidations - ([File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs])

### Summary
The price aggregator derives the fair value of Aquarius LP share tokens from the pool's **live** `get_reserves()` reading inside the same transaction that consumes the price (`read` at `contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs:90-114`). This is the same bug class as reading Uniswap `slot0`: an unprivileged attacker can move the pool's reserve balance with their own trade or token transfer on Aquarius immediately before calling a controller entrypoint (`borrow`, `liquidate`, `multiply`, `flash_position`), so the LP token's oracle price is computed from attacker-influenced state rather than a manipulation-resistant reference.

### Finding Description
`read` resolves both leg prices via `engine::resolve_nested` and then calls `aquarius_pool_reserves_call` and `aquarius_total_shares_call` (lines 90-93), feeding them into `fair_lp_price_wad` (`2 * sqrt(value_a * value_b) / supply`, `common/src/oracle/lp.rs:57-87`) or `fair_stable_lp_price_wad` (`D * min(price_a, price_b) / supply`, `common/src/oracle/lp_stable.rs:80-110`).

The geometric-mean/invariant formulas only neutralize manipulation when the reserve product/invariant is preserved — i.e., for a pure swap. Two reserve-moving primitives break that assumption, both reachable by any address in the same Soroban transaction as the controller call:

1. **Direct token transfer (donation).** `get_reserves` reflects balances attributable to the pool. Sending token A to the pool increases `value_a` with no offsetting decrease in `value_b`, so `sqrt(va*vb)` grows ~`sqrt(1 + d/ra)`. For the stable path, `D` is bounded by `xa + xb`, so the same donation raises `D` roughly linearly. An attacker who already holds most of the pool's shares recovers nearly all of the donation afterward via `remove_liquidity`, making the cost only fees plus slippage on their minority share.
2. **Imbalanced single-sided deposit**, which likewise adds to one leg's reserve without preserving the invariant.

No defense filters this: `attest` runs once at configuration time, `tolerance` is 0 bps / single-source (no cross-check), and the sanity bands are extremely wide — e.g. `XLMAQUA_LP` in `configs/mainnet/markets.json` allows a 10x band (`min_sanity_price_wad` 5.36e15 to `max_sanity_price_wad` 5.36e16), and `min_pool_value_wad` only increases under inflation. The manipulated price flows into `Context`-cached strict prices used by the controller for `min_borrow_collateral_usd`, health factor, and liquidation plans.

### Impact Explanation
Theft of user funds / protocol insolvency. An attacker supplies LP shares as collateral, inflates the LP share price via same-transaction reserve manipulation, and calls `controller.borrow` for more than the collateral is worth; the resulting bad debt is socialized to suppliers. The symmetric path deflates or inflates prices to trigger `liquidate` on healthy users or to extract bonus via `repay_debt_with_collateral`/`swap_collateral` settlements.

### Likelihood Explanation
High for pools where the attacker can cheaply move reserves: only a same-transaction Aquarius trade/transfer plus one controller call is needed, and capital can be sourced from `flash_loan`/`flash_position`. Cost scales with how much reserve must be injected to hit the sanity-band ceiling; for thin Aquarius pools collateralizing a large hub this is practical, and a majority share position recovers most of the injected capital.

### Recommendation
Do not consume `get_reserves()` at face value in the same transaction. Options: require a reserve-snapshot taken at a previous ledger (TWAP-style accumulation), cross-check the derived LP price against an independent source (dual-source tolerance) rather than a single `AquariusLp` source, reject reads where reserves deviate beyond a bound from a stored attested ratio, and tighten `min_sanity_price_wad`/`max_sanity_price_wad` to a narrow band.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker acquires ≥51% of shares of an Aquarius pool whose LP token is a collateral market (e.g. `XLMSolvBTC_LP`, `XAUMUSDC_LP`, `XLMAQUA_LP` in `configs/mainnet/markets.json`).
2. In one transaction: flash-borrow token A via `controller.flash_loan`, transfer it to the Aquarius pool (raising `reserve_a`, hence `sqrt(va*vb)` or `D`), then call `controller.supply` of LP shares and `controller.borrow` of the debt asset. `engine::resolve_nested` → `read` → `aquarius_pool_reserves_call` reads the inflated reserves, and the borrow passes the `min_borrow_collateral_usd`/HF check priced at the manipulated share value.
3. Remove liquidity to reclaim the donated tokens, repay the flash loan, keep the borrowed assets. The position is under-collateralized at the true LP price; losses fall on pool suppliers.
