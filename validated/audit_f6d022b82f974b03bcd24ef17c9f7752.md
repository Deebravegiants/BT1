### Title
Unchecked accrued-market-value multiplication can permanently freeze a market - (File: common/src/rates/simulate.rs)

### Summary
Interest accrual multiplies total scaled debt and supply by their respective indexes before computing utilization, and `scaled_to_original` panics when the RAY-valued product exceeds `i128`. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1)  Once a large market’s index grows enough for either product to become unrepresentable, every pool mutation accrues first through `synced_market` and therefore reverts before users can repay, withdraw, liquidate, or reduce the dangerous balance. [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

### Finding Description
`accrue_step` evaluates `borrowed * borrow_index` and `supplied * supply_index` unconditionally, even though the index ceiling only bounds the index and does not ensure the product remains representable. [5](#0-4)  The conversion delegates to `Ray::mul`, whose fixed-point arithmetic reports `MathOverflow` when the result cannot fit in `i128`. [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 

An unprivileged user can create or use their own account and call `borrow(caller, account_id, borrows, to)` while sufficient collateral, spoke capacity, pool liquidity, and utilization headroom exist. [8](#0-7)  The pool then mints scaled debt after syncing the market and stores that scaled amount in both the account position and market-wide `borrowed` total. [9](#0-8) 

Later, the public `update_indexes(caller, assets)` path forwards the selected market to the pool for accrual. [10](#0-9)  The corresponding pool entrypoint calls `ops::market::accrue`, and all other mutation legs obtain their cache through `synced_market`, so a panic inside `accrue_step` prevents every state-changing recovery path from reaching its own logic. [11](#0-10) [3](#0-2) 

### Impact Explanation
This can permanently freeze all funds and debt operations in the affected `(hub_id, asset)` market. [12](#0-11)  Suppliers cannot withdraw because withdrawal resolves the position only after `load_leg` syncs the market. [13](#0-12)  Borrowers and third-party payers cannot reduce the overflowing debt because repayment likewise calls `resolve_repay` only after `load_leg` completes accrual. [14](#0-13)  Liquidation cannot recover the market because liquidation debt repayment and collateral withdrawal invoke the same owner-gated pool legs that accrue first. [15](#0-14) 

Because the stored scaled totals remain unchanged and time only makes the index problem worse, ordinary calls cannot reduce the product below the `i128` limit after the cliff is crossed. [16](#0-15)  Even `recapitalize` cannot provide a general rescue where it must load the synchronized market before changing cash, so this is a market-liveness failure rather than a bounded temporary denial. [17](#0-16) 

### Likelihood Explanation
The trigger requires a very large admitted market balance and enough elapsed time at positive borrow rates for the index to make `scaled * index / RAY` exceed `i128::MAX`. [18](#0-17)  A borrower cannot directly submit an overflowing index, but can establish the large scaled debt through the ordinary `borrow` entrypoint and then rely on normal time-based accrual; any later user can trigger the freeze through `update_indexes`. [19](#0-18) [10](#0-9)  Likelihood is therefore constrained by market size, caps, collateral value, and rate configuration, but the impact is severe when those controls admit balances near the numeric domain. [9](#0-8) 

### Recommendation
Make every state admitted by supply and borrow caps remain representable throughout the configured index range. [20](#0-19)  Enforce scaled supply and debt caps against the worst-case permitted index, reject a new position or total when `scaled * index / RAY` approaches `i128::MAX`, or lower the index ceiling so admitted totals cannot overflow during accrual. [21](#0-20)  Accrual should also provide a non-trapping progression rule at the boundary, such as clamping index growth before the value product can overflow, so repayment, liquidation, withdrawal, and bad-debt cleanup remain executable for any previously accepted state. [22](#0-21) 

### Proof of Concept
1. Assume an active market `(hub_id = H, asset = D)` admits enough debt that its scaled borrow total is near the representable RAY domain, while another listed collateral market gives the attacker sufficient borrowing power.
2. The attacker calls `supply(attacker, 0, spoke_id, [(collateral_hub_asset, collateral_amount)])`, obtaining an account id. [23](#0-22) 
3. The attacker calls `borrow(attacker, account_id, [(HubAssetKey { hub_id: H, asset: D }, amount)], None)` for an amount that passes collateral, cap, liquidity, and utilization checks. [8](#0-7) [9](#0-8) 
4. Ledger time advances while the debt remains outstanding, raising `borrow_index` without reducing `borrowed`. [16](#0-15) 
5. Once `borrowed_scaled * borrow_index / RAY` exceeds `i128::MAX`, a call to `update_indexes(anyone, [HubAssetKey { hub_id: H, asset: D }])` reaches `accrue_step` and reverts in `scaled_to_original`. [10](#0-9) [1](#0-0) 
6. Subsequent `repay`, `withdraw`, `liquidate`, `clean_bad_debt`, flash-position settlement, or strategy calls touching that market re-enter `synced_market` and hit the same overflow before they can mutate the scaled totals. [24](#0-23)

```rust
pub(crate) fn global_sync(env: &Env, cache: &mut Cache) {
    if !cache.needs_accrual() {
        return;
    }

    let mut remaining = cache.elapsed_ms();
    while let Some(nonzero) = NonZeroU64::new(remaining) {
        let chunk = nonzero.get().min(MAX_COMPOUND_DELTA_MS);
        accrue_chunk(env, cache, chunk);
        remaining = remaining.saturating_sub(chunk);
    }

    cache.mark_accrued();
```

```rust
fn accrue_chunk(env: &Env, cache: &mut Cache, delta_ms: u64) {
    let step = accrue_step(
        env,
        cache.params(),
        cache.borrowed(),
        cache.supplied(),
        cache.borrow_index(),
        cache.supply_index(),
        delta_ms,
    );

    cache.set_borrow_index(step.borrow_index);
    cache.set_supply_index(step.supply_index);
    cache.accrue_revenue(step.revenue_shares);
```

```rust
pub(crate) fn accounting(env: &Env, action: &PoolAction) -> RepayOutcome {
    let (mut cache, position) = ops::load_leg(env, action);
    let amount = action.amount;

    let (burned, overpayment) = cache.resolve_repay(amount, position);
    let net_repay = amount
        .checked_sub(overpayment)
        .unwrap_or_else(|| panic_with_error!(env, GenericError::MathOverflow));
    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        net_repay == 0 || burned.raw() > 0,
        GenericError::RepayRoundsToZeroShares
    );

    let position = position.checked_sub(env, burned);
    cache.burn_debt(burned);
```
