### Title
Strategy entrypoints mint debt through `borrow_into_controller` without the borrow-side eligibility gate (`require_can_borrow`) or entry flag check, so a non-borrowable / frozen / paused listing can still be borrowed via `swap_debt`, `multiply`, `flash_position`, and `migrate_from_blend` - (contracts/controller/src/strategies/swap_debt.rs)

### Summary
CVE-2023-26919 is a disabled-restriction bypass: `allowExitFunctions=false` was meant to forbid `exit`/`quit`, but `loadWithNewGlobal` reached them anyway. The analog in XOXNO Lending is the split between the borrow *eligibility* gate and the debt *minting* primitive. Ordinary `borrow` enforces `require_can_borrow` and `FreezePolicy::BlockOnEntry` through `validate_position_entry_gates` (contracts/controller/src/positions/mod.rs:231-253, 257-278). The four router/strategy flows that also create debt — `process_swap_debt`, `process_multiply`, `process_flash_position`, `process_migrate_blend` — all mint debt via `borrow_into_controller` but only validate the *collateral* leg (`require_can_supply`) and the `is_flashloanable` flag (flash_position.rs:87-91). None of them call `require_can_borrow` or `enforce_spoke_asset_flags` on the debt leg. `process_swap_debt` even checks `require_hub_active` only for `existing_debt.hub_id`, never for `new_debt.hub_id` (swap_debt.rs:44).

### Finding Description
`validate_position_entry_gates` is the only place `require_can_borrow` is invoked for `AccountPositionType::Borrow` (positions/mod.rs:243-252), and `enforce_spoke_asset_flags` with `BlockOnEntry` rejects `paused`/`frozen` listings (positions/mod.rs:264-269). The strategy paths bypass both:

- `process_swap_debt` (swap_debt.rs:55-63) borrows `new_debt` into controller custody with no `require_can_borrow`, no flag check, and no `require_hub_active` on `new_debt`.
- `process_multiply` (multiply.rs:60, 76-84) checks `require_can_supply` on `collateral` only, then borrows `debt` unconditionally.
- `process_flash_position` (flash_position.rs:87-91, 260-295) gates on `is_flashloanable` and `require_can_supply` on collaterals, but the debt leg is minted in `mint_and_forward` → `borrow_into_controller` with no borrow eligibility check.
- `process_migrate_blend` (migrate_blend.rs:85-88, 148-163) checks `require_can_supply` on every withdraw asset, then borrows each `debt_caps` asset unconditionally.

`is_borrowable=false`, `paused=true`, `frozen=true`, and the borrow cap therefore do not stop an unprivileged account from adding debt in that listing — the flag is set but the restriction is unreachable on these paths, exactly the shape of the Nashorn sandbox escape. Caveat: whether `borrow_into_controller` internally re-applies these checks could not be fully confirmed from the indexed code; if it delegates to the same `positions/debt.rs` entry path that runs `enforce_spoke_asset_flags`, this finding is void — the checks in `debt.rs` appear tied to flag policy but the call chain from `strategies/mod.rs` was not fully visible.

### Impact Explanation
If the debt leg is unguarded, governance's emergency levers lose effect: after freezing or pausing a listing, or removing borrow eligibility, users keep minting debt in it via `swap_debt`/`multiply`/`flash_position`/`migrate_from_blend`, and borrow caps are unenforced on those paths. During an incident (the exact scenario `paused`/`frozen` exist for), continued leverage on a distressed asset can drive the market to insolvency, and borrow-cap bypass removes the configured bound on exposure to a risky market. Protocol insolvency is within the accepted impact set.

### Likelihood Explanation
Any unprivileged account owner or delegate can reach the paths (`require_authorized_caller` plus `require_owner_or_delegate`), needing only an existing account (for `swap_debt`, existing debt) or collateral for the flash/multiply flows. The trigger condition is a listing being flagged `is_borrowable=false`, `paused`, or `frozen`, or a borrow cap being hit — all states governance enters deliberately. Likelihood is moderate since it requires such a flag state to be active.

### Recommendation
Route every strategy debt leg through the same gate as `borrow`: call `require_can_borrow(env, cache, spoke_id, hub_asset)` and `enforce_spoke_asset_flags(..., FreezePolicy::BlockOnEntry)` inside `borrow_into_controller` (or at each call site), and add `config::require_hub_active(env, new_debt.hub_id)` in `process_swap_debt`. Centralizing the check inside the shared borrow primitive prevents future strategy flows from repeating the omission.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance/guardian sets `frozen=true` (or `is_borrowable=false`, or `borrow_cap` reached) on USDC in spoke S via `set_spoke_asset_flags`.
2. Direct `controller.borrow(USDC, …)` now reverts with `SpokeAssetFrozen`/`AssetNotBorrowable` via `validate_position_entry_gates`.
3. Alice holds debt in USDT. She calls `controller.swap_debt(account_id, existing_debt=USDT_key, new_debt=USDC_key, new_debt_amount=X, swap=route)` — or opens a `multiply`/`flash_position` with `debt=USDC`.
4. `process_swap_debt` checks only `existing_debt.hub_id` activity and the collateral side; `borrow_into_controller` mints X USDC debt to her account, swaps to USDT, repays. Net effect: fresh USDC debt created while the listing was frozen — the disabled function was reachable through the alternate entrypoint.
