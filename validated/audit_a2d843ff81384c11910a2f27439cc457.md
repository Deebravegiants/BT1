### Title
Unprivileged third-party dust top-up force-restamps a victim's stored LTV and can block borrows/withdrawals — (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs)

### Summary
CVE-2022-21340 is an unauthenticated, network-reachable partial denial of service. The analog in XOXNO Lending: any unprivileged address may call `supply` to top up an *existing* supply leg of someone else's account (new slots are rejected, top-ups are not). That top-up unconditionally rewrites the leg's stored `loan_to_value` to the current listing value via `merge_supply_leg` → `refresh_supply_risk_params`, outside the 1.05 health-factor gate that protects threshold/bonus/fee changes. After governance lowers an asset's LTV, a one-stroop donation retroactively applies the new LTV to a victim's account, reducing its LTV-weighted collateral so subsequent `borrow` and `withdraw` calls revert with `InsufficientCollateral` / `min_borrow_collateral_usd` failures.

### Finding Description
- `require_third_party_existing_supply` in `contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs:78-97` permits a non-owner, non-delegate caller to supply to any leg already present on the target account. [1](#0-0) 
- Every supply merge calls `refresh_supply_risk_params(..., RiskRefreshScope::FullTuple)`, which restamps the stored LTV; per `docs/reference/runbooks/liqvid-listing-params.md:377-379`, LTV refresh is *unconditional* — the `THRESHOLD_UPDATE_MIN_HF_RAW` gate only shields threshold/bonus/fee changes. [2](#0-1) 
- The regression tests confirm the asymmetry: `poc_third_party_top_up_force_restamps_ltv` shows BOB's 1-unit top-up drops ALICE's stored LTV 8000→5000 and her subsequent `borrow` reverts with `INSUFFICIENT_COLLATERAL` [3](#0-2) , and `regression_supply_skips_bonus_only_raise_below_min_hf` / `regression_third_party_supply_cannot_force_adverse_tuple_below_min_hf` pin that LTV "rides outside the gate" even when the adverse tuple is held. [4](#0-3) 
- Borrow/withdraw limits use `min(stored LTV, stored LT)` on cached risk totals, so the restamped lower LTV immediately shrinks the account's borrowing ceiling and the `min_borrow_collateral_usd` headroom on withdrawals (`docs/reference/runbooks/liqvid-listing-params.md:367-368`).

### Impact Explanation
Partial denial of service of a victim account's core functions: borrows and collateral withdrawals that were valid under the grandfathered stored LTV now revert. Since the victim cannot restore the old LTV (it no longer exists in the listing), the freeze persists for as long as the account relies on the higher vintage LTV — collateral is effectively locked behind the reduced limit until debt is repaid or collateral value rises. Cost to the attacker is one base unit of the asset. This maps to "temporary freezing of funds" for affected accounts; if the account's HF is already marginal, the restamp can also trigger liquidation eligibility sooner via threshold interactions, though the threshold itself stays gated.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a governance `editAssetInSpoke` that lowers `loan_to_value` while accounts still hold the higher stored LTV — a normal operational event (de-risking a listing, runbook-documented). Once lowered, any unprivileged address can grief every borrower who holds that leg, for dust cost, with no authorization beyond the attacker's own. The LTV refresh being ungated is an intentional-looking asymmetry (gating only what "favors the liquidator"), but a lowered LTV harms the borrower, not the liquidator, so it escapes the gate entirely.

### Recommendation
Apply the same gate used for threshold/bonus/fee to the LTV restamp when the new LTV is *lower* than the stored one and the account has debt: only apply it if the resulting health factor (or LTV-weighted headroom) clears a floor, or skip it below the floor. Alternatively, restrict third-party top-ups' side effects: credit the shares without invoking `refresh_supply_risk_params` when `!is_owner_or_delegate`, so donations cannot rewrite the victim's risk tuple.

### Proof of Concept
1. ALICE supplies USDC and borrows near her limit under stored LTV 8000.
2. Governance lowers the USDC listing LTV to 5000 (`edit_asset_in_spoke`).
3. BOB (no relation to ALICE) calls `supply(caller=BOB, account_id=ALICE, spoke_id, assets=[(hub,USDC), 1])`. Allowed by `require_third_party_existing_supply` since the leg exists.
4. `merge_supply_leg` restamps ALICE's stored LTV to 5000 unconditionally.
5. ALICE's next `borrow` (and any `withdraw` that would breach the new `min_borrow_collateral_usd` headroom) reverts `InsufficientCollateral` — reproduced in `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/security_audit.rs:507-531`.

#[test]
fn poc_third_party_top_up_force_restamps_ltv() {
    let mut t = LendingTest::new().standard_two_asset_dust_disabled();

    t.supply(ALICE, "USDC", 10_000.0);
    let id = t.resolve_account_id(ALICE);

    t.edit_asset_config("USDC", |c| {
        c.loan_to_value = 5_000;
        c.liquidation_threshold = 5_500;
    });

    t.try_supply_to_account(BOB, ALICE, "USDC", 1.0)
        .expect("third-party top-up of existing leg allowed");
    let (ltv, _) = supply_ltv_and_lt(&t, id, "USDC");
    assert_eq!(
        ltv, 5_000,
        "H-RISK-03: third-party top-up force-restamps LTV"
    );

    let blocked = t.try_borrow(ALICE, "ETH", 3.5);
    assert_contract_error(blocked, errors::INSUFFICIENT_COLLATERAL);

    t.borrow(ALICE, "ETH", 2.0);
    t.assert_healthy(ALICE);
