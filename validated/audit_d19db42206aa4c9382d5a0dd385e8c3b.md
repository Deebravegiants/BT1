### Title
Withdraw permanently reverts and freezes LP funds when reserve imbalance drives one side's pro-rata share to zero - ([File: program/src/processor.rs])

### Finding Description
`process_withdraw` computes each LP holder's redeemable `coin_amount` and `pc_amount` using floor-rounded pro-rata division against the pool's current (post-swap, post-pnl) reserves: [1](#0-0) 

It then unconditionally rejects the withdrawal if *either* side rounds down to zero: [2](#0-1) 

`InvariantPool::exchange_pool_to_token` performs `token_total_amount * token_input / token_total` with `RoundDirection::Floor`, i.e. integer floor division: [3](#0-2) 

This is the exact same bug class as the Dopex `PerpetualAtlanticVaultLP.redeem()` finding: a `require`/early-return that treats "one side of a dual-asset redemption computed to 0" as a hard failure for the *entire* withdrawal, instead of allowing the non-zero side to be paid out. In Raydium's constant-product AMM, ordinary and fully permissionless activity (swaps skewing the pool coin/pc ratio, `withdrawpnl` draining one side of the reserves to the configured PNL owner, or simple decimal/precision mismatch between `coin_decimals`/`pc_decimals`) can push the pool reserves so far out of proportion to `amm.lp_amount`/`lp_mint.supply` that a small LP holder's floor-rounded share of one token computes to exactly `0`, while their share of the other token is still non-zero and rightfully redeemable.

Because the check ORs together `withdraw.amount == 0`, `coin_amount == 0`, and `pc_amount == 0` and returns `AmmError::InvalidInput` for any of them, any LP whose share of one token rounds to zero can never withdraw *any* amount — not even the non-zero side — for that LP token amount. Unlike a normal AMM where a holder could simply reduce/increase the LP `amount` parameter to try to get a non-zero result on both sides, here the affected holder's *entire* remaining LP balance can be stuck: if their whole position is small enough relative to one side of the pool that `coin_amount` (or `pc_amount`) is 0 for the full balance, then no partial withdraw amount they choose will ever produce non-zero on both sides simultaneously (since decreasing `withdraw.amount` only decreases both computed amounts further), permanently freezing their LP tokens and their claim to the pool.

### Impact Explanation
This is a permanent freezing-of-funds bug reachable by any unprivileged LP holder simply calling the public `Withdraw` instruction with their own LP tokens and accounts — no privileged signer or off-chain condition is required. Small/late LP providers in pools that become highly imbalanced (via normal swap activity or `withdrawpnl` skimming) can end up with LP shares whose pro-rata redemption of one token floors to zero, at which point `process_withdraw` will reject the transaction with `AmmError::InvalidInput` for that entire LP balance and there is no floor value of `withdraw.amount` that fixes it. This directly parallels the referenced Medium-severity Dopex finding, which was confirmed and fixed for the identical bug pattern (blocking full withdrawal because one side rounds to zero).

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate: it requires a pool to become sufficiently imbalanced (e.g., through normal trading activity pushing the coin/pc ratio to an extreme, or through the AMM owner periodically calling `WithdrawPnl` to skim one side of the reserves via `process_withdrawpnl`) combined with a small enough LP position that the floor-rounded amount for one side is exactly zero. Pools with highly disparate coin/pc decimals or that trade to extreme price ratios (as commonly happens for illiquid/long-tail tokens) make this condition realistically reachable without any privileged or malicious action — this mirrors exactly the external report's "external condition reliance" reasoning that led the original finding to be judged Medium.

### Recommendation
Do not reject the withdrawal solely because one of the two computed amounts is zero. Instead:
- Allow the withdrawal to proceed and skip the token transfer only for the side whose amount is `0` (still transferring the non-zero side and burning the corresponding LP tokens), analogous to the recommended Dopex fix of checking `(assets + rdpxAmount) != 0` rather than requiring both to be non-zero.
- Alternatively/additionally, enforce a pool-level minimum LP-to-reserve ratio at deposit time (already partially addressed by `NotAllowZeroLP`) so that a single LP token can never represent a fractional amount that rounds to zero for either asset given realistic reserve ratios.

### Proof of Concept
1. Initialize a pool and have LP A deposit a very small proportional stake (small `lp_amount`) alongside a large depositor.
2. Through normal swaps (`process_swap_base_in`/`process_swap_base_out`) or repeated `WithdrawPnl` calls by the configured `pnl_owner`, drive the pool's `pc`/`coin` vault ratio to an extreme (e.g., pc reserves shrink drastically relative to coin reserves, or vice versa) while `amm.lp_amount` stays roughly constant.
3. LP A calls `Withdraw` with their full LP balance. In `process_withdraw`, `InvariantPool::exchange_pool_to_token` floors `pc_amount` (or `coin_amount`) to `0` because `withdraw.amount * total_pc_without_take_pnl / amm.lp_amount < 1`. [2](#0-1) 
4. The instruction reverts with `AmmError::InvalidInput` regardless of `withdraw.amount` chosen (any smaller amount only decreases both `coin_amount` and `pc_amount` further), permanently locking LP A's tokens and their claim on the non-zero side of the pool.

    pub fn exchange_pool_to_token(
        &self,
        token_total_amount: u64,
        round_direction: RoundDirection,
    ) -> Option<u64> {
        Some(if round_direction == RoundDirection::Floor {
            U128::from(token_total_amount)
                .checked_mul(self.token_input.into())
                .unwrap()
                .checked_div(self.token_total.into())
                .unwrap()
                .as_u64()
        } else {
            U128::from(token_total_amount)
                .checked_mul(self.token_input.into())
                .unwrap()
                .checked_ceil_div(self.token_total.into())
                .unwrap()
                .as_u64()
        })
    }
