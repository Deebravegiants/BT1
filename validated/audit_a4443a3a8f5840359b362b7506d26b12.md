### Title
Direct-transfer donation to AMM coin/pc vaults manipulates deposit/withdraw exchange rate via raw vault-balance accounting - (File: program/src/processor.rs, program/src/math.rs)

### Summary
The Raydium AMM computes the pool's effective reserves for `Deposit` and `Withdraw` directly from the live SPL token balances of the `amm_coin_vault` / `amm_pc_vault` accounts, rather than from an internally tracked ledger. Because these vault accounts are ordinary SPL token accounts, any unprivileged actor can transfer ("donate") tokens into them directly, outside of the `Deposit` instruction, instantly changing the coin/pc ratio and LP exchange rate used by `process_deposit` and `process_withdraw`. This is the same root-cause pattern as the reported `StHEU.donate()` issue: an externally injectable balance increase distorts an exchange-rate computation that other users' claims/withdrawals depend on.

### Finding Description
`calc_total_without_take_pnl_no_orderbook` derives `total_pc_without_take_pnl`/`total_coin_without_take_pnl` straight from the vault token accounts' `.amount` field: [1](#0-0) 

Both `process_deposit` and `process_withdraw` feed these raw vault balances into `InvariantToken`/`InvariantPool` to compute the LP mint/burn exchange rate: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

Because `amm_coin_vault_info`/`amm_pc_vault_info` are plain SPL token accounts owned by the AMM authority PDA, any account (not just the AMM program) can transfer tokens into them via a standard `spl_token::instruction::transfer` — no special authority is required to *receive* tokens. This lets any unprivileged actor inflate `total_coin_without_take_pnl`/`total_pc_without_take_pnl` at will, exactly like the reported `donate()` inflating `totalHEU` and the derived exchange rate.

`Withdraw` only guards against withdrawing to exactly zero LP supply, not against draining it to a dust amount: [5](#0-4) 

This allows an attacker holding (or having acquired) the LP supply to repeatedly withdraw down to `amm.lp_amount = 1`, then donate a large amount of coin/pc tokens directly into the vaults. The subsequent `mint_lp_amount` computed for any depositor is `deduct_amount * amm.lp_amount / total_amount` (via `InvariantPool::exchange_token_to_pool`), so with `amm.lp_amount = 1` and an inflated `total_amount`, a victim's large deposit is diluted to at most 1 new LP unit while the attacker's pre-existing 1 unit now represents ~50% of the (donation + victim deposit) pool value, which the attacker can then redeem via `Withdraw`.

### Impact Explanation
This is a classic first-depositor / donation inflation attack on constant-product AMMs. It allows an unprivileged attacker to steal value from subsequent depositors' funds by manipulating the LP exchange rate the same way the reported `donate()` bug let a privileged owner extract more HEU than they were entitled to. The impact is direct theft of LP/user funds and insolvent LP accounting (LP token no longer represents proportional claim on pool assets), matching the required "Medium/High/Critical, concrete theft" bar.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires the attacker to control (or reduce to near-zero) the outstanding LP supply of a given pool — most practically exploitable against freshly created pools where the pool creator is initially the sole LP holder — combined with normal, unprivileged SPL token transfers and the existing `Withdraw`/`Deposit` instructions. No privileged signer or off-chain component is needed; all steps are ordinary instructions with attacker-chosen accounts and amounts.

### Recommendation
Track pool reserves in internal state (e.g., persisted `coin_amount`/`pc_amount` fields updated only by `Deposit`/`Withdraw`/`Swap` logic) rather than reading live SPL token balances of the vaults for exchange-rate math, or alternatively lock a minimum LP supply/reserve floor (as Uniswap V2 does) and reject deposits/withdrawals whenever `mint_lp_amount`/reserve ratios deviate beyond what internal accounting would produce absent unauthorized transfers.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker calls `Initialize2` (or otherwise becomes sole LP holder) to create pool with reserves `(pc0, coin0)`, minting `L0` LP tokens to itself minus the locked minimum, per [6](#0-5) .
2. Attacker repeatedly calls `Withdraw` with `withdraw.amount = amm.lp_amount - 1` (allowed since it only forbids `withdraw.amount >= amm.lp_amount`, per [7](#0-6) ) until `amm.lp_amount == 1`.
3. Attacker issues a raw `spl_token::instruction::transfer` sending a large amount of coin and pc tokens directly to `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault` (no AMM instruction involved).
4. A victim calls `Deposit`; `calc_total_without_take_pnl_no_orderbook` picks up the inflated vault balances [8](#0-7) , and `mint_lp_amount = deduct_amount * 1 / total_amount` rounds to ≤1 LP unit for the victim despite their large deposit, per [3](#0-2) .
5. Attacker calls `Withdraw` for its 1 LP unit, now redeemable for roughly half of the combined donated + victim-deposited reserves, per the same proportional math in [4](#0-3) .
