### Title
Sub-3-decimal liquidation boundary can make an unhealthy account temporarily unseizable - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs)

### Summary
An unprivileged borrower can enter a narrow solvent-but-unhealthy state in which every `liquidate` payment is normalized to an amount that rounds down to zero whole collateral units. `whole_unit_repayment` then declines to raise the quote because raising it would cross the whole-debt boundary, leaving liquidation unavailable until interest or prices move the boundary. [1](#0-0) 

### Finding Description
`process_liquidation` accepts a permissionless `liquidator`, `account_id`, `debt_payments`, and `SeizeMode`, then plans repayments and seizures through `build_liquidation_plan`. [2](#0-1) 

For a solvent account whose partial quote is below total debt, `normalize_repayment_plan` calls `whole_unit_repayment` for one low-decimal supply leg. [3](#0-2) 

That function returns early only when the account does not have exactly one supply position, the collateral has at least three decimals, or no whole collateral unit is held. [4](#0-3) 

For an eligible leg, it raises a quote below `ceil((unit + margin) / (1 + bonus))` to that value, but explicitly refuses when the raised quote is at least `total_debt`. [5](#0-4) 

Consequently, when:

```text
floor(unit / (1 + bonus)) - one_unit_per_debt_leg
    < total_debt
    <= ceil((unit + margin) / (1 + bonus))
```

and the original curve quote backs less than one unit, the quote remains too small to seize a whole unit while any larger submitted payment is trimmed back to that quote. [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 

The resulting seizure has zero token amount and is skipped, so the plan has no effective seizure; execution consequently rejects the liquidation rather than repaying debt. [8](#0-7) 

### Impact Explanation
This creates a temporary freezing impact: while the position remains inside the boundary interval, a liquidator cannot seize the low-decimal collateral through any offered amount. Smaller offers do not back one whole unit, while larger offers are normalized down to the same insufficient quote. [6](#0-5) [1](#0-0) 

The affected account can still have `HF < 1`, yet its collateral cannot be liquidated during that interval. The resulting temporary loss of liquidation access can allow insolvency to accumulate while collateral remains locked in the protocol. [9](#0-8) 

### Likelihood Explanation
The borrower controls the number of debt positions, debt size, and whether the account has exactly one collateral leg. An unprivileged borrower can therefore create the required shape with `supply` and `borrow`, then enter the boundary interval through ordinary index accrual or a permissible collateral-price movement. [10](#0-9) [11](#0-10) 

The interval is narrow because it depends on one collateral unit, one unit of each debt leg, and the configured bonus, but it is deterministic rather than requiring privileged state, leaked keys, oracle dishonesty, or malformed parameters. [5](#0-4) 

### Recommendation
Handle the equality boundary by allowing `unit_repayment == total_debt` to promote the quote to a full close, or otherwise select `min(unit_repayment, total_debt)` when `unit_repayment` crosses the debt amount. The condition at `math.rs:287` should not return the sub-unit quote simply because the raised quote equals or exceeds total debt. [1](#0-0) 

Alternatively, when the raised quote would exceed debt, mark the plan as a full close and seize the held collateral unit explicitly, then let the normal repayment cap and refund logic remove any excess repayment. [12](#0-11) 

### Proof of Concept
1. A user creates a `Normal` account with exactly one collateral position in an asset having fewer than three decimals, for example two decimals, and holds at least one whole unit. [4](#0-3) 
2. The user borrows debt such that the account is unhealthy but still solvent and satisfies:

   ```text
   floor(U / (1 + bonus)) - R < D <= ceil((U + max(U / 1_000_000, 1)) / (1 + bonus))
   ```

   where `U` is the WAD value of one collateral unit, `D` is total WAD debt, and `R` is the sum of one native unit over all debt legs. [13](#0-12) 
3. Any liquidator calls:

   ```rust
   liquidate(
       liquidator,
       account_id,
       debt_payments,
       SeizeMode::Transfer,
   )
   ```

   or an equivalent `SeizeMode::Credit(receiver)` call. [14](#0-13) 
4. `whole_unit_repayment` computes a raised quote that is at least `total_debt`, returns the lower curve quote instead, and leaves `full_close` false. [1](#0-0) 
5. Any payment above the returned quote is trimmed by `process_excess_payment`; the low-decimal collateral seizure then rounds down to zero whole units and produces no seizure leg. [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 
6. With no positive seizure amount remaining, the liquidation cannot complete while that state persists, temporarily freezing the unhealthy account’s collateral seizure and debt-close path. [8](#0-7)
