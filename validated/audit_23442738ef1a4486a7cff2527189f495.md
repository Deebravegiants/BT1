Raydium's swap direction determination doesn't rely on array-position ordering like the Balancer `assets` array in the referenced report. Both `process_swap_base_in` and `process_swap_base_out` (and their `_v2` counterparts) independently derive `swap_direction` by directly comparing `user_source.mint`/`user_destination.mint` against `amm_coin_vault.mint`/`amm_pc_vault.mint`, which is symmetric and correct regardless of whether the swap is base-in (`EXACT_IN`-like) or base-out (`EXACT_OUT`-like). [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

There is no "assets array" or "reverse-ordering" convention in this codebase whose positional lookup could be misapplied between the two swap types, so the specific root cause of the Balancer `SwapAction::getSwapToken` bug (returning the wrong end of an asset path array depending on swap type) has no reachable analog here.
