### Title
Flash-loan manipulation of live Aquarius stable-pool reserves depresses the LP oracle price, enabling forced underpriced liquidations - (File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs)

### Summary
The price aggregator derives Aquarius stable-LP collateral prices from the pool contract's *live* `get_reserves()` inside the same transaction that performs risk checks (`contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs:90-93`, `common/src/oracle/lp_stable.rs:96-109`). An unprivileged attacker can fund a one-sided swap on the underlying Aquarius stable pool with a fee-bearing `flash_loan` from the pool itself, drive the stable-swap invariant `D` (and therefore the fair LP price) down within the configured sanity band, and immediately call `controller.liquidate` on a victim whose collateral is that LP token. Because liquidation sizing and the HF gate consume the manipulated price via `Context::fetch_prices`/`calculate_account_risk_totals` (`contracts/controller/src/risk/totals.rs:171-198`), the victim is judged liquidatable at a depressed valuation and the liquidator seizes more real LP shares per USD repaid than fair value warrants, then unwinds the swap and repays the flash loan in one transaction.

### Finding Description
- `read` in `contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs` resolves leg prices via `engine::resolve_nested` but reads `aquarius_pool_reserves_call` and `aquarius_total_shares_call` directly from the live Aquarius pool contract at call time (lines 90-93). There is no TWAP, no historical snapshot, and no reserve-sanity check versus prior state.
- `fair_stable_lp_price_wad` (`common/src/oracle/lp_stable.rs:80-110`) computes `min(pa,pb) * D(reserves) / shares`. `D` solved by `solve_stable_d` is monotonically sensitive to reserve imbalance; pushing one reserve toward depletion collapses `D` toward the constant-product regime, materially lowering the LP fair price. The `swap_cannot_move_the_price` unit test only exercises a moderate ~1.5:1 skew (drift < 1e9 WAD); it does not bound the drift under flash-loan-scale imbalance.
- The controller's `Context` caches whatever the aggregator returns for that call (`contracts/controller/src/context.rs:141-160`), and `liquidate`/`build_liquidation_plan` gate on `health_factor < WAD` computed from those prices. So a single attacker transaction — flash loan → Aquarius swap → liquidate → swap back → repay — sees the depressed price throughout.
- The only residual mitigations are the admin-set sanity band (`min_sanity_price_wad`/`max_sanity_price_wad`) and `min_pool_value_wad`. Both bound *how far* the price can be pushed, not whether it can be pushed at all; a band wide enough to admit normal volatility admits an attacker-chosen depression sufficient to cross HF = 1 for marginal accounts, and reserve skew moves `D` continuously, letting the attacker land the price just inside the band.

### Impact Explanation
Theft of user funds: borrowers holding Aquarius stable-LP collateral who are healthy at fair value become liquidatable at the manipulated price, and seizure computed at that price transfers more collateral shares to the attacker than the repaid debt plus fair bonus justifies. Attack cost is only the flash-loan fee plus swap slippage on the manipulated leg (partially recovered on the unwind). In the extreme, repeated or full-seizure liquidations extract the liquidation bonus and the price-gap differential from victim collateral.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires (a) a listed market whose oracle uses a single-source `AquariusStableLp` source (a dual-source config would fail closed on leg deviation), (b) an Aquarius stable pool shallow enough relative to available flash liquidity that the attacker can push reserves into a strongly imbalanced regime, and (c) a sanity band wide enough to admit the pushed price. All are configuration-dependent but within normal parameterization; the code path is fully reachable by an unprivileged caller (`flash_loan`, own Aquarius swaps, `liquidate` are all permissionless). Rated High rather than Critical because it depends on single-source LP oracle configuration and pool depth.

### Recommendation
- Do not price LP collateral from instantaneous on-pool reserves within the same transaction as risk decisions. Read reserves/amplification from a committed snapshot, or require a minimum observation age for LP oracle reads used by liquidation.
- Add a reserve-imbalance bound to `read`/`attest` (e.g., reject or clamp when `max(xa,xb)/min(xa,xb)` exceeds a threshold), so extreme skew fails closed instead of pricing through `solve_stable_d` at degenerate inputs.
- Prefer tighter `min_sanity_price_wad`/`max_sanity_price_wad` bands for LP-backed markets, and prefer dual-source configs so a manipulated leg triggers `deviation` rejection (INV-ORACLE-02).

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker deploys a WASM receiver implementing `execute_flash_loan`.
2. Victim supplies Aquarius stable-LP token X as collateral in a market whose oracle is a single-source `AquariusStableLp` for that pool, and borrows near the threshold.
3. In one transaction the attacker's contract calls `controller.flash_loan(hub_asset, amount=large, receiver=self)`; inside `execute_flash_loan` it swaps the borrowed leg into the Aquarius stable pool, shifting reserves to a heavy imbalance (e.g., >10:1), reducing `D` and thus `fair_stable_lp_price_wad` below the victim's liquidation trigger but inside the sanity band.
4. Still inside the callback window (or immediately after approving repayment, before any external trade restores balance), the attacker calls `controller.liquidate(caller, victim_account, debt_payments, SeizeMode::Transfer)`. `Context::fetch_prices` pulls the depressed LP price; `calculate_account_risk_totals` yields HF < 1; the plan seizes LP shares sized by the depressed price.
5. The attacker swaps back on Aquarius (recovering most of the slippage), the pool pulls `amount + fee` via `transfer_from`, and the attacker retains seized LP collateral worth more than debt repaid + fair bonus.

Verification steps: register a mock Aquarius pool with `pool_type == "stable"`, seed balanced reserves, list the share token as collateral via `set_oracle` with `PriceSource::AquariusStableLp`, supply it and borrow to HF ≈ 1.05, then in one transaction call `MockAquariusPoolClient::set_reserves` to a skewed tuple (mirroring a flash-funded swap) and invoke `try_liquidate`; observe `HealthFactorTooHigh` no longer fires and seizure exceeds the fair-value amount.
