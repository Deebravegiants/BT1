### Title
Self-liquidation lets an underwater borrower capture the liquidation bonus and offload the uncollateralized deficit onto vault depositors via `socialize-debt` - ([File: mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar])

### Summary
`liquidate()` in `v0-4-market.clar` does not restrict who may act as `liquidator` for a given `borrower` — the only sender check is `(asserts! (is-eq contract-caller tx-sender) ERR-AUTHORIZATION)`, which allows a `borrower` to call `liquidate` against their own position. When a position's collateral is insufficient to fully cover `debt + liq-penalty`, the code caps `coll-actual` at the user's balance and proportionally shrinks the debt that is actually repaid (`calc-final-liquidation-amounts`), while the unrepaid remainder is written off via bad-debt socialization (`debt-remove-scaled` + `socialize-debt-asset` → vault `socialize-debt`), which mutates vault-wide `principal-scaled`/`total-borrowed`/`lindex` to spread the loss across all depositors of that vault.

### Finding Description
In `liquidate` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1382-1608`), collateral is capped at the borrower's actual balance: [1](#0-0) 

and if `coll-actual < coll-expected`, `debt-final-usd` is recomputed downward proportionally to the available collateral (i.e., the liquidator is only required to repay the fraction of debt that the collateral can actually cover, discounted for the penalty), not the full outstanding debt: [2](#0-1) 

The remaining, uncovered debt is removed from the position and — when no collateral remains — socialized onto the vault via `socialize-debt-asset`/`vault-socialize-debt`: [3](#0-2) 

`vault-socialize-debt` writes down `principal-scaled`, `total-borrowed`, and `lindex` for the entire vault (i.e. spreads the loss across all zToken holders of that reserve): [4](#0-3) 

The only sender validation in `liquidate` is: [5](#0-4) 
which confirms `contract-caller == tx-sender`, but never checks `contract-caller != borrower`. This means the delinquent borrower can call `liquidate` on their own account, using `collateral-receiver` defaulting to the caller (themselves): [6](#0-5) 

**Value-identity broken:** the per-user debt ledger vs. vault aggregate. Normally, when a third party liquidates, the discount/bonus captured on `coll-final` (collateral seized in excess of the discounted debt repaid) is transferred to a disinterested party as compensation for performing the liquidation, and any unrecoverable shortfall is socialized as an already-accepted cost of doing business. When the *borrower* is the liquidator, they instead (a) repay only the collateral-capped/discounted debt amount (strictly less than their true outstanding debt), (b) receive their *own* collateral back via `collateral-receiver`, and (c) push the uncovered shortfall onto the vault's `socialize-debt`, which is funded by all depositors' shares. This nets the borrower a gain relative to normal `repay()` (where the full debt must be repaid to reclaim collateral) that is exactly offset by a loss to vault depositors — a deliberate transfer of principal from depositors to the delinquent borrower that a rational borrower can trigger simply by liquidating themselves as soon as their position crosses `ltv-liq-partial` and the collateral gap opens up (`coll-actual < coll-expected`).

### Impact Explanation
This is a transfer of value (principal) from vault liquidity providers to the delinquent borrower, extractable at will once a position is eligible for liquidation and the collateral value cannot cover `debt × (1+penalty)`. It constitutes theft of principal from depositors / protocol insolvency risk, matching the High-impact category (protocol insolvency / theft of funds), consistent with the original report's severity assessment.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is Low, as noted in the original report: it requires the position to already be in the "redistribution" state (collateral value degraded to the point that the discount curve no longer covers the full debt+penalty), which is not the common case, but is fully reachable through normal price movement without needing any privileged action, oracle bug, or DAO compromise — any borrower monitoring their own position can trigger it unilaterally at the right moment.

### Recommendation
Disable self-liquidation (require `contract-caller != borrower` in `liquidate`/`liquidate-multi`/`liquidate-redeem`), or restrict liquidation of positions in the "collateral-insufficient" / redistribution state (where `coll-actual < coll-expected`) to privileged/DAO-controlled liquidator addresses only, so the liquidation-bonus capture and bad-debt socialization cannot be self-triggered by the borrower.

### Proof of Concept
1. Borrower `B` opens a position with collateral C worth $100 and borrows debt D worth $105, at LTV where `liq-penalty-max` implies a required $110+ collateral value to fully cover debt+penalty.
2. Collateral price drops such that `current-ltv >= ltv-liq-partial`, making the position liquidatable, and collateral value remains capped below `coll-expected` for full debt+penalty coverage.
3. `B` calls `liquidate(borrower=B, ..., debt-amount=D, collateral-receiver=none)` themselves (`contract-caller == tx-sender == B`).
4. `process-collateral-asset` caps `coll-actual` at `user-coll-balance` ($100) [1](#0-0) ; `calc-final-liquidation-amounts` shrinks `debt-final-usd` proportionally (≈$90.9 instead of $105) [2](#0-1) .
5. `B` repays only ≈$90.9 of debt tokens (`vault-system-repay`) and receives the full $100 collateral back (`actual-receiver` defaults to `B`) [7](#0-6) .
6. Since `coll-removed` exhausts the position, `no-collateral-left` triggers `socialize-debt-asset`, writing off the remaining ≈$14.1 of debt onto the vault via `socialize-debt` [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) .
7. Net result: `B` spent $90.9 to reclaim $100 of their own collateral (a $9.1 gain, versus a $5 loss under normal full `repay()`), and vault depositors absorb the $14.1 shortfall that `B` chose to crystallize by self-liquidating rather than repaying in full or letting a neutral third party liquidate.

(define-public (socialize-debt (scaled-amount uint))
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (current-assets (var-get assets))
        (current-lindex (var-get lindex))
        (old-total-assets (total-assets))
        (debt-reduction (mul-div-down scaled-amount idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (principal-reduction (if (> scaled-principal u0)
                                (mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal)
                                u0))
        ;; Write down lindex proportionally to loss in total-assets
        (new-lindex (if (and (> old-total-assets u0) (> old-total-assets debt-reduction))
                       (mul-div-down current-lindex (- old-total-assets debt-reduction) old-total-assets)
                       u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (> scaled-amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (var-set lindex new-lindex)
    (var-set principal-scaled (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0))
    (var-set total-borrowed (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0))
    (var-set assets (if (> current-assets principal-reduction) (- current-assets principal-reduction) u0))

    (print {
      action: "socialize-debt",
      caller: contract-caller,
      data: {
        scaled-amount: scaled-amount,
        debt-reduction: debt-reduction,
        principal-reduction: principal-reduction,
        old-lindex: current-lindex,
        new-lindex: new-lindex,
        old-total-assets: old-total-assets,
        principal-scaled: (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0),
        total-borrowed: (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0),
        index: idx
      }
    })

    (ok true)))
