### Title
`liquidate` accepts debt repayment while seizing zero collateral — liquidator pays and receives nothing - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/plan.rs)

### Summary
Analogous to the Swapper report — where a caller's funds are taken while the promised reward is never delivered — the controller's `liquidate` pulls the liquidator's debt-token repayment and burns the borrower's debt even when every collateral seizure leg rounds to zero tokens/shares and is silently dropped. `LiquidationPlan::validate` only iterates `seized` entries and checks the repayment sum; it never requires `seized` to be non-empty relative to `repaid`. The protocol itself warns integrators: "The contract accepts a liquidation that repays debt and seizes nothing, so the liquidator would pay and receive no collateral" (`skills/xoxno-lending-liquidations/SKILL.md:178-180`).

### Finding Description
`build_liquidation_plan` normalizes the liquidator's `debt_payments` into `repaid` legs and computes pro-rata `SeizeEntry` legs via `calculate_seized_collateral`. Legs whose seizure rounds to zero tokens or zero shares are omitted from `seized` rather than causing a revert (`docs/reference/formulas.md:351`, "Zero-share or zero-token planned seizure legs are omitted"; confirmed by `a_leg_whose_seizure_rounds_to_zero_is_skipped_entirely` in `contracts/controller/tests/positions/liquidation_math.rs:1830-1838`). `LiquidationPlan::validate` (`math.rs:54-69`) checks each seized entry's positivity but accepts `seized.len() == 0` with `repaid` non-empty. The only guard that releases the repayment when nothing is seized is gated on `unbacked_usd > 0` (`plan.rs:76-79`), i.e. it only fires when the repayment exceeded what collateral backs — it does not fire when the repayment is "backed" in USD terms but every per-leg token seizure floors to zero. Execution then calls `apply_liquidation_repayments`, which pulls the liquidator's tokens and burns debt shares, and `apply_liquidation_seizures`/`apply_liquidation_share_credit` over an empty vector (`mod.rs:68-94`).

Reachability for an unprivileged address: any `liquidator` can call `liquidate(liquidator, account_id, debt_payments, seize_mode)` on an account with HF < 1. The zero-seizure condition arises legitimately when collateral is a low-decimal, high unit-value asset (listing validation admits `decimals = MIN_ASSET_DECIMALS` and `price_wad = MAX_REASONABLE_PRICE_WAD`): `an_expensive_low_decimal_collateral_makes_a_floor_sized_liquidation_seize_nothing` (`liquidation_math.rs:2361-2402`) shows a $5.45-backed repayment on a 3-decimal $1,000,000/unit asset produces an empty seizure while the repayment "still settles and the debt is still burned". More commonly, any repayment small enough that `seizure_ray_i` rescales below one base unit on every collateral leg produces the same outcome — the analog of the report's "swap less ⇒ reward is missed but funds are flushed anyway".

### Impact Explanation
Theft/loss of caller funds with protocol gain: the liquidator's repayment tokens are transferred to the pool and the borrower's debt is reduced, but the liquidator receives no collateral and no shares — the entire payment is a net transfer of value from the caller to the protocol/borrower. This matches the reported class: caller cannot capture the advertised outcome, yet the value-moving leg still executes.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires an unhealthy account whose collateral legs all floor to zero seizure at the offered size — most plausible on low-decimal/high-price listings (admitted by `validate_sanity_bounds`) or dust-sized partial liquidations. The trigger is a normal `liquidate` call with a modest offer; no privileged action, oracle manipulation, or timing race is needed. Frequency is low but nonzero, and the loss is unconditional once triggered.

### Recommendation
After `calculate_seized_collateral` and `release_unbacked_repayment`, revert when `seized_collaterals.is_empty()` while `repayment.repaid` is non-empty (e.g. `InvalidPayments`), symmetric with the whole-unit-leg rule that already reverts a plan "left with no seizure". Alternatively, drop/trim repayment legs whose collateral allocation rounds to zero seizure before pulling tokens.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance lists collateral `EXP`: `decimals = 3`, `price = MAX_REASONABLE_PRICE_WAD` ($1e9/unit) — admitted by listing validation (`liquidation_math.rs:2365-2385`).
2. Alice supplies 1 unit of EXP (~$1e9)… or, at smaller scale per the checked test, holds collateral worth ~$9 backing $8.57 debt on a $10-per-unit asset; price moves push HF < 1.
3. Liquidator calls `get_liquidation_estimate(account_id, [(EXP_debt, small_amount)], Transfer)` — the plan keeps the repayment leg (it is USD-backed, so `unbacked_usd == 0` and `release_unbacked_repayment` does not fire at `plan.rs:76-79`) but every seizure leg rounds to zero and is omitted.
4. `liquidate` executes: `apply_liquidation_repayments` pulls the offered tokens and burns debt shares; `seized` is empty so no transfer or credit occurs; `LiquidationEvent` reports positive `repaid_usd_wad`. Liquidator balance decreased; received collateral = 0.
