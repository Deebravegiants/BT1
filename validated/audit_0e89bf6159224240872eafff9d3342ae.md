**Reasoning:** `initialize` requires `system_addresses::assert_aptos_framework(aptos_framework)`, meaning only the framework signer (invoked once during genesis) can call it at all [1](#0-0) . There is no unprivileged entrypoint that can call `initialize`, and the `DKGState` mutation functions (`start`, `finish`) are `public(friend)` restricted to `aptos_framework::block` and `aptos_framework::reconfiguration_with_dkg` [2](#0-1) , while `try_clear_incomplete_session` (the only other state-mutating public function) also requires the framework signer [3](#0-2) . Since no unprivileged transaction, package, view, or API can reach `initialize` or corrupt `DKGState` in the first place, the premised "prior exploited race" that leaves a dangling `in_progress` has no unprivileged trigger, and the scenario requires pre-existing framework-level permissions, which is out of scope per the review bounds and decision standard.
