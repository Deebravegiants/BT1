### Title
Direct SPL-token burn of LP tokens desynchronizes `amm.lp_amount` from actual LP supply, permanently freezing a proportional share of pool funds - (File: `program/src/processor.rs`)

### Summary
Raydium's AMM pool tracks total LP supply in an internal field `amm.lp_amount` that is incremented on `Deposit` and decremented on `Withdraw` [1](#0-0) , [2](#0-1) . This field, rather than the LP mint's actual `supply`, is used as the denominator for the pro-rata payout calculation on withdrawal. Because the LP token mint has no freeze authority and LP token accounts are owned by ordinary users, any LP holder can call the standard `spl_token::instruction::burn` directly on their own LP token account, bypassing `process_withdraw` entirely. This decreases the real circulating `lp_mint.supply` without decrementing `amm.lp_amount`, permanently desynchronizing the tracked total from the true outstanding supply — analogous to the reported ERC20Votes issue where a transferable token bypasses intended state updates, breaking an internal "total" invariant with no recovery path.

### Finding Description
- During `Initialize2`, the LP mint is created with `freeze_authority` explicitly required to be `None`, and mint authority set to the AMM `$authority` PDA [3](#0-2) . This means the AMM program controls minting but has no control over burning: burn authority under SPL Token defaults to the token account owner, i.e., the LP holder themselves.
- `process_deposit` mints LP tokens to the user and increments the internal counter: `amm.lp_amount = amm.lp_amount.checked_add(mint_lp_amount).unwrap();` [4](#0-3) 
- `process_withdraw` computes payout using `amm.lp_amount` (not `lp_mint.supply`) as the pool-share denominator:
```
let invariant = InvariantPool {
    token_input: withdraw.amount,
    token_total: amm.lp_amount,
};
``` [5](#0-4) 
  and only decrements `amm.lp_amount` when the withdrawal is routed through this program path via `Invokers::token_burn` [2](#0-1) .
- Because LP tokens are ordinary, freely burnable SPL tokens, any holder can issue a bare `spl_token::instruction::Burn` instruction against their own LP token account outside of the Raydium program. This action is entirely unprivileged, requires only the holder's own signature, and is indistinguishable on-chain from a legitimate holder simply destroying value they own — the AMM program has no way to observe or prevent it, and no instruction exists to resynchronize `amm.lp_amount` to the mint's actual `supply` afterward (`process_set_params` only adjusts status/state/fees/open time [6](#0-5) ).
- After such an out-of-band burn, `lp_mint.supply` drops but `amm.lp_amount` stays inflated. Every subsequent withdrawal by a remaining LP holder is computed as `withdraw.amount / amm.lp_amount * pool_reserves`, i.e., against an artificially large denominator, so it yields a strictly smaller coin/pc payout than the holder's true pro-rata share of the pool. The residual, corresponding to the destroyed LP tokens' rightful claim, remains locked in `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault` forever, since no remaining or future LP token can ever claim it (the sum of all payouts under the inflated denominator can never exhaust the vaults corresponding to the burned share).

### Impact Explanation
This causes a permanent, protocol-level freezing of LP funds: a portion of the pool's coin/pc reserves proportional to any LP tokens burned outside `process_withdraw` becomes permanently unclaimable by any party, and all remaining LP holders receive systematically reduced payouts relative to their fair share. There is no admin or program function capable of correcting `amm.lp_amount` to resynchronize it with actual `lp_mint.supply`, matching the Medium-severity "permanent freezing of user or LP funds" criterion.

### Likelihood Explanation
The action requires only a single, ordinary SPL Token `Burn` instruction signed by the LP token account owner — no privileged signer, no malicious validator, and no non-default build is needed. Any LP holder who wants to gift, burn, or otherwise dispose of their LP position outside the AMM (e.g., by mistake, via a wallet's "burn" feature, or accidentally sending to a burn address then burning) triggers this desync. Given LP tokens are freely transferable/burnable and the AMM places no restriction (no freeze authority, no delegate requirement) on this, the path is straightforward and highly likely to occur over the lifetime of any actively used pool.

### Recommendation
Stop treating `amm.lp_amount` as an independently authoritative value; instead derive the pool-share denominator directly from the LP mint's live `supply` (`lp_mint.supply`) at withdrawal time in `process_withdraw`, or reconcile `amm.lp_amount` against `lp_mint.supply` before computing `InvariantPool`. Alternatively, prevent all LP burns outside program control (e.g., by requiring the AMM `$authority` to co-sign/delegate burns), or add a permissionless "sync" instruction that lets anyone update `amm.lp_amount = lp_mint.supply` when they diverge, ensuring pro-rata math always reflects real circulating LP supply.

### Proof of Concept
1. Two LPs, Alice and Bob, each deposit into a fresh pool; `amm.lp_amount` and `lp_mint.supply` both equal the sum of their minted LP tokens (e.g., 200 total, 100 each).
2. Bob, instead of calling Raydium's `Withdraw` instruction, submits a plain SPL Token program instruction `Burn { amount: 100 }` on his own LP token account (he is account owner, no delegate/freeze needed). This destroys his 100 LP tokens: `lp_mint.supply` becomes 100, but `amm.lp_amount` remains 200 (unchanged, since `process_withdraw` was never invoked) [7](#0-6) .
3. Alice now calls `Withdraw` with `amount = 100` (her full balance). The payout is computed as `InvariantPool{ token_input: 100, token_total: amm.lp_amount = 200 }.exchange_pool_to_token(total_coin_without_take_pnl, ...)` [5](#0-4) , giving her only ~50% of the pool's coin/pc reserves, even though she now holds 100% of the real circulating LP supply.
4. `amm.lp_amount` is decremented to 100 after her withdrawal [8](#0-7) , but the remaining ~50% of vault reserves that should have been hers are permanently stranded in `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault` with no outstanding LP token able to claim them, since `lp_mint.supply` is now 0 and no `SetParams` path can reset `amm.lp_amount` to match.
