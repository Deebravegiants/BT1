The reported issue concerns a lending protocol's collateral liquidation logic — specifically that a lender can seize a borrower's entire collateral position via `CollateralManager.withdraw` even when only a fraction of the debt remains outstanding, rather than liquidating collateral proportional to the unpaid amount.

Raydium AMM has no lending, borrowing, collateral, or liquidation concept anywhere in its instruction set. The only reachable, unprivileged instructions are `Initialize2`, `Deposit`, `Withdraw`, `SwapBaseIn`/`SwapBaseOut` (and V2 variants) [1](#0-0) . Its `Withdraw` instruction burns a user's own LP tokens and returns a proportional share of the pool's coin/pc reserves computed via `InvariantPool::exchange_pool_to_token`, based on the caller's own LP balance — it never allows one party to seize another party's assets in full, since `user_source_lp.owner != *source_lp_owner_info.key` is checked and the withdrawal amount is bounded by the caller's own LP holdings [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) . There is no analogous "borrower defaults, lender takes all collateral regardless of partial repayment" pattern in this codebase, so the reported bug class does not map to any in-scope Raydium AMM instruction reachable by an unprivileged swapper, liquidity provider, or pool creator.
