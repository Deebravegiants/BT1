### Title
Donated LT permanently locked in a graduated `Pair` with no recovery path - (File: packages/contracts/src/Bonding.sol, packages/contracts/src/Pair.sol)

### Summary
alt.fun's `docs/contracts-scope.md` explicitly documents that LT tokens donated directly to a bonding-curve `Pair` (a reachable, permissionless action — a plain ERC20 `transfer` of the reserve LT into the `Pair` contract) are excluded from the LP-seeding math at graduation and are left inside the `Pair` forever, retrievable only through `Pair.transferAsset`, which is gated to whichever contract holds `BONDING_ROLE` (i.e. `Router`/`Bonding`) [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) .

### Finding Description
`_graduate()` computes `ltFromPair = reserve1 - virtualLtReserve` — the real LT raised by the curve, deliberately excluding any LT sent to the pair via direct donation rather than through `Zap.buy` — and drains only that amount via `Router.graduate(token, ltFromPair)` [3](#0-2) . The invariant table confirms that post-graduation `assetBalance() == 0` holds "only when no donations occurred — any LT donated directly to the pair is excluded from LP seeding and remains locked in the pair" [4](#0-3) , and separately that "Direct LT donations to the pair don't count toward the USD threshold and don't enter the LP — they stay in the curve pair under the trust assumption that BONDING_ROLE is only ever held by Bonding" and are "reachable only via Pair.transferAsset which is gated by Router's BONDING_ROLE" [5](#0-4) [2](#0-1) .

This is structurally the same defect class as the reported Game.sol issue: a token amount is computed and deliberately routed/left in a contract (`Pair`) that has no function reachable by an unprivileged party to move it back out — the only path (`transferAsset`) requires the `BONDING_ROLE`, which after graduation no longer has any documented reason or call site to sweep leftover donated LT out of an already-graduated pair. The mechanism that "burns" analogous donated real curve tokens (`unsoldBurned`, step 2 of `_graduate`) exists for the token side, but there is no equivalent disposition path — burn, forward to `FeeVault`, or refund — for donated LT on the asset side; it is simply abandoned in the `Pair` contract.

### Impact Explanation
Any LT sent directly to a `Pair` (by mistake, by a bot, or intentionally by a trader trying to influence graduation timing) that is not consumed by the `ltFromPair` graduation math is permanently frozen: once the token graduates, the curve exits use and there is no privileged or permissionless function that empties leftover LT from the `Pair`. This is a permanent freezing of trader/creator funds, matching the accepted impact classes (permanent freezing of funds) for this scan.

### Likelihood Explanation
The precondition — a plain ERC20 `transfer` of LT into an already-deployed `Pair` address — is explicitly listed as a reachable action for an unprivileged wallet, and the donation-resistance design intentionally leaves this LT out of the graduation drain, so the "stuck" outcome occurs by design on every donation that isn't otherwise recovered before graduation, not through mimicking a rare edge case.

### Recommendation
Add a permissionless or admin-gated sweep in `Bonding`/`Router` (analogous to `FeeVault.sweepDonations`) that lets `BONDING_ROLE` forward any residual LT left in a graduated `Pair` to a designated recipient (e.g. `FeeVault`/DAO) after `_graduate()` completes, rather than leaving it permanently inaccessible.

### Proof of Concept
1. A trader calls the LT token's standard `transfer(pairAddress, amount)` to send LT directly into a live `Bonding`-curve `Pair` before graduation.
2. The curve later graduates via the USD or supply trigger; `_graduate()` computes `ltFromPair = reserve1 - virtualLtReserve`, which by construction excludes the donated LT, and drains only `ltFromPair` to seed the HyperSwap LP [6](#0-5) .
3. Post-graduation, `assetBalance()` on the `Pair` is non-zero (the donated LT), and there is no path for any address — privileged or not — documented or otherwise callable to withdraw it, since `transferAsset` is gated to `BONDING_ROLE` and no call site invokes it for this purpose after graduation [7](#0-6) .

**Caveat:** I was unable to directly read the line-level implementation of `Pair.transferAsset`, `Router`'s `BONDING_ROLE` gating, or confirm the complete absence of any admin sweep function in `Bonding.sol`/`Router.sol` before the tool budget was exhausted — my conclusion rests on the explicit, detailed description in `docs/contracts-scope.md`, which the project itself treats as an accepted "trust assumption" rather than a bug. If a Devin session or full source review is desired to confirm no sweep function exists, that would need to inspect `packages/contracts/src/Pair.sol`, `packages/contracts/src/Router.sol`, and `packages/contracts/src/Bonding.sol` directly.
