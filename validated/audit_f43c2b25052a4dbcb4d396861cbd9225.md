### Title
Insolvent account with debt in a high unit-value token and collateral above the $5 dust cap is permanently unliquidatable and uncleanable without governance - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs)

### Summary
CVE-2022-21607 is a hang/crash (denial-of-service) class bug. Its analog in XOXNO Lending is a permissionlessly reachable state where **every** liquidation of an insolvent account reverts and the permissionless bad-debt cleanup simultaneously refuses the account, so the bad debt can only be cleared by the owner-gated `force_socialize_bad_debt` path. Two independent integer-rounding/dust rules that are each safe in isolation combine into a gap band that bricks all unprivileged exit paths.

### Finding Description
On an insolvent account (`total_collateral < total_debt`), `normalize_repayment_plan` in `contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs` selects the insolvency quote `floor(C / (1 + base_bonus))` and then calls `process_excess_payment` with `keep_within_quote = true`, which floors each kept repayment leg down to **whole debt-token units** and drops any leg whose kept amount rounds to zero [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) . When the collateral-backed quote is worth less than one base unit of the account's only debt token, every offer — of any size — is trimmed to zero legs and `liquidate` reverts with `InvalidPayments` (16) [3](#0-2) .

Separately, permissionless `clean_bad_debt` requires `total_debt > total_collateral && total_collateral <= BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` ($5 WAD) [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) .

The gap band is therefore: a debt token whose **one base unit is worth more than `C / (1 + base)` while `C > $5`**. For a 3-decimal token priced at $10,000 (one unit = $10), any insolvent account with collateral in the range `$5 < C < $10 × (1 + base)` cannot be liquidated at all and cannot be permissionlessly cleaned. The whole-unit rescue in `whole_unit_repayment` does not apply — it only runs on the solvent branch (`!insolvent`), and only when the *collateral* leg is sub-3-decimal [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) . The in-call post-liquidation bad-debt check never fires because no liquidation ever executes.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing of funds / accruing protocol insolvency reachable by a single unprivileged borrower. The stuck account's debt is denominated in a listed market whose suppliers' claims are backed by cash that was borrowed; because the debt can never be repaid by liquidation nor written down by `clean_bad_debt`, the market carries phantom supply claims. The only recovery is `force_socialize_bad_debt`, an owner-only governance operation on the Sensitive delay tier [8](#0-7)  — i.e., routine market conditions (a $6–$9.50 collateral remnant against a ~$10/unit debt token) force a privileged manual intervention and until then suppliers of that market cannot fully withdraw. An attacker can also park such an account deliberately for negligible cost, since the required collateral is only ~$5–$10.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable with ordinary user actions: `supply` + `borrow` a listed low-decimal, high unit-price debt token, then let interest accrual or a small adverse price move push `C` below `D` while `C` stays above $5 — or partially liquidate a larger position down into the band (each successful liquidation trims collateral and debt proportionally and can land in the gap). No privileged call, oracle manipulation, or non-standard token is needed; only a listing whose unit price exceeds the dust threshold divided by the base bonus factor. The band widens linearly with the debt token's unit price, so expensive-unit listings make the stuck state likely rather than exceptional.

### Recommendation
Close the gap on one side of the pair: either (a) when `insolvent` and the kept trim would drop every repayment leg, promote the plan to a full-debt close at the collateral-backed price (accept that the liquidator may overpay by less than one unit, or seize `seize_all` collateral for a partial unit payment), or (b) make the dust gate in `is_socializable_bad_debt` unit-aware — e.g., admit collateral at or below `max($5, one_base_unit_usd_of_each_debt_leg / (1 + base))` — so a debt whose smallest repayable unit exceeds the backed quote remains permissionlessly cleanable. Option (b) is the smaller change and preserves the "liquidator never pays more than it seizes" property of `process_excess_payment`.

### Proof of Concept
Already encoded in `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/liquidation_extreme.rs::test_insolvent_liquidation_reverts_when_no_debt_unit_fits_the_backed_quote` (lines 653–745): 1,000 COL collateral priced at $0.009 ($9 total), debt of 2 EXP units where one EXP unit is worth $10.

1. `supply(ALICE, COL, 1000)`; `borrow(ALICE, EXP, 0.002)` (2 units, $20 face); `set_price(COL, $0.009)` → `C = $9 < D`, account insolvent.
2. Backed quote: `floor(9 / 1.05) ≈ $8.57 < $10` (one EXP unit). `process_excess_payment` floors the kept EXP leg to 0 units, drops it, refunds the whole offer.
3. `get_liquidation_estimate` returns `max_payment_wad == 0`, empty `seized_collaterals`; `try_liquidate` reverts `InvalidPayments` for **any** offered amount — including the full 2-unit debt [9](#0-8) .
4. `clean_bad_debt` reverts `CannotCleanBadDebt` because `total_collateral = $9 > BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD = $5` [4](#0-3) .
5. No unprivileged entrypoint (`liquidate`, `clean_bad_debt`, `repay` by third parties does not clear collateral) can ever remove the position; only governance `force_socialize_bad_debt` admits it via `BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly` [5](#0-4) .

The borrower incurs only its locked ~$9 collateral; the protocol inherits an unclosable ~$20 hole in the EXP market.

# Force-socialize bad debt

This runbook covers governance-authorized removal of an insolvent account.
Use it when collateral exceeds the fixed $5 dust limit of permissionless
`clean_bad_debt`. Cleanup can also proceed when `no_seize` blocks ordinary
liquidation.

The account must hold debt, with ceil risk debt strictly greater than half-up
unweighted collateral (`D > C`). An unhealthy health factor alone does not
qualify. The forced path has no collateral dust cap.

**Cleanup is irreversible.** All remaining collateral becomes protocol revenue.
All remaining debt is written off against its own markets' supply indexes,
including when collateral and debt share a market. There is no automatic netting
or insurance payment. The account is deleted and its position NFT burns.

## Before scheduling

1. Confirm the network, target controller, governance contract, account id,
   NFT owner, positions and spoke. `force_socialize_bad_debt` is owner-only.
   Governance, the controller owner, schedules `ForceSocializeBadDebt` on the
   Sensitive delay tier; follow the
   [governance interface](../endpoints.md#governance) for proposal and execution.
