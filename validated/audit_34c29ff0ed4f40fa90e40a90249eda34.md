### Title
Dust collateral donation evades permissionless `clean_bad_debt`, forcing the timelocked forced-socialize path while bad debt accrues - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs)

### Summary
`clean_bad_debt` is permissionless but gated by `BadDebtGate::DustCapped`: it only admits an insolvent account whose total collateral is at or below the fixed $5 dust threshold (`is_socializable_bad_debt`). Anyone can supply into an existing `account_id` (the GH-22 third-party-top-up path), so a griefer — most cheaply, the underwater borrower from a second address — can donate a few cents of collateral to push the account's half-up unweighted collateral just above the threshold. Every subsequent `clean_bad_debt` call reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt`, exactly as `Cure.cage()`'s premature `live = 0` flip blocks `End.cage()`: a small, reachable state change disables the intended fast path and forces the slower coordinated one.

### Finding Description
In `process_clean_bad_debt`, any authenticated caller may trigger cleanup, but `socialize_bad_debt` requires `is_socializable_bad_debt(total_debt, total_collateral)`, i.e. collateral ≤ the compile-time ~$5 dust cap, per docs "permissionless cleanup requires collateral at or below the fixed $5 dust threshold" (`docs/reference/invariants.md` INV-LIQ-04). Supply is not restricted to the account owner: `supply(caller, account_id, …)` credits the target account (confirmed by `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/third_party_supply_and_risk_restamp.rs`, where CAROL's one-stroop top-up lands on ALICE's account). The only fallback is `force_socialize_bad_debt`, which is owner-only and routed through governance's `ForceSocializeBadDebt` op on the Sensitive delay tier (`docs/reference/runbooks/force-socialize-bad-debt.md`). A donation sized `collateral_target = $5 + ε` therefore flips the account out of the permissionless regime with cost ≈ $5 (recoverable only as protocol revenue at cleanup, i.e. destroyed), and can be re-applied after every accidental price dip back under the cap.

### Impact Explanation
The permissionless cleanup path is permanently blocked for the targeted account; socialization must wait for propose → Sensitive-delay → execute of `ForceSocializeBadDebt`. During that delay the dead debt keeps accruing borrow interest, enlarging the supply-index write-down borne by that market's suppliers (ADR-0012), and the attacker can re-donate dust to any account that drifts back under $5 before the op executes, repeatedly invalidating eligibility rechecks the runbook demands ("another repayment or liquidation can change eligibility"). The result is a griefing/insolvency-amplification vector: realized supplier losses grow with time-to-cleanup, and the fast mitigation lane is denied by design-level state, not by flags or pause.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires only a standard `supply` call by any address against a known insolvent `account_id` — no roles, no flash loan, no oracle manipulation. Cost is bounded by the dust cap itself (~$5 per eviction), and the owner of the insolvent position is naturally incentivized to do it to delay revenue seizure of their residual collateral. Accounts routinely sit insolvent with small residual collateral after partial liquidations, so eligible targets occur in normal operation.

### Recommendation
- Gate `supply` into an existing `account_id` on the recipient's consent for accounts that are already insolvent (e.g. reject third-party top-ups when `D > C`), or
- Base the `DustCapped` admission on collateral excluding supply legs added after insolvency was first observable (record an "insolvency epoch"/block at first failed HF), or
- Raise the permissionless dust cap / make cleanup eligibility monotonic: once an account qualifies as `DustCapped`, persist eligibility so later dust cannot un-admit it.

### Proof of Concept
1. ALICE's account holds $4 residual collateral and $20 debt → `clean_bad_debt(keeper, acct)` would succeed.
2. Mallory calls `supply(mallory, acct, spoke, [(hub_asset, ~$1.50)])`; collateral becomes $5.50.
3. `clean_bad_debt(keeper, acct)` reverts `CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt` (error #114) — see `process_clean_bad_debt` → `BadDebtGate::DustCapped` → `is_socializable_bad_debt` at `contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs:196-235`, and the live-denial assertion `fs_permissionless_denied` in `tests/integration/flows/liquidation.sh:360`.
4. Governance must schedule and wait out `ForceSocializeBadDebt`; interest accrues to the written-down market, and Mallory re-donates dust whenever accrual/price moves collateral back under $5 before execution.
