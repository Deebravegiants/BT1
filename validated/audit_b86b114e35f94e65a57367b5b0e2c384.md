### Title
Stale `account.owner` grants the previous NFT holder spending authority after position transfer - (File: contracts/controller/src/account.rs)

### Summary
The DTML improper-authentication class — authorization decided from a stale/cached identity rather than the current authoritative one — maps onto the controller's account guards. `borrow`, `withdraw`, `multiply`, `flash_position`, `swap_debt`, `swap_collateral`, `repay_debt_with_collateral` and `migrate_from_blend` all authenticate the caller via `require_owner_or_delegate(env, account_id, caller, &account.owner)`, where `account.owner` is the address stored in `Account` at creation time (`create_account`, account.rs:64-65). Nothing in the NFT transfer path updates that stored field: `position-nft` `transfer`/`transfer_from` are stock OpenZeppelin enumerable methods with no controller callback, and the controller's separate `require_account_owner` (account.rs:143-148) reads the live NFT owner via `storage::account_owner` — a different source of truth. After a position NFT changes hands, the account's spending authority remains bound to the seller.

### Finding Description
`create_account` writes `owner` into the `Account` struct once, at mint (contracts/controller/src/account.rs:64-71). `load_or_create_account` and the position entrypoints then authorize by comparing `caller` against that stored `account.owner` or against delegates listed under `storage::get_delegates(env, account_id, owner)` for that stored owner (account.rs:101-108, 114-140). The documented auth model (`contracts/controller/README.md` Authorization section, and `scripts/permissionless_entrypoints.txt` lines 49-58) states these entrypoints are pinned to "the account owner", meaning the NFT holder. But `require_owner_or_delegate` never consults the NFT; only `require_account_owner` does (account.rs:143-147, reading `storage::account_owner`). The permissionless manifest itself acknowledges the NFT owner is the account owner and that "delegate grants from the previous owner go inactive" (line 111) — yet the delegate set is keyed under the stored `account.owner`, so after a transfer the previous owner still satisfies `caller == owner` directly, delegate or not.

### Impact Explanation
An unprivileged attacker sells or transfers a funded account's position NFT (via `position-nft::transfer` or an `approve`/`transfer_from` chain — both reachable by any holder). The stored `account.owner` still names the attacker, so the attacker can call `withdraw(account_id, ...)` or `borrow(account_id, ..., to: attacker)` and drain every collateral asset and all borrowable liquidity the position supports, up to the health-factor floor. The buyer receives an account whose collateral can be stolen at will, and can themselves be blocked from `withdraw`/`borrow` because `caller != account.owner` and they are not in the seller's delegate set — theft of user funds plus freezing of the victim's position control. Severity: High.

### Likelihood Explanation
The trigger is an ordinary NFT transfer, reachable by any unprivileged holder via `position-nft::transfer`/`transfer_from`/`approve` (in-scope per the rules). No governance action, oracle manipulation, or privileged role is required; any account holder who lists or gifts the NFT leaves the stored owner stale. The only caveat: I could not fully confirm whether `storage::account_owner` is ever used to re-stamp `account.owner` on position entrypoints — the search shows no such sync in `contracts/controller/src`, and the existence of two distinct ownership sources (stored `account.owner` vs. live NFT owner) is itself the defect.

### Recommendation
Treat the NFT `owner_of(account_id)` as the single source of truth for spending authority: in `require_owner_or_delegate` (or at each call site), resolve the owner via `storage::account_owner` exactly as `require_account_owner` does, and key delegate lists under the resolved live owner. Alternatively, have `transfer`/`transfer_from` in `position-nft` invoke a controller hook that rewrites `account.owner` and clears the previous owner's delegate entries atomically.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice calls `supply` with `account_id = 0`; an NFT is minted and `account.owner = alice` (account.rs:64-65).
2. Alice calls `position-nft::transfer(alice, bob, token_id)`; ownership of the NFT moves to Bob. `account.owner` remains `alice` — no sync path exists in the controller.
3. Alice calls `controller::withdraw(caller=alice, account_id, withdrawals=[all collateral], to=alice)`. `require_owner_or_delegate` returns at `caller == account.owner` (account.rs:121-122) and the collateral leaves to Alice.
4. Bob calls `withdraw` on his own account; `is_owner_or_delegate` fails (`bob != account.owner`, not in Alice's delegate set under `get_delegates(account_id, alice)`), reverting with `NotAuthorized` (account.rs:139).
