### Title
Borrower can block their own liquidation by supplying dust of a `no_seize` asset — (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/plan.rs)

### Summary
XOXNO Lending's liquidation seizes collateral **pro-rata across every supply position** of the unhealthy account, and `build_liquidation_plan` reverts the entire plan if *any* seized leg targets a listing flagged `no_seize`. Because `no_seize` does not gate deposits (entry only rejects `paused`/`frozen`), a borrower facing liquidation can permissionlessly `supply` a dust amount of any `no_seize`-flagged asset into their own account. From that point, every `liquidate` call reverts during plan validation, so the borrower's collateral can never be seized while the flag persists. The invariants documentation confirms the behavior: "Nonzero `no_seize` collateral can block the whole proportional liquidation, even if supplied after the flag is set" (INV-HALT-02).

### Finding Description
`process_liquidation` builds its plan via `build_liquidation_plan`. After computing `proportion_seized` and enumerating `seized_collaterals` pro-rata over **all** of the account's supply positions, the plan iterates every seizure leg and calls `enforce_spoke_asset_flags(env, cache, account.spoke_id, &entry.hub_asset, FreezePolicy::SeizureLeg)` (plan.rs lines 81–89). A `no_seize` flag on any leg's hub asset makes this call revert, aborting the whole liquidation — there is no mechanism to skip or drop poisoned legs.

On the supply side, `supply`/`finalize_position_flow` only enforces `FreezePolicy::AllowOnExit`-style entry checks that reject `paused` and `frozen`; `no_seize` does not block new deposits. So a borrower whose account is liquidatable can call `supply` with a minimal (e.g., 1 stroop) amount of a `no_seize` asset — even after the flag was set — adding a permanent poison leg to their collateral set. Liquidators cannot work around it: the plan is computed from the account's positions, not from liquidator-supplied parameters, and `SeizeMode::Transfer` vs `Credit` does not change the seizure-leg enumeration.

This is the direct analog of the Beedle `buyLoan` reset: a cheap, permissionless self-action by the borrower that makes the position structurally unliquidatable.

### Impact Explanation
- **Temporary freezing of funds / liquidation DoS**: while any `no_seize` listing exists in the spoke, an affected borrower can keep their account unseizable indefinitely, regardless of health factor. Lenders/suppliers cannot recover collateral through `liquidate`.
- **Protocol insolvency risk**: if the account's debt grows (interest accrual, collateral price decline) while liquidation is blocked, the position drifts into bad debt. The only remaining resolution is `clean_bad_debt`/`force_socialize_bad_debt`, which socialize the loss onto suppliers via `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` — i.e., the borrower's dust-deposit converts a normal profitable liquidation into a socialized loss.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires only that the spoke contains at least one listing with `no_seize` set — a legitimate configuration state the protocol supports (flags are independent per INV-HALT-02). The attack costs the borrower a dust deposit plus fees, needs no privileged role, no timing race, and can be repeated/prepositioned. It can also be done preemptively before becoming unhealthy, since the flag check is at liquidation time, not supply time.

### Recommendation
In `build_liquidation_plan`, do not revert on `no_seize` legs. Either:
- omit `no_seize` assets from `seized_collaterals` and rescale the seizure over the remaining seizable collateral (or cap the recovered amount at the seizable collateral's value), or
- keep the pro-rata plan but skip-and-rescale flagged legs so a single flagged dust position cannot veto the entire liquidation.

Alternatively, block *new* supply of `no_seize` assets (extend the entry flag check to cover `no_seize`) **and** treat pre-existing flagged collateral as excluded from the seizure plan — blocking supply alone is insufficient because the docs note collateral supplied before the flag also blocks liquidation.

### Proof of Concept
Sequence (all unprivileged, assuming spoke contains an asset `NS` flagged `no_seize`):

1. Borrower opens account via `supply` of collateral `C`, then `borrow` of asset `D`.
2. Price of `C` drops; `calculate_account_risk_totals` yields `health_factor < 1 WAD` — account is liquidatable.
3. Borrower calls `supply` with a dust payment of `NS` (e.g., 1 unit) to their own `account_id`. `no_seize` does not block entry, so the position is added to `account.supply_positions`.
4. Any liquidator calls `liquidate(liquidator, account_id, debt_payments, SeizeMode::Transfer)`.
5. `build_liquidation_plan` computes `seized_collaterals` pro-rata, which now includes an `NS` leg; the loop at plan.rs:81–89 hits `enforce_spoke_asset_flags(..., FreezePolicy::SeizureLeg)` for `NS` and reverts the transaction.
6. Repeat for `SeizeMode::Credit` — same revert. The account cannot be liquidated while the `NS` position exists and the flag is set; the borrower never withdraws `NS`.
