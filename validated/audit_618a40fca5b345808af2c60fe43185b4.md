### Title
Excess native-token overpayment in `EvmHost.dispatch()` is permanently trapped in the host contract - (File: `evm/src/core/EvmHost.sol`)

### Summary
`EvmHost.dispatch(DispatchPost)` and `EvmHost.dispatch(DispatchGet)` accept `msg.value` as native-token fee payment and forward the entire `msg.value` to Uniswap's `swapETHForExactTokens`, but the leftover ETH that Uniswap refunds is never routed back to the original caller — it settles permanently on the `EvmHost` contract itself, mirroring the "value locked due to mismatched fee/amount accounting" pattern in the referenced Sublime `PoolFactory` finding.

### Finding Description
When a caller pays dispatch fees with native token, `EvmHost.dispatch()` executes: [1](#0-0) 

`swapETHForExactTokens{value: msg.value}(post.fee, path, address(this), block.timestamp)` computes the exact ETH input needed (`amounts[0]`) for `post.fee` fee-tokens and, per the standard Uniswap V2 Router implementation, refunds `msg.value - amounts[0]` to `msg.sender` of that call. Because `EvmHost` itself is the direct caller of the router (not a delegatecall/passthrough), the refund's `msg.sender` is `EvmHost`, not the original app/user who sent the ETH. Any callers upstream — `IntentGatewayV2.placeOrder` (which forwards `msgValue` to `IDispatcher(hostAddr).dispatch{value: msgValue}`), `WrappedHyperFungibleToken(Upgradeable).send`, `HyperbridgeLzEndpoint.send`, or any third-party `HyperApp` using `dispatch{value: msg.value}` — have no way to recover this refunded excess since it lands as bare ETH inside `EvmHost` with no tracked accounting and (from the reviewed contract) no corresponding sweep/withdraw path for native ETH. [2](#0-1) 
The identical pattern repeats in the GET-request dispatch function.

This is structurally analogous to the Sublime report: the contract computes an `amount`/fee based on one parameter (`post.fee` in feeToken) while `msg.value` (native ETH) is a separate, loosely-related quantity, and the mismatch (overpayment) results in ETH being stranded in the contract instead of being refunded to the payer.

### Impact Explanation
Any user or integrating contract that slightly overestimates the native fee required for `dispatch()` (e.g. due to price movement between quoting `nativeFee` off-chain/on-chain and the actual swap execution, or simply sending a safety margin) permanently loses the excess ETH into `EvmHost`. Given `dispatch()` is the single unprivileged entry point used by essentially all Hyperbridge apps (token bridges, intents, LayerZero endpoint) for native-fee payment, this is reachable from an ordinary unprivileged dispatch transaction and results in permanent freezing of user funds, satisfying the "permanent freezing of funds" impact bar.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is significant: native-fee quoting is inherently imprecise since the exact Uniswap price at execution time can differ from the quoted `nativeFee`, and all documented usage patterns (see `docs/content/developers/evm/messaging/post-requests.mdx`) tell integrators to send `msg.value` covering fees, without any indication that overpayment is unsafe. Every `dispatch{value: ...}` caller across the codebase (`IntentGatewayV2`, `WrappedHyperFungibleToken*`, `HyperbridgeLzEndpoint`) is exposed to this same trap whenever `msg.value` exceeds the exact `amounts[0]` computed by the router.

### Recommendation
After the `swapETHForExactTokens` call in both `dispatch(DispatchPost)` and `dispatch(DispatchGet)`, capture the returned `amounts` array and forward the router's actual ETH refund to `msg.sender` (i.e., either avoid delegating the refund destination to the router and instead pass `address(this)`/track balances explicitly and forward the delta with `.call{value: delta}("")`, or add a public sweep function limited to refunding the exact excess to the calling app/EOA in the same transaction).

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker/user computes `nativeFee` for a `post.fee` amount using a stale/slightly-high quote and calls `dispatchWithNative` (or any `HyperApp` wrapper) with `msg.value = nativeFee`.
2. `EvmHost.dispatch()` at [3](#0-2)  calls `swapETHForExactTokens{value: msg.value}(post.fee, ...)`.
3. Uniswap V2 Router computes `amounts[0] < msg.value` and refunds `msg.value - amounts[0]` to its caller, `EvmHost`.
4. The refunded ETH increases `EvmHost`'s native balance but is never credited back to the original payer nor is it withdrawable through any fee-token-oriented accounting reviewed in `EvmHost.sol`, permanently freezing that portion of the user's funds.
