### Title
LP collateral holder can permanently shield bad debt by draining the referenced Aquarius pool below `min_pool_value_wad` - ([File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs](contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs))

### Summary
Analogous to the JSSE partial-DoS class (a remote, unauthenticated availability failure): the price aggregator hard-fails any Aquarius LP price whose pool value drops below a stored `min_pool_value_wad` floor. Because controller valuation is fail-closed per leg, a single unpriced dust collateral leg poisons health-factor computation for the whole account — liquidation, `clean_bad_debt`, and even owner `force_socialize_bad_debt` all revert on that account. An unprivileged Aquarius LP can withdraw their own liquidity to push the pool under the floor, bricking liquidation of every account holding that LP token as collateral and converting their own undercollateralized borrow into permanent bad debt.

### Finding Description
`providers::aquarius::read` computes `pool_value_wad = price_wad * total_shares / share_unit` and returns `Err(InsufficientAquariusLiquidity)` when it falls below `lp.min_pool_value_wad` — a configured-but-attacker-influenceable threshold. The error is unconditional; there is no fallback observation. On the controller side, `Context::cached_price`/`cached_market_index` feed `risk::position_value` inside `calculate_seized_collateral` and the health-factor snapshot, so a single failing leg aborts the entire pro-rata liquidation plan rather than skipping that leg. The threat model itself registers this as DoS.1: a dust leg of any listed collateral lets the borrower "choose which feed outage shields the account," and the same leg blocks bad-debt cleanup and force-socialization.

### Impact Explanation
Protocol insolvency: an account whose debt exceeds collateral cannot be liquidated (every `liquidate` call reverts inside price resolution) and cannot be cleaned (`clean_bad_debt` / `force_socialize_bad_debt` evaluate collateral USD through the same strict path). Interest keeps accruing on the uncollectable debt until socialization is impossible without repricing — i.e., the bad debt persists and suppliers bear the shortfall via the pool's backing checks. Any third-party supplier holding the same LP collateral also has their liquidations bricked — temporary freezing of their collateral-backed exit. The attacker keeps both the borrowed assets and the LP collateral.

### Likelihood Explanation
Fully unprivileged and on-chain: the attacker only needs to (a) supply a dust amount of the Aquarius LP share as a collateral leg, (b) borrow, and (c) withdraw their own LP position (or buy LP shares and redeem) to push `pool_value_wad` under `min_pool_value_wad`. Pool-value floors are necessarily set near real liquidity levels, so the required withdrawal is bounded by the floor, not the whole pool. No privileged role, oracle signer, or timing assumption is needed; medium severity given the prerequisite of LP exposure and collateral listing.

### Recommendation
Make LP collateral valuation fail-soft: exclude a leg whose LP source returns `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity`/`NoLastPrice` from the pro-rata seizure plan and health-factor denominator instead of aborting (or seize it at a floor price / transfer it whole in `Credit` mode), so dust-priced legs cannot veto liquidation of the remaining collateral. Alternatively, let `clean_bad_debt`/`force_socialize_bad_debt` skip unpriced legs and socialize the debt against the priced residue.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker provides liquidity to Aquarius pool `P` and receives share token `S` (listed as collateral via `AquariusLpSource { pool: P, min_pool_value_wad: F, .. }`).
2. `controller.supply(attacker, id, spoke, [(hub(P_share_market), dust_S)])`, then `controller.borrow(attacker, id, [(USDC_market, X)])` against other collateral.
3. Collateral price drops (or attacker structures the account) so HF < 1.
4. Attacker calls `P.withdraw_liquidity` removing their share of reserves so `2*sqrt(v_a*v_b)` (or stable equivalent) times share price over supply < `F`.
5. `liquidator.liquidate(attacker_id, payments, Transfer)` → `aquarius::read` hits `pool_value_wad < lp.min_pool_value_wad` → `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity` → whole plan reverts. Same for `clean_bad_debt(attacker_id, ...)` and `force_socialize_bad_debt`.
6. Result: debt is unseizable and unsocializable while the oracle floor holds — sustained as long as the attacker keeps pool value below `F`. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2)
