The reported bug class is specific to an Optimism L1↔L2 bridge adapter design: a message-replay mechanism where a failed `mint` call can revert after the bridged token's minter role has been revoked during a native-USDC migration, permanently locking funds on L1. Raydium's on-chain program has no analogous architecture — there is no L1/L2 messaging, no message-replay path, no minter-role-revocation-during-migration flow, and no cross-chain token bridging logic anywhere in the reachable instruction set (`Initialize2`, `Deposit`, `Withdraw`, the four swap instructions) or in the SPL CPIs in `invokers.rs` [1](#0-0) . The program's only mint-authority-related operations are the LP-mint `token_mint_to` calls performed directly by the AMM authority during `Initialize2`/`Deposit`, which are synchronous SPL CPIs with no cross-chain retry/replay semantics [2](#0-1) . The `AmmStatus` enum governs local pause states (`Disabled`, `WithdrawOnly`, etc.) but this is unrelated to bridged-token minter-permission revocation or message replay [3](#0-2) .

No mapping to concrete theft, permanent freezing via replay, unbacked LP minting, or insolvent pool accounting exists for this bug class in the in-scope surface.
