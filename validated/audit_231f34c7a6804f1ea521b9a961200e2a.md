### Title
Relay flat fee can be paid in an arbitrary `feeToken` untracked by the swap's balance accounting, draining `ExternalActionSwap`'s own token balance - (`File: contracts/external-actions/swaps/ExternalActionSwap.sol`)

### Summary
`ExternalActionSwap.swap()` pays the flat relay fee in `circomData.feeStructure.feeToken`, a value read directly from calldata, without ever validating that this token is one of the two tokens actually involved in the swap (`inputToken`/`outputToken`). When `feeToken` differs from both, the fee is paid out of the contract's own balance of that token, a balance not sourced from, or accounted against, the current transaction's tracked deltas.

### Finding Description
`swap()` only special-cases `feeToken` against `inputToken` and `outputToken`: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

- If `feeToken == inputToken`, the fee is deducted from the deposited `inputAmount` before swapping (accounted for).
- If `feeToken == outputToken`, the fee is deducted from `swappedAmount` before crediting the user via `amountToSendToHinkal` (accounted for).
- If `feeToken` is neither, `sendToRelay(circomData.relay, relayFee, circomData.feeStructure.feeToken)` pays the flat fee straight out of `ExternalActionSwap`'s own balance of `feeToken` [3](#0-2) . This balance is never deposited by, or reconciled against, this transaction: `Hinkal._externalTransact` only moves and reconciles the two tokens present in `circomData.erc20TokenAddresses` (index 0/1) [4](#0-3) , so `feeToken` balance changes on `ExternalActionSwap` are entirely outside the balance equation Hinkal enforces.

This is structurally the same pattern as the ELFI report: a fee is paid from a pool/vault (`ExternalActionSwap`'s general token balance) that has no relationship to the funds actually deposited for the specific request (the swap's input/output tokens), instead of being carved out of the transaction's own accounted funds. Any ERC20 the shared `ExternalActionSwap` contract happens to hold (dust left from other users' swaps, rounding remainders, or stray transfers) can be drained this way, one `flatFee` at a time, by any caller who can submit a valid withdrawal/swap proof and simply chooses a `feeStructure.feeToken` unrelated to the swap and a `circomData.relay` of their choosing.

### Impact Explanation
This lets an unprivileged EOA redirect and drain a token balance sitting in the shared `ExternalActionSwap` contract that was never counted as part of their transaction's deposited/withdrawn value - a theft of protocol/relay-adjacent funds and an unauthorized asset movement (`sendToRelay` moves funds that neither the wallet owner nor the proof's committed amounts authorized). Repeated calls can fully drain any stray balance of an arbitrary ERC20 held by the contract.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires only a normal user-submitted swap transaction with a crafted `feeStructure.feeToken`/`relay` in calldata and a legitimate proof for the swap itself (the fee token choice is not constrained by the circuit's committed input/output tokens in this contract). No admin, relayer, or signer privileges are needed - likelihood is high whenever the contract accrues any non-zero balance of a token other than the pair being swapped.

### Recommendation
Require `circomData.feeStructure.feeToken` to equal `inputToken` or `outputToken` (revert otherwise), or explicitly account for/deposit the fee token amount as part of the transaction's tracked deltas before paying it out, mirroring how `inputAmount`/`swappedAmount` deductions are handled for the matching-token cases.

### Proof of Concept
1. Some balance of token `X` (unrelated to any specific swap) accumulates in `ExternalActionSwap` (e.g., rounding dust from prior swaps, or a stray transfer).
2. Attacker submits a valid shielded withdrawal/swap proof with `inputToken = A`, `outputToken = B`, but sets `circomData.feeStructure.feeToken = X` and `circomData.feeStructure.flatFee = <X balance>` and `circomData.relay = attacker`.
3. `swap()` executes the A→B swap normally, then hits the `else` branch at [3](#0-2)  and calls `sendToRelay(attacker, flatFee, X)`, transferring `X` tokens from the contract's own balance to the attacker, with no deposit of `X` ever having occurred in this transaction.
4. Repeating this drains any `X` balance the contract holds, with no on-chain accounting of `X` ever debited from the attacker's shielded balance.
