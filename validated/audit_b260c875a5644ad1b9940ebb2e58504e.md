**Analysis:**

The claimed identity `assets == underlying held by vault` is enforced by the accounting flow, and the `receiver` parameter in `borrow` does not create the residue described.

Tracing the path: `borrow` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1238-1314`) resolves `funds-receiver` from the optional `receiver` argument, but the debt is always recorded against `account`, which is hardcoded to `contract-caller`, never the receiver [1](#0-0) . The `receiver`/`funds-receiver` value only controls where the borrowed underlying tokens are sent via `vault-system-borrow`/`send-underlying`; it has no bearing on the scaled-debt bookkeeping.

`add-user-scaled-debt` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-market-vault.clar:237-242`) is invoked only through `debt-add-scaled`, which is gated by `check-impl-auth` and requires `scaled-amount > u0` [2](#0-1) . The `default-to u0` on a missing map entry is the standard "initialize on first use" pattern used identically for collateral (`add-user-collateral`, line 200) and is not itself a source of divergence — it simply avoids requiring a pre-existing map row for a brand-new borrower.

On the vault side, `system-borrow` enforces `(asserts! (<= amount available-assets) ERR-INSUFFICIENT-VAULT-LIQUIDITY)` before incrementing `total-borrowed` and sending underlying [3](#0-2) , which prevents lending out more than the vault actually holds regardless of which principal (including a contract) is chosen as receiver. Passing a contract principal as `receiver` only affects where tokens land, not the scaled-debt or `assets`/`total-borrowed` accounting, so no invariant divergence or "unreconciled residue" is created by this path.
