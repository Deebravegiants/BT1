### Title
Borrower can make their account unliquidatable by holding a dust supply position in a `no_seize` market - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/plan.rs)

### Summary
This is a direct analog of the "unpokeable veNFT" bug. In Velocimeter, `poke` iterated every gauge the user ever voted for, and a single killed gauge reverted the whole call. In XOXNO Lending, `liquidate` builds a pro-rata seizure plan over **every** supply position on the account and calls `enforce_spoke_asset_flags(..., FreezePolicy::SeizureLeg)` for each seized collateral entry — a single market configured `no_seize` reverts the entire liquidation.

### Finding Description
`build_liquidation_plan` computes `calculate_seized_collateral` across all of the account's `supply_positions` (pro-rata seizure, not liquidator-chosen legs), then enforces the seizure-leg flags on each entry:

`contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/plan.rs:73-89` — seizure entries are derived from `account.supply_positions` wholesale, then each leg is validated via `FreezePolicy::SeizureLeg`. The file's own contract states "seizure rejects only `no_seize`" (`plan.rs:13`), and `no_seize` is a per-market flag in `SpokeAssetConfig`.

Attack path for a single unprivileged address:
1. Borrower identifies a market likely to be flagged `no_seize` (or already flagged before their last debt opens — the flag may be set after supply is admitted).
2. Borrower calls `supply` with a dust amount in that market and their main collateral elsewhere, then `borrow` normally.
3. Once the admin sets `no_seize` on the dust market, every `liquidate` call against the account panics at `enforce_spoke_asset_flags` in `plan.rs:81-89`, exactly as `poke` reverted on the dead gauge. `get_liquidation_estimate` and `clean_bad_debt` paths that build the same plan are blocked as well.

The borrower controls the trigger (the dust position) while the kill switch is a routine market-config operation, mirroring the M-5 pattern where the user voted dust to a gauge the admin then killed. Unlike the repay leg, the liquidator cannot route around this — seizure legs are not selectable; they are computed pro-rata over the full collateral set, so skipping the poisoned market in `debt_payments` does not help.

### Impact Explanation
The account becomes permanently unliquidatable: any attempt reverts before repayment or seizure executes. The borrower keeps a deeply underwater position uncollateralized-seizable, interest keeps accruing against the bad debt book, and the loss is ultimately socialized onto suppliers via `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` — protocol insolvency borne by other users. This satisfies "protocol insolvency" and, in the interim, blocks `clean_bad_debt`/liquidation settlement.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires only a dust `supply` position (cheap; supply is uncapped up to market supply cap and `no_seize` does not block entry) plus an admin flag change the borrower anticipates — the same precondition as the original finding (an admin killing a gauge). Multi-market flags change routinely (deprecation, risk-off), and the borrower can even hold the dust position speculatively across several candidate markets at negligible cost.

### Recommendation
Mirror the upstream fix ("`continue` instead of reverting"): in `build_liquidation_plan`, skip — rather than reject — seizure entries whose market is `no_seize`, recomputing `proportion_seized` over the seizable subset so liquidation can still clear the eligible collateral. Alternatively, treat `no_seize` collateral as excluded from `total_collateral` in the seizure proportion so the remaining markets absorb the plan.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice `supply(USDC, large)` and `supply(DUST, 1 unit)`.
2. Alice `borrow(ETH, …)` to a healthy HF.
3. Governance/admin sets `no_seize = true` on `DUST` (equivalent of killing the gauge).
4. `DUST` price or collateral prices move so HF < 1 (`risk::calculate_account_risk_totals` passes the check at `plan.rs:40-44`).
5. Liquidator calls `liquidate(liquidator, account_id, payments, SeizeMode::Transfer)` — `calculate_seized_collateral` emits a pro-rata `DUST` seizure entry, `enforce_spoke_asset_flags(..., SeizureLeg)` reverts, and the call fails regardless of `payments` composition. Repeat indefinitely; the debt can never be liquidated.

Caveat: I could not open `contracts/controller/src/positions/mod.rs` to read `enforce_spoke_asset_flags`'s `SeizureLeg` arm before the iteration limit; the conclusion rests on the documented contract at `plan.rs:13` ("seizure rejects only `no_seize`") and the per-entry enforcement loop at `plan.rs:81-89`.
