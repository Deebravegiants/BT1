### Title
Aquarius LP collateral earns venue/gauge rewards that the pool can never claim or distribute — (File: docs/explanation/threat-model.md)

### Summary
XOXNO Lending accepts Aquarius LP tokens as collateral. The LP token holder of record is the pool contract itself, so Aquarius venue rewards (and especially gauge rewards) accrue to the pool address. The pool contract exposes no entrypoint to claim or forward those rewards, and gauge claims require the holder's authorization — which the pool cannot produce. This is the exact analog of Derby's `IdleProvider.claim` / `BetaProvider.claim` being unimplemented while the integrated protocols distribute reward tokens: rewards accrue to the protocol's custody address and are permanently stranded. Severity: Medium.

### Finding Description
The project threat model states the fact pattern directly: "Aquarius LP collateral earns venue rewards for its holder, which is the pool. The pool has no entrypoint to claim or forward them… Gauge rewards need the pool's authorization and therefore cannot be claimed at all" [1](#0-0) .

The pool's full external surface confirms there is no reward-claim entrypoint: `create_market`, `update_params`, `update_indexes`, `supply`, `borrow`, `withdraw`, `repay`, `net_settle`, `seize_positions`, `claim_revenue`, `recapitalize`, `flash_loan`, `create_strategy`, `upgrade` — none of which invoke external venue contracts [2](#0-1) . `claim_revenue` only burns internal revenue shares and transfers the pool's own market asset to the owner [3](#0-2) ; it cannot call an Aquarius gauge or rewards distributor.

Unlike the permissionless venue claims (which "any caller can push… into the pool address as an unbooked donation" — and donations do not rewrite market books [4](#0-3) ), gauge rewards strictly require the LP holder's `require_auth`. The pool never signs anything, so those rewards are frozen permanently, not merely uncollected. Even pushed venue-reward donations are unbooked tokens: they are not credited to `supplied`, `revenue`, or `cash`, and no sweep exists to recover them, so suppliers receive nothing either way.

Users reach this state through ordinary unprivileged paths: supplying Aquarius LP tokens as collateral via `supply`, or acquiring LP exposure through `multiply` / `swap_collateral` routes that mint Aquarius LP via the router's Aquarius venue (`contracts/swap-aggregator/src/venues/aquarius/mint.rs`).

### Impact Explanation
Theft or freezing of unclaimed yield: all gauge rewards and any auth-gated venue rewards attributable to pool-held Aquarius LP collateral are permanently unclaimable. Suppliers of LP collateral forgo the reward component of LP yield for the entire duration their collateral sits in the pool — precisely the "gamers and depositors lose rewards" outcome of the Derby finding, except here the loss is permanent (gauge rewards cannot even be pushed permissionlessly).

### Likelihood Explanation
Certain whenever Aquarius LP is used as collateral and the venue runs a gauge/reward program. No attacker action is required; the yield is structurally unclaimable because the holder of record is a contract with no claiming capability.

### Recommendation
Add a pool entrypoint (e.g., `claim_external_rewards`) that lets an authorized keeper call the venue's claim function with the pool's `require_auth`, then books the received tokens — either as protocol revenue in that token's market, or forwarded to an accumulator for distribution. If claiming is intentionally out of scope, governance should at minimum disclose that LP-collateral suppliers forfeit venue/gauge rewards, and consider disallowing reward-bearing LP as collateral.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice calls controller `supply` with an Aquarius LP token market; the LP tokens are custodied by the pool contract (`contracts/pool` address).
2. Aquarius accrues gauge rewards to the pool address as LP holder.
3. Any attempt to call the gauge's `claim` on behalf of the pool fails: the venue requires the holder's authorization and the pool's code contains no path to invoke the venue — confirmed by the complete entrypoint list in `contracts/pool/README.md` and by the threat-model statement that gauge rewards "cannot be claimed at all" [1](#0-0) .
4. The rewards remain frozen at the venue for as long as the collateral is pooled; neither `claim_revenue` [3](#0-2)  nor any other entrypoint can reach them.

pub(crate) fn apply(env: &Env, hub_asset: HubAssetKey) -> PoolAmountMutation {
    let outcome = accounting(env, hub_asset);

    if outcome.mutation.actual_amount != 0 {
        let owner = ownable::get_owner(env)
            .unwrap_or_else(|| panic_with_error!(env, GenericError::OwnerNotSet));
        outcome
            .cache
            .transfer_out(&owner, outcome.mutation.actual_amount);
    }

    events::emit_market_state(env, outcome.cache.snapshot());
    outcome.mutation
}
