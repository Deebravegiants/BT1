### Title
Unprivileged borrower can park a debt in the whole-unit "dead band" where every liquidation offer reverts - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs)

### Summary
`whole_unit_repayment` in `contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs:235-291` raises the ideal partial-liquidation quote for a solvent account whose only supply leg is in a sub-3-decimal asset. There is a deliberately-unhandled gap between rule 1 (full close) and rule 2 (raise to one unit): while `floor(U / (1 + b)) - R < D <= ceil((U + m) / (1 + b))` and the curve quote backs less than one whole unit, neither promotion applies and every offer ends with zero seizure, reverting with `InvalidPayments` (controller #16). A single unprivileged borrower can size their own borrow so `D` lands in this band while `HF < 1`, making the account unliquidatable — a reachable denial-of-service analog of the CVE-2018-3070 crash/hang class.

### Finding Description
For a solvent account (`C >= D`) whose only supply position is a whole-unit (sub-3-decimal) collateral, `normalize_repayment_plan` calls `whole_unit_repayment` [1](#0-0) . The helper applies two promotions: return `total_debt` when `unit_at_bonus >= D + R` [2](#0-1) , or return the raised one-unit quote when `raised < D` [3](#0-2) . When `unit_at_bonus - R < D <= raised`, neither applies and the raw curve quote stays. Any payment whose seizure is then below one whole unit rounds down to zero units, the plan is left with no seizure, and `liquidate` reverts with `InvalidPayments` — including a full-debt offer in Transfer mode, since a partial plan never rounds a unit up. This is confirmed by the documented invariant ("every offer reverts until accrual or a price move ends that state") [4](#0-3)  and by the harness sweep asserting `INVALID_PAYMENTS` when no whole unit is seizable [5](#0-4) .

### Impact Explanation
While an account sits in the dead band with `HF < 1`, no liquidator can execute a liquidation at any size: every plan reverts before settlement. The borrower's debt keeps accruing and the position cannot be de-risked, so the protocol is exposed to a window where an unhealthy account is fully immune to liquidation — a temporary freezing of the liquidation path that can transition into unbacked bad debt if the collateral price drifts further down during the window. This is not a privileged-path or budget-limits DoS: it is a deterministic arithmetic gap reachable through ordinary `supply`/`borrow`/`liquidate` calls on a listed sub-3-decimal collateral market.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachability requires a listed collateral asset with `asset_decimals < MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS` (the runbook shows such listings exist) and a borrow whose `D` lands in a band of width `raised - (unit_at_bonus - R) ≈ U·b/(1+b) + R + m` — with typical bonuses this is a few percent of a unit's value, so a borrower can choose the borrow amount to land inside it, and a single borrow can also re-enter the band after re-sizing. Cost to the attacker is just their own collateral. The condition is self-resolving once accrual pushes `D` above `raised`, which caps the freeze duration and keeps severity at Medium rather than High.

### Recommendation
Close the gap in `whole_unit_repayment`: when `unit_at_bonus - R < D <= raised`, either promote the quote to `D` (full close taking one unit) or to `raised` even when `raised >= D` is not strictly required — e.g., treat `raised` as a ceiling that may equal `D`, letting the whole-unit leg round up and close the account. Alternatively, add a final fallback in `normalize_repayment_plan`: if a solvent single-leg whole-unit account yields a plan with zero seizure and `D` lies in the dead band, clamp the ideal repayment to `D` so the full-close path executes instead of reverting.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance lists a sub-3-decimal collateral (e.g., 0 decimals, unit NAV `U` in USD) — this listing shape already exists (`zero_decimal_collateral` fixtures).
2. Attacker calls `supply(caller, account_id=0, spoke_id, [(hub_asset, k units)])` then `borrow(caller, account_id, [(hub_debt, amount)], to)` choosing `amount` so the debt `D` satisfies `floor(U/(1+b)) - R < D <= ceil((U+m)/(1+b))` while `HF < 1` after a small price move.
3. Any liquidator calls `get_liquidation_estimate(account_id, payments, SeizeMode::Transfer)`: `max_payment_wad` is 0 and `seized_collaterals` is empty; `liquidate(...)` reverts with `Error(Contract, #16)` for every offer size, as pinned by `zdc_sub_unit_liquidation_reverts_without_charging` [6](#0-5) .
4. The account remains unliquidatable until interest accrual or an oracle price move pushes `D` out of the band, while its debt continues to grow against the pool.

fn zdc_sub_unit_liquidation_reverts_without_charging() {
    let mut z = setup(nav(1_000));
    let id = open(&mut z, "alice", 10, 1_000, 5_800);
    z.t.set_price(LIQ, nav(800));
    let debt = z.debt_raw(id);
    let est = z.estimate(id, usdc_raw(1), SeizeMode::Transfer);
    assert_eq!(est.seized_collaterals.len(), 0);
    assert_eq!(est.max_payment_wad, 0, "no payment is quoted");

    let err = z
        .liquidate(id, usdc_raw(1), SeizeMode::Transfer)
        .unwrap_err();
    assert_eq!(err, Error::from_contract_error(errors::INVALID_PAYMENTS));
    assert_eq!(z.collateral_units(id), 10);
    assert_eq!(z.debt_raw(id), debt);
}
