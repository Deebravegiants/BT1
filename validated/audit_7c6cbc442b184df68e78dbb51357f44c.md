### Title
Fee-on-transfer tokens cause unbacked LP minting in `Deposit` - (File: `program/src/processor.rs`)

### Summary
`process_deposit` calculates the LP tokens to mint based on a *requested* deduction amount (`deduct_coin_amount`/`deduct_pc_amount`) computed from the pool's pre-transfer reserves, then separately issues an SPL `Transfer` for that same nominal amount. If the coin or PC mint charges a transfer fee (fee-on-transfer / deflationary token), the AMM vault actually receives less than `deduct_coin_amount`/`deduct_pc_amount`, but `mint_lp_amount` and the pnl-tracking fields (`target_orders.calc_pnl_x/y`) are computed and updated as if the full nominal amount arrived.

### Finding Description
In `process_deposit` [1](#0-0) , the pool's current reserves are read from the vault's on-chain SPL balance (`amm_coin_vault.amount`, `amm_pc_vault.amount`), which correctly reflects real, already-fee-adjusted balances. However, the *new* deposit amounts (`deduct_coin_amount`, `deduct_pc_amount`) used to size `mint_lp_amount` are purely arithmetic values derived from the invariant relative to `deposit.max_coin_amount`/`deposit.max_pc_amount` [2](#0-1) , not the amount that will actually land in the vault.

The program then transfers `deduct_coin_amount` and `deduct_pc_amount` via a plain SPL `Transfer` CPI [3](#0-2) , and mints `mint_lp_amount` LP tokens and updates `amm.lp_amount` and `target_orders.calc_pnl_x/calc_pnl_y` all based on the nominal `deduct_coin_amount`/`deduct_pc_amount`, not on the vault's balance delta [4](#0-3) . `Invokers::token_transfer` itself performs no balance-before/after check and blindly issues the `spl_token::instruction::transfer` CPI [5](#0-4) .

Because the pool has no allowlist or check restricting `coin_mint`/`pc_mint` to fee-free tokens, and the vault mints are fixed permanently at `Initialize2` (any unprivileged pool creator selects them) [6](#0-5) , a pool creator can freely pick a fee-on-transfer SPL token as `coin_mint` or `pc_mint`. Every subsequent `Deposit` by any (honest) LP will then mint LP shares based on an amount larger than what the vault actually received, silently diluting the backing of previously issued LP tokens.

Note: `Initialize2` itself is not vulnerable, since it computes `amm.lp_amount`/initial minted LP directly from the vault's post-transfer balance (`amm_coin_vault.amount`, `amm_pc_vault.amount` read *after* the transfer) [7](#0-6) . The vulnerable path is specifically the ongoing `Deposit` instruction, where the nominal deducted amount (not the realized transfer) drives LP minting and pnl accounting.

### Impact Explanation
Each deposit into a pool whose coin or PC mint charges a transfer fee results in the vault receiving strictly less than `deduct_coin_amount`/`deduct_pc_amount`, while LP tokens are minted as if the full amount arrived. This creates unbacked LP tokens: the pool's real token reserves grow more slowly than `amm.lp_amount` (total LP supply) implies. Over repeated deposits, the ratio of real assets per LP share degrades, so LPs (and the deposit depositor themselves, and later withdrawers) redeem proportionally less than expected — a direct value transfer/loss for depositors and other LPs, and an insolvent pool accounting state (`target_orders.calc_pnl_x/y` also drift from real balances, potentially breaking the `TakePnlError` invariant in the pnl-taking function). This matches the pattern described in the reference Cooler report: using an arbitrary/fee-charging token results in the tracked/expected balance diverging from the real balance, causing loss of funds for other participants.

### Likelihood Explanation
Any unprivileged pool creator can call `Initialize2` and pick an arbitrary SPL mint with fee-on-transfer semantics (deflationary tokens, e.g., tokens implementing SPL Token-2022 transfer fee extension, or custom fee-charging SPL tokens) as `coin_mint` or `pc_mint`; the program does no check to reject such mints. Every subsequent `Deposit` transaction from any user is automatically affected — no special conditions or races are required, only a normal deposit call against such a pool.

### Recommendation
For `process_deposit`, read the vault's SPL token balance immediately before and immediately after the `Invokers::token_transfer` calls, and use the actual balance delta (not the nominal `deduct_coin_amount`/`deduct_pc_amount`) to (a) compute `mint_lp_amount`, and (b) update `target_orders.calc_pnl_x`/`calc_pnl_y` and `amm.lp_amount`. Alternatively, explicitly disallow non-standard tokens by verifying that the transferred delta equals the requested amount, and revert with a dedicated error (e.g., `AmmError::InvalidInput`) otherwise, so fee-on-transfer tokens cannot be used with the AMM's `Deposit` instruction.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker calls `Initialize2` with `coin_mint` set to a fee-on-transfer SPL token (e.g., 5% fee on transfer) and a normal `pc_mint`, funding the pool with `init_coin_amount`/`init_pc_amount`. Since `Initialize2` reads post-transfer vault balances, the initial LP amount is correctly backed [8](#0-7) .
2. A victim LP calls `Deposit` with `max_coin_amount = 1000` (fee-on-transfer coin) and a corresponding `max_pc_amount`. `process_deposit` computes `deduct_coin_amount = 1000` and a proportional `mint_lp_amount` based on `total_coin_without_take_pnl` [9](#0-8) .
3. `Invokers::token_transfer` transfers nominal `1000` tokens from the depositor, but due to the 5% fee, only `950` tokens actually land in `amm_coin_vault` [10](#0-9) .
4. The program still mints `mint_lp_amount` LP tokens computed from the nominal `1000` and updates `amm.lp_amount`/`target_orders.calc_pnl_x/y` accordingly [4](#0-3) , over-crediting LP supply relative to real vault assets. Repeating deposits progressively dilutes the pool's real backing per LP share, causing subsequent withdrawers to receive less than their fair share and potentially triggering `AmmError::TakePnlError` failures due to state divergence [11](#0-10) .
