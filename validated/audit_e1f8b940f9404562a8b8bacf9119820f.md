### Title
LP depositors can front-run bad-debt socialization triggered by liquidation to avoid loss - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar` / `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`)

### Summary
Each Zest vault (`v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`) values LP shares (`zft`) against a shared, monotonically-changing `lindex` (liquidity index) used by `convert-to-assets-preview`/`convert-to-shares-preview` inside `redeem` and `deposit`. When a borrower has no collateral left after liquidation, `v0-4-market.clar`'s `liquidate` function calls `socialize-debt-asset`, which in turn calls the vault's public `socialize-debt` function [1](#0-0)  to immediately write down `lindex` proportionally to the loss [2](#0-1) . Because `socialize-debt` is only reachable through a normal, publicly visible `liquidate()` transaction (not a privileged/atomic oracle update), any depositor watching the mempool can front-run the liquidation transaction with a `redeem()` call and exit at the pre-write-down share price, shifting the entire loss onto the depositors who remain in the vault.

### Finding Description
The vault's LP share price is `assets_per_share = total_assets(index, lindex) / total_supply(zft)`. `redeem()` computes the payout using `convert-to-assets-preview`, which is a function of the *current* `lindex` at the time the transaction executes [3](#0-2) , and only decrements the vault's `assets` accounting during the withdrawal itself.

The `socialize-debt` write-down, however, is triggered lazily and only when a liquidator calls `liquidate()` on a position that has no collateral left [4](#0-3) . Until that transaction lands, the loss exists economically (the debtor's collateral is gone / insufficient) but is not yet reflected in `lindex`, so LP shares remain overvalued. Since `liquidate()` is a normal permissionless public transaction visible in the mempool, and there is no atomicity/queueing between the moment the bad debt becomes provable on-chain and the moment `socialize-debt` actually executes, a depositor can detect the pending liquidation (or the conditions that make one imminent, e.g. a price update making a position deeply underwater) and submit a `redeem()` transaction ahead of it.

This breaks the intended identity: `sum(share_value_i) == total_assets_backing_shares` before and after a loss event. Before the front-run: all shares are valued at the same (overstated) `lindex`. After the front-runner exits with the full pre-loss value, the fixed-size loss `debt-reduction` is subtracted from a *smaller* remaining pool of assets/shares (since `socialize-debt` still reduces the same absolute amount of `assets`/`total-borrowed`, but the write-down of `lindex` is computed against `old-total-assets` measured *after* the front-runner's outflow has already reduced `assets`) [5](#0-4) , so the remaining depositors absorb a proportionally larger share of the loss than they would have if the front-runner had redeemed after the write-down (or not at all).

### Impact Explanation
This is a temporary/permanent freezing-and-redistribution-of-funds issue for the remaining LPs: the loss that should be pro-rata across all depositors at the time bad debt occurs is instead concentrated onto whoever fails to exit before the liquidation transaction lands. This is directly analogous to the ToB "depositors can front-run request-blocking transactions" finding: the fund-locking/loss-imposing event (`setRequesterBlockStatus` / here, `socialize-debt` via `liquidate`) is known in advance and public, and unprivileged actors can race it to escape a loss/restriction meant to apply to them, at the expense of good-faith remaining participants. Given multiple vaults and the reserve/fee-share mechanism minting treasury LP tokens on `accrue`, protocol accounting still balances at the vault level, but user-level fund allocation is corrupted (theft of principal from the perspective of the LPs who remain and absorb more than their fair share of the loss) — meeting the "theft of user funds" / "temporary or permanent freezing of funds" bar for the remaining depositors.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. It requires: (1) an unhealthy position with no collateral left becoming imminent/visible (e.g., after an oracle price update or through public state), (2) a depositor monitoring mempool activity for market contract calls, and (3) redeeming ahead of the liquidator's transaction (same block or slightly earlier). Since `liquidate()` and oracle price writes (`write-feeds`) are ordinary public transactions with no time-lock or private ordering guarantee, this is technically straightforward for any sophisticated LP or MEV searcher to automate.

### Recommendation
- **Short term:** Introduce a withdrawal delay/queue (two-step withdrawal, as recommended in the analog ToB report) for vault `redeem`, or accrue/apply pending bad-debt write-downs pre-emptively (e.g., mark positions eligible for socialization as soon as they become undercollateralized, and freeze/pro-rate withdrawals against that pending loss) so that the loss is reflected in `lindex` before any withdrawal referencing stale pricing can execute.
- **Long term:** Consider batching liquidation-triggered `socialize-debt` calls with `redeem` in the same settlement window, or apply a global "loss accrual" step atomically prior to processing any redeem in the same block as a liquidation event, removing the race window entirely.

### Proof of Concept
1. Borrower's position becomes fully undercollateralized (e.g., due to a price move); this becomes determinable on-chain once oracle price is updated via `write-feeds`.
2. Attacker (an existing LP in the relevant vault, e.g. `v0-vault-usdc`) observes the pending `liquidate()` call in the mempool that will trigger `socialize-debt-asset` → `vault-socialize-debt` → vault `socialize-debt`.
3. Attacker submits `redeem(amount, min-out, recipient)` with higher fee/priority so it lands before the liquidation transaction, receiving `inkind` assets computed via `convert-to-assets-preview` at the current, not-yet-written-down `lindex` [6](#0-5) .
4. The liquidation transaction executes afterward, calling `socialize-debt` which write-downs `lindex` based on `old-total-assets` measured after the attacker's withdrawal has already reduced `assets` [7](#0-6) , concentrating the loss onto the remaining share holders.

Note: I was not able to fully trace `total-assets`/`convert-to-assets-preview` internals within the token budget available (the tool truncated before showing their exact formula), so the precise numeric magnitude of the loss-shift per front-run cannot be computed here without further review of those helper functions in `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar`.

(define-private (socialize-debt-asset
                (debt-entry { aid: uint, scaled: uint })
                (acc { borrower: principal, success: bool }))
  ;; Early return if previous socialization failed
  (if (not (get success acc))
      acc
      (let ((borrower (get borrower acc))
            (failed-status { borrower: borrower, success: false })
            (asset-id (get aid debt-entry))
            (scaled-debt (get scaled debt-entry)))

            ;; Socialize in vault - pass scaled directly to avoid rounding
            (unwrap! (vault-socialize-debt asset-id scaled-debt) failed-status)
            ;; Refresh cache with new indexes post-write-down (lindex decreased)
            (map-set index-cache
                     { timestamp: stacks-block-time, aid: asset-id }

                                      debt-remove-scaled
                                      borrower
                                      scaled-debt

        ))

(define-public (socialize-debt (scaled-amount uint))
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (current-assets (var-get assets))
        (current-lindex (var-get lindex))
        (old-total-assets (total-assets))
        (debt-reduction (mul-div-down scaled-amount idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (principal-reduction (if (> scaled-principal u0)
                                (mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal)
                                u0))
        ;; Write down lindex proportionally to loss in total-assets
        (new-lindex (if (and (> old-total-assets u0) (> old-total-assets debt-reduction))
                       (mul-div-down current-lindex (- old-total-assets debt-reduction) old-total-assets)
                       u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (> scaled-amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (var-set lindex new-lindex)
    (var-set principal-scaled (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0))
    (var-set total-borrowed (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0))
    (var-set assets (if (> current-assets principal-reduction) (- current-assets principal-reduction) u0))
