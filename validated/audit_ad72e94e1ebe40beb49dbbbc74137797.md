### Title
Permissionless `update_account_threshold` restamps a foreign account's LTV below its debt, freezing withdrawals — (`File: contracts/controller/src/risk/params.rs`)

### Summary
`update_account_threshold(caller, has_risks, account_ids)` is a permissionless keeper entrypoint: any authenticated address can restamp the cached risk parameters on any account. When `has_risks = false` it runs `RiskRefreshScope::LtvOnly`, which unconditionally copies the *currently listed* `loan_to_value` into each of the victim's stored supply positions without loading debt positions and without any health-factor or solvency gate. The post-action risk gate `require_post_pool_risk_gates` — applied to `withdraw`, `borrow`, `swap_debt`, `swap_collateral`, `repay_debt_with_collateral`, `multiply`, and `flash_position` — requires `ltv_collateral >= total_debt` and `ltv_collateral >= min_borrow_collateral_usd`, both computed from that stored LTV. After governance lowers a listed LTV (a condition entirely outside the account owner's control, mirroring the "specific condition" in the advisory), any stranger can force the new, lower LTV onto the victim and push `ltv_collateral` below debt, reverting every risk-gated action while the health factor itself stays comfortably above 1.

### Finding Description
- `update_account_threshold` only authenticates the caller via `require_authorized_caller` (a bare `require_auth` plus flash guard); there is no owner/delegate check on the targeted `account_ids` (`contracts/controller/src/risk/params.rs:124-144`).
- `refresh_supply_risk_params` writes `position.loan_to_value = effective_config.loan_to_value` unconditionally; only the liquidation tuple is gated, and that gate (`apply_gated_liquidation_params` → `favors_liquidator`/`clears_min_hf`) does not exist at all for the LTV write (`params.rs:34-38`, `params.rs:76-93`).
- In `LtvOnly` mode `sync_account_thresholds` passes an empty `borrow_positions` map and skips the final `hf >= 1.05` assertion, which is only executed under `full_tuple` (`params.rs:169-174`, `params.rs:221-234`). The same unconditional LTV restamp also happens in `FullTuple` mode — the HF assertion there cannot catch an LTV-only regression because `health_factor` never reads `loan_to_value`.
- The stored LTV is consumed by `calculate_account_risk_totals` as `min(LTV, liquidation_threshold)` weighting, and `require_post_pool_risk_gates` reverts with `InsufficientCollateral` when `ltv_collateral < total_debt` and with `MinBorrowCollateralNotMet` below the USD floor (`contracts/controller/src/risk/validation.rs:41-58`).
- The declared-permission list justifies the entrypoint with "restamps LTV only, which the health factor does not read" (`scripts/permissionless_entrypoints.txt:76`) — but the LTV-weighted collateral gate is a separate, enforced check, so that justification misses the actual attack surface.

### Impact Explanation
Temporary freezing of funds. Once the victim's stored LTV is restamped below their debt-weighted requirement, `withdraw`, `borrow`, and all debt-bearing strategies revert, even though the account's health factor is above 1 and it is not liquidatable. The owner cannot undo the restamp themselves (the same permissionless path only moves LTV toward the listed value). Funds are recoverable by fully repaying debt — `repay` is permissionless and skips risk gates — or if governance restores a higher LTV, so the freeze is temporary rather than permanent. Additionally, an attacker can grief a specific borrower right before a needed refinancing or deleveraging action, sandwiching the restamp against `swap_debt`/`repay_debt_with_collateral` transactions.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a listed LTV decrease for some collateral asset (a normal risk-management operation) and a victim whose `ltv_collateral` sits between the old and new LTV weightings of their debt — a realistic band, since borrowing is bounded by LTV, so freshly-borrowed accounts sit near the boundary whenever LTV is cut. From then on, the attack is one permissionless call by any funded address: `update_account_threshold(attacker, false, [victim_id])`. No price movement, oracle manipulation, or victim cooperation is needed. Medium likelihood conditioned on an LTV reduction; the window persists until the victim repays or parameters change again.

### Recommendation
Apply a symmetry guard to the LTV leg of the refresh, analogous to the liquidation-tuple gate: when the new `loan_to_value` is lower than the stored one and the account has debt, either (a) require `ltv_collateral` recomputed with the new LTV to remain `>= total_debt` (and above `min_borrow_collateral_usd`), skipping or reverting otherwise, or (b) restrict `update_account_threshold` on accounts with debt to the owner/delegate. The same check should apply inside `refresh_supply_risk_params` on the `merge_supply_leg`/`merge_withdraw_leg` paths, where third-party top-ups can also trigger a restamp.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance lists XLM at `ltv = 7500`; Alice supplies $10,000 XLM and borrows $7,000 USDC (`ltv_collateral = $7,500 ≥ $7,000`; HF healthy).
2. Via timelocked governance, listed XLM LTV is reduced to 6000 — outside Alice's control.
3. Any attacker calls `controller.update_account_threshold(attacker, false, [alice_id])`. `sync_account_thresholds` stamps `loan_to_value = 6000` with no debt check.
4. Now `ltv_collateral = $6,000 < $7,000 debt`. Alice's `withdraw`, `borrow`, `repay_debt_with_collateral`, `swap_collateral`, and `swap_debt` all revert in `require_post_pool_risk_gates` with `InsufficientCollateral` (#100), despite `health_factor > 1` (threshold unchanged at, e.g., 7800 → HF ≈ 1.11).
5. Alice remains frozen until she repays the full debt through the permissionless `repay` path or LTV is raised again.
