### Title
Unprivileged borrower can brick `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` on their own account by planting a dust Aquarius-LP collateral leg and draining the underlying pool below `min_pool_value_wad` - ([File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs](contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs))

### Summary
The pricing of an Aquarius LP share fails closed with `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity` whenever the pool's total value drops below the configured `min_pool_value_wad`. Because liquidation values **every** collateral leg of an account with strict prices, and `supply` accepts a dust-sized new collateral leg without requiring a fresh price, a borrower can: (1) open a large borrow, (2) supply a dust amount of an Aquarius LP token as an extra collateral leg, (3) become liquidatable, and (4) withdraw their own liquidity from the Aquarius pool so the feed errors. Every subsequent `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` call on that account reverts until liquidity is restored — a self-inflicted, unprivileged liquidation DoS that converts a liquidatable position into accruing bad debt.

### Finding Description
- `aquarius::read` recomputes the LP fair price from live pool reserves and rejects the read when `pool_value_wad < lp.min_pool_value_wad`, returning `OracleError::InsufficientAquariusLiquidity` [1](#0-0) .
- On the controller side, `calculate_seized_collateral` iterates `account.supply_positions` and calls `cache.cached_price(&hub_asset.asset)` for each leg [2](#0-1) . The account risk totals do the same, so one unpriceable leg aborts the entire multi-leg pro-rata liquidation, as proven by `audit_liquidate_and_clean_bricked_by_unpriceable_dust_leg`: a dust WBTC leg with a stale feed is accepted by `supply`, then both `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` revert with `PRICE_FEED_STALE` until the feed recovers [3](#0-2) .
- The threat model documents exactly this shape (DoS.1): "an indebted borrower can add a dust leg of any listed collateral and choose which feed outage shields the account. For an Aquarius LP leg, liquidity providers can cause that outage by withdrawing pool value below `min_pool_value_wad`. The same leg blocks bad-debt cleanup and force-socialization" [4](#0-3) .
- Reaching `min_pool_value_wad` requires only ordinary Aquarius LP withdrawals — in-scope "own trades on Aquarius" — not oracle dishonesty or privileged action.

### Impact Explanation
Temporary freezing of funds / path to protocol insolvency: while the LP pool stays below `min_pool_value_wad`, the attacker's underwater account cannot be liquidated (every leg must price strictly), cannot be cleaned via the permissionless `clean_bad_debt`, and owner-only `force_socialize_bad_debt` hits the same strict-price gate. Interest keeps accruing, deepening the shortfall borne by suppliers via the eventual supply-index write-down. The attacker controls the duration: they can re-add liquidity to restore the feed whenever they choose, and can re-trigger the outage each time liquidity is restored, so the freeze is renewable at the cost of LP capital they own anyway.

### Likelihood Explanation
- Reachable by a single unprivileged address: `supply` (dust LP leg), `borrow` (against other collateral), and an Aquarius `withdraw_liquidity` on a pool they are already an LP in — or one they seed themselves.
- The dust leg needs only enough LP value to register as a supply position; pro-rata seizure still touches it, so it cannot be skipped.
- Cost is bounded by the dust leg plus temporary loss of LP liquidity; no capital burn.
- Mitigating factors: requires a listed market whose oracle is an Aquarius LP source; the outage ends if any LP restores pool value; debt keepers could monitor and the owner can still act once the feed recovers. Consistent with Medium severity — availability impact analogous to the referenced CVE class (crash/hang DoS), here a renewable hang of liquidation and bad-debt cleanup.

### Recommendation
- Decouple seizure from strict per-leg pricing where safe: e.g., allow liquidation to skip legs whose price fails while accounting their USD value as zero in `total_collateral` (fails-open legs shift seizure weight onto priceable collateral), or cap seizure strictly to legs with valid strict prices.
- Alternatively, treat `InsufficientAquariusLiquidity` (and equivalent provider outages) as price zero rather than abort for collateral legs, while keeping strict pricing for debt valuation and HF.
- At minimum, gate `supply` of an Aquarius-LP asset on a currently valid strict price, so an account cannot plant an already-unpriceable leg — though this does not cover outages induced after planting.
- For `clean_bad_debt`/`force_socialize_bad_debt`, consider a path that socializes debt using the last-known or zero collateral value for failed legs so cleanup cannot be held hostage to one feed.
