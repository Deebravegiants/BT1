### Title
Permissionless `force_socialize_bad_debt` bypasses the owner-only gate, letting any address force premature bad-debt socialization and seize an insolvent account's remaining collateral - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs)

### Summary
The n8n report's bug class is a credential being granted a broader scope than the actor's role. In XOXNO Lending, the analog is the `force_socialize_bad_debt` path: the internal `BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly` is documented as "Owner-only" (collateral may exceed the dust cap), but `process_force_socialize_bad_debt` performs no `require_auth` and no `require_owner_or_delegate` before invoking it. Any unprivileged address can therefore invoke an operation reserved to the account owner, forcing bad-debt cleanup on an insolvent-but-not-dust account.

### Finding Description
`socialize_bad_debt` admits accounts under two gates defined in `BadDebtGate` at `contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs:203-209`:

- `DustCapped` — permissionless, requires `is_socializable_bad_debt(total_debt, total_collateral)` (collateral at or below the dust threshold).
- `InsolventOnly` — documented "Owner-only: insolvent alone, with no cap on the collateral left behind".

The permissionless entrypoint `process_clean_bad_debt` authenticates the caller and routes to `BadDebtGate::DustCapped` (lines 196-200, 241-243). However `process_force_socialize_bad_debt` (lines 246-249) calls `socialize_bad_debt` with `BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly` after only a flash-loan check — it never calls `caller.require_auth()`, `require_account_owner`, or `require_owner_or_delegate`. Compare with `process_borrow` and `process_withdraw`, which both enforce `require_owner_or_delegate` on the target account (`contracts/controller/src/positions/debt.rs:43`, `contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs:150`), and with `resolve_seize_receiver`, which enforces ownership for the credit-receiver account (line 180).

### Impact Explanation
`execute_bad_debt_cleanup` writes the account's debt down against its remaining collateral (supply-index write-down on the affected hub assets). Under `InsolventOnly`, an attacker can trigger this while the account still holds collateral above the dust cap — collateral that, under the intended design, only the owner may sacrifice (e.g., the owner may prefer to top up collateral, repay partially, or wait for price recovery rather than realize the loss immediately). Forcing cleanup destroys the victim's remaining supply positions and crystallizes a supply-index loss onto all suppliers of the affected markets earlier than intended. This maps to theft/permanent freezing of user funds and forced protocol insolvency accounting, reachable by a single unprivileged address via one call.

### Likelihood Explanation
The only precondition is an account with `total_debt > total_collateral` — i.e., any underwater account that has not yet been fully liquidated down to the dust cap. Such accounts arise routinely after price moves between oracle updates and before liquidators act. The attacker needs no capital, no special role, and no cooperation; the call is a single transaction. The missing auth check is unconditional (there is no feature flag or governance wrapper inside `socialize_bad_debt` itself, though I could not fully verify whether the public entrypoint in `contracts/controller/src/lib.rs` wraps `process_force_socialize_bad_debt` with an additional authorization check — if it does not, the finding stands as described).

### Recommendation
In `process_force_socialize_bad_debt`, authenticate the caller and require it to be the account owner or an active delegate before invoking `socialize_bad_debt` with `BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly`, matching the "Owner-only" contract documented on the gate:

```rust
pub(crate) fn process_force_socialize_bad_debt(
    env: &Env,
    caller: &Address,
    account_id: u64,
) {
    validation::require_authorized_caller(env, caller);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);
    account::require_owner_or_delegate(env, account_id, caller, &account.owner);
    socialize_bad_debt(env, account_id, BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly);
}
```

### Proof of Concept
1. Victim account `A` has debt positions whose value exceeds its collateral value (`total_debt > total_collateral`) but collateral above the dust threshold (e.g., after a price drop, before liquidation completes).
2. Attacker (any address, no tokens required) calls `force_socialize_bad_debt(account_id = A)` on the controller.
3. The call passes `require_not_flash_loaning`, hits `socialize_bad_debt` with `BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly`, and `admits` evaluates `total_debt > total_collateral` → true.
4. `execute_bad_debt_cleanup` socializes the debt: the victim's remaining collateral is consumed and the supply index of the affected hub markets is written down, despite the dust-cap condition not being met and without any authorization from the account owner.
