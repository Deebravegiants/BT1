Based on my investigation, deposit() in the Zest vault contracts is missing a zero-shares-minted guard that redeem() has, creating a direct analog of the reported rounding bug.

### Title
Deposit rounds shares-minted to zero for small deposits, permanently transferring underlying assets to the vault with no shares issued - (File: mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar and other v0-vault-*.clar)

### Summary
### Finding Description
In every vault contract (`v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`), `deposit` computes shares via `convert-to-shares-preview`, which performs `mul-div-down amount ts ta` [1](#0-0) . When the vault's total-assets-to-total-supply ratio (`ta/ts`) is large — e.g. after significant interest accrual increases `ta` relative to `ts` — a small `amount` deposit can round `inkind` (shares minted) down to zero.

Unlike `redeem`, which explicitly guards against a zero output with `(asserts! (> inkind u0) ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO)` [2](#0-1) , `deposit` has no equivalent check. It only validates `(> amount u0)`, `(>= inkind min-out)`, and the supply cap [3](#0-2) . Since `min-out` defaults acceptably to `u0`, a caller (or an unaware caller) can deposit real underlying tokens, receive `inkind = 0` zTokens via `(try! (ft-mint? zft inkind recipient))`, while `(var-set assets (+ current-assets amount))` still increases the vault's recorded assets [4](#0-3) .

This breaks the core share-to-backing identity: `assets_in == shares_minted * price_per_share` is violated — assets are added to the vault (`assets += amount`) while `total_supply` remains unchanged, silently diluting the depositor's principal into the pool for the benefit of all other shareholders, and leaving the depositor with a permanently zero-value position (no zTokens minted to redeem).

### Impact Explanation
This matches "Critical - direct theft of user funds at rest" and "permanent freezing of funds": the depositor's underlying principal is transferred into the vault and irrecoverably lost from their perspective (0 shares means 0 claim), while other depositors' existing shares appreciate in value from the added `assets`. This is a direct analog to the reported limit-order rounding bug, where a legitimate action based on rounding produces a non-recoverable near-zero (here exactly zero) balance.

### Likelihood Explanation
The condition requires `ts/ta` to be small enough (i.e., `ta` sufficiently larger than `ts`) that `amount * ts / ta` truncates to 0. This becomes increasingly likely over time as a vault accrues interest (`total-assets-preview` grows via `interest`) relative to `total-supply`, and for vaults with high-decimal underlying assets or long uptime with accumulated yield. Any user making a very small deposit (dust or scripted micro-deposit) into a mature, high-yield vault could trigger this without any special privilege.

### Recommendation
Add `(asserts! (> inkind u0) ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO)` in the `deposit` function of all `v0-vault-*.clar` contracts, mirroring the existing guard already present in `redeem`, to disallow deposits that would mint zero shares.

### Proof of Concept
1. Let a vault accrue substantial interest over time so that `total-assets-preview` (`ta`) is much larger than `total-supply-preview` (`ts`) (e.g., `ta = 1_000_000`, `ts = 1`).
2. A user calls `deposit(amount=1, min-out=0, recipient)`.
3. `convert-to-shares-preview(1)` computes `mul-div-down(1, ts, ta)` = `mul-div-down(1, 1, 1_000_000)` = `0` [1](#0-0) .
4. `deposit` proceeds: `(> amount u0)` passes (amount=1), `(>= inkind min-out)` passes (`0 >= 0`), no cap exceeded.
5. `receive-underlying` pulls 1 unit of underlying from the user; `ft-mint? zft 0 recipient` mints zero shares; `assets` is incremented by 1 [5](#0-4) .
6. The user has permanently lost their 1 unit of underlying with no corresponding zToken balance to redeem it back. [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6)

```text
(define-private (convert-to-shares-preview (amount uint))
  (let ((ta (total-assets-preview))
        (ts (total-supply-preview)))
    (if (is-eq ts u0)
        amount
        (if (is-eq ta u0)
            u0
            (mul-div-down amount ts ta)))))
```
