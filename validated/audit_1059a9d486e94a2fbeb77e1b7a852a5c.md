### Title
Third-party dust top-up force-restamps a victim's LTV to tightened listing values, freezing their borrow and withdraw paths - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs)

### Summary
`supply` admits an unprivileged top-up of any supply leg a foreign account already holds (`require_third_party_existing_supply` only rejects *new* slots). Processing that leg re-stamps its cached risk parameters from the current listing. The liquidation-threshold/bonus/fee part of the restamp is gated behind the 1.05 HF update floor (`HealthFactorTooLow`, #102), but the LTV stamp "rides outside the gate" — it is overwritten unconditionally. After governance legitimately tightens an asset's `loan_to_value`, a stranger can send a one-stroop top-up to any account holding that asset and silently downgrade its cached LTV. Because every risk-increasing action — including `withdraw` while any debt remains — must pass `ltv_collateral ≥ total_debt` in `require_post_pool_risk_gates`, the victim's collateral is locked behind an LTV floor they never agreed to, until they repay debt or add collateral. This is the protocol-native analogue of ransomware's "pay to unlock": an unprivileged attacker can freeze a counterparty's exit path at negligible cost.

### Finding Description
- `process_deposit` calls `validate_position_entry_gates`, then `account.get_or_create_supply_position` and `merge_supply_leg` for each leg. For an existing position held by a foreign account, `require_third_party_existing_supply` (supply.rs:78-97) only asserts the hub asset is already a supply position — it does not gate the risk restamp.
- The restamp applies listing fields to the position record. Tests pin the asymmetry: `poc_third_party_top_up_force_restamps_ltv` (`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/security_audit.rs:507-532`) shows governance lowering USDC `loan_to_value` 8000→5000, then `try_supply_to_account(BOB, ALICE, "USDC", 1.0)` succeeds and Alice's stamped LTV becomes 5000 — labeled "H-RISK-03: third-party top-up force-restamps LTV" — and her subsequent `borrow` reverts `InsufficientCollateral`. Meanwhile `regression_third_party_supply_cannot_force_adverse_tuple_below_min_hf` (security_audit.rs:603-633) and `test_third_party_supply_cannot_force_low_threshold_update` (supply.rs:264-289) confirm LT/bonus/fees are protected by the HF floor while LTV is not ("LTV rides outside the gate").
- `require_post_pool_risk_gates` (`contracts/controller/src/risk/validation.rs`) runs after `withdraw` whenever debt remains and requires `ltv_collateral ≥ total_debt` and `ltv_collateral ≥ min_borrow_collateral_usd` (docs/reference errors #100, #126). So a downgraded LTV blocks not only new `borrow` calls but the victim's own `withdraw` of collateral.

### Impact Explanation
Temporary freezing of funds / griefing lock, reachable by any unprivileged address for the cost of a dust deposit. Concretely: after a governance LTV tightening, an attacker dusts every supply leg of every account holding that asset. Any account whose `ltv_collateral` under the new LTV falls below its outstanding `total_debt` can no longer `withdraw` any collateral (each withdraw runs the post-pool risk gate) and cannot `borrow`, `multiply`, `swap_collateral`, or `repay_debt_with_collateral` out of the position. The lock persists until the victim repays enough debt to satisfy the downgraded LTV, adds more collateral, or waits for a price move — i.e., the attacker can hold the exit hostage and the victim's only release is to pay down debt at whatever terms the market offers, exactly the "locked until payment" shape of the ransomware class. The attacker can repeat this across accounts at dust cost per top-up.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires one precondition: governance tightening `loan_to_value` on an already-listed asset while accounts hold stale, looser LTV stamps — a routine risk-management action. Everything after that is fully unprivileged: `supply(caller, account_id, spoke_id, assets)` is permissionless for existing legs, needs only the caller's own auth, and costs a few base units of token. Accounts sitting near the LTV boundary (any borrower with LTV-weighted collateral close to debt) are immediately locked; the attacker can scan `get_account_positions`/`get_ltv_collateral_usd` off-chain to pick victims. Medium likelihood — gated on a routine admin event, then trivially executed.

### Recommendation
Apply the same update-floor protection to the LTV stamp that guards the threshold tuple: when a third-party (or any) top-up would tighten `loan_to_value` and the resulting post-restamp health check would fail the `THRESHOLD_UPDATE_MIN_HF_RAW` buffer — or when LTV-weighted collateral would drop below outstanding debt — retain the position's existing stamped LTV instead of overwriting it, or revert the top-up leg. At minimum, skip the LTV restamp for legs topped up by a caller that is neither the account owner nor an active delegate (`is_owner_or_delegate` is already computed in `require_third_party_existing_supply`).

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice supplies 10,000 USDC (stamped `ltv=8000`, `lt=8000`) and borrows ETH such that `ltv_collateral` is comfortably above `total_debt`.
2. Governance executes a routine risk tightening: `edit_asset_in_spoke` sets USDC `loan_to_value` to 5000 (threshold unchanged or above the LT gate floor).
3. Attacker calls `supply(attacker, alice_account_id, alice_spoke, [(hub, usdc, 1_base_unit)])`. `require_third_party_existing_supply` passes because Alice already holds a USDC supply leg; `merge_supply_leg` restamps the position, and — per the pinned regression `poc_third_party_top_up_force_restamps_ltv` — the cached `loan_to_value` becomes 5000 while the LT/bonus/fee tuple stays at its vintage under the HF floor.
4. Alice calls `withdraw` on her USDC collateral while debt remains. `require_post_pool_risk_gates` now computes `ltv_collateral` at LTV 5000; if `5000/8000 × prior_ltv_collateral < total_debt` the call reverts `InsufficientCollateral`. Her collateral is frozen until she repays debt; identical dust applies to `borrow`, `multiply`, `flash_position`, `swap_collateral`, and `repay_debt_with_collateral`. fn require_third_party_existing_supply(
    env: &Env,
    account_id: u64,
    resolved_account_id: u64,
    caller: &Address,
    account: &Account,
    aggregated: &AggregatedPayments,
) {
    if account_id != 0
        && !account::is_owner_or_delegate(env, resolved_account_id, caller, &account.owner)
    {
        for (hub_asset, _) in aggregated.iter() {
            assert_with_error!(
                env,
                account.supply_positions.contains_key(hub_asset.clone()),
                GenericError::NotAuthorized
            );
        }
    }
}

#[test]
fn regression_third_party_supply_cannot_force_adverse_tuple_below_min_hf() {
    let mut t = LendingTest::new().standard_two_asset_dust_disabled();

    t.supply(ALICE, "USDC", 10_000.0);
    t.borrow(ALICE, "ETH", 3.0);
    let id = t.resolve_account_id(ALICE);
    let (_, lt_before, bonus_before, fees_before) = supply_risk_stamp(&t, id, "USDC");
    assert_eq!(
        (lt_before, bonus_before, fees_before),
        (8_000, 500, 1_200),
        "preset tuple"
    );

    t.edit_asset_config("USDC", |c| {
        c.loan_to_value = 5_000;
        c.liquidation_threshold = 6_100;
        c.liquidation_bonus = 1_000;
        c.liquidation_fees = 50;
    });

    t.try_supply_to_account(BOB, ALICE, "USDC", 1.0)
        .expect("third-party top-up of an existing leg stays allowed");

    let (ltv_after, lt_after, bonus_after, fees_after) = supply_risk_stamp(&t, id, "USDC");
    assert_eq!(ltv_after, 5_000, "LTV rides outside the gate");
    assert_eq!(
        (lt_after, bonus_after, fees_after),
        (lt_before, bonus_before, fees_before),
        "M1: threshold, bonus, and fees hold their vintage together under the HF floor"
    );
}

#[test]
fn a_stranger_cannot_tighten_a_stale_threshold_below_the_update_floor() {
    let mut t = LendingTest::new().standard_two_asset().build();
    t.supply(BOB, "ETH", 100.0);
    t.supply(ALICE, "USDC", 10_000.0);
    t.borrow(ALICE, "ETH", 3.5);
    let account = t.account_id(ALICE);
    assert_eq!(stamped_threshold(&t, account), 8_000);
    // Health factor with the old threshold: 0.8 * 10_000 / 7_000 = 1.14.
    // Governance tightens to 70 percent: the hypothetical HF would be 1.0, below 1.05.
    t.edit_asset_in_spoke("USDC", HARNESS_SPOKE, true, true, 6_500, 7_000, 500);
    t.supply_to(CAROL, account, "USDC", 0.0000001);
    assert_eq!(
        stamped_threshold(&t, account),
        8_000,
        "the tightening is gated on the update floor"
    );
}

```markdown
`contracts/controller/src/risk/validation.rs::require_post_pool_risk_gates` runs after borrow, withdraw, and every strategy that leaves debt. Debt-free accounts skip it. With debt, all three must hold or the call reverts:

```text
ltv_collateral   ≥ total_debt                      // else InsufficientCollateral
health_factor    ≥ 1e18                            // else InsufficientCollateral
ltv_collateral   ≥ min_borrow_collateral_usd_wad   // when the floor is non-zero; else MinBorrowCollateralNotMet
```

`get_min_borrow_collateral_usd` returns the floor (default `5 × WAD`; API `StellarLendingLiveStateDto.minBorrowCollateralUsdWad`). The gate applies to `withdraw` while any debt remains, so close a position in the order repay → withdraw. Listing, cap, pause/freeze, and `max_utilization` gates apply in addition.
