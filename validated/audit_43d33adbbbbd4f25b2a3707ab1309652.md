### Title
Direct token transfers into `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault` are misclassified as protocol PnL, diverting LP funds - (File: `program/src/processor.rs`)

### Summary
Raydium's `calc_take_pnl` derives the pool's "profit" solely from the live SPL token balances of the AMM's coin/PC vaults, without distinguishing legitimate trading-fee growth from arbitrary external token transfers into those same vault accounts. Because the vault accounts are ordinary SPL Token accounts, anyone can permissionlessly inflate their balance with a plain `Transfer`/`TransferChecked` instruction, causing the very next `Deposit` or `Withdraw` call to misattribute that donated balance as accrued PnL and siphon it into `need_take_pnl_pc`/`need_take_pnl_coin`, removing it from the pool that LPs are entitled to. This mirrors the reported CollateralManager bug: revenue is computed from total balance deltas instead of tracking the protocol's actual owned share/position.

### Finding Description
`Calculator::calc_total_without_take_pnl_no_orderbook` computes the pool's tradable totals purely from the raw vault token-account amounts passed in: [1](#0-0) 

Those raw amounts are obtained directly from the live SPL vault accounts via `unpack_token_account`, e.g. in `process_deposit` and `process_withdraw`: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

`Processor::calc_take_pnl` then compares the *current* invariant (`x1`, `y1`, derived from the possibly-inflated totals) against the *last recorded* invariant (`target.calc_pnl_x` / `target.calc_pnl_y`) and treats any growth as fee-derived profit, crediting a share of it to `amm.state_data.need_take_pnl_pc`/`need_take_pnl_coin`: [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

Because `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault` are standard SPL Token accounts owned by the AMM authority PDA, any unprivileged party can move tokens into them directly with a normal SPL `Transfer` instruction, bypassing `process_deposit` entirely. This inflates the raw balance used by `calc_total_without_take_pnl_no_orderbook`, which is invoked before every `calc_take_pnl` call in `process_deposit`, `process_withdraw`, and `process_withdrawpnl`. The next such call therefore perceives an invariant "growth" that never came from trading fees, and moves a slice of it into `need_take_pnl_pc/coin` — funds that are thereafter excluded from `total_pc_without_take_pnl`/`total_coin_without_take_pnl` used for LP redemption math in every subsequent swap/deposit/withdraw. This mirrors the root cause of the reported bug: total-balance-based accounting fails to separate externally-donated assets from protocol-owned economic gain.

### Impact Explanation
Once `need_take_pnl_pc/coin` is inflated by a direct donation, the pool's LP-redeemable totals (`total_pc_without_take_pnl`, `total_coin_without_take_pnl`) are permanently reduced by that amount across all future `Deposit`/`Withdraw`/swap instructions, even before `process_withdrawpnl` is ever called. This is an insolvent-pool-accounting condition: value that should be shared proportionally among LPs (or remain as an untracked donation benefiting them) is instead segregated out of the LP pool and reserved for extraction via `process_withdrawpnl`: [6](#0-5) 
This reduces the assets available to honor LP withdrawals and misstates the pool's true reserve accounting, directly analogous to the "excessive withdrawal of assets... impacting liquidity" described in the reference report.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is high: sending tokens to a known public token account is a trivial, unprivileged SPL Token instruction requiring no special permissions, and `calc_take_pnl` is invoked on virtually every `Deposit`/`Withdraw` call, so the misclassification triggers automatically on ordinary pool activity following any such donation — no cooperation from a privileged party is needed to corrupt the pool's internal accounting (only the final token transfer via `process_withdrawpnl` needs the `pnl_owner`).

### Recommendation
Do not derive `need_take_pnl` deltas from the vault's raw SPL balance. Instead, track the pool's owned reserves internally (e.g., update a ledger on every program-mediated deposit/withdraw/swap) and compute PnL growth from that internally tracked reserve rather than `vault.amount`, so that unsolicited external transfers into the vault cannot be misattributed as protocol-realizable PnL.

### Proof of Concept
1. Pool exists with some state; `target.calc_pnl_x`/`calc_pnl_y` reflect the last-recorded "no-pnl" invariant.
2. Attacker submits a bare SPL Token `Transfer` instruction (not a Raydium instruction) sending extra PC tokens directly into `amm.pc_vault`.
3. Attacker (or anyone) then submits a `Deposit` or `Withdraw` instruction on the pool.
4. `calc_total_without_take_pnl_no_orderbook` reads the inflated `amm_pc_vault.amount`; `calc_take_pnl` sees `x1*y1` exceed `target.calc_pnl_x*calc_pnl_y` and credits a share of the inflated delta to `amm.state_data.need_take_pnl_pc`.
5. From this point on, every `total_pc_without_take_pnl` computation site-wide subtracts this inflated `need_take_pnl_pc`, reducing what LPs can redeem, and the segregated amount becomes withdrawable to `pnl_owner` via `process_withdrawpnl`.

    pub fn calc_total_without_take_pnl_no_orderbook<'a>(
        pc_amount: u64,
        coin_amount: u64,
        amm: &'a AmmInfo,
    ) -> Result<(u64, u64), AmmError> {
        let total_pc_without_take_pnl = pc_amount
            .checked_sub(amm.state_data.need_take_pnl_pc)
            .ok_or(AmmError::CheckedSubOverflow)?;
        let total_coin_without_take_pnl = coin_amount
            .checked_sub(amm.state_data.need_take_pnl_coin)
            .ok_or(AmmError::CheckedSubOverflow)?;
        Ok((total_pc_without_take_pnl, total_coin_without_take_pnl))
    }
