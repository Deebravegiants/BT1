### Title
Supply-index floor clamp resurrects wiped-out supplier claims, letting drained positions steal fresh deposits - (File: contracts/pool/src/interest.rs)

### Summary

`apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` socializes bad debt by scaling the supply index down toward zero, but then clamps the result *up* to `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW` (RAY/1000) "to avoid a zero index". When the socialized debt meets or exceeds total supplied value, the clamp leaves every pre-existing scaled supply share with a positive unscaling factor against zero real backing — a phantom claim. Because withdrawal only checks `require_reserves` (cash on hand) rather than whether claims are backed, the first fresh deposit after a full wipeout can be withdrawn out by a wiped supplier. This is the analog of the CVE-2022-2624 heap-buffer-overflow class: a bounds/guard value written past the intended accounting domain corrupts adjacent state (another user's balance), producing an out-of-bounds read of pool cash.

### Finding Description

In `contracts/pool/src/interest.rs`, `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` computes:

```rust
let capped = bad_debt.min(total_supplied_value);
let remaining = total_supplied_value.checked_sub(cache.env(), capped);
let reduction_factor = remaining.div_floor(cache.env(), total_supplied_value);
let new_supply_index = cache.supply_index().mul_floor(cache.env(), reduction_factor);
cache.set_supply_index(new_supply_index.max(Ray::from(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)));
``` [1](#0-0) 

When `bad_debt >= total_supplied_value`, `remaining = 0`, `reduction_factor = 0`, `new_supply_index = 0`, and the `.max(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)` lifts the index to `RAY/1000`. All existing `scaled` supply shares still exist, so `unscale_supply_floor(scaled)` returns a positive asset amount even though the socialization was supposed to write supplier claims down to zero.

The write path is reachable permissionlessly: `controller::process_liquidation` ends in `apply::check_bad_debt_after_liquidation` [2](#0-1) , and `process_clean_bad_debt` admits any caller once the position is insolvent with dust-level collateral (`BadDebtGate::DustCapped`) [3](#0-2) . Both produce a `PoolSeizeEntry` with `side = Borrow`, which `pool::seize::apply` routes into `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` [4](#0-3) .

The pool's own unit test proves the theft: after the clamp, `resolve_withdrawal(i128::MAX, old_scaled)` returns `gross > 0`, `require_reserves` passes once a fresh deposit adds cash, and the wiped position "drains exactly the fresh deposit", leaving `cache.cash() < fresh_claim` [5](#0-4) .

### Impact Explanation

Theft of user funds / protocol insolvency. After a bad-debt wipeout, every legacy supplier retains a claim valued at `scaled × RAY/1000` with no backing. Any subsequent `supply` deposits real cash into the market; the wiped suppliers can then `withdraw` and take that cash before the fresh supplier does. The new supplier's claim becomes unpayable — a direct loss transferred from the newest depositor to accounts whose claims were supposed to be written to zero.

### Likelihood Explanation

Triggering requires a market where socialized bad debt equals or exceeds total supplied value — i.e., near-total wipeout. This happens when an insolvent account's debt is socialized into a market whose supplier base is small relative to the debt, which is precisely the regime `clean_bad_debt` is designed for (dust collateral, all debt). The dust-threshold gate is satisfiable by permissionless callers, and the post-liquidation hook fires it automatically. The attacker side (being a wiped supplier who front-runs the next deposit, or a fresh depositor being victimized) requires no privilege. Likelihood is medium: it needs a real wipeout event, but no admin action, no oracle manipulation, and no timing exoticism — an attacker can even supply fresh cash in a market they know is clamped if they hold wiped shares.

### Recommendation

When `remaining == 0`, zero out the scaled supply itself (burn `supplied` shares / reset the market) rather than clamping the index to a nonzero floor — the index and the share supply must be zeroed together, not independently. Alternatively, if `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW` is kept to avoid div-by-zero in `calculate_scaled_supply`, gate withdrawal on the claim's backing: cap `gross` in `resolve_withdrawal` at the market's *allocated* supplier value (e.g., `total_supplied_value` snapshot at wipeout), not merely at `cash`. A third option is to record a `wipeout` flag in the market snapshot and reject supply/withdraw until `recapitalize` restores solvency.

### Proof of Concept

1. Market M has supplier S_old holding scaled shares `S`, borrow index and supply index normal.
2. An account's debt in M becomes fully insolvent; a liquidator calls `controller::liquidate` (or anyone calls `clean_bad_debt` once collateral ≤ dust). `pool.seize(Borrow)` runs `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` with `bad_debt ≥ total_supplied_value` → `supply_index` clamps to `RAY/1000` while `supplied = S` shares remain.
3. Supplier S_old now owns a phantom claim `S × RAY/1000 > 0` against `cash = 0`.
4. Victim V calls `supply(amount)`; `mint_supply` + `credit_cash` put real tokens in the market.
5. S_old calls `withdraw(i128::MAX)`: `resolve_withdrawal` unscales `S` at the floored index, `require_reserves` passes against V's cash, `debit_cash` pays V's deposit to S_old.
6. V's own withdraw now fails `require_reserves` — funds stolen. This is the exact sequence asserted in `contracts/pool/tests/interest.rs:372-427`.

    validation::require_not_flash_loaning(env);
    clean_bad_debt_standalone(env, account_id);

/// Admission condition for bad-debt socialization.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq)]
enum BadDebtGate {
    /// Permissionless: insolvent *and* collateral at or below the dust threshold.
    DustCapped,
    /// Owner-only: insolvent alone, with no cap on the collateral left behind.
    InsolventOnly,
}

/// Requires open debt and the selected insolvency gate, then cleans up the account.
fn socialize_bad_debt(env: &Env, account_id: u64, gate: BadDebtGate) {
    let mut cache = Context::new(env);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        !account.borrow_positions.is_empty(),
        CollateralError::DebtPositionNotFound
    );

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    let admits = match gate {
        BadDebtGate::DustCapped => {
            is_socializable_bad_debt(totals.total_debt, totals.total_collateral)
        }
        BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly => totals.total_debt > totals.total_collateral,
    };
    assert_with_error!(env, admits, CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt);

    bad_debt::execute_bad_debt_cleanup(env, &mut cache, account_id, &account, &totals);
}

/// Socializes insolvent debt when remaining collateral is at or below the dust cap.
pub(crate) fn clean_bad_debt_standalone(env: &Env, account_id: u64) {
    socialize_bad_debt(env, account_id, BadDebtGate::DustCapped);
}

#[test]
fn test_raw_cache_floor_clamp_strands_claim_without_supply_guard() {
    let t = TestSetup::new();
    t.as_contract(|| {
        let old_scaled_raw = 1_000 * RAY;
        let mut cache = t.fresh_cache(PoolStateRaw {
            supplied: old_scaled_raw,
            borrowed: 0,
            revenue: 0,
            borrow_index: RAY,
            supply_index: RAY,
            last_timestamp: 0,
            cash: 0,
        });
        let old_scaled = Ray::from(old_scaled_raw);

        apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(&mut cache, Ray::from(5_000 * RAY));
        assert_eq!(
            cache.supply_index().raw(),
            SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW,
            "wipeout clamps supply_index UP to RAY/1000 instead of resetting shares to 0",
        );

        let stranded = cache.unscale_supply_floor(old_scaled);
        assert!(stranded > 0, "floor clamp leaves S_old a phantom claim");
        assert_eq!(
            cache.cash(),
            0,
            "no cash yet: invariant only masked by require_reserves"
        );

        let fresh_cash = stranded;
        let fresh_scaled = cache.calculate_scaled_supply(fresh_cash);
        cache.mint_supply(fresh_scaled);
        cache.credit_cash(fresh_cash);

        let fresh_claim = cache.unscale_supply_floor(fresh_scaled);
        assert_eq!(
            fresh_claim, fresh_cash,
            "fresh supplier's claim equals deposit"
        );

        let (burn, gross) = cache.resolve_withdrawal(i128::MAX, old_scaled);
        cache.require_reserves(gross);
        cache.burn_supply(burn);
        cache.debit_cash(gross);

        assert!(gross > 0, "stranded wiped position pays out real tokens");
        assert_eq!(gross, fresh_cash, "S_old drains exactly the fresh deposit");
        assert!(
            cache.cash() < fresh_claim,
            "pool cash ({}) can no longer cover fresh supplier claim ({}): funds lost",
            cache.cash(),
            fresh_claim,
        );
    });
