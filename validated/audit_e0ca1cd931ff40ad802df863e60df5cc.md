### Title
Accrual `MathOverflow` panic permanently freezes a market once `borrowed * borrow_index` exceeds `i128` - ([File: common/src/rates/simulate.rs])

### Summary
The bug class is memory-corruption-via-overflow in `H5FL_arr_malloc`. The Soroban analog is the unchecked-domain `i128` product inside the shared accrual step: `accrue_step` computes `scaled_to_original(borrowed, borrow_index)` (i.e. `borrowed * borrow_index / RAY` via `mul_div_half_up`) before the `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` cap is applied, and panics with `GenericError::MathOverflow` when the product leaves the `i128`/`I256` result domain. Because every market mutation (`supply`, `borrow`, `withdraw`, `repay`, `liquidate`, `clean_bad_debt`, `recapitalize`, flash paths) runs `interest::global_sync` → `accrue_chunk` → `accrue_step` first, crossing that value ceiling bricks the market: no exit, no repayment, no liquidation, forever.

### Finding Description
`common/src/rates/simulate.rs:60-61` calls `scaled_to_original` on both `borrowed` and `supplied` at the top of every step, before `update_borrow_index` clamps the index at `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` (`common/src/rates/index.rs:13-19`). `scaled_to_original` (`common/src/rates/scaling.rs:14-16`) is `scaled.mul(env, index)` → `mul_div_half_up`, which widens the *intermediate* to `I256` but still panics `MathOverflow` when the *quotient* exceeds `i128::MAX` (`common/src/math/fp_core.rs:145-159`). The overflow therefore triggers when `borrowed * borrow_index / RAY > i128::MAX`, i.e. when total debt value exceeds ~1.7e38 RAY — a point reachable while `borrow_index` is still far below the 1e36 cap on a large, high-utilization market. The panic is unconditional in the accrual prologue; no entrypoint can skip it, and time only moves forward, so the state never leaves the overflowing domain. The repo's own harness demonstrates the freeze end-to-end: `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:320-356` shows `update_indexes`, `withdraw` and `repay` all reverting `MATH_OVERFLOW` once the cliff is crossed, with `borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`. `docs/reference/formulas.md:432-437` acknowledges the mechanism ("value overflow can occur before the index ceiling and block repayment/withdrawal") but treats it as a limit rather than handling it — the cap on the index does not protect the value domain.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing of funds: once `borrowed * borrow_index` crosses the `i128` quotient bound, all suppliers' deposits and all outstanding debt in that market are unreachable by any unprivileged path — `withdraw`, `repay`, `liquidate`, `clean_bad_debt`, `flash_loan`, `net_settle` and `claim_revenue` all accrue first and revert. The panic is fail-open for nobody; it is a hard brick, and the overflow condition can only worsen with time since `borrow_index` is monotone.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. The trigger requires a very large market: total debt value must reach ~1.7e38 RAY-units, e.g. ~1 billion whole tokens of debt on an 18-decimal asset with the borrow index grown ~170x, or proportionally less index growth on a larger book. Caps validate against `i128::MAX / 10^(27-d)` (~170 billion whole tokens), so the debt ceiling sits *inside* admitted cap range — an unprivileged whale can supply and drive utilization high, and sustained utilization on a steep curve segment grows the index past the cliff without any privileged action beyond the listing's own cap. It is not reachable on small markets.

### Recommendation
Saturate rather than panic in the accrual prologue: compute `scaled_to_original` for utilization via `mul_div_floor_saturating` (already used by `calculate_scaled_cap`), so utilization pins at 100% and `update_borrow_index` clamps to `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` instead of reverting; equally, clamp `borrowed`/`supplied` valuation before the multiply in `calculate_supplier_rewards` and `update_supply_index` (both also run `x.mul(index)` on the same overflowing product). At minimum, cap `borrow_index` at `i128::MAX / borrowed` when the computed cap is lower than `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`, so the market degrades to zero-accrual rather than freezing.

### Proof of Concept
`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs::a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` (lines 320-361) supplies ~1e9 whole tokens, borrows 98% of the market, advances time, and asserts `try_update_indexes_for`, `try_withdraw_raw` and `try_repay` all fail with `MATH_OVERFLOW` while `borrow_index` remains below `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` — a self-contained demonstration of the permanent freeze reachable through `supply`/`borrow`/`update_indexes` alone.
