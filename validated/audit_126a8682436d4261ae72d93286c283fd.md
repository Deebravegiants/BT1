### Title
Unprivileged borrower can strand insolvent low-decimal collateral debt beyond the reach of `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/math.rs)

### Summary
CVE-2019-2752 is an availability bug class: a reachable call path that always fails, leaving the system unable to service a required operation. The analog in XOXNO Lending is the whole-unit seizure logic for collateral listed below `MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS` (3 decimals). When an insolvent account's single collateral leg is worth less than roughly one whole unit at the liquidation bonus but more than the $5 `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD`, every `liquidate` offer reverts with `InvalidPayments` and permissionless `clean_bad_debt` reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt`. The bad debt is unreachable by any unprivileged entrypoint and can only be removed by the owner-gated, timelocked `force_socialize_bad_debt`.

### Finding Description
`whole_unit_repayment` (the quote adjustment that keeps sub-3-decimal legs liquidatable) only applies while the account is solvent; it returns the raw curve quote otherwise, and an insolvent plan uses `floor(C / (1 + base_bonus))` [1](#0-0) . In `calculate_seized_collateral`, a partial plan on a sub-3-decimal leg floors the seizure to whole units; when the backed quote is below one unit, `seizure_ray` becomes zero, the leg is dropped, and a plan with no seizure reverts with `InvalidPayments` [2](#0-1) . The documented consequence: "every offer reverts until accrual or a price move ends that state" — and for an insolvent account neither rule 1 nor rule 2 rescue applies [3](#0-2) .

The permissionless fallback fails too: `socialize_bad_debt` with `BadDebtGate::DustCapped` requires collateral ≤ the fixed $5 dust threshold on top of `D > C` [4](#0-3) . A unit worth more than $5 (the in-scope RWA-style listings have units of $1,000) blows straight through that cap. Only `force_socialize_bad_debt` (owner-only, Sensitive-tier timelock via governance) omits the cap [5](#0-4) .

### Impact Explanation
A borrower can deliberately construct this state: supply 2 whole units of a high-value sub-3-decimal asset (the minimum permitted to hold debt per `MIN_WHOLE_UNIT_COLLATERAL`), borrow near the LTV limit, then wait for a price decline or let interest accrue until `D > C` while `C` remains between $5 and one unit's `U × (1 + b)`. From that point:

- Every `liquidate` call reverts (`InvalidPayments`) — no offer size can seize, because the only leg rounds to zero whole units.
- Every `clean_bad_debt` call reverts (`CannotCleanBadDebt`) — collateral exceeds $5.
- The position keeps accruing borrow interest, deepening the shortfall, while suppliers' backing is trapped behind the stuck account.

This matches the accepted impact "temporary freezing of funds" escalating toward insolvency: supplier funds backing the bad debt are frozen until privileged governance intervention, and the freeze duration is unbounded absent that intervention. It is the same DoS shape as the CVE — a repeatable crash/forced failure of a required operation — mapped onto liquidation liveness.

### Likelihood Explanation
Fully reachable by a single unprivileged address through `supply`, `borrow`, and passive waiting (or self-triggered via the natural accrual/`update_indexes` path). No privileged action, oracle manipulation, or third-party cooperation is needed; the attacker only needs a listed sub-3-decimal collateral market whose unit value exceeds $5. The cost is the attacker's own collateral, which is already consumed by the bad debt, so griefing is cheap relative to the frozen supplier principal. The main mitigations keeping this at Medium rather than High: the privileged `force_socialize_bad_debt` escape hatch exists, and the stuck amount per account is bounded by roughly one unit of collateral plus accrued debt.

### Recommendation
Extend the insolvent-plan path so a whole-unit leg rounds up to seize the last unit when it is the only thing standing between the account and bad debt — i.e., apply a rule-1-style full close when `C < D` and the floored seizure is zero but `held_units >= 1`, letting the liquidator repay `floor(C / (1+b))`-equivalent for the unit. Alternatively, tie `is_socializable_bad_debt`'s dust cap to the lesser of $5 and one unit's current USD value for single-leg sub-3-decimal accounts, so `clean_bad_debt` admits "collateral that cannot be liquidated because it is smaller than one seizable unit" as dust.

### Proof of Concept
1. Governance has listed a 0-2 decimal asset `LIQV` at NAV $1,000 (mirroring `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/liqvid_rwa_collateral.rs` parameters).
2. Attacker calls `supply(caller=attacker, account_id=0, assets=[LIQV × 2])`, then `borrow` to the LTV limit (~$1,000 debt at LT 50%).
3. Price falls (or interest accrues) until `D > C = $1,000` → account insolvent with 2 units; after any liquidation that sells one unit the same condition recurs at `C = $1,000` (1 unit), `D > $1,000`.
4. Any liquidator calling `liquidate(account_id, payments, SeizeMode::Transfer)` reverts with `InvalidPayments` (#16): `quote = floor(C/(1+base)) ≈ $952` backs `< 1` whole unit of `LIQV`, the leg is dropped, and the plan seizes nothing — matching `an_insolvent_trim_drops_a_leg_whose_smallest_unit_exceeds_the_backed_quote` [6](#0-5) .
5. Any address calling `clean_bad_debt` reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt` (#114): `total_collateral ≈ $1,000 > BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD = $5` [4](#0-3) .
6. The debt accrues indefinitely; only the timelocked owner path `force_socialize_bad_debt` can clear it [7](#0-6) .

/// Raises a solvent partial quote to one whole unit when the account's only
/// supply leg is below `MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS` and the quoted seizure
/// is below one unit. The raised repayment backs one unit plus one millionth

/// leg rounds up to one unit. Keeps the quote

        return quote_usd;
    };
    let feed = cache.cached_price(&hub_asset.asset);
    if feed.asset_decimals >= MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS {
        return quote_usd;
    }
    let supply_index = cache.cached_market_index(&hub_asset).supply_index;
    let held_units = position
        .scaled_amount
        .mul(env, supply_index)

        return quote_usd;

/// $10 of collateral backs $9.52, less than one $10 unit: the leg keeps
/// nothing, its whole offer is refunded and no repayment leg remains.
#[test]
fn an_insolvent_trim_drops_a_leg_whose_smallest_unit_exceeds_the_backed_quote() {
    let env = Env::default();
    let (keys, plan) = plan_on_insolvent_book(&env, &[(10_000 * WAD, 3, 5, 2)], 10 * WAD, 50 * WAD);

    assert!(plan.repaid.is_empty(), "no leg fits the backed quote");
    assert_eq!(plan.repay_usd, Wad::ZERO);
    assert_eq!(plan.refunds.len(), 1);
    let refund = plan.refunds.get_unchecked(0);
    assert_eq!(refund.asset, keys.get_unchecked(0).asset);
    assert_eq!(refund.amount, 2, "the whole offer is refunded");
}
