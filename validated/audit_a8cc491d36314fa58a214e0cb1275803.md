### Title
A single market whose interest accrual overflows `MathOverflow` freezes every controller operation for every account holding that market — pool-level freeze plus account-level risk loop - ([File: contracts/controller/src/risk/totals.rs])

### Summary
Analogous to the Perennial vault `_settleUnderlying` loop, XOXNO Lending has a monolithic all-markets traversal on every risk-gated path: `calculate_account_risk_totals` iterates **every** supply and debt position of an account and forces a fresh index simulation for each market via `Context::load_markets` → `fetch_pool_bulk_indexes` → `LiquidityPoolClient::get_bulk_indexes`. If any one `(hub_id, asset)` market can no longer simulate accrual without panicking, the entire call reverts — the account cannot `withdraw`, `borrow`, `repay`-and-`withdraw`, `liquidate`, `clean_bad_debt`, or `update_account_threshold`, and the frozen market itself rejects every pool verb (`supply`, `withdraw`, `repay`, `seize_positions`, `recapitalize`), because each one accrues first.

The repo's own test suite demonstrates that such a permanently-broken market is reachable: `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` shows `update_indexes`, `withdraw`, and `repay` all reverting with `MathOverflow` once a market's `borrow_index` growth makes `scaled_to_original` overflow `i128` — and the `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` cap never engages before that cliff.

### Finding Description
- `calculate_account_risk_totals_body` calls `cache.load_markets(&portfolio_hub_keys(supply_positions.keys(), &borrow_positions.keys()))`, then reads `cache.cached_price` and `cache.cached_market_index` inside per-position loops over the full book (contracts/controller/src/risk/totals.rs:163-201).
- `Context::fetch_market_indexes`/`cached_market_index` unconditionally call the pool's `get_bulk_indexes` view (contracts/controller/src/context.rs:111-134; contracts/controller/src/external/pool.rs:150-156), which forward-simulates each market's accrual. A panic in any single leg aborts the whole bulk call — there is no try/catch or exclusion mechanism, exactly like `_settleUnderlying`.
- On the pool side, every mutating entrypoint runs `synced_market`/`renewed_market` → `interest::global_sync` before doing anything (contracts/pool/src/ops/mod.rs:30-40), and `update_indexes` loops `Cache::load` + `global_sync` per market with no isolation (contracts/pool/src/ops/market.rs:65-73).
- The breakable market: the harness test drives an 18-decimal market at ~98% sustained utilization until accrual panics inside `scaled_to_original` with `MathOverflow` **before** `borrow_index` reaches `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` — the index cap is dead code at the cliff (tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:316-360). The panic is in `scaled_to_original`/`unscale` math used by `calculate_utilization` and all unscaling (contracts/pool/src/cache/scale.rs:19-27).

An unprivileged whale reaches this state through ordinary `supply`/`borrow` calls: push a high-decimals market to maximum utilization with a large position and keep it there (borrowing the interest gap as needed). Utilization caps only gate *new* borrows; accrual continues regardless, so the overflow is a matter of time, not permission.

### Impact Explanation
- **Permanent freezing of funds**: once the market overflows on accrual, `pool.withdraw`, `pool.repay`, `seize_positions`, and `recapitalize` for that market all panic on `global_sync`, so every supplier's deposit in that market is locked forever.
- **Account-level freeze (the analog core)**: any account whose supply or borrow book includes the broken market can no longer pass `calculate_account_risk_totals`, so `withdraw` of *other* collateral, `repay`, and `update_account_threshold` revert as long as any debt exists — unrelated healthy collateral is hostage to the broken market.
- **Protocol insolvency**: `liquidate` also runs the same risk-totals load over the victim's full position set, so underwater positions touching the frozen market can never be liquidated; `clean_bad_debt`'s `seize_positions` leg panics on accrual too. Bad debt accrues with no cleanup path.
- Same blast radius as the original report: one broken market bricks core functionality rather than being excludable.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. It requires a whale-scale position and sustained near-maximum utilization on a high-decimals market — the test used disabled `max_utilization` caps and multi-decade accrual — but every step is an unprivileged `supply`/`borrow`/`update_indexes` call, no governance or oracle manipulation is needed, and nothing in `global_sync` or `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` prevents crossing the `i128` cliff. Once crossed it is irreversible (no `upgrade`-free remediation path exists for the stored state).

### Recommendation
- Clamp `borrow_index`/`supply_index` growth inside `interest::global_sync` *before* the multiplication that overflows (enforce `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` as a saturating cap rather than letting `scaled_to_original` panic), so accrual can never permanently brick a market.
- Make the account-level valuation loop fault-tolerant in the same spirit as the report's recommendation: e.g., evaluate `get_bulk_indexes` per market (or via `try_` invocation) so a single panicking market can be excluded from `calculate_account_risk_totals`, letting users withdraw healthy collateral and liquidators act on the remaining legs.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker calls `controller.supply` and `controller.borrow` to hold a high-decimals market (e.g., 18 decimals, steep interest curve) at ~98% utilization.
2. Interest accrues through `update_indexes` (permissionless, `caller` unchecked) and through every subsequent verb's `global_sync`.
3. After sufficient accrual, `scaled_to_original(borrowed, borrow_index)` overflows `i128` → `MathOverflow`; `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` never engages (demonstrated by `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap`, which asserts `MathOverflow` on `update_indexes`, `withdraw`, and `repay` for that market).
4. Any account with a supply or debt position in that market now fails `cache.load_markets` inside `calculate_account_risk_totals_body`, so `withdraw` of unrelated collateral, `repay`, `liquidate`, and `clean_bad_debt` all revert — permanently.

Uncertainty note: I could not fully verify whether `max_utilization` caps under default governance parameters still permit the sustained utilization needed within a realistic horizon, or whether remediation via contract `upgrade` is considered in scope — but the on-chain defect (overflow reachable before the index cap, no per-market isolation in the risk loop) is directly evidenced by the in-repo test.
