### Title
Caller-selected flash-position receivers can execute arbitrary authorized token transfers - ([File: contracts/controller/src/strategies/flash_position.rs](contracts/controller/src/strategies/flash_position.rs))

### Summary
`flash_position` accepts a caller-selected contract receiver, mints and forwards debt, and invokes that contract inside the caller-authorized transaction. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1)  A malicious receiver can use the callback to invoke token transfers from the victim's address; if the victim signs the resulting authorization tree, unrelated wallet assets can be transferred to the attacker.

### Finding Description
`process_flash_position` authenticates only the root `caller`, validates that `receiver` is a Wasm contract other than the controller and pool, then invokes the receiver's `execute_flash_position` callback. [3](#0-2)  The receiver address and callback `data` are supplied through user-controlled parameters, and the callback receives the initiator address, account ID, debt asset, amount received, controller address, and opaque data. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4)
