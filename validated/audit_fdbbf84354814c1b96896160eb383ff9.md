### Title
Permanent market freeze from i128 overflow in interest accrual — all repay/withdraw/liquidate calls panic - (File: contracts/pool/src/interest.rs)

### Summary
Analogous to the MySQL "hang or frequently repeatable crash (complete DOS)" class, the XOXNO Lending pool can be pushed into a state where its arithmetic overflows and every state-changing entrypoint reverts permanently. `global_sync` in `contracts/pool/src/interest.rs` runs `accrue_step` (from `common/src/rates/simulate.rs`) before any mutation, and `accrue_step` multiplies scaled share totals by the live index in `scaled_to_original` — an i128 multiplication that panics once `supplied × supply_index` or `borrowed × borrow_index` exceeds `i128::MAX`. Since `supplied` and `borrowed` are RAY-scaled (27 decimals on top of token decimals), a large market with a rising index crosses that ceiling while the indexes are still far below their `MAX_*_RAY` caps, so the caps never engage. A checked-cast/mul panic inside `global_sync` bricks the market: no supply, borrow, repay, withdraw, liquidation seizure, or bad-debt cleanup touching that market can ever execute again.

### Finding Description
The root cause is that index bounds (`MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`/`MAX_SUPPLY_INDEX_RAY` at 10^36) are enforced on the index, while the overflow site is `scaled_amount × index` on positions/markets that can themselves approach ~10^36 RAY or more. `update_borrow_index` caps the index after multiplying, but `accrue_step`'s `scaled_to_original` on `borrowed × borrow_index` has no saturation: it panics with `MathOverflow`. The invariants doc acknowledges the gap — "Debt-value overflow can still revert accrual before that ceiling is reached" — but the freeze is total and irreversible rather than a bounded failure. The harness test `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` demonstrates the cliff end-to-end: after the panic point, `try_update_indexes`, `try_withdraw`, and `try_repay` all revert with `MATH_OVERFLOW`.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing of funds: all suppliers' deposits in the affected market are unrecoverable, borrowers cannot repay, liquidators cannot liquidate or trigger bad-debt cleanup, and governance `claim_revenue`/`recapitalize` on that market also accrues first and reverts. Because every mutating path calls `Cache::load` → `global_sync`, the market is dead the moment the overflow condition is reached. This matches the accepted "permanent freezing of funds" / "contract unable to operate" impacts.

### Likelihood Explanation
An unprivileged address can seed the condition with only `supply` and `borrow`: deposit a very large token amount (the test uses ~10^27 base units of an 18-decimal token) and borrow at high utilization on a steep rate curve; compounding then does the rest. The barrier is capital — the attacker must supply whale-scale liquidity and the state must sit at high utilization long enough for the index to grow ~170× on a billion-token market — but no privileged action, leaked key, or off-chain dependence is required, and nothing in the protocol rejects oversized markets or de-rates accrual as the ceiling approaches. Medium severity: high impact, significant capital/time precondition.

### Recommendation
Saturate, don't panic, in the accrual path: in `common/src/rates` (`scaled_to_original` and `accrue_step`), clamp the scaled-to-original product to `i128::MAX` (or a defined ceiling) and derive utilization/rate/rewards from the clamped value, so `global_sync` completes and `mark_accrued` advances `last_timestamp`. Alternatively, enforce a hard total-market cap (`supplied × MAX_SUPPLY_INDEX_RAY ≤ i128::MAX` scaled, or a token-denominated cap per market) at supply/borrow entry in the controller's spoke-usage checks so the overflowing region is unreachable.

### Proof of Concept
Existing executable proof: `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs::a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` supplies 10^9 whole 18-decimal tokens, borrows ~98%, advances time, and asserts `update_indexes`, `withdraw`, and `repay` all revert with `MathOverflow` while `borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`. Reproduction flow: `supply` whale amount of an 18-decimal asset → `borrow` ~98% of it → let interest accrue until `borrowed × borrow_index ≥ i128::MAX` → every subsequent pool call on that market reverts in `global_sync`/`accrue_step`.
