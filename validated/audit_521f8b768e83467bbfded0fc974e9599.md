### Title
Stale-price dust collateral leg bricks liquidation and bad-debt cleanup for the whole account — (File: contracts/controller/src/risk/totals.rs)

### Summary
`calculate_account_risk_totals` values **every** supply and borrow leg of an account via `cache.cached_price`, which panics (`PriceFeedStale` / `OracleNotConfigured`) when any single leg's feed is stale or missing. Because `supply` accepts a new collateral leg even while that asset's feed is stale, a borrower can plant a dust-sized "poison" leg that makes `build_liquidation_plan` — and `clean_bad_debt` — revert unconditionally until the feed recovers, mirroring CVE-2019-10153's "one malformed field kills the fencing agent" class.

### Finding Description
- `calculate_account_risk_totals_body` iterates all `supply_positions` and calls `cache.cached_price(&hub_asset.asset)` per leg (`contracts/controller/src/risk/totals.rs:171-180`). `cached_price`/`fetch_prices` panic with `OracleError` on any stale/missing feed (`contracts/controller/src/context.rs:154-160`).
- `build_liquidation_plan` calls this unconditionally before sizing any repayment (`contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/plan.rs:34-44`), so one unpriceable leg aborts the entire liquidation even though the liquidator only named a healthy debt asset.
- `process_clean_bad_debt` goes through the same risk totals, so the permissionless bad-debt path is blocked identically.
- The leg can be planted by the borrower itself: `supply` of a dust amount succeeds while the asset's feed is stale (there is no freshness gate on adding collateral to an existing solvent-at-call account), and once collateral value drops the account becomes liquidatable-but-unliquidatable.
- The repository already contains a test pinning this behavior: `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_liquidate_and_clean_stale_leg.rs` asserts that after planting a dust WBTC leg with a stale feed, both `liquidate` and `clean_bad_debt` revert with `PRICE_FEED_STALE`, and only succeed once the feed is fresh.

### Impact Explanation
- **Temporary freezing of funds / blocked recovery**: during any staleness window of *any* asset the account supplied, no liquidator can repay that account's debt and no one can clean its bad debt. Interest keeps accruing on unbacked debt.
- **Permanent insolvency risk**: if a feed is ever deprecated, misconfigured, or never recovers, the account is permanently unliquidatable and `clean_bad_debt` permanently reverts — the debt can only be removed via the owner-only `force_socialize_bad_debt`, socializing the loss into the supply index.
- The attacker needs only their own account: borrow to the limit, plant a dust leg in an asset whose oracle is intermittently stale, and let collateral prices fall. Recovery is impossible for the duration.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. Requires a naturally stale feed window (feeds do go stale in practice) and the borrower planting the leg during it — cheap (dust amount) and repeatable. Exploitability is bounded by feed-recovery time for the temporary variant, but the permanent variant triggers on any feed removal.

### Recommendation
In `calculate_account_risk_totals_body` (and the liquidation seize leg selection in `plan.rs`/`math.rs`), treat a supply leg whose price fetch fails as zero-valued (or skip legs with `PriceStatus != Ok` for collateral weighting) rather than panicking the whole computation, and exclude unpriceable collateral legs from `seized_collaterals`. Alternatively, reject `supply` of a new asset leg while its feed is not fresh, so a stale-price leg can never be planted.

### Proof of Concept
See the existing harness test `audit_liquidate_and_clean_bricked_by_unpriceable_dust_leg` (`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/audit_liquidate_and_clean_stale_leg.rs:1-54`):

1. Borrower supplies 10,000 USDC, borrows 3 ETH.
2. WBTC feed is set 3,600 s stale; borrower calls `supply` with 0.001 WBTC — it succeeds.
3. USDC price drops to $0.50, making the account liquidatable.
4. `liquidate(liquidator, account, ETH, 1.0)` → `PriceFeedStale`; `clean_bad_debt` → `PriceFeedStale`.
5. Only after the WBTC feed is refreshed does the identical liquidation succeed.
