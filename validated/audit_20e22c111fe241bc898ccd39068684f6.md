### Title
Permissionless third-party supply lets any address keep an insolvent account above the bad-debt dust gate, indefinitely blocking `clean_bad_debt` - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs)

### Summary
`supply` deliberately permits any authenticated third party to top up an existing supply leg of a foreign account (`require_third_party_existing_supply`, `supply.rs:86-96`). `clean_bad_debt` — the only permissionless way to resolve an insolvent, unprofitable-to-liquidate account — is admitted only while `total_collateral <= BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` ($5) (`curve.rs:25-27`, `mod.rs:229-235`). For the cost of roughly $5 of an asset the account already supplies, an unprivileged attacker can push the collateral total one WAD unit over the cap, and can repeat the top-up every time accrual/price movement would drop the account back under the gate. The account then requires the owner-only, governance-timelocked `force_socialize_bad_debt`, so the attack converts a one-transaction permissionless cleanup into a governance-dependent operation, leaving bad debt to accrue interest and socialize larger losses into supply indexes later.

### Finding Description
- `controller::supply(caller, account_id, spoke_id, assets)` only requires `caller.require_auth()` and, for non-owner/non-delegate callers, that each supplied `hub_asset` already exists in `account.supply_positions` — no consent from the victim's owner is needed (`require_third_party_existing_supply`). INV-AUTH-03 and `permissionless_entrypoints.txt` document this as intended.
- The permissionless socialization gate is `is_socializable_bad_debt(total_debt, total_collateral) = total_debt > total_collateral && total_collateral <= $5`. `socialize_bad_debt` reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt` when it fails. The same gate runs inside `check_bad_debt_after_liquidation`, so the residual-cleanup path after partial liquidations is also shut.
- The Certora rule `bad_debt_straddle_blocks_dust_gate` (`certora/controller/spec/boundary_rules.rs:44-66`) explicitly encodes this straddle: `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD + 1` of collateral under a larger debt blocks the dust gate while remaining insolvent.
- Because collateral is valued in USD, the cheapest sustained attack is topping up the account's *volatile* collateral leg to just above $5, or topping up whenever oracle/accrual drift would re-open the gate. Each top-up also restamps that leg's risk params, but `apply_gated_liquidation_params` silently keeps the old tuple when the HF gate fails, so the top-up cannot accidentally make the account healthy enough to matter.
- The account stays insolvent (`debt > collateral` is unaffected by adding ~$5 when the shortfall is larger), so liquidation remains economically pointless for third parties — seized value is capped at held collateral — and `clean_bad_debt` now reverts. Resolution is left to `force_socialize_bad_debt`, which is owner-only behind the Sensitive governance delay tier (`runbooks/force-socialize-bad-debt.md`), not callable by the public at all.

### Impact Explanation
Temporary freezing of funds / delayed insolvency resolution. Bad debt that the protocol designed to be cleared atomically and permissionlessly (protecting suppliers from compounding losses) is instead pinned on the books for at least a full governance timelock cycle, during which borrow-index accrual grows the shortfall that is ultimately written down against supply indexes (`apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`). The attacker's cost is a ~$5 donation per straddle (recoverable by no one — it becomes protocol revenue at cleanup). If governance is slow, unreachable, or the shortfall is in a paused/edge-case listing, the freeze can extend far beyond one timelock. Severity: Medium, matching the availability-only impact class of the reference CVE.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires only: an insolvent account whose remaining collateral is at/near the $5 gate, and an attacker holding a few dollars of any asset already in that account's supply set. Both conditions arise naturally at the end of every liquidation cascade — the gap band between "liquidatable" and "socializable" is exactly where accounts land after unprofitable liquidations stop. No privileged access, flash capital, or oracle manipulation is needed; `supply` is permissionless for existing legs and cannot be blocked by the victim. The main limiting factor is that the attacker gains nothing directly — the motive is griefing (delaying socialization, e.g., to worsen a competitor's or the protocol's books, or to keep interest accruing on a manipulated rate path).

### Recommendation
Decouple the dust gate from an attacker-controllable quantity: either (a) charge third-party top-ups to a separate non-collateral accounting or require owner/delegate auth when the account is insolvent (e.g., gate `require_third_party_existing_supply` on `total_debt <= total_collateral`), or (b) count only the account's own accrued collateral toward `total_collateral` in `is_socializable_bad_debt` — e.g., track a "collateral excluding third-party top-ups after insolvency" figure, or make `clean_bad_debt` first refund/seize recent third-party supply legs to the supplier before evaluating the gate. The simplest fix consistent with existing shape: in `process_supply`, reject third-party top-ups when `calculate_account_risk_totals` shows `total_debt > total_collateral`, since topping up a foreign insolvent account has no legitimate use case.

### Proof of Concept
1. ALICE supplies 1,000 USDC, borrows 0.3 ETH (≈$600). USDC price crashes to $0.30 → collateral $300, debt $600+: insolvent.
2. Liquidators repay ~$295 worth, seizing ~$295 of collateral at near-zero effective bonus; remaining state: debt ≈ $310, collateral ≈ $4.90 → `is_socializable_bad_debt` true; `check_bad_debt_after_liquidation` would fire, or any keeper calls `clean_bad_debt(account_id)`.
3. Attacker BOB calls `supply(bob, alice_account_id, spoke_id, [(USDC_hub_asset, 20 cents)])` — ALICE's account already holds a USDC supply leg, so `require_third_party_existing_supply` passes. Collateral is now $5.10 > `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD`.
4. Any subsequent `clean_bad_debt(alice_account_id)` reverts `CannotCleanBadDebt` (`mod.rs:235`); liquidation remains unprofitable (repaying $310 of debt can seize at most $5.10).
5. Whenever price/accrual drift would drop collateral back under $5, BOB repeats step 3 for cents. Resolution then requires the owner-gated Sensitive-tier governance operation `force_socialize_bad_debt`, while interest continues accruing on the unsocialized debt.

Harness sketch (mirroring `test_bad_debt_gap_band_resolved_by_liquidation` in `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/keeper.rs:143-164`): after the price crash, before liquidating, call `t.try_supply_to_account(BOB, ALICE, "USDC", 0.2)` and observe `try_clean_bad_debt_by_id` now failing with `CANNOT_CLEAN_BAD_DEBT` and `can_be_liquidated` remaining true only for unprofitable closes.
