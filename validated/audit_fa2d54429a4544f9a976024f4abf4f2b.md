### Title
Suppliers can withdraw at the pre-write-down index after an account becomes insolvent, concentrating the entire bad-debt loss on remaining suppliers - (contracts/pool/src/ops/withdraw.rs)

### Summary
XOXNO Lending socializes realized bad debt by reducing the market's `supply_index` (`apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`), but the write-down is only applied inside a liquidation or `clean_bad_debt`/`force_socialize_bad_debt` call. Between the moment a price crash makes an account insolvent and the moment someone executes that write-down, `withdraw` performs no solvency gate: `gate_and_debit` checks only `require_reserves` (cash availability), utilization, and `require_supply_for_debt`. A supplier who exits in this window is paid at the unimpaired index while `backing_shortfall` still values the doomed debt at face. This is the same first-come-first-served overpayment class as the Blueberry SoftVault report: early withdrawers are made whole and late suppliers absorb more than their pro-rata share of the loss.

### Finding Description
- Withdraw path: `accounting` → `gate_and_debit` in `contracts/pool/src/ops/withdraw.rs:57-119`. The final gate calls `cache.require_reserves(net_transfer)`, `guards::require_utilization_below_max`, and `guards::require_supply_for_debt`, then `debit_cash` — no `require_backed_market` and no check that pending insolvencies were written down.
- `guards::backing_shortfall` (contracts/pool/src/guards.rs:61-66) computes backing as `cash + unscale_borrow_ceil(borrowed)`, i.e., all outstanding debt at face value. Uncollateralized debt that will inevitably be written off still counts as full backing, so even if a backing check were applied it would not trigger before the write-down.
- The write-down is event-driven, not state-driven: `socialize_bad_debt` (contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs:212-237) reduces `supply_index` only when `clean_bad_debt` (permissionless, dust-capped at $5 collateral) or a liquidation executes. A price update alone changes nothing in the pool books.
- The repository's own test `supplier_can_exit_ahead_of_bad_debt_writedown` (tests/test-harness/tests/controller/bad_debt_index.rs:402-473) demonstrates the exploit end-to-end: after the collateral price crash, Bob withdraws his entire 75% share of the ETH market at the pre-write-down index (`bob_recovered >= bob_before`), and Carol's loss as the remaining supplier is amplified ~4x versus the passive case (`amplification > 3.0` asserted at line 456).

### Impact Explanation
Theft of user funds / unfair loss allocation identical to the reported class. A fast-acting supplier recovers 100% of their deposit while slower suppliers absorb a disproportionately large write-down — in the pinned test, the remaining 25% supplier's loss roughly quadruples. Since the bad-debt deduction is then applied against a shrunken `supplied`, late withdrawers may find the pool unable to honor even their written-down claims if cash is exhausted (FCFS on `require_reserves`).

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. The trigger is a public price update (e.g., a dual-leg price change in the price aggregator) that pushes an account insolvent — fully on-chain observable state. Any unprivileged supplier can monitor oracle updates and front-run the liquidator/cleanup transaction with an ordinary `withdraw` (`WithdrawKind::Normal` path, `amount = 0` sentinel for full exit). No privileged role, no timing assumptions beyond one transaction ordering, and the utilization gate is unlikely to block because borrow-side insolvency does not raise utilization above `max_utilization` while debt is still booked.

### Recommendation
Gate non-liquidation withdrawals on market backing: call `guards::require_backed_market` (or a variant that discounts debt backed by flagged-insolvent accounts) in `gate_and_debit`, or mark a market/account as pending-write-down when a price update makes risk totals insolvent and block exits until `clean_bad_debt`/liquidation applies the index write-down. Alternatively, apply bad-debt write-down at index-sync time for accounts already proven insolvent.

### Proof of Concept
Already encoded as `supplier_can_exit_ahead_of_bad_debt_writedown` (tests/test-harness/tests/controller/bad_debt_index.rs:402-473):
1. Bob supplies 75 ETH, Carol 25 ETH; Alice supplies 10 USDC and borrows 0.003 ETH.
2. USDC price crashes to $0.10 — Alice is insolvent (`assert_liquidatable` passes), but no write-down has run.
3. Bob calls `withdraw` for his full ETH position and recovers his entire deposit at the pre-crash index.
4. A liquidation then applies `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`; Carol's realized loss is asserted to be >3x the loss she would have taken had Bob not exited.
