### Title
Partial `Zap.sell()` calls can strand a sub-floor dust remainder of curve tokens that can never be sold - ([File: packages/contracts/src/Zap.sol])

### Summary
`Zap._sellInternal` only checks that the *slice being sold* clears the BounceTech mint/redeem floor (`minUsdcAmount()`); it never checks that the *remaining* token balance after the sale is either zero or still above that floor. A trader who sells anything less than their full position can be left holding a residual token balance worth less than `minUsdcAmount()` (the live BounceTech `minTransactionSize()`, $10 by default) that can never again be sold through the only reachable sell path.

### Finding Description
`Zap._sellInternal` transfers in `tokenAmount`, converts it to LT via `_sellOnCurve`/`_sellOnUniswapV2`, and pre-checks the *resulting LT's* USDC value against the floor before calling `redeem`: [1](#0-0) 

This mirrors exactly the reported bug class: a check on the amount being moved, with no check on what is left behind. Crucially, curve-side selling is only reachable via `Bonding.sell`, which is `onlyRouter`: [2](#0-1) 

and `Router.sell` is gated to `BONDING_ROLE`, i.e. only `Bonding` can call it: [3](#0-2) 

Since `Zap` is the only allowlisted, unprivileged-reachable router for curve trades, `Zap.sell` is the *sole* path an ordinary trader has to exit curve-token holdings pre-graduation. If a trader sells a partial amount that leaves a remainder whose curve-implied LT value, once converted to USDC via `exchangeRate()`, is below `minUsdcAmount()`, that remainder permanently fails the same `grossUsdcEstimate/1e12 < minUsdcAmount()` guard on every future attempt to sell it — until either the curve price appreciates it back over the floor or the trader buys more tokens to combine with the dust. Newly launched tokens open at ~$3K market cap with 750M sellable supply, so the implied per-token price is a small fraction of a cent, meaning ordinary partial-sell behavior (not selling 100% in one call) can easily strand dust in the sub-$10 range.

This is the direct on-chain analog of the reported `transferWearables()`/`sellPrivateWearables()` issue: a floor check applied to the transacted amount with no check that the resulting remaining balance also clears the floor (or is zero).

### Impact Explanation
Impact is Medium: the stranded dust is not stolen, but it is frozen from the trader's perspective — it cannot be redeemed for USDC through any unprivileged path on the curve. The absolute USD amount lost to freezing per incident is small, but it compounds across every trader who does a partial sell, and is worse for tokens with a low per-token USD price (fresh launches, high supply, or unappreciated LT rate), matching exactly the "low curveAdjustmentFactor or high supply" impact framing from the source report.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is Medium: it requires nothing more than an ordinary trader calling `Zap.sell(tokenAddress, tokenAmount, minUsdcOut)` with `tokenAmount` less than their full balance, sized so the remainder's LT-equivalent value falls under `minUsdcAmount()`. No privileged role, no LT-side griefing, and no timing dependency is required — any trader who doesn't manually compute and sell down to exactly zero risk hitting it, especially given per-token USD granularity on freshly launched curves.

### Recommendation
In `Zap._sellInternal`, after computing the post-sale remaining balance, either require the sold amount equal the trader's full balance, or verify that the *remaining* balance's curve-implied USDC value is either `0` or `>= minUsdcAmount()` (analogous to checking `balance - amount == 0 || balance - amount >= BASE_WEARABLE_UNIT` in the original report), rejecting sells that would otherwise strand dust.

### Proof of Concept
1. Trader buys tokens on a freshly launched curve via `Zap.buy`, ending up holding `N` tokens.
2. Trader calls `Zap.sell(tokenAddress, tokenAmount, 0)` with `tokenAmount = N - dust`, sized so the sale itself clears `minUsdcAmount()` (e.g. it's a large sale) but the remaining `dust` tokens' curve-implied LT value at current `exchangeRate()` is under `minUsdcAmount()` (e.g. under $10).
3. Trader now calls `Zap.sell(tokenAddress, dust, 0)`: `_sellOnCurve` converts `dust` tokens to a small `ltReceived`; `grossUsdcEstimate/1e12 < minUsdcAmount()` evaluates true, and the call reverts with `BelowMinAmount` (see `_sellInternal`, `Zap.sol:444-445`).
4. Since curve `sell` is only reachable through `Zap` (`Bonding.sell` is `onlyRouter`, `Router.sell` is `onlyRole(BONDING_ROLE)`), the trader has no way to redeem the `dust` position pre-graduation; it stays stranded until price appreciation or an additional buy pushes it back over the floor.
