No vulnerability found for this question.

The reported bug is about a statistically biased volatility estimator used in a TWAP/oracle-style windowed variance calculation (QuickSwap's `DataStorage.sol`), where fees derived from a rolling variance are systematically underestimated absent Bessel's correction. Alt.fun's fee model has no analog to this: fees are flat percentages (`buyFeeBps`/`sellFeeBps`/`creatorFeeBps`) applied directly to trade size in `Zap._executeBuy` and `Zap._sellInternal`, with no rolling-window variance, standard-deviation, or statistical-sample-based fee computation anywhere in `packages/contracts/src`. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

Graduation thresholds and curve pricing in `Bonding.canGraduate` and `Router` use deterministic reserve math (`assetReserve`, `exchangeRate()`, `k()`), not any sampled/averaged statistical estimator that could carry a bias term. [3](#0-2) 

No file in `packages/contracts/src` implements a windowed price/volatility accumulator comparable to `DataStorage.sol`, so there is no reachable analog for this finding.
