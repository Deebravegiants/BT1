### Title
Direct token donation to `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault` inflates LP-mint/withdraw ratio, diluting new depositors in favor of existing LPs - (File: `program/src/processor.rs`)

### Summary
`process_deposit` and `process_withdraw` compute the coin/pc reserves used for LP-token mint and redemption math directly from the SPL token account balances of `amm_coin_vault` / `amm_pc_vault` (`amm_coin_vault.amount`, `amm_pc_vault.amount`), rather than from an internal, protocol-tracked reserve value. Because these vaults are ordinary SPL Token accounts, any unprivileged actor can transfer extra `coin`/`pc` tokens into them directly (outside of `Deposit`/`Swap`), inflating the balances the program reads. This is the same root cause as the referenced ElasticSwap H-02 finding: LP-share math derived from a manipulable wallet balance instead of an internally accounted reserve.

### Finding Description
In `process_deposit`, the pool reserves are fetched from the live vault balances: [1](#0-0) 

and are then used, unmodified aside from a pnl adjustment, both to determine the required counter-side amount (`InvariantToken`) and — critically — to determine the LP tokens minted: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

`mint_lp_amount` is computed as `amm.lp_amount * deduct_coin_amount / total_coin_without_take_pnl` (or the pc-side equivalent), where `total_coin_without_take_pnl` / `total_pc_without_take_pnl` come straight from `amm_coin_vault.amount` / `amm_pc_vault.amount` via `Calculator::calc_total_without_take_pnl_no_orderbook`: [4](#0-3) 

`process_withdraw` uses the same reserve values to size the coin/pc payout on redemption: [5](#0-4) 

Because `total_coin_without_take_pnl`/`total_pc_without_take_pnl` are just (donation-inflatable) vault balances minus a pnl adjustment (`calc_take_pnl`), and `calc_take_pnl` only tracks pnl relative to normalized invariant prices rather than validating that the vault balance matches an internally accounted reserve, an attacker can inflate the denominator used in the deposit-side LP-mint ratio by sending extra tokens straight to the vault token account before a victim's `Deposit` transaction. This is structurally identical to the ElasticSwap bug: LP token issuance is keyed off a wallet balance an outsider can freely manipulate, rather than an internal ledger insulated from unsolicited transfers.

### Impact Explanation
An attacker who already holds LP tokens can transfer extra `coin` and/or `pc` tokens directly into `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault` immediately before a victim's `Deposit`. This inflates `total_coin_without_take_pnl`/`total_pc_without_take_pnl`, which lowers the `mint_lp_amount` the victim receives for the same deposited amount (per the `exchange_token_to_pool` ratio). The victim ends up owning a smaller proportional claim on the pool than their contribution warrants. When the attacker later calls `Withdraw`, `exchange_pool_to_token` pays out coin/pc proportional to their (unchanged) LP share against the now-larger total reserve (which includes the donated funds and the victim's under-compensated deposit), letting the attacker extract value contributed by the victim. This is a direct fund-loss vector for LPs and an unbacked/mispriced LP-share accounting issue, matching the High severity classification of the analogous ElasticSwap report.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires only: (1) existing LP position (or none, if just trying to grief/extract from a targeted deposit), (2) an ordinary SPL `Transfer`/`TransferChecked` instruction sending tokens to the publicly-known `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault` addresses (no special authority needed since token transfers only require sender signature), and (3) sequencing it before a target's `Deposit` transaction (e.g., via mempool/slot ordering or simply front-running any deposit, or continuously donating small amounts to skew all future deposits). No privileged signer, leaked key, or non-default build is needed — it is reachable by any unprivileged actor from a single submitted transaction with attacker-chosen accounts/data (a raw SPL token transfer) combined with normal `Deposit`/`Withdraw` instructions.

### Recommendation
Track `coin`/`pc` reserves in the `AmmInfo` account itself (an internal ledger updated only by the program's own `Deposit`/`Withdraw`/`Swap` logic) instead of reading live SPL vault balances for the LP-mint and withdrawal ratio calculations. If vault balances must be read for pnl reconciliation, cross-check them against the internal ledger and reject/clamp unexpected surplus balances (e.g., only ever mint/redeem LP based on the internally tracked reserve, treating any excess vault balance as pnl/protocol surplus subject to `WithdrawPnl` rather than folding it into the LP-mint ratio).

### Proof of Concept
1. Pool exists with `amm.lp_amount = L`, `amm_coin_vault.amount = C`, `amm_pc_vault.amount = P` (already net of pnl).
2. Attacker (any wallet, no special permission) issues a plain SPL `Transfer` of `ΔC` coin tokens directly to `amm_coin_vault` (and optionally `ΔP` pc tokens to `amm_pc_vault`) — a standalone transaction unrelated to any Raydium instruction.
3. Victim submits `Deposit` with `max_coin_amount = c`, `base_side = 0`. `process_deposit` computes `total_coin_without_take_pnl ≈ C + ΔC` per [4](#0-3) , then `mint_lp_amount = L * c / (C + ΔC)` per [6](#0-5) , which is smaller than the fair `L * c / C` the victim would have received absent the donation.
4. Attacker calls `Withdraw` for their existing LP tokens; `coin_amount = total_coin_without_take_pnl * withdraw.amount / amm.lp_amount` per [7](#0-6)  now includes a proportional share of `ΔC` (and the victim's under-compensated deposit), letting the attacker recoup the donated tokens plus a cut of the victim's contribution.

Note: I was unable to fully trace `Calculator::calc_take_pnl`'s internal logic (only its call sites and inputs were inspected) to confirm whether the pnl-normalization step (`normalize_decimal_v2` + `calc_take_pnl`) could partially offset small donations under some market-price conditions; the analysis above holds for the mint/withdraw ratio math regardless, since both explicitly consume `total_coin_without_take_pnl`/`total_pc_without_take_pnl` derived from the raw vault balances.
