No valid analog exists in alt.fun for this vulnerability class.

The Union `UserManager` bug is a sybil/trust-network exploit: a fixed, non-consumable staked amount can be reused across unlimited `updateTrust()` vouches to cheaply grant privileged "member" status (with reward-multiplier benefits) to unlimited new accounts, because the membership check (`_validateNewMember`) only checks `staker.stakedAmount > 0` per voucher without ever debiting or capping reuse of that stake.

alt.fun has no membership, trust, vouching, or staking-for-privilege system at all. Its permissionless entry points (`Zap.createToken`/`buy`/`sell`, `Bonding.triggerGraduation`/`finalizeGraduation`/`transferCreator`, `FeeVault.claim`/`claimProtocol`/`sweepDonations`) do not gate any economic benefit behind a "count of distinct backers whose collateral is checked but never consumed" pattern:

- `Bonding.triggerGraduation` requires only that `canGraduate()` is already true — it does not consume or check any staked collateral, so there is nothing analogous to "reusable stake" to exploit here. [1](#0-0) 
- `FeeVault.accrue`/`claim` track per-creator balances that are debited to zero on claim (`$.creatorBalance[msg.sender] = 0`), so there is no way to "reuse" an accrual across many claimants. [2](#0-1) 
- `Zap.createToken`/`_buyInternal` enforce a hard, non-bypassable minimum (`minSeedUsdc()` / `minUsdcAmount()`) on every call rather than a one-time reusable "stake" backing multiple privileged registrations.
