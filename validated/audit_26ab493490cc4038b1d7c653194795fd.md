### Title
Suppliers can withdraw at the stale pre-writedown supply index and shift bad debt onto remaining suppliers - ([File: contracts/pool/src/ops/withdraw.rs](contracts/pool/src/ops/withdraw.rs))

### Summary
An insolvent position does not affect supplier claims until `liquidate` or `clean_bad_debt` reaches the pool's borrow-side seizure path, so a supplier can withdraw at the pre-writedown `supply_index` before the loss is socialized. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

### Finding Description
`Controller::withdraw` permits the account owner or delegate to withdraw a supply position, with a zero amount denoting a full withdrawal. [3](#0-2)  The controller forwards the currently stored scaled position to the pool without checking whether another account has already become eligible for bad-debt cleanup. [4](#0-3) 

The pool values the position using the current `supply_index` and only enforces cash reserves, the post-withdrawal utilization cap, and nonzero supply while debt remains. [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5)  The withdrawal does not account for the fact that outstanding debt may already be unrecoverable economically; that loss is applied only later, when a borrow-side `PoolSeizeEntry` calls `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` and burns the debt shares. [7](#0-6) 

`clean_bad_debt` builds those seize entries only when it executes, so the market continues to value every supplier's shares at the old index during the interval between the collateral price update and cleanup. [8](#0-7) 

### Impact Explanation
This is a first-exit transfer of losses between suppliers. For example, if Bob holds 40% of an ETH market with 1,000 ETH of supply and 500 ETH of unrecoverable debt, Bob can withdraw 400 ETH before cleanup. The remaining 600 ETH of shares then absorbs the entire 500 ETH write-down, while Bob avoids his 200 ETH pro-rata share. [2](#0-1) 

The exiting supplier receives cash that should economically have borne part of the realized loss, while remaining suppliers' claims are written down more severely. This constitutes theft of user funds from the suppliers that remain in the affected market. [9](#0-8) 

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires an account to become permissionlessly cleanable or liquidatable, sufficient cash and post-withdrawal utilization headroom, and withdrawal ordering before another caller performs liquidation or cleanup. All actions are reachable by unprivileged addresses: `withdraw` for the supplier's own account and `clean_bad_debt` or `liquidate` for any caller. [10](#0-9) [11](#0-10) 

### Recommendation
Introduce a market-level pending-bad-debt state or haircut that is recorded before ordinary withdrawals can consume cash at the pre-loss index. Once an insolvency condition is publicly markable, subsequent withdrawals from the affected debt market should either be blocked until cleanup executes or priced using the pending write-down, so losses remain pro-rata rather than first-come, first-served. [2](#0-1) [12](#0-11) 

### Proof of Concept
1. Bob and Carol supply 400 ETH and 600 ETH, respectively, to the same `(hub_id, ETH)` market.
2. Alice supplies 600 USD of collateral and borrows 500 ETH while the position is solvent.
3. The collateral price falls until Alice has at most 4 USD of collateral, making her position insolvent and eligible for `clean_bad_debt`.
4. Before anyone liquidates or cleans Alice, Bob calls `withdraw(caller=BOB, account_id=bob_id, withdrawals=[(ETH_KEY, 0)], to=None)`, where zero selects his full position. [13](#0-12) 
5. The withdrawal succeeds at the unchanged ETH `supply_index`, leaving Carol as the sole supplier and leaving only 100 ETH of cash. [14](#0-13) 
6. Any caller invokes `clean_bad_debt(caller, alice_id)`, which sends Alice's 500 ETH debt as a borrow-side pool seizure. [15](#0-14) 
7. `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` reduces the ETH supply index by `500 / 600`, leaving Carol's original 600-share claim worth 100 ETH. [16](#0-15) 
8. Carol loses all 500 ETH of bad debt. Had Bob remained, the write-down would have been `500 / 1000`, costing Carol 300 ETH and Bob 200 ETH; Bob's early withdrawal transferred his 200 ETH loss share to Carol.

pub(crate) fn apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(cache: &mut Cache, bad_debt: Ray) {
    let total_supplied_value = cache.supplied().mul(cache.env(), cache.supply_index());

    if total_supplied_value == Ray::ZERO {
        return;
    }

    let capped = bad_debt.min(total_supplied_value);
    let remaining = total_supplied_value.checked_sub(cache.env(), capped);

    let reduction_factor = remaining.div_floor(cache.env(), total_supplied_value);
    let new_supply_index = cache
        .supply_index()
        .mul_floor(cache.env(), reduction_factor);

    cache.set_supply_index(new_supply_index.max(Ray::from(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)));

/// Burns `burned` from market supply and returns the user's remaining scaled position.
fn burn_position(env: &Env, cache: &mut Cache, position: Ray, burned: Ray) -> Ray {

/// Enforces reserve, utilization, and solvency guards, then debits cash for

    guards::require_supply_for_debt(env, cache);

/// Authorizes permissionless dust-gated cleanup outside flash loans.
pub(crate) fn process_clean_bad_debt(env: &Env, caller: &Address, account_id: u64) {
    caller.require_auth();
    validation::require_not_flash_loaning(env);
    clean_bad_debt_standalone(env, account_id);
}

/// Admission condition for bad-debt socialization.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq)]
enum BadDebtGate {
    /// Permissionless: insolvent *and* collateral at or below the dust threshold.
    DustCapped,
    /// Owner-only: insolvent alone, with no cap on the collateral left behind.
    InsolventOnly,
}

/// Requires open debt and the selected insolvency gate, then cleans up the account.
fn socialize_bad_debt(env: &Env, account_id: u64, gate: BadDebtGate) {
    let mut cache = Context::new(env);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        !account.borrow_positions.is_empty(),
        CollateralError::DebtPositionNotFound
    );

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    let admits = match gate {
        BadDebtGate::DustCapped => {
            is_socializable_bad_debt(totals.total_debt, totals.total_collateral)
        }
        BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly => totals.total_debt > totals.total_collateral,
    };
    assert_with_error!(env, admits, CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt);

    bad_debt::execute_bad_debt_cleanup(env, &mut cache, account_id, &account, &totals);
}

/// Socializes insolvent debt when remaining collateral is at or below the dust cap.
pub(crate) fn clean_bad_debt_standalone(env: &Env, account_id: u64) {
    socialize_bad_debt(env, account_id, BadDebtGate::DustCapped);
}
