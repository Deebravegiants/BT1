### Title
`flash_position` mints debt without checking the debt market's `is_borrowable`, `paused`, or `frozen` spoke-listing controls, letting any user borrow a disabled asset - (File: contracts/controller/src/strategies/flash_position.rs)

### Summary
The Keycloak report concerns a low-privilege user bypassing a control intended to disable a flow by manipulating request data. The analog in XOXNO Lending: the controller's `borrow` entrypoint enforces the spoke listing's `is_borrowable` flag and `paused`/`frozen` halt flags through `require_can_borrow` / `require_listed_unhalted_config` (`contracts/controller/src/positions/mod.rs:188-213`), but the `flash_position` strategy mints pool debt into the controller and forwards it to a caller-chosen receiver while checking only `require_hub_active` and `is_flashloanable` on the debt market — never `is_borrowable` or the entry-halt flags on the debt leg. An unprivileged caller therefore obtains debt (and tokens) in a market governance deliberately made non-borrowable or halted.

### Finding Description
`process_flash_position` validates the debt leg with only two checks: `config::require_hub_active(env, debt.hub_id)` and `cache.cached_pool_sync_data(debt).params.is_flashloanable` (`flash_position.rs:59-91`). It then calls `validate_collaterals` and `validate_refund_assets` (`flash_position.rs:103-111`), which gate the *collateral* legs via `require_can_supply` and listing existence — the debt asset is only pushed onto `extra_assets` for price prefetching, never through `require_can_borrow` or `enforce_spoke_asset_flags` with `FreezePolicy::BlockOnEntry`. `mint_and_forward` then calls `borrow_into_controller(..., PositionAction::FlashPos, ...)` (`flash_position.rs:258-296`), minting real debt shares on the account and forwarding the measured tokens to `receiver` before the callback.

By contrast, ordinary borrow entry is gated: `validate_position_entry_gates` → `require_can_borrow` → `require_listed_unhalted_config`, which enforces active listing, `is_borrowable`, `!paused`, `!frozen` (`positions/mod.rs:188-253`). The harness test `test_flash_position_rejects_paused_collateral_before_callback` confirms pause checks exist only for *collateral* legs, and the documented debt-market precondition for `flash_position` is only `is_flashloanable` (`docs/reference/endpoints.md:75`). I could not inspect the body of `borrow_into_controller` in this pass; if it internally re-checks `is_borrowable` this finding collapses, but the presence of `require_can_borrow` as an explicit per-entrypoint gate (rather than inside the shared mint helper) and the docs naming only the flashloan flag both indicate the check is absent on this path.

### Impact Explanation
A single unprivileged address can keep drawing debt from a market after governance disables borrowing (`can_borrow=false`) or halts entry (`paused`/`frozen`), exactly the "disabled flow bypassed" shape of the reference bug. The consequences accepted under the stated rules:

- **Protocol insolvency**: `edit_asset_in_spoke` forces `can_borrow=false` for assets with `asset_decimals < MIN_BORROWABLE_ASSET_DECIMALS` (`config/asset.rs:72-77`). Whole-unit rounding in liquidation assumes that floor; borrowing such an asset via `flash_position` produces sub-3-decimal debt legs the liquidation plan rounds in the protocol's disfavor, and can leave residual bad debt requiring write-down of the supply index.
- **Bypass of risk containment**: a `paused`/`frozen` listing is meant to stop exposure growth immediately (ADR-0008); the attacker grows debt anyway, and since the position only needs to pass post-hoc solvency gates, the protocol carries new exposure in the very market being halted (e.g., during an oracle-incident freeze).
- The minted tokens are real pool cash forwarded to the attacker's receiver; the account keeps open debt, so pool liquidity and supplier principal fund a flow that was supposed to be closed.

### Likelihood Explanation
Any address can call `flash_position` with `account_id = 0` (creating its own Multiply/Long/Short account), any listed collateral its account can cover, a debt market that merely has `is_flashloanable = true` (independent of `is_borrowable`), and a self-deployed Wasm receiver that pushes declared collateral to the controller. No privileged role, leaked key, or external cooperation is needed — only an existing listing where governance has set `can_borrow=false`, `paused`, or `frozen`, which is a routine operational state (e.g., low-decimal assets listed collateral-only, or a freeze during an incident).

### Recommendation
In `process_flash_position`, gate the debt leg with the same checks as `borrow`: call `require_can_borrow(env, &mut cache, account.spoke_id, debt)` (or equivalently `require_listed_unhalted_config` plus the `is_borrowable` assertion) before `mint_and_forward`. Audit `multiply`, `swap_debt`, `repay_debt_with_collateral`, and `migrate_from_blend` for the same gap — any path reaching `borrow_into_controller` should enforce the debt leg's entry flags at the spoke level.

### Proof of Concept
1. Admin lists asset `D` on spoke `S` with `can_borrow=false` (or a guardian pauses/freezes the listing after it was borrowable); `D`'s pool market keeps `is_flashloanable=true`.
2. Attacker deploys a Wasm receiver implementing `execute_flash_position` that, on callback, transfers declared collateral `C` to the controller.
3. Attacker supplies `C` to an account (or declares callback-delivered `C` in `collaterals`) and calls `flash_position(caller, 0, S, Multiply, debt=(hub,D), amount, receiver, data, collaterals=[(C, min)], refund_assets=[D])`.
4. The call passes `require_hub_active` and the `is_flashloanable` check, skips `require_can_borrow`/`enforce_spoke_asset_flags` on `D`, mints debt and forwards `amount` of `D` tokens to the receiver, accepts the pushed collateral, and passes final HF/LTV gates — leaving the attacker holding `D` and the account holding debt in a market where `borrow` reverts with `AssetNotBorrowable`/`SpokeAssetPaused`.

Caveat: this conclusion rests on the absence of a debt-leg listing check in `process_flash_position` and the assumption that `borrow_into_controller` does not internally enforce `is_borrowable`; if that helper does enforce it, this analog does not hold.
