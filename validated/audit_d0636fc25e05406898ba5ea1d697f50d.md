### Title
Dust-insolvent debt remains counted as backing, letting suppliers exit before `clean_bad_debt` and shift the write-down to depositors - (File: contracts/pool/src/guards.rs)

### Summary
A market with pending socializable bad debt still counts that debt at face value when deciding whether new supply is backed. [1](#0-0)  An informed supplier can withdraw at the pre-write-down supply index, invoke permissionless `clean_bad_debt`, and force the remaining suppliers to absorb the entire loss through a lower supply index. [2](#0-1) 

### Finding Description
`controller::supply` accepts a deposit when `backing_shortfall == 0`. [3](#0-2)  The backing calculation adds the full ceiled value of outstanding debt to cash, without checking whether that debt belongs to an account eligible for bad-debt cleanup. [1](#0-0) 

A supplier can then call `controller::withdraw` with amount `0`, which burns its shares and pays its full pre-cleanup balance subject only to liquidity, utilization, and nonzero-supply checks. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

After the informed supplier exits, any address can call `controller::clean_bad_debt(caller, bad_debt_account_id)` once debt exceeds collateral and collateral is at or below the dust threshold. [6](#0-5)  Cleanup sends the account’s debt positions to `pool::seize_positions`; the borrow-side branch converts the debt to `bad_debt`, reduces the market supply index, and burns the debt shares. [7](#0-6) 

### Impact Explanation
This permanently transfers the bad-debt loss from suppliers who exit before cleanup to suppliers who remain or enter while the debt is still counted as collectible. [8](#0-7) 

For example, an attacker supplies 50 ETH, a borrower draws 30 ETH, and collateral impairment makes that account eligible for `clean_bad_debt`. A victim supplies another 50 ETH because `cash + debt = 70 + 30` still equals the 100 ETH supplier claim. The attacker withdraws 50 ETH, leaving 20 ETH cash and 30 ETH debt against the victim’s supply; cleanup then reduces the supply index by 60%, leaving the victim with only a 20 ETH claim. The attacker can immediately redeposit at the reduced index and retains its full 50 ETH principal while the victim absorbed the entire 30 ETH loss.

### Likelihood Explanation
The path requires an account that has become eligible for permissionless cleanup and enough cash/utilization headroom for the attacker’s withdrawal. [9](#0-8)  Those conditions are realistic after abrupt collateral-price movements, and the attacker needs only ordinary `withdraw`, `clean_bad_debt`, and `supply` calls. [10](#0-9) [11](#0-10) 

### Recommendation
Track pending eligible bad debt at market level and exclude its unrecoverable portion from `backing_shortfall` before admitting new supply. Alternatively, block ordinary withdrawals from the affected market once a pending bad-debt write-down is identified, or virtualize the write-down during withdrawal so exiting suppliers cannot avoid their pro-rata share.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker supplies 50 ETH to the `(hub_id, ETH)` market through `controller::supply(attacker, 0, spoke_id, [(eth_key, 50 ETH)])`.
2. A borrower supplies collateral and borrows 30 ETH; the collateral price later falls so the account has `debt > collateral` and collateral no greater than the cleanup dust threshold.
3. A victim supplies 50 ETH. The deposit succeeds because `backing_shortfall` still values the 30 ETH bad debt as collectible backing. [1](#0-0) 
4. Attacker calls `controller::withdraw(attacker, attacker_account_id, [(eth_key, 0)], None)` and receives its full pre-cleanup supply balance. [4](#0-3) 
5. Attacker calls `controller::clean_bad_debt(attacker, borrower_account_id)`, which reaches `seize_positions` and reduces the ETH supply index. [12](#0-11) [13](#0-12) 
6. Attacker supplies 50 ETH again. The victim’s claim has been written down to approximately 20 ETH, while the attacker exits the sequence with its full principal and a fresh position that bears none of the prior bad debt.

/// Asset units by which supplier claims exceed cash + debt (0 if solvent).
pub(crate) fn backing_shortfall(cache: &Cache) -> i128 {
    let supplied_claim = cache.unscale_supply_floor(cache.supplied());
    let outstanding_debt = cache.unscale_borrow_ceil(cache.borrowed());
    let backing = cache.cash().saturating_add(outstanding_debt);
    supplied_claim.saturating_sub(backing).max(0)
```

pub(crate) fn apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index(cache: &mut Cache, bad_debt: Ray) {
    let total_supplied_value = cache.supplied().mul(cache.env(), cache.supply_index());

    if total_supplied_value == Ray::ZERO {
        return;
    }

    let capped = bad_debt.min(total_supplied_value);
    let remaining = total_supplied_value.checked_sub(cache.env(), capped);

    let reduction_factor = remaining.div_floor(cache.env(), total_supplied_value);
    let new_supply_index = cache
        .supply_index()
        .mul_floor(cache.env(), reduction_factor);

    cache.set_supply_index(new_supply_index.max(Ray::from(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)));
```

fn gate_and_debit(env: &Env, cache: &mut Cache, net_transfer: i128, skip_utilization_check: bool) {

#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq)]
enum BadDebtGate {
    /// Permissionless: insolvent *and* collateral at or below the dust threshold.
    DustCapped,
    /// Owner-only: insolvent alone, with no cap on the collateral left behind.
    InsolventOnly,

/// Requires open debt and the selected insolvency gate, then cleans up the account.
fn socialize_bad_debt(env: &Env, account_id: u64, gate: BadDebtGate) {
    let mut cache = Context::new(env);
    let account = storage::get_account(env, account_id);

    assert_with_error!(
        env,
        !account.borrow_positions.is_empty(),
        CollateralError::DebtPositionNotFound
    );

    let totals = risk::calculate_account_risk_totals(
        env,
        &mut cache,
        &account.supply_positions,
        &account.borrow_positions,
    );

    let admits = match gate {
        BadDebtGate::DustCapped => {
            is_socializable_bad_debt(totals.total_debt, totals.total_collateral)
        }
        BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly => totals.total_debt > totals.total_collateral,
    };
    assert_with_error!(env, admits, CollateralError::CannotCleanBadDebt);

    bad_debt::execute_bad_debt_cleanup(env, &mut cache, account_id, &account, &totals);
