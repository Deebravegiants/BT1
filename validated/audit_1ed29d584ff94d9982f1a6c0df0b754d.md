Raydium's `Deposit`/`Withdraw` instructions mint/burn LP tokens strictly proportional to the actual coin/pc tokens transferred into the AMM's own vaults, computed via `InvariantPool::exchange_token_to_pool` against `amm.lp_amount` and the vault balances themselves [1](#0-0) . There is no external "custom token" or synth creation mechanism, no member "weight" field derived from an attacker-manipulable price oracle, and no separate `harvest`/reward-distribution instruction that pays out pro-rata based on a decoupled weight tracker [2](#0-1) . Since LP minting is tied directly to the tokens actually deposited into the pool's own vaults (not a self-created arbitrary asset priced via an attacker-controlled sub-pool), the Vader vault-reward-gaming bug class has no reachable analog in this AMM's deposit/withdraw/swap instruction set.
