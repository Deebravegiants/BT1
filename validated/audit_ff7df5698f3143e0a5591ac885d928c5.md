No Vulnerability found for this question.

Analysis: The reported bug requires a router (`MarginRouter`) that (1) queries an *external, attacker-controllable* "pair" contract for a swap quote, (2) transfers funds to that untrusted contract, and only *afterwards* records a `startingBalance`/`endingBalance` pair to validate the trade, and (3) never rejects `fromToken == toToken`. This ordering flaw lets a fake pair contract absorb the funds while still passing the balance check.

Raydium's on-chain swap instructions (`process_swap_base_in`, `process_swap_base_out`, and their `_v2` variants) have a structurally different design that closes off this exact path:

- The AMM's vaults are not attacker-suppliable "fake pairs" — they are validated against the on-chain `AmmInfo` struct via `check_assert_eq!(*amm_coin_vault_info.key, amm.coin_vault, ...)` and `check_assert_eq!(*amm_pc_vault_info.key, amm.pc_vault, ...)`, i.e., against the program's own state rather than untrusted external code. [1](#0-0) 

- Both vault mints are explicitly disallowed from being equal, which is the direct analog of the recommended `fromToken != toToken` check, and it is enforced immediately after loading the AMM state, before any transfer happens: `if amm.pc_vault_mint == amm.coin_vault_mint { return Err(AmmError::NotAllowed.into()); }`. [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

- `swap_direction` is derived strictly from comparing `user_source.mint`/`user_destination.mint` against the real vault mints (`amm_coin_vault.mint`/`amm_pc_vault.mint`); any other combination is rejected with `InvalidUserToken`, so there is no way to construct a cyclical/self-referential trade. [6](#0-5) 

- The swap output amount is computed directly from the real, on-chain vault balances (`Calculator::swap_token_amount_base_in`/`swap_token_amount_base_out`) rather than from a quote fetched from an attacker-controlled external contract, and the SPL token transfers are issued via `Invokers::token_transfer`/`token_transfer_with_authority` directly to/from the validated vault accounts — there is no separate "startingBalance"-before/"endingBalance"-after check on an attacker-chosen destination that could be bypassed by fund withdrawal happening first. [7](#0-6) 

Because the vault accounts are validated against the immutable on-chain `AmmInfo` state (not an attacker-suppliable "pair"), the same-mint case is explicitly rejected, and the transfer amount is computed from real vault balances rather than an externally-supplied quote, the specific root cause of H-02 (withdraw-then-check ordering combined with a missing `fromToken != toToken` guard against a fake pair) has no reachable analog in the in-scope swap/deposit/withdraw instructions of this program.
