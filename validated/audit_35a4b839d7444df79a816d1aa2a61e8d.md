The reported bug class is L2-sequencer-downtime handling for a Chainlink price oracle that drives liquidations of borrowers in a lending protocol (JUSD/JOJO). alt.fun has no Chainlink oracle, no L2 sequencer dependency, and no borrowing/liquidation mechanism at all — its only "price" reads are `exchangeRate()` / `baseToLtAmount()` / `ltToBaseAmount()` calls directly against the BounceTech LT contract [1](#0-0) , used only for curve pricing and the dual graduation trigger [2](#0-1) . There is no liquidation path, no borrower/collateral concept, and no Chainlink-style oracle feed that could stall during sequencer downtime and cause a batch of unfair liquidations. Consequently there is no reachable analog in `Zap`, `Bonding`, `Router`, `Pair`, or `FeeVault` that reproduces theft or fund-freezing from this bug class.
