### Title
Borrowing the maximum LTV amount reverts due to double ceiling of debt (mint ceil + risk valuation ceil) - ([File: common/src/rates/scaling.rs](common/src/rates/scaling.rs))

### Summary
Analogous to the JUSD `t0BorrowBalance` round-up bug: XOXNO mints debt shares with ceiling rounding at the borrow index (`calculate_scaled_borrow` → `div_ceil`, `common/src/rates/scaling.rs:54-56`), then the post-borrow solvency gate re-values those shares with **ceil** rounding again (`position_value_ceil` in `sum_debt_usd_loaded`, `contracts/controller/src/risk/totals.rs:201`) and compares against LTV-weighted collateral that is **floored** (`position_value_floor` + `apply_to_wad_floor`, `contracts/controller/src/risk/totals.rs:181-197`). A borrower who draws exactly the max amount implied by `ltv_collateral` (e.g. via the `get_ltv_collateral_usd` view, `skills/xoxno-lending/math.md:264,286`) reverts at `require_post_pool_risk_gates` (`contracts/controller/src/risk/validation.rs:41-45`).

### Finding Description
In `pool::ops::borrow::mint_debt`, the minted shares are `ceil(amount_ray * RAY / borrow_index)` (`contracts/pool/src/ops/borrow.rs:68`). Unscaling those shares back with `position_value_ceil` gives `ceil(ceil(A/B)*B) > A` whenever `A` is not an exact multiple — i.e. almost always. The gate then asserts `totals.ltv_collateral >= totals.total_debt` in WAD. Because collateral is floored and debt is double-ceiled, a borrow whose true USD value equals the LTV limit fails the check by up to ~1 base unit plus the per-step ceil residuals, identical in structure to `roundUp(maxBorrow/tRate)*tRate <= maxBorrow` being false in JUSD. An unprivileged user hits this via `Controller::borrow(account_id, borrows=[(hub_asset, max_amount)])` → `process_borrow` → `settle_borrow` → `enforce_post_pool_solvency` (`contracts/controller/src/positions/debt.rs:33-66`).

### Impact Explanation
A user who sizes a borrow from the advertised borrow capacity (`ltv_collateral − total_debt`) will have the transaction revert with `InsufficientCollateral` in the generic (non-divisible) case. No funds are lost, but the full LTV utilization is unreachable at the boundary; the account must retry with a smaller amount. Temporary unavailability of the documented max-borrow path — Medium at most.

### Likelihood Explanation
Triggers whenever `amount_ray * RAY mod borrow_index != 0` or the subsequent ceil valuation steps leave a residual — i.e., for almost every max-sized borrow, same frequency argument as the JUSD report.

### Recommendation
Have the risk gate value newly minted debt consistently with what was disbursed (e.g. compare against `floor`-unscaled debt, or subtract the ≤1-share ceil residual at the mint boundary), or cap the view's borrowable figure by the worst-case double-ceil overhead so a reported max always succeeds.

### Proof of Concept
1. Supply collateral worth `V` USD at LTV `l`; `ltv_collateral = floor(V·l)` (floored at all three steps).
2. Call `borrow` for amount `A` such that `A·price == ltv_collateral`.
3. Pool mints `ceil(A·10^(27−d)·RAY / B)` shares — one ray-share above the exact quotient.
4. Risk totals compute `total_debt = position_value_ceil(shares, B, price) > ltv_collateral` by 1+ wei.
5. `require_post_pool_risk_gates` reverts with `CollateralError::InsufficientCollateral` (`validation.rs:43`).
