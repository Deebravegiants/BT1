**Analysis:**

The premise is incorrect. `record`, `commit`, and all readers (`for_each_resource_group_key_and_tags`, `modified_resource_keys`, `for_each_resource_key_no_aggregator_v1`, `materialize`, `publish_module_write_set`) all acquire the *same* per-transaction `Mutex<OutputWrapper<T, O>>` guard before touching either `output_status_kind` or `output` <cite repo="Alyssadaypin/aptos-core--037" path="aptos-move/block-executor/src/txn_last_input_output.rs" start="200="207" end="200" /> [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) .

`record` writes the entire `OutputWrapper` in a single atomic assignment while holding the lock: `*self.output_wrappers[txn_idx as usize].lock() = OutputWrapper::from_execution_status(...)` [3](#0-2) . Since `status_kind` and `output` are fields of one struct replaced together under one lock acquisition, there is no intermediate state where a reader taking the same lock could observe a mismatched pair (e.g., `Abort` status with a `Some` output, or vice versa). The `with_success_or_skip_rest!` macro's `unreachable!()` arms exist purely as a defensive invariant check for logic bugs, not because such states are actually reachable via concurrent access — they can't occur precisely because the Mutex serializes all readers/writers against the same combined state [4](#0-3) .

`commit` also holds the same lock for the duration of its transition logic (including flipping `Success` to `SkipRest`), so it cannot interleave with a concurrent reader mid-update [5](#0-4) . There is therefore no window in which `output` is readable while `output_status_kind` is `Abort`/`SpeculativeExecutionAbortError`.

Separately, this is a block-executor internal consistency question about MVHashMap write-set bookkeeping, not an unprivileged-input custody boundary affecting ownership, minting, burning, freezing, or recovery of assets, so it falls outside the required custody impact scope even if the race were real.
