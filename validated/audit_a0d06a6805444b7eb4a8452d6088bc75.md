### Title
Third-party dust supply pushes an insolvent account's collateral above `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD`, blocking permissionless `clean_bad_debt` and silent post-liquidation socialization - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/curve.rs)

### Summary
`clean_bad_debt` only socializes an insolvent account when `total_collateral <= BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` ($5 WAD). `process_supply` explicitly lets any third party top up an **existing** supply position on someone else's account. An unprivileged griefer can therefore add a dust amount of collateral to a nearly-empty insolvent account, pushing its collateral just over $5 so that permissionless cleanup reverts `CannotCleanBadDebt` and the in-liquidation cleanup silently skips — the exact blocking class reported against `JOJOBank::handleBadDept`.

### Finding Description
The permissionless bad-debt gate is `is_socializable_bad_debt`, which requires `total_debt > total_collateral && total_collateral <= Wad::from(BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD)` ($5). [1](#0-0) 

Both callers enforce it:

- `socialize_bad_debt` (via `process_clean_bad_debt`, permissionless, `caller.require_auth()` only) asserts the gate and reverts with `CannotCleanBadDebt` when collateral exceeds the cap. [2](#0-1) 
- `check_bad_debt_after_liquidation` applies the same gate but **skips silently** instead of reverting, mirroring JOJO's silent failure. [3](#0-2) 

The blocking primitive is `process_supply`: `require_third_party_existing_supply` only rejects a non-owner when the supplied `hub_asset` is *not already* in `account.supply_positions`. [4](#0-3)  So for any insolvent account that still holds a nonzero supply position valued at or under $5, an attacker calls `supply(attacker, victim_account_id, spoke_id, [(existing_hub_asset, dust)])` with enough dust to push `total_collateral` to `$5 + ε`. The gate then closes until a liquidation or price move re-opens it. [5](#0-4) 

There is no minimum supply amount — `aggregate_positive_payments` only requires positive legs — so the cost of blocking is bounded by `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD - residual_collateral`, i.e. at most ~$5 per re-block. Liquidators can in principle seize the added collateral, but at dust scale liquidation can be unprofitable, and in the documented narrow band where the curve quote backs less than one whole unit every liquidation offer reverts with `InvalidPayments`, leaving the block stuck until accrual or a price move ends it. [6](#0-5) 

### Impact Explanation
Bad debt left unsocialized keeps accruing debt-share interest against markets while the account exists, increasing the eventual write-down borne by suppliers via the supply-index drop in `execute_bad_debt_cleanup`. The attacker can repeatedly re-block cheaply: each time a liquidation or price move drags collateral back under $5, another dust supply re-closes the gate. Cleanup then depends on the owner-only `force_socialize_bad_debt` governance path (Sensitive delay tier), converting a permissionless accounting operation into a governance-dependent one — a degradation of protocol solvency handling reachable by any address for single-digit-dollar cost per cycle. [7](#0-6) 

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires an insolvent account that retains a nonzero supply position at or below $5 — the normal residual left after a liquidation that cannot fully drain the account (whole-unit legs, under-delivery, or partial fills leave exactly this state). The attack is a single `supply` call with no special permissions and costs at most ~$5 of collateral, which is only recoverable if a liquidator finds the dust seizure profitable; at that scale and inside the `InvalidPayments` band it frequently will not be. The post-liquidation path failing silently makes the block easy to miss operationally.

### Recommendation
Enforce a minimum USD value for third-party supply top-ups to existing positions (or a minimum *new* supply position size), so pushing collateral above `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` costs strictly more than the dust gate — preferably more than the guaranteed liquidation bonus on the added amount, making the block self-defeating. Alternatively, make the gate compare collateral excluding dust positions valued below a floor, or let `clean_bad_debt` admit accounts where each remaining supply position is itself below the dust threshold even if the aggregate slightly exceeds $5.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice supplies 8 USDC and borrows 0.002 ETH (fixture as in `test_keeper_clean_bad_debt_decreases_supply_index`). `set_price("USDC", usd_cents(5))` makes her collateral ≈ $0.40, debt ≈ $4 → `is_socializable_bad_debt` holds. [8](#0-7) 
2. Attacker calls `supply(attacker, alice_account_id, alice_spoke_id, [(hub_asset(USDC), 5 USDC)])`. `require_third_party_existing_supply` passes because the USDC position already exists; `total_collateral` becomes ≈ $5.40.
3. Any keeper calling `clean_bad_debt(alice_account_id)` now reverts `CannotCleanBadDebt` (#114), and any `liquidate` that leaves the residual applies `check_bad_debt_after_liquidation`, which silently skips socialization. [9](#0-8) 
4. Whenever liquidation or price drift pushes collateral back to ≤ $5, repeat step 2. The account's debt keeps accruing for all suppliers until governance runs `force_socialize_bad_debt` — the owner-only path documented for exactly this collateral-above-dust case. [10](#0-9)

# Force-socialize bad debt

This runbook covers governance-authorized removal of an insolvent account.
Use it when collateral exceeds the fixed $5 dust limit of permissionless
`clean_bad_debt`. Cleanup can also proceed when `no_seize` blocks ordinary
liquidation.

The account must hold debt, with ceil risk debt strictly greater than half-up
unweighted collateral (`D > C`). An unhealthy health factor alone does not
qualify. The forced path has no collateral dust cap.

**Cleanup is irreversible.** All remaining collateral becomes protocol revenue.
All remaining debt is written off against its own markets' supply indexes,
including when collateral and debt share a market. There is no automatic netting
or insurance payment. The account is deleted and its position NFT burns.
