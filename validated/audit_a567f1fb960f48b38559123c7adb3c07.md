### Title
Spot-reserve donation inflates single-source Aquarius LP oracle, enabling over-borrow against inflated LP collateral - (File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs)

### Summary
The price-aggregator derives the fair value of every Aquarius LP-collateral market from a single source: the pool's *instantaneous* reserves read via `aquarius_pool_reserves_call`, combined as `2 * sqrt(value_a * value_b) / total_shares`. An unprivileged address can inflate the reported LP price by directly donating either underlying token to the pool — reserves rise while `total_shares` stays constant — then borrow against the inflated LP collateral on the controller, leaving bad debt. The only backstop is the sanity band, which for deployed LP oracles is deliberately wide (e.g. mainnet XLMAQUA_LP spans a 10x range).

### Finding Description
`providers/aquarius.rs::read` resolves leg prices through `resolve_nested` but reads `(reserve_a, reserve_b)` and `total_shares` directly from the live pool contract on every call — no smoothing, no TWAP, no second source (LP oracles are sole-source by construction: `has_lp && sources.len() != 1` panics, and tolerance/smoothing checks are waived for them). [1](#0-0) 

`fair_lp_price_wad` computes `2 * sqrt(reserve_a*price_a * reserve_b*price_b) / share_supply`. Swaps preserve the reserve product `k`, but a raw token `transfer` to the pool address raises `reserve_a` (Aquarius reserves reflect actual balances) with no shares minted, scaling the LP price by `sqrt(1 + d/reserve_a)`. Repeated or large donations push the price arbitrarily; it only reverts once it crosses `max_sanity_price_wad`. [2](#0-1) 

Deployed bands leave real headroom: XLMAQUA_LP allows `5_356_670_329_052_813`–`53_566_703_290_528_136` (10x) and XLMUSDC_LP `0.45`–`2.5` WAD (~5.5x), all passing `validate_lp_sanity_band` (`MAX_LP_SANITY_BAND_BPS`). [3](#0-2) 

The controller consumes this price synchronously: `Context` caches strict prices when the position's threshold/LTV is evaluated for `borrow`, `multiply`, `flash_position`, or `withdraw`. The attacker transfers tokens to the Aquarius pool, calls `borrow` against supplied LP shares at the inflated price, then withdraws/reverses — the donated capital is partially recoverable by redeeming LP value, while the protocol is left with under-collateralized debt cleaned only via `clean_bad_debt`/`recapitalize`, i.e. a supply-index write-down on all suppliers. [4](#0-3) 

### Impact Explanation
Theft of user funds / protocol insolvency: borrowed assets are real and exfiltrated, while the LP collateral is worth less than the inflated price used to justify the borrow. When liquidation cannot cover the debt, the bad-debt path writes down the supply index, socializing the loss across all suppliers of the borrowed asset.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. Requires capital for the donation equal to roughly `(inflation² − 1) × reserve_leg_value` — e.g. a 2x price inflation needs a donation ≈ 3x the pool's token-A reserve value — and the pool must pass `min_pool_value_wad` while staying inside the sanity band. For the wide-band LP markets (5–10x allowed range), modest pools make profitable inflation feasible; the attacker must also own LP shares to post as collateral, which is normal market participation. Profitability is bounded by borrowable liquidity in the paired hub market.

### Recommendation
Do not trust instantaneous reserves for single-source LP pricing. Options: (a) require a second independent source for LP oracles instead of exempting them from tolerance checks; (b) tighten `MAX_LP_SANITY_BAND_BPS` so the band admits at most the honest drift of the LP price; (c) reconstruct reserves from cached/historical reserve data or use a TWAP over pool reserves; (d) raise `min_pool_value_wad` floors so the donation capital needed to reach the band edge exceeds borrowable depth.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker acquires `XLMAQUA_LP` shares and supplies them via controller `supply` on the LP spoke.
2. In the same ledger window, attacker calls `token_a.transfer(attacker → aquarius_pool, d)` with `d` chosen so `sqrt(1 + d/reserve_a)` yields the target inflation factor `f` (band edge allows `f²` up to the band width).
3. Attacker calls `borrow` (or `multiply`) against the LP collateral; `Context` resolves `price_wad` through `aquarius::read`, which reads the inflated `reserve_a` from `aquarius_pool_reserves_call` and returns `f × fair_price` (still inside `[min_sanity_price_wad, max_sanity_price_wad]`).
4. Attacker keeps the borrowed assets; after price normalization the position is under-water and `clean_bad_debt` writes the loss into the supply index of the borrowed market.
