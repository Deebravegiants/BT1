### Title
Unbounded `i128` accumulation in market totals lets a whale-scale depositor push a market past the RAY value ceiling, permanently freezing all withdrawals, repayments, and liquidations - (File: common/src/rates/simulate.rs)

### Summary
The audit hint is an unchecked arithmetic overflow reachable by a single unprivileged caller that permanently DoSes a shared accounting path. XOXNO Lending's analog is the RAY-domain value ceiling in `accrue_step`: every market mutation first runs `global_sync` → `accrue_step`, which computes `scaled_to_original(borrowed, borrow_index)` and `scaled_to_original(supplied, supply_index)` with `mul`/`mul_floor` that panic on `i128` overflow. Once `borrowed * borrow_index` exceeds `i128::MAX`, every subsequent verb on that `(hub, token)` market reverts — supply, borrow, withdraw, repay, liquidate, `clean_bad_debt`, `update_indexes` — because accrual runs before any state change. There is no recovery path: repaying or withdrawing also accrues first, and bad-debt write-down cannot execute either.

### Finding Description
`accrue_step` at `common/src/rates/simulate.rs:60-62` unscales market totals with panicking multiplies:

```rust
let borrowed_original = scaled_to_original(env, borrowed, borrow_index);
let supplied_original = scaled_to_original(env, supplied, supply_index);
```

`scaled_to_original` is `scaled.mul(env, index)` (`common/src/rates/scaling.rs:14-16`), which panics with `GenericError::MathOverflow` when the RAY product exceeds `i128::MAX` (`common/src/math/fp_core.rs:145-159`). `global_sync` calls this before every mutating op (`contracts/pool/src/interest.rs:20-53`), and the admission bound only limits the *token-unit* input (`i128::MAX / 10^(27-d)`, ~170B whole tokens at 18 decimals — `docs/reference/formulas.md:425-437`); it does not bound the product `supplied × supply_index` or `borrowed × borrow_index`. The borrow index is capped at `10^36` (~8.5×), but the value ceiling is hit before the index cap engages — confirmed by the harness test `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` (`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:316-362`), which shows `MATH_OVERFLOW` on `update_indexes`, `withdraw`, and `repay` while `borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`.

Like the OpenQ finding, the trigger is an unprivileged user-controlled magnitude (`amount` on `supply`/`borrow`, or amplified via `multiply`/`flash_position`) rather than a privileged setter: a single address can supply near the admitted cap and borrow against its own collateral to place `borrowed × borrow_index` one accrual step away from `i128::MAX`. Any later `update_indexes` — callable by anyone — then permanently bricks the market for all users.

### Impact Explanation
Permanent freezing of all funds in the affected `(hub, token)` market: suppliers cannot withdraw, borrowers cannot repay, liquidators cannot liquidate, and `clean_bad_debt`/`recapitalize` cannot recover because accrual panics first. Equivalent severity to the reference report's blocked `refundDeposit`.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium-low: it requires a whale-scale position (tens-to-hundreds of billions of whole tokens, or caps lifted to the admitted maximum) plus either sustained high utilization or a borrow index already elevated from prior accrual. However it needs no privilege, no timing, and no cooperation — only funds and time — and once reached it is irreversible, with no index-cap safeguard (the cap on the index does not cap the `scaled × index` product).

### Recommendation
Saturate rather than panic in `scaled_to_original` when used for market-level utilization/accrual, or clamp `supplied`/`borrowed` growth so `scaled × index` provably stays under `i128::MAX` (e.g., enforce `borrowed ≤ i128::MAX / MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` at borrow time and analogous supply bound, or emit a hard supply/borrow ceiling derived from the index ceiling). Alternatively, add an index-growth guard that stops accrual (freezing rates, not the market) before the value product overflows, so repayment and withdrawal remain possible.

### Proof of Concept
The in-repo witness is `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:321-361`: it supplies `BILLION * 10^18` units of an 18-decimal asset, borrows 98% of it against collateral, advances time, and observes `update_indexes`, `withdraw`, and `repay` all revert with `MATH_OVERFLOW` before `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` is reached — the same one-user-triggered, all-users-blocked shape as the OpenQ `type(uint256).max` expiration attack.
