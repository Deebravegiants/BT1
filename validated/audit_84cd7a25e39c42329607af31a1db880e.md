### Title
`socialize-debt` double-counts the write-down by subtracting `principal-reduction` from cash `assets` in addition to shrinking `lindex` by `debt-reduction`, causing real backing to fall below what `lindex` implies - ([File: mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar])

### Summary
`socialize-debt` computes two *different* ratios for the same bad-debt event: `debt-reduction` (idx-based, used to rescale `lindex`) and `principal-reduction` (raw `borrowed`/`scaled-principal`-based, used to reduce the cash reserve `assets`). Because these ratios are not equal, the function removes `principal-reduction` STX of real cash *on top of* the debt write-off that `lindex` already accounts for, so total backing shrinks by more than what depositors' share price (`lindex`) reflects — an unaccounted, permanent shortfall (protocol insolvency).

### Finding Description
The identity that must hold is:

`old-total-assets - new-total-assets == debt-reduction`, where `new-total-assets` is exactly what `new-lindex` implies (`new-lindex/current-lindex * old-total-assets`).

`total-assets()` is composed of the cash reserve `assets` plus the outstanding debt valued via the compounding index `idx` (`total-debt = scaled-principal * idx / INDEX-PRECISION`), as evidenced by how `assets` is only ever incremented by the *interest* portion on repay, never by principal: [1](#0-0) 

and how `system-borrow` sends real STX out via `send-underlying` and increases `total-borrowed`, without ever touching `assets`: [2](#0-1) 

This confirms `assets` is the cash reserve that is *disjoint* from outstanding debt, and `total-debt()` (via `scaled-principal`/`idx`) is the only representation of principal owed by borrowers.

In `socialize-debt`: [3](#0-2) 

- `debt-reduction = scaled-amount * idx / INDEX-PRECISION` is the current (interest-inclusive) value of the written-off debt.
- `new-lindex` is computed to shrink `lindex` so that `total-assets` after socialization equals `old-total-assets - debt-reduction` exactly (lines 956-958).
- `scaled-principal` is reduced by `scaled-amount`, which — by construction of `total-debt = scaled-principal * idx / INDEX-PRECISION` — reduces `total-debt` by exactly `debt-reduction`. This alone already makes real `total-assets()` drop by exactly `debt-reduction`, matching what `new-lindex` assumes.
- However, the function *also* subtracts `principal-reduction` from the cash reserve `assets` (line 966). `principal-reduction = scaled-amount * borrowed / scaled-principal` uses the ratio of raw `total-borrowed` to `scaled-principal`, which tracks the *weighted-average entry index* at which loans were taken out, not the current `idx`. Since `idx` only grows (monotonic interest accrual), this ratio is generically different from (and typically less than) `idx / INDEX-PRECISION`, so `principal-reduction ≠ 0` even though the corresponding drop in `total-debt()` was already fully captured by `debt-reduction`.

The net effect: actual `new-total-assets = old-total-assets - debt-reduction - principal-reduction`, i.e. an extra, unjustified `principal-reduction` STX is removed from the pool's real backing that `new-lindex` never compensates for. Depositors' claims (via `lindex`) are now collectively worth more STX than the vault actually holds/is owed — a permanent, unbacked shortfall. `check-caller-auth` only gates *who* can call `socialize-debt` (the authorized market contract); it does not validate that the two write-down ratios agree, so the guard does not prevent this divergence once a legitimate bad-debt/liquidation event triggers the call.

### Impact Explanation
Every call to `socialize-debt` permanently removes `principal-reduction` extra STX of cash backing beyond what `lindex` accounts for. This is directly protocol insolvency: `sum-of-all-lender-claims-at-new-lindex > total-assets()` after the call, by exactly `principal-reduction`. The loss is borne collectively by all depositors (whoever withdraws last cannot redeem their full `lindex`-implied claim), and is repeatable on every bad-debt/default event the market processes — the divergence compounds with each socialization. This matches the Critical category "protocol insolvency."

### Likelihood Explanation
The trigger precondition (a legitimately liquidatable position defaulting, causing the market to call `socialize-debt`) is explicitly accepted as in-scope by the prompt. No special privilege is needed by the attacker beyond creating and defaulting on a position; the divergence occurs deterministically whenever `borrowed/scaled-principal != idx/INDEX-PRECISION`, which is the normal case once any interest has accrued since the position(s) were opened (i.e., essentially always, except immediately after the very first borrow with no accrual). This makes the bug highly likely to manifest on any real bad-debt event, not a contrived edge case.

### Recommendation
Do not subtract `principal-reduction` from `assets` in `socialize-debt`. The write-off of bad debt should only reduce `scaled-principal` (and consequently `total-debt()`/`total-assets()`), which is already fully reflected by `debt-reduction` and the `new-lindex` calculation. If `total-borrowed` bookkeeping must be kept consistent with `scaled-principal`, reduce `total-borrowed` using the same `idx`-based ratio as `debt-reduction` (or derive it consistently from `debt-reduction`), and remove the erroneous `assets` decrement entirely, so that `old-total-assets - new-total-assets == debt-reduction` holds exactly.

### Proof of Concept
Clarinet/vitest simnet plan:
1. Deploy vault + market; have a lender deposit `D` STX, recording `total-supply` and `convert-to-assets-preview(total-supply)` (should equal `D`).
2. Have a borrower borrow `B` STX via `system-borrow`, then let several blocks pass so `idx` accrues interest (so `idx/INDEX-PRECISION > borrowed/scaled-principal` at time of write-off — e.g. also perform one `system-repay` of a middle borrower to further desynchronize `total-borrowed` vs `scaled-principal` from `idx`).
3. Trigger a default and have the market call `socialize-debt(scaled-amount)` for the full unrecoverable scaled principal of the defaulted position.
4. Read `old-total-assets` (captured before the call), `debt-reduction`, `principal-reduction` from the emitted print event.
5. Assert: `principal-reduction != 0` (i.e., not equal to the amount already reflected via `debt-reduction`'s effect on `total-debt()`).
6. Assert: `total-assets()` after the call `== old-total-assets - debt-reduction - principal-reduction`, while `convert-to-assets-preview(total-supply)` after the call (computed from `new-lindex`) `== old-total-assets - debt-reduction` (i.e. only debt-reduction was subtracted from lender claims).
7. Confirm the numeric gap equals `principal-reduction`, demonstrating lender claims exceed real backing by that amount — the broken identity.

(define-public (socialize-debt (scaled-amount uint))
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (current-assets (var-get assets))
        (current-lindex (var-get lindex))
        (old-total-assets (total-assets))
        (debt-reduction (mul-div-down scaled-amount idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (principal-reduction (if (> scaled-principal u0)
                                (mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal)
                                u0))
        ;; Write down lindex proportionally to loss in total-assets
        (new-lindex (if (and (> old-total-assets u0) (> old-total-assets debt-reduction))
                       (mul-div-down current-lindex (- old-total-assets debt-reduction) old-total-assets)
                       u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (> scaled-amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (var-set lindex new-lindex)
    (var-set principal-scaled (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0))
    (var-set total-borrowed (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0))
    (var-set assets (if (> current-assets principal-reduction) (- current-assets principal-reduction) u0))
