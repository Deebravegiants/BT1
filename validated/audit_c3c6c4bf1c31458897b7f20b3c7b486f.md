### Title
Bad-debt supply-index floor clamp leaves stranded supplier shares that drain later deposits — ([File: contracts/pool/src/interest.rs](contracts/pool/src/interest.rs))

### Summary
Analogous to the Bribe `totalVoting` desync — where a balance was deflated while the aggregate accounting stayed inflated — `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` deflates the *value* of all supply shares on bad debt but clamps the index upward at `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW` instead of letting it reach the true post-loss value. The result is the same shape: the share book (`supplied`) is never reduced, but each share's claim is floored at a value larger than the backing justifies, leaving "phantom" claims that are redeemable against cash belonging to future depositors or recapitalizers.

### Finding Description
`apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index` (contracts/pool/src/interest.rs:73-89) socializes a loss by computing `reduction_factor = (total_supplied_value - bad_debt) / total_supplied_value` and setting `supply_index *= reduction_factor`, then applies `new_supply_index.max(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)`. When `bad_debt` wipes out most or all of the supplied value, the true index should fall toward zero, but the floor clamps it back up to `RAY/1000`. Share totals (`supplied`, per-position `scaled_amount`) are untouched — the accounting deflation is only partial while the claims remain whole.

The repository's own test proves the consequence (`contracts/pool/tests/interest.rs:372-427`): a supplier holding `1_000 * RAY` scaled shares, after a wipeout that clamps the index to the floor, still has `stranded > 0` claim; once a victim supplies `fresh_cash = stranded`, the wiped supplier's full withdrawal pays out `gross == fresh_cash` and `cash < fresh_claim` — the new depositor's funds are drained and the pool is insolvent.

Unprivileged path:
1. A market accrues bad debt; any caller invokes `liquidate`/`clean_bad_debt` on the controller, which calls `pool_seize_positions_call` and inside the pool runs `apply_bad_debt_to_supply_index`, clamping the index at `SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW`.
2. A victim later calls `supply` (or `recapitalize`, which credits cash without minting shares) on the controller.
3. The attacker — a wiped supplier whose `scaled_amount` was never reduced — calls `withdraw` (full close via `resolve_withdrawal`, which pays the floored claim), extracting the fresh cash.

Root cause support: the index is the only deflation mechanism; `burn_supply`/`supplied` are never written down, and the `max(SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW)` clamp fabricates claim value with no backing (contracts/pool/src/interest.rs:83-89). The stranded-claim drain is demonstrated end-to-end in `test_raw_cache_floor_clamp_strands_claim_without_supply_guard` (contracts/pool/tests/interest.rs:414-426). The design docs acknowledge residual claims "requiring recapitalization" (docs/reference/invariants.md, INV-ACCT-04), but nothing prevents the stranded holder from withdrawing *before* recapitalization or extracting *more* than the recapitalized shortfall.

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft of user funds and protocol insolvency: wiped suppliers retain a phantom claim worth `scaled_amount × SUPPLY_INDEX_FLOOR_RAW`, payable out of cash deposited by later suppliers or recapitalizers. First-come withdrawal ordering means the stranded claimant steals real tokens and leaves new depositors unbacked — exactly the "front-run the reset, then claim an inflated share while others can't" shape of the reference bug.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a near-total bad-debt event in a market (achievable via ordinary liquidation/clean_bad_debt during a price crash or on a thin market), then a subsequent supply or recapitalize. All steps use unprivileged entrypoints (`clean_bad_debt`, `supply`, `withdraw`). The precondition is a severe wipeout, so likelihood is conditional on a large loss event occurring; once it does, the theft is deterministic and confirmed by the in-repo test.

### Recommendation
Do not clamp the index above its true post-loss value without also neutralizing the excess claims. Options: write `supplied` (and position shares) down proportionally so `scaled_amount × floored_index` does not exceed remaining value; block withdrawals of stranded claims until `recapitalize` restores backing (extend `backing_shortfall` gating to withdrawals of the stranded portion); or track a per-market "stranded claim" escrow payable only from future revenue.

### Proof of Concept
Already encoded as `test_raw_cache_floor_clamp_strands_claim_without_supply_guard` in `contracts/pool/tests/interest.rs:372-427`: seed `supplied = 1000 RAY` scaled at index `RAY`, apply `bad_debt = 5000 RAY` → index clamps to `RAY/1000` leaving `stranded > 0`; a fresh deposit of `fresh_cash = stranded` is minted; the old position's full withdrawal pays `gross == fresh_cash` and leaves `cash < fresh_claim`.
