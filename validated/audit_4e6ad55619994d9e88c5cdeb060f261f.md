## Analog Analysis

The Splitter bug is a class of "administrative recompute mutates the share of a fixed/pending pool using a stale set of inputs while unclaimed amounts remain outstanding, disproportionately favoring some accounts over others." The strongest reachable analog in agave is in the **partitioned epoch rewards recalculation path**, specifically `Bank::calculate_alpenglow_points` in `runtime/src/inflation_rewards/points.rs` combined with `Bank::recalculate_stake_rewards`/`recalculate_partitioned_rewards_if_active` in `runtime/src/bank/partitioned_epoch_rewards/calculation.rs`.

### Title
Alpenglow point calculation uses live (post-boundary) stake as numerator against a frozen denominator during reward recalculation - ([File: runtime/src/inflation_rewards/points.rs])

### Summary
`calculate_alpenglow_points` computes each stake account's share of a vote account's already-fixed `pending_delegator_rewards` pool as `stake_amount / total_stake`, where `total_stake` (the denominator) is deliberately frozen to the original epoch-boundary value via `RewardEpochDelegatedStakes`, but `stake_amount` (the numerator) is read live from the current `StakesCache` at calculation/recalculation time [1](#0-0) . Since ordinary unprivileged stake operations (delegate, merge, split, redelegate) can change an account's current delegated stake before the recalculation runs, one staker's numerator can grow relative to the fixed denominator while the denominator that constrains everyone else's share stays the same.

### Finding Description
During epoch-boundary reward calculation, `total_active_stake`/`total_stake` denominators are explicitly pinned to a `RewardEpochDelegatedStakes` snapshot so that later intervening mutations do not change the total reward pie [2](#0-1) . The test suite even has an explicit regression test asserting the **denominator** must remain fixed across a `recalculate_stake_rewards` call performed after partial distribution (i.e., after a snapshot restore mid-distribution) [3](#0-2) .

However, the **numerator** side (`stake_amount`, from `delegation_effective_stake(&stake.delegation, ...)`) is taken from whatever `StakeAccount<Delegation>` is currently sitting in `StakesCache` at the time `calculate_alpenglow_points`/`calculate_block_reward` runs [4](#0-3) , and `runtime/src/bank/partitioned_epoch_rewards/calculation.rs:174-231` (`calculate_block_reward`) similarly draws `stake` from the live `delegation` while `total_active_stake` comes from the frozen `RewardEpochDelegatedStakes` map [2](#0-1) . `get_epoch_params_for_recalculation`, used by `recalculate_stake_rewards` (invoked when `recalculate_partitioned_rewards_if_active` runs, e.g. after a bank/snapshot restore mid-distribution), explicitly re-reads `stake_delegations` straight from the live `self.stakes_cache.stakes()` [5](#0-4) .

Because distribution of `pending_delegator_rewards`/inflation reward pools spans multiple blocks/partitions (an active `EpochRewardStatus::Distribution` phase with `unclaimed`/undistributed partitions, analogous to the Splitter's "unclaimed funds"), and ordinary user-triggered stake mutations (delegate more, `merge-stake`, `split-stake`, exposed via `cli/src/stake.rs` `process_merge_stake`/`process_split_stake` and the on-chain stake program) alter the live `StakesCache` between the original calculation and any later recalculation, an unprivileged staker who increases their own effective delegated stake before recalculation occurs receives a numerator computed against the *new*, larger stake while the pool's total (denominator) they compete against for the still-unpaid rewards remains the value computed at the epoch boundary. This lets that staker capture a disproportionate share of the fixed/pending reward pool at the expense of other stakers whose partitioned rewards have not yet been distributed — the same "dilution of unclaimed pending shares by a state-mutating recompute" pattern described in the Splitter report.

### Impact Explanation
This can cause one staker's calculated `PartitionedStakeReward` for the still-outstanding partitions to be larger than intended, at the expense of the fixed reward pool shared with other not-yet-paid stakers, producing an incorrect/inflated payout for one party and an implicit reduction for others whose partitioned rewards haven't been distributed yet. This is a value-transfer bug affecting bank commit / reward-distribution accounting, not merely a display issue, because the recalculated `stake_rewards` are subsequently stored to accounts via `store_stake_accounts_in_partition` / `distribute_epoch_rewards_in_partition`.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is limited by the specific trigger condition: recalculation only happens when `epoch_rewards_sysvar.active` is true, i.e., mid-distribution, and typically after a bank/snapshot restore during that narrow window (`recalculate_partitioned_rewards_if_active`). An attacker/staker would need to increase their own effective stake (via ordinary stake instructions available to any user) precisely within that active distribution window before the bank restarts/recalculates — a real but narrow window, and I could not fully verify from the available index whether normal (non-snapshot-restore) validator restart paths trigger this recalculation on every restart or only in rarer circumstances, since the call sites in `runtime/src/bank.rs` were only located by line-count grep and their surrounding context was not retrievable within the tool budget.

### Recommendation
Ensure that both the numerator (`stake_amount`/`delegation.stake` used in `calculate_alpenglow_points` and `calculate_block_reward`) and the denominator are drawn from the same frozen, epoch-boundary snapshot (e.g., extend `RewardEpochDelegatedStakes` or an equivalent frozen per-account delegation snapshot) rather than mixing a frozen denominator with a live numerator during recalculation.

### Proof of Concept
Not independently reproducible from the indexed code alone; a concrete PoC would require constructing a bank in the `EpochRewardPhase::Distribution` state (as in `test_recalculate_alpenglow_rewards_after_partial_distribution_uses_original_denominator`), then, instead of only bumping the vote account's `epoch_credits` as in that test, having a staker issue a `merge-stake`/delegate instruction to increase their own stake account's live delegation before calling `bank.recalculate_stake_rewards`, and comparing the resulting numerator-driven reward share against the pre-mutation expected share — this exact test scaffolding exists at [6](#0-5)  but currently only varies vote-account credits, not stake-account delegation, so it does not cover the numerator-dilution scenario described here.

    /// Retrieves stake history and delegations for stake reward recalculation
    /// after snapshot restore.
    fn get_epoch_params_for_recalculation<'a>(
        &'a self,
        rewarded_epoch: Epoch,
        stakes: &'a Stakes<StakeAccount<Delegation>>,
    ) -> EpochRewardCalculateParamInfo<'a> {
        // Use `stakes` for stake-related info
        let stake_history = stakes.history().clone();
        let stake_delegations = stakes.stake_delegations_vec();

        // Use the VAT-filtered vote-account snapshot from epoch_stakes.
        // Recalculation should match the vote-account admission policy used for
        // distribution.
        let leader_schedule_epoch = self.epoch_schedule().get_leader_schedule_epoch(self.slot());
        let distribution_epoch_vote_accounts = self
            .epoch_stakes(leader_schedule_epoch)
            .expect("calculation should always run after Bank::update_epoch_stakes()")
            .stakes()
            .vote_accounts();
        let cached_vote_accounts =
            self.get_cached_vote_accounts(rewarded_epoch, distribution_epoch_vote_accounts);

        EpochRewardCalculateParamInfo {
            stake_history,
            stake_delegations,
            cached_vote_accounts,
        }
    }
