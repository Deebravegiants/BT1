### Title
Accrued RAY value overflow permanently freezes a market - (File: common/src/rates/simulate.rs)

### Summary
Interest accrual converts aggregate scaled supply and debt back into raw `i128` RAY values before calculating utilization. The borrow index is capped, but that cap is checked only after these aggregate-value multiplications. Consequently, a sufficiently large market can reach `i128::MAX` in `scaled_to_original` before the borrow index reaches `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`. Once this happens, every operation that accrues the market reverts, permanently blocking repayment, withdrawal, liquidation, and further index updates.

### Finding Description
`contracts/pool/src/interest.rs::global_sync` invokes `accrue_step` for every elapsed-time chunk before market operations commit. In `common/src/rates/simulate.rs`, `accrue_step` computes `borrowed_original` and `supplied_original` with `scaled_to_original`. That function performs `scaled.mul(env, index)`, and overflow produces `GenericError::MathOverflow`.

Although `update_borrow_index` caps the resulting index at `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`, this does not prevent the aggregate `borrowed * borrow_index` or `supplied * supply_index` product from overflowing first. The project’s own long-horizon test confirms the resulting state: after the overflow, both `withdraw` and `repay` fail with `MATH_OVERFLOW`, while the stored borrow index remains below its cap.

### Impact Explanation
This permanently freezes all user funds and debt servicing in the affected market:

- Suppliers cannot withdraw collateral or supplied assets.
- Borrowers cannot repay.
- Liquidators cannot liquidate underwater positions.
- Permissionless index updates cannot advance the market.
- Bad-debt cleanup and recapitalization paths that accrue first also cannot recover the market.

Because the accrual runs before action-specific logic, no amount, repayment size, or liquidation leg can bypass the overflow once the stored market state reaches the invalid numeric domain.

### Likelihood Explanation
An unprivileged borrower/supplier can establish the precondition through normal `supply` and `borrow` calls: provide collateral, supply the debt-market asset, and borrow a large fraction of that market. The issue then triggers through ordinary time accrual when utilization remains high enough for index growth to push an aggregate RAY value beyond `i128::MAX`.

The repository’s test demonstrates this using one billion whole 18-decimal tokens at 98% utilization and a steep 175% maximum-rate curve. It requires a very large admitted market and sustained high utilization over multiple years, so the practical likelihood is lower than an immediately triggerable flaw. However, admitted caps and index bounds do not prevent this state, and the repository explicitly demonstrates the permanent freeze.

### Recommendation
Add an overflow-safe bound before accrual:

1. Detect when `borrowed`, `supplied`, `borrow_index`, or `supply_index` can make `scaled * index / RAY` exceed `i128::MAX`.
2. Cap or clamp the index before converting aggregate scaled balances when multiplication would exceed the RAY value domain.
3. Prefer checked/saturating aggregate-value calculation in `accrue_step`, then explicitly cap accrual at the largest index that keeps both market totals representable.
4. Add regression tests proving that once the cliff would be reached, `repay`, `withdraw`, `liquidate`, and `clean_bad_debt` still execute conservatively rather than reverting during mandatory accrual.

### Proof of Concept
The existing test `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` demonstrates the issue:

```rust
let principal = BILLION * 10i128.pow(18);
t.supply_raw(BOB, "BIG18", principal);
let debt = principal / 100 * 98;
t.supply_raw(ALICE, "COL", BILLION * 10_000_000 * 3);
t.borrow_raw(ALICE, "BIG18", debt);
```

It then advances time yearly until `update_indexes` fails with `MATH_OVERFLOW`. Afterward:

```rust
assert_contract_error(t.try_withdraw_raw(BOB, "BIG18", 1), errors::MATH_OVERFLOW);
assert_contract_error(t.try_repay(ALICE, "BIG18", 1.0), errors::MATH_OVERFLOW);
```

The test is located in `tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs` and confirms that the market freezes before `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` is reached.
