### Title
Treasury reserve-share minting silently skipped when `total-supply` is zero, permanently losing accrued interest to the DAO treasury - ([File: mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar])

### Summary
The `accrue()` function in the Zest vault contracts (present identically across `v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`) mints the protocol's reserve-factor share of accrued interest (`treasury-lp`) to `.dao-treasury` only by dividing by `total-supply`. This mirrors the reported Alluvial `donate()`/`_onEarnings` bug class: whenever `total-supply` of the vault's `zft` token is `0` at the moment interest accrues, the computed treasury share is forced to `0` and that portion of interest is never minted to anyone — it is silently lost from the reserve-factor accounting, exactly as in the referenced report where `_onEarnings` returns `0` when `totalSupply()` is `0`.

### Finding Description
`accrue()` computes the DAO treasury's cut of freshly accrued interest as: [1](#0-0) 

```
(debt-delta (if (> new-debt old-debt) (- new-debt old-debt) u0))
(reserve-inc (mul-div-down debt-delta (var-get fee-reserve) BPS))
(treasury-lp (if (> reserve-inc u0) (mul-div-down reserve-inc (total-supply) (- (total-assets-preview) reserve-inc)) u0))
...
(if (> treasury-lp u0)
    (try! (ft-mint? zft treasury-lp .dao-treasury))
    false)
```

`treasury-lp` is derived by multiplying `reserve-inc` by `(total-supply)`. If `(total-supply)` is `0` at the time `accrue()` executes (i.e. `ft-get-supply zft` returns `0`, as defined at [2](#0-1) ), then `treasury-lp` evaluates to `0` regardless of how large `reserve-inc` is. The interest itself is *not* lost from the vault's asset accounting (it is captured later via `system-repay`, which adds `interest-paid` directly to the `assets` variable — [3](#0-2) ), but the treasury's proportional claim on that interest (share minting) never happens, since it can only ever be minted at the exact moment `accrue()` runs with `total-supply == 0`.

This is structurally identical to the reported bug: `_onEarnings`/`donate()` in Alluvial computes `sharesToMint` as a function of `_totalSupply()` in the numerator, so a zero total supply forces the reward-mint to zero even though real value (ETH donated / interest accrued) exists. Here, `total-supply` (the `zft` supply) plays the same role, and `debt-delta`/`reserve-inc` (interest accrued and the protocol's cut of it) plays the role of the donated ETH: "interest created versus interest distributed" is broken exactly when supply is transiently zero.

A zero-total-supply state with outstanding debt can arise because `redeem()` does not check `total-borrowed`/outstanding debt against the remaining `total-supply`; it only checks the caller's own share balance and the vault's available liquidity: [4](#0-3) . If the sole/last remaining depositor is able to fully redeem while a separate outstanding loan (drawn via `system-borrow`, gated by `check-caller-auth`/market: [5](#0-4) ) continues to accrue interest, subsequent `accrue()` calls will compute a non-zero `debt-delta`/`reserve-inc` while `total-supply` is `0`, causing the treasury's share for that period to be permanently skipped.

### Impact Explanation
This falls into the "interest created versus interest distributed" value-identity category explicitly listed as in-scope. The DAO treasury's entitled reserve-factor cut of interest accrued during any zero-total-supply window is never minted and cannot be recovered afterward (each `accrue()` call only mints the *current* delta's share; there is no retroactive top-up mechanism). This is a permanent freezing/loss of unclaimed yield belonging to the protocol treasury — classified as High impact (theft/loss of unclaimed yield), not merely a cosmetic rounding issue.

### Likelihood Explanation
Exploitability depends on the vault reaching a `total-supply == 0` state while debt remains outstanding and interest continues to accrue — i.e., the last depositor withdraws fully while a borrower (via the market contract) still owes accruing interest, and the redeeming depositor's claim happens to be fully coverable by available liquidity even though debt remains open. I was not able to fully verify, within the available tool budget, whether `get-available-assets`/`total-assets` accounting always prevents this exact sequence (i.e., whether `redeem()`'s liquidity check inherently forbids `total-supply` hitting `0` whenever debt is outstanding). The core mint-skipped-when-supply-zero logic in `accrue()` is confirmed directly from the code, but the full end-to-end reachability of the zero-supply-with-live-debt precondition needs further runtime verification (e.g., via a Devin session running the Clarity test suite) before this can be considered fully proven exploitable in practice.

### Recommendation
Add an explicit guard/fallback in `accrue()` (mirroring the "SOLVED" fix referenced in the report) so that when `(total-supply)` is `0` but `reserve-inc > 0`, the reserve share is either minted to `.dao-treasury` using an alternative basis (e.g., 1:1 against `reserve-inc`, similar to the `ts == 0` branch already used in `convert-to-shares-preview`) or is escrowed/accounted separately until a future depositor exists, rather than being silently dropped to `0`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Depositor A deposits into `v0-vault-stx`, becoming sole holder of 100% of `zft` supply — [6](#0-5) .
2. Market contract calls `system-borrow` on behalf of a borrower, creating outstanding `principal-scaled`/`total-borrowed` — [5](#0-4) .
3. Depositor A calls `redeem()` for their full balance; if available liquidity happens to cover it, `total-supply` becomes `0` while debt remains outstanding — [4](#0-3) .
4. Time passes; any subsequent call that triggers `accrue()` (e.g., another market operation) computes `debt-delta > 0` and `reserve-inc > 0`, but because `(total-supply)` is `0`, `treasury-lp` evaluates to `0` and no shares are minted to `.dao-treasury` — [1](#0-0) .
5. A new depositor B later deposits; since `total-supply` is `0`, `convert-to-shares-preview` mints shares 1:1 against B's deposit only, ignoring any interest/cash already sitting in the vault from step 4 — [7](#0-6) , meaning the treasury's rightful cut of that interest is never recovered and is instead absorbed into the pool for whichever depositor happens to re-enter first.

(define-public (system-repay (amount uint))

        (states (var-get pause-states))

        (idx (var-get index))
