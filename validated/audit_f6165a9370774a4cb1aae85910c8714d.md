### Title
Direct token donation to an Aquarius pair inflates pool reserves and the fair-value price of its LP collateral, enabling over-borrowing - (File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/providers/aquarius.rs)

### Summary
Like the DTXT incident — where a small unsolicited transfer to the pair address forged a "liquidity addition" signal — XOXNO's Aquarius LP oracle derives the fair value of LP-share collateral from the pair's *self-reported reserves* (`get_reserves`). An unprivileged `token.transfer` to the Aquarius pool address raises the reserve of one leg without minting a single LP share, and the resulting reserve growth flows directly into `fair_lp_price_wad` / `fair_stable_lp_price_wad`, overvaluing LP collateral used in `borrow`/`multiply`/`flash_position`.

### Finding Description
`aquarius::read` pulls `(reserve_a, reserve_b)` live from `aquarius_pool_reserves_call(&env, &lp.pool)` and `total_shares` from `aquarius_total_shares_call`, then computes the share price from those raw reserves [1](#0-0) .

- Constant-product path: `total_value = 2 * sqrt(value_a * value_b)`, so a donation of token A raising `value_a` by factor `k` multiplies the LP price by `sqrt(k)` [2](#0-1) .
- Stable path: the invariant `D` is solved over WAD-normalized reserves and multiplied by `min(price_a, price_b)`; a one-sided donation still grows `D` roughly proportionally to the imbalance [3](#0-2) .

No mechanism distinguishes reserves produced by real LP deposits from reserves produced by a naked `transfer` to the pair — exactly the forgeable detection in the reference incident. LP sources are sole-source (`sources.len() == 1`), so the dual-leg tolerance check does not bound the move, and the only guards are `min_pool_value_wad` (a floor, not a cap) and the configurable `max_sanity_price_wad` band [4](#0-3) .

### Impact Explanation
The attacker: (1) acquires a large fraction of an Aquarius pool's LP shares, (2) supplies them as collateral via `controller.supply` in a spoke listing that LP token, (3) donates the cheaper leg directly to the Aquarius pool address, inflating `reserve_a`, (4) calls `borrow` (or `multiply`/`flash_position`) against the now-overvalued collateral via the Context-cached price, and (5) abandons the position. Realizable borrow proceeds exceed the true redeemable value of the collateral, leaving the market with bad debt absorbed by `clean_bad_debt`/supply-index write-down — i.e., protocol insolvency / theft of supplier funds, matching the incident's "bypassed fee → drained pool" outcome.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires a listed `PriceSource::AquariusLp`/`AquariusStableLp` collateral market and enough donated capital to move `sqrt(reserve_a)` materially; profit requires controlling most of the LP supply so the inflated per-share price applies to the attacker's own collateral. The donation itself is a plain unprivileged token transfer — the same primitive as the reference exploit. The sanity band and the fact that donated reserves may be partially recoverable by LPs (skim mechanics depend on the Aquarius pool implementation) temper the severity; Medium is appropriate.

### Recommendation
Value LP collateral from *virtual* reserves (minimum of the two legs, e.g., `2 * min(value_a, value_b)` style invariant pricing) rather than the geometric mean/invariant over raw balances, or cap LP price growth against a smoothed/TWAP reserve baseline. Alternatively treat per-read reserve jumps beyond a configured bound as `InvalidPrice`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker deposits into Aquarius pool `(token_a, token_b)` until holding ~all `total_shares`, then supplies the share token via `controller.supply(hub_asset={hub, share}, ...)`.
2. `token::Client(token_a).transfer(attacker, lp.pool, X)` with `X ≈ reserve_a` — no LP shares minted.
3. Next `borrow` call: `aquarius_pool_reserves_call` returns `(2·r_a, r_b)`; `fair_lp_price_wad` returns `~sqrt(2)×` the honest price [2](#0-1) .
4. Collateral value in the health-factor computation doubles-ish; attacker borrows up to the inflated LTV and abandons the account. The position goes unhealthy only after the donation is absorbed; `clean_bad_debt` writes the loss down through the supply index, socializing it across suppliers.
