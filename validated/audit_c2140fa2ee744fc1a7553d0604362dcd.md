### Title
`socialize-debt` can zero out `assets` while `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` shares remain minted to `NULL-ADDRESS`, permanently bricking/looting deposits via the `ta=u0` branch of `convert-to-shares-preview` - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar`)

### Summary
`initialize` bootstraps the vault by minting `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` (1000) `zft` shares to `NULL-ADDRESS` against `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` underlying, intending to enforce `total-supply()>0 => total-assets()>0` forever. `socialize-debt` however can drive `(var-get assets)` down to `u0` while leaving `total-supply` at (or above) `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY`, since it writes down `assets`/`total-borrowed`/`principal-scaled` independently of the `zft` supply, breaking that invariant and re-opening the exact zero-share-mint path `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` was meant to close.

### Finding Description
Invariant claimed: `ts(zft-supply) > 0 ⇒ ta(total-assets) > 0`, guaranteed at bootstrap by `initialize` -> `deposit(MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY, u0, NULL-ADDRESS)` which, because `ts=0` at that point, takes the `amount` branch of `convert-to-shares-preview`: [1](#0-0) 

leaving `ts = MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY`, `assets = MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY`, locked at `NULL-ADDRESS` (unspendable — nobody can call `redeem` as `NULL-ADDRESS`): [2](#0-1) 

`system-borrow` never decrements `assets` on borrow (only `principal-scaled`/`total-borrowed` change), so `assets` continues to represent “total value ever deposited,” while accrued yield is captured separately as `interest = debt − total-borrowed`: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

`socialize-debt` (called by the market, an authorized contract, when a position defaults with bad debt) reduces `principal-scaled`, `total-borrowed`, **and `assets`** by `principal-reduction`, floored at `u0`, without touching `zft` supply at all: [5](#0-4) 

If the entire outstanding `scaled-principal` is written off (`scaled-amount == scaled-principal`), then `principal-reduction == borrowed`. If `borrowed == current-assets` (fully-utilized vault, e.g. only the `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` bootstrap capital was ever deposited and fully lent out), then `assets` is set to exactly `u0`, `total-borrowed` and `principal-scaled` also become `u0`, so `interest = debt-preview(u0) − total-borrowed(u0) = u0` and thus `total-assets-preview() = u0` afterward — while `total-supply-preview()` is still `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` (the locked `NULL-ADDRESS` balance), since `socialize-debt` never burns any `zft`.

At that point, any subsequent `deposit` call hits `convert-to-shares-preview`'s `ts != u0` branch, then `ta == u0`, returning `u0` shares regardless of `amount` deposited: [6](#0-5) 

`deposit` only checks `(>= inkind min-out)` — there is no `ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO`-style guard (unlike `redeem`, which does check `(> inkind u0)`), so a depositor who supplies `min-out=u0` (a common/naive default) has their underlying tokens pulled in and added to `assets`, but receives `0` `zft` shares — direct loss of principal. Even careful depositors who require `min-out>0` are permanently blocked from depositing at all, since `available-assets` will also be `u0` (no more borrows possible either), freezing the vault.

`socialize-debt` is gated by `check-caller-auth`, restricted to the market contract: [7](#0-6) 
It is not directly callable by an unprivileged attacker, but it is invoked by the market as ordinary, expected system behavior in response to a real bad-debt/liquidation shortfall — a state an unprivileged borrower can bring about (e.g., taking a loan that later becomes undercollateralized and gets liquidated with insufficient collateral). No `DAO` compromise, oracle manipulation, or privileged key is required to reach the `socialize-debt` call itself; only ordinary borrow/liquidation flow plus the market's own accounting logic.

### Impact Explanation
Once `assets` reaches `u0` while `ts >= MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY`, every subsequent depositor with `min-out=u0` loses 100% of their deposited principal (0 shares minted, funds added to a pool from which they can never withdraw). The vault also becomes structurally unable to recover: with `available-assets = assets − total-borrowed = u0`, no further `system-borrow` (hence no interest accrual to lift `total-assets-preview` above `u0`) is possible until a deposit with `min-out=u0` occurs to top up `assets` — at the cost of that depositor's funds. This matches "permanent freezing of funds" and, for any depositor who deposits with `min-out=0`, "direct theft of user funds at rest" — Critical severity. The magnitude scales with whatever amount the affected depositor(s) contribute; it is repeatable each time the vault's `assets` fully drains to `u0` post-writeoff (e.g., in a low-TVL vault where the only remaining capital is the `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` bootstrap deposit that gets fully lent and then defaulted on).

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires: (1) the vault's only/majority liquid capital to be fully borrowed out (plausible for low-TVL vaults, or shortly after bootstrap before organic deposits arrive), and (2) a bad-debt event serious enough that the market writes off the *entire* outstanding `scaled-principal` via `socialize-debt` (a genuine, expected liquidation-shortfall code path, not requiring privileged access — only a real economic default). The capital cost to an attacker to engineer such a shortfall themselves (e.g. via a self-inflicted under-collateralized loan the market cannot fully seize) depends on the market's liquidation-shortfall accounting, but the underlying vault flaw is independent of intent — it can equally occur through ordinary market risk (price crash) with no attacker at all. The failure mode is deterministic given the state, and reproducible via the exact arithmetic shown above.

### Recommendation
Enforce the `ts>0 ⇒ ta>0` invariant defensively in `deposit`/`convert-to-shares-preview`, e.g., reject deposits that would compute `u0` shares from a nonzero `amount` regardless of `min-out` (add an `ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO` check for `deposit`, mirroring `redeem`). Additionally, `socialize-debt` should never be able to floor `assets` to `u0` while `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` shares remain outstanding — e.g., reserve/exclude the `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` worth of `assets` from being wiped, or re-derive `total-assets` in a way that cannot fall to `u0` as long as `total-supply >= MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY`.

### Proof of Concept
Clarinet/vitest simnet plan:
1. Deploy vault, call `initialize` — assert `get-total-supply` == `MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY` (1000), `get-total-assets` == 1000, and `zft` balance of `NULL-ADDRESS` == 1000.
2. As the (mock/authorized) market contract, call `system-borrow(1000, borrower)` — assert `get-available-assets` == 0.
3. Advance `stacks-block-time`, call `accrue` to let `index` compound so `total-debt` > `total-borrowed` (e.g., debt grows to 1500).
4. As market, call `socialize-debt(scaled-principal)` writing off the *entire* scaled principal — assert post-call: `get-principal-scaled` == 0, `get-assets`/internal `assets` == 0 (via `get-available-assets` == 0 and reasoning `assets - borrowed` both 0), and `get-total-assets` == 0.
5. Assert `get-total-supply` still == 1000 (`>= MINIMUM-LIQUIDITY`).
6. Call `deposit(amount, u0, victim)` from a fresh depositor with a nonzero `amount` (e.g. 500) and `min-out=u0` — assert the call succeeds and returns `0` shares (`(ok u0)`), while `get-balance(victim)` == 0 and the victim's underlying tokens have been transferred into the vault (`assets` now == 500), demonstrating total loss of the victim's deposited principal for zero shares.

(define-public (socialize-debt (scaled-amount uint))
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (current-assets (var-get assets))
        (current-lindex (var-get lindex))
        (old-total-assets (total-assets))
        (debt-reduction (mul-div-down scaled-amount idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (principal-reduction (if (> scaled-principal u0)
                                (mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal)
                                u0))
        ;; Write down lindex proportionally to loss in total-assets
        (new-lindex (if (and (> old-total-assets u0) (> old-total-assets debt-reduction))
                       (mul-div-down current-lindex (- old-total-assets debt-reduction) old-total-assets)
                       u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (> scaled-amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (var-set lindex new-lindex)
    (var-set principal-scaled (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0))
    (var-set total-borrowed (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0))
    (var-set assets (if (> current-assets principal-reduction) (- current-assets principal-reduction) u0))

    (print {
      action: "socialize-debt",
      caller: contract-caller,
      data: {
        scaled-amount: scaled-amount,
        debt-reduction: debt-reduction,
        principal-reduction: principal-reduction,
        old-lindex: current-lindex,
        new-lindex: new-lindex,
        old-total-assets: old-total-assets,
        principal-scaled: (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0),
        total-borrowed: (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0),
        index: idx
      }
    })

    (ok true)))
