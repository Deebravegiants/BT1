### Title
Permanent market freeze from RAY value overflow during interest accrual - (contracts/pool/src/interest.rs)

### Summary
A market whose scaled supply or debt value exceeds the `i128` domain permanently traps all subsequent operations because `accrue_step` converts scaled balances back to RAY values before applying the borrow-index cap. [1](#0-0) 

### Finding Description
Every pool mutation loads the market through `synced_market`, which calls `interest::global_sync` before the operation-specific logic runs. [2](#0-1)  `global_sync` calls `accrue_step`, whose first actions calculate `borrowed * borrow_index` and `supplied * supply_index` through `scaled_to_original`. [3](#0-2)  `scaled_to_original` delegates to `Ray::mul`, which raises `MathOverflow` when the exact result does not fit `i128`. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5)  The borrow-index ceiling is applied only after these already-falling valuation multiplications, so it cannot prevent this state-transition cliff. [1](#0-0) [7](#0-6) 

### Impact Explanation
Once either aggregate position value crosses the representable RAY bound, all entrypoints that touch the market panic before they can reduce supply or debt, permanently freezing suppliers' funds and preventing repayment, withdrawal, liquidation, revenue claims, and further index updates. [2](#0-1) [8](#0-7) [9](#0-8)  The repository's regression test demonstrates that the market reaches `MATH_OVERFLOW` before the borrow-index cap and that both withdrawal and repayment subsequently fail. [10](#0-9) 

### Likelihood Explanation
The attacker path is permissionless: `supply` and `borrow` create the exposure, while `update_indexes` only requires caller authorization to force accrual. [11](#0-10) [12](#0-11)  Reaching the condition requires an exceptionally large market and sustained high utilization—the checked scenario uses one billion 18-decimal tokens and approximately 98% utilization—so the likelihood is low despite the permanent market-wide impact. [13](#0-12) 

### Recommendation
Make accrual cap-aware and overflow-safe by clamping `borrow_index` before unscaled valuation, using saturating or widened aggregate-value calculations for utilization, and allowing debt-reducing or supply-reducing operations to bypass future accrual once an aggregate value is already unrepresentable. [14](#0-13) [2](#0-1) 

### Proof of Concept
1. Supply a very large amount of an 18-decimal asset and supply sufficient collateral in another market. [15](#0-14) 
2. Borrow approximately 98% of the first market's liquidity. [15](#0-14) 
3. Let time advance and repeatedly call `update_indexes(caller, [hub_asset])`; accrual eventually panics in `scaled_to_original` with `MathOverflow` while `borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY`. [12](#0-11) [16](#0-15) 
4. Subsequent `withdraw` and `repay` calls also revert with `MATH_OVERFLOW`, leaving the market's balances frozen. [17](#0-16)
