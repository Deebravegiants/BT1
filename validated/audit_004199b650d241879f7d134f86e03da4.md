### Title
Fundamental oracle legs bypass cross-source freshness checks and inflate collateral - ([File: contracts/price-aggregator/src/engine.rs](contracts/price-aggregator/src/engine.rs))

### Summary

A two-source oracle can blend a fresh `Market` price with a nearly day-old `Fundamental` price because the timestamp-spread stale check applies only when both legs are `Market`. [1](#0-0) 

### Finding Description

`blend` computes `spread_bounded` as `primary.nature == Market && anchor.nature == Market`, so a `Market`/`Fundamental` pair does not reject an age spread above `MAX_LEG_AGE_SPREAD_SECONDS`. [2](#0-1) 

The stale anchor is then averaged into the accepted midpoint price whenever it remains within the configured tolerance band. [3](#0-2) 

The controller uses that returned price to calculate collateral, debt, LTV coverage, and health factor. [4](#0-3) 

### Impact Explanation

An attacker can wait for a slow `Fundamental` feed to retain a favorable old value while the `Market` feed updates, then call `borrow` with inflated collateral. [5](#0-4) 

The checked proof scenario uses a frozen `$1.00` anchor against a fresh `$0.91` market price, producing a `$0.955` blended price and increasing recognized collateral by over 4%. [6](#0-5) 

That inflated valuation admits a `$70,000` borrow that fails with `InsufficientCollateral` when the anchor is fresh, creating undercollateralized debt and potential protocol insolvency. [7](#0-6) 

### Likelihood Explanation

This does not require forging either oracle: the stale leg only needs to retain an old value that remains inside the configured dual-source tolerance and below its configured per-feed staleness bound. [8](#0-7) 

An unprivileged borrower can submit the `borrow` path once those market conditions exist; the harness demonstrates the complete path with only normal supply and borrow calls. [9](#0-8) 

### Recommendation

Apply the cross-leg timestamp-spread check to every dual-source resolution, or explicitly bound `Fundamental` legs with a much tighter `max_stale_seconds` that reflects their publication and volatility characteristics. Reject the blended price when either leg is too old relative to the other or to ledger time; do not rely only on per-feed staleness.

### Proof of Concept

1. Configure XLM with a fresh Reflector `Market` leg and a RedStone `Fundamental` leg.
2. Let the fundamental leg retain `$1.00` for 82,800 seconds while the market leg updates to `$0.91`.
3. Supply `100,000` XLM as collateral.
4. Call `borrow` for `70,000` USDC.
5. The aggregator accepts the midpoint near `$0.955`, inflates collateral, and admits the borrow; with a fresh fundamental leg, the same borrow fails for insufficient collateral. [10](#0-9) [11](#0-10)
