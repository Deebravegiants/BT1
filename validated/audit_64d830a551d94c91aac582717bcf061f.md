### Title
Repeated Deposit/Withdraw calls let anyone extract excess "pnl" from LPs by compounding the `calc_take_pnl` checkpoint reset - (File: `program/src/processor.rs`)

### Summary
The `calc_take_pnl` routine skims a fixed fraction (`fees.pnl_numerator/pnl_denominator`, 12% by default) of the pool's invariant growth since the last checkpoint and re-baselines `TargetOrders.calc_pnl_x/calc_pnl_y` every time it runs. Because this checkpoint reset is triggered on **every** unprivileged `Deposit`/`Withdraw` call (not just `WithdrawPnl`), an attacker can force the routine to run many times over small increments of pool growth instead of once over the full growth. As in the referenced Optimism `FeeDisburser` report, applying a fixed-ratio split repeatedly to sub-intervals of growth yields a larger cumulative payout to the "protocol" side (`pnl_owner`) than applying the same ratio once to the aggregate growth, because the underlying formula is non-additive (it involves a square-root re-basing of price/K rather than a linear split).

### Finding Description
`Processor::calc_take_pnl` computes, from the last checkpoint `(calc_pnl_x, calc_pnl_y)` and the current pool reserves `(x1, y1)`:
- `x2 = sqrt(calc_pnl_x * calc_pnl_y * x1 / y1)` (price-adjusted baseline)
- `diff_x = x1 - x2`, `diff_y = y1 - y2`
- `delta = diff * pnl_numerator / pnl_denominator` is credited to `need_take_pnl_pc/coin` (owed to `pnl_owner`)
- The checkpoint is then reset to `x1 - delta_x`, `y1 - delta_y` (see `program/src/processor.rs:159-281`, and the checkpoint reset at `program/src/processor.rs:1531-1532` and `1818-1838`).

This function is invoked from `process_deposit`/`process_deposit2` (`program/src/processor.rs:1166-1173`) and `process_withdraw` (`program/src/processor.rs:1741-1748`), both of which are permissionless instructions reachable by any signer holding LP tokens or pool tokens, with no interval, cooldown, or minimum-growth gate comparable to `FEE_DISBURSEMENT_INTERVAL` in the referenced report. There is no minimum deposit/withdraw size beyond rounding to a non-zero LP amount (`AmmError::InvalidInput`/`NotAllowZeroLP` checks at `program/src/processor.rs:1323`, `1716-1717`).

Because the growth-to-payout mapping uses a square-root re-basing rather than a linear function of the raw growth, splitting one growth interval into two sub-intervals and re-running `calc_take_pnl` after each strictly increases the total amount skimmed to `need_take_pnl_pc/coin`. Modeling proportional reserve growth by a factor `g` (constant price) split into two steps `g1, g2` with `g1*g2 = g`:
- single-call total skim: `r * x0 * (g - 1)`
- two-call total skim: `r * x0 * (g - 1) + r^2 * x0 * (g1 - 1)`

The two-call total is strictly larger by `r^2 * x0 * (g1-1) > 0`. This extra amount is deducted from LPs' share of `total_pc_without_take_pnl`/`total_coin_without_take_pnl` (via the checkpoint reduction) even though no additional real "pnl" was generated - it is purely a side effect of re-running the checkpoint reset more often. ### Impact Explanation
Any unprivileged user can permissionlessly call `Deposit`/`Deposit2` and `Withdraw` back-to-back with minimal amounts (limited only by rounding to a non-zero LP amount) purely to force additional `calc_take_pnl` invocations between organic trading activity. Each extra invocation compounds an extra skim above the intended 12% split of the pool's true long-term invariant growth, permanently transferring value from LP reserves to `need_take_pnl_pc/need_take_pnl_coin` (ultimately paid out to `pnl_owner` via `WithdrawPnl`). This is a real, ongoing misallocation of LP funds — not merely a rounding dust issue — and it grows proportionally with the number of forced calls an attacker is willing to pay gas for.

### Likelihood Explanation
Deposit and Withdraw require no special privileges, only ordinary SPL token balances and a signer, and are core swapper/LP-reachable instructions explicitly in scope. Since the AMM sees continuous organic trading activity (which increases K via trade fees), any actor motivated to grief LPs, or a party colluding with/being the `pnl_owner`, can interleave minimal deposit/withdraw pairs between normal swaps to repeatedly trigger `calc_take_pnl` at high frequency, with cost limited to transaction fees. No cooldown, minimum-interval, or minimum-growth threshold exists to prevent this.

### Recommendation
Adopt one of the mitigations suggested in the referenced report, adapted to this codebase:
1. Rate-limit checkpoint resets in `calc_take_pnl` (e.g., only reset the checkpoint at most once per slot/epoch or after a minimum elapsed time/growth threshold), rather than on every `Deposit`/`Withdraw` call.
2. Restrict pnl-checkpointing to genuinely necessary events, and consider only recalculating pnl lazily at `WithdrawPnl` time using a formula that is provably additive/idempotent regardless of how many intermediate calls occurred, so the total amount skimmed does not depend on the number of times the function is invoked.

### Proof of Concept
1. Initialize a pool at reserves `(x0, y0)` with checkpoint `calc_pnl_x = x0, calc_pnl_y = y0`.
2. Let organic swap fees grow reserves proportionally to `(x0*g, y0*g)` (constant price, e.g., balanced fee accrual on both sides).
3. Scenario A (single call): call `Deposit`/`Withdraw` once when reserves reach `(x0*g, y0*g)`. `calc_take_pnl` computes `diff_x = x0*(g-1)`, skims `delta_x = r*x0*(g-1)`.
4. Scenario B (attacker-forced double call): call a minimal `Deposit` followed by a minimal `Withdraw` (or vice versa) once when reserves first reach an intermediate `(x0*g1, y0*g1)` (`1 < g1 < g`), forcing an extra `calc_take_pnl` invocation there, and again when reserves reach `(x0*g, y0*g)`.
5. Sum the two skims in Scenario B: `r*x0*(g1-1) + r*x0*(g - h1)` where `h1 = g1(1-r)+r`, which algebraically equals `r*x0*(g-1) + r^2*x0*(g1-1)` — strictly greater than the Scenario A total by `r^2*x0*(g1-1)`.
6. The extra amount is added to `amm.state_data.need_take_pnl_pc/coin` (see `program/src/processor.rs:244-262`) and is later paid to `pnl_owner` via `process_withdrawpnl` (`program/src/processor.rs:1505-1532`), permanently reducing the coin/pc backing available to LPs relative to what a single aggregate calculation would have skimmed.
