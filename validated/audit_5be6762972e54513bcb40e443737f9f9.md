### Title
Permissionless dust top-up straddles the `clean_bad_debt` gate and blocks bad-debt socialization - (File: contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs)

### Summary
`clean_bad_debt` only admits an insolvent account whose remaining collateral is at or below the fixed `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` ($5) dust cap (`BadDebtGate::DustCapped` → `is_socializable_bad_debt`). At the same time, any unprivileged address may `supply` into a foreign account as long as it tops up a supply leg that already exists (`require_third_party_existing_supply`). An attacker who observes a pending `clean_bad_debt` call (or simply monitors for accounts approaching the dust gate) can donate a small amount of the collateral asset into the victim's existing supply leg, pushing `total_collateral` just above $5 and making every permissionless `clean_bad_debt` revert with `CannotCleanBadDebt` (114). This mirrors the Mastodon bug class: an actor who knows of an incoming object/action can preempt it so it can never be correctly processed.

### Finding Description
- The dust gate is evaluated on live USD totals: `socialize_bad_debt` computes `totals` via `calculate_account_risk_totals` and asserts `is_socializable_bad_debt(total_debt, total_collateral)`, which requires `debt > collateral` **and** `collateral <= BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` (contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs, `socialize_bad_debt`; certora/controller/spec/boundary_rules.rs `bad_debt_straddle_blocks_dust_gate` pins exactly this straddle).
- `controller::supply` on an existing account requires only that each paid `HubAssetKey` already be a supply position of the account — the caller need not be owner or delegate (`require_third_party_existing_supply` in contracts/controller/src/positions/supply.rs:78-97; INV-AUTH-03).
- Supply credit uses the measured pool receipt, so the donation is real collateral; `merge_supply_leg` grows the leg. Entry gates (`require_can_supply`) pass whenever the listing is unpaused/unfrozen and collateralizable, which is the normal state. No post-supply solvency gate blocks topping up an insolvent account (INV-RISK-01 notes supply does not universally apply the final gate).
- Repetition is cheap in the worst case: if a liquidator seizes the donated collateral, the attacker re-donates enough to re-straddle the gate before each `clean_bad_debt`. Because the gate is value-based and continuous, the attacker only needs to keep `total_collateral` strictly above $5 while `debt > collateral` holds — i.e., a donation of roughly `(dust threshold − current collateral) + epsilon`, then ~$5+ per refill.
- While the account remains insolvent-but-straddled, the residual debt is never written down: the supply index is not reduced, the shares/cash shortfall persists, and per INV-ACCT-04 a positive backing shortfall blocks new supply into the affected hub market. The denial therefore propagates from one account to the market's supply side.

### Impact Explanation
Bad debt that would otherwise be socialized permissionlessly stays on the books indefinitely under pure permissionless flow. The market keeps an uncovered liability: suppliers' claims remain nominally intact but unbacked, the backing-shortfall check rejects new supply, and interest keeps accruing on phantom debt — a temporary freezing of funds / degraded market operation caused entirely by an unprivileged address. Recovery requires the owner-gated `force_socialize_bad_debt` (`BadDebtGate::InsolventOnly`), which under repository wiring is a governance operation on the Sensitive timelock tier — so each straddled account needs a timelocked governance intervention, and the attacker can re-straddle a new account while governance is in flight. Liquidators can also claw the donated collateral, but that only re-opens the gate after additional profitable-liquidation work and the attacker can refill.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. Preconditions: an account that is insolvent with residual collateral at or under $5 — a state the protocol explicitly expects to occur (dust-debt promotion, `check_bad_debt_after_liquidation`). The attack needs no privilege, no flash loan, and no cooperation: a single `supply(caller, account_id, spoke_id, [(hub_asset, dust_amount)])` call with the account's existing collateral asset. Cost is bounded by the dust threshold (≈$5) plus refill donations if liquidators consume it; griefing incentives exist for bad-debt holders positioned to benefit from delayed write-downs (e.g., keeping supplier balances nominally higher for exiting first, or simply stalling a market). The one-sided cost (donated collateral is lost to liquidation/revenue on eventual cleanup) and the existence of a governance escape path keep this at Medium rather than High.

### Recommendation
Make the dust gate robust to donations, e.g.:
- Evaluate `is_socializable_bad_debt` on collateral excluding top-ups received after the account became insolvent, or
- Let `clean_bad_debt` accept an optional explicit max-collateral parameter and sweep up to the first N supply legs, or
- Exempt newly-supplied legs opened after a recorded insolvency marker from the dust computation, or
- Simply document and accept that accounts above the dust cap route through `force_socialize_bad_debt`, and reduce friction by lowering the delay tier for that operation or allowing a permissionless "over-dust" cleanup when `debt > collateral` by a large margin (e.g., collateral < some higher cap while deeply insolvent), since cleanup already reclassifies collateral as revenue and cannot profit the donor.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice supplies 30 USDC and borrows 0.011 ETH; the USDC price crashes to $0.10. Collateral = $3, debt ≈ $22 → insolvent and inside the dust gate (mirrors `test_bad_debt_socialization_triggers_under_threshold` in tests/test-harness/tests/controller/liquidation_boundary.rs:213-240).
2. A keeper submits `clean_bad_debt(caller, alice_id)`, which should succeed via `BadDebtGate::DustCapped` (contracts/controller/src/positions/liquidation/mod.rs:229-235).
3. Attacker Bob, watching the mempool/state, front-runs with `supply(bob, alice_id, alice_spoke, [(hub_asset(USDC), 3 USDC)])`. USDC is already a supply leg of Alice's account, so `require_third_party_existing_supply` passes; measured receipt credits ~3 USDC.
4. `total_collateral` is now ~$6 > `BAD_DEBT_USD_THRESHOLD` ($5) while `total_debt` (~$22) > collateral — the straddle state pinned by `bad_debt_straddle_blocks_dust_gate` (certora/controller/spec/boundary_rules.rs:44-67).
5. `clean_bad_debt` now reverts `CannotCleanBadDebt` (114), matching `fs_permissionless_denied` in tests/integration/flows/liquidation.sh:360. The debt is never socialized; the ETH market retains the unbacked liability and INV-ACCT-04 blocks new supply until a timelocked `force_socialize_bad_debt` executes. Bob lost only ~$3 of donated collateral.

Caveat: this analysis assumes the collateral asset's listing stays unpaused/collateralizable and that prices needed by the risk totals remain valid — both are normal preconditions for `clean_bad_debt` itself, since it also reads prices.
