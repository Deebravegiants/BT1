### Title
`Pool::repay` refunds the declared `overpayment` out of real custody even when no tokens were ever transferred in — ([File: contracts/pool/src/ops/repay.rs])

### Summary
The analog of `matchCrossMarketOrders`'s "last order pays the remainder (possibly zero) while surplus stays in the contract" is XOXNO's pool repay leg. `ops::repay::accounting` computes `overpayment = amount - current_debt_ceil` from the **declared** `PoolAction.amount`, not from the pool's measured inbound balance, and `ops::repay::apply` then executes `transfer_out(payer, overpayment)` — a real token transfer out of pool custody that neither debits `cash` nor passes `require_reserves`. When the market has no debt, `current_debt_ceil == 0`, the full-close branch makes the **entire declared amount** overpayment, `net_repay == 0`, and the `RepayRoundsToZeroShares` assert passes on its `net_repay == 0` disjunct. The payer receives tokens it never deposited.

### Finding Description
In `contracts/pool/src/ops/repay.rs:40-66`, `resolve_repay` (`common/src/rates/scaling.rs:171-192`) compares the caller-declared `amount` against the ceiling-rounded debt and returns the difference as `overpayment`; `apply` at `contracts/pool/src/ops/repay.rs:32` calls `outcome.cache.transfer_out(payer, outcome.overpayment)` unconditionally. Because the refund is sized from the declared amount rather than the measured balance delta, a call that transfers nothing in still refunds the full "excess" from the pool's real token balance, leaving `cash` untouched so the book/custody mismatch is silent. The repository's own regression test documents this: `test_unfunded_repay_overpayment_refund_also_pays_out_of_custody` (`contracts/pool/tests/flows.rs:3589-3611`) declares `ract(0, custody_before)` with zero transferred and asserts the payer drains `custody_before`; the same "refund derived from a declared inbound amount" gap is noted for `ops::recapitalize::apply` (excess over the shortfall). This mirrors the report's class: a remainder/excess computed from declared quantities lets one party receive assets it never paid for, funded by other users' collateral in custody.

### Impact Explanation
An unprivileged address can call `repay` with a declared amount up to the pool's full token balance on any market with zero outstanding debt (or declare above the real debt on a live market) and receive the entire declared overpayment — a direct theft of supplier funds from pool custody, with the `cash` ledger unchanged so the loss is invisible to reserve checks until a later withdrawal or borrow fails. Severity: Critical (theft of user funds, unprivileged path).

### Likelihood Explanation
The path requires no privileged role, no oracle manipulation, and no timing: only a market whose debt (or shortfall for recapitalize) is smaller than the declared amount. Zero-debt markets exist whenever a new listing is supplied but not yet borrowed, or after bad-debt cleanup. The only caveat is reachability: if `Pool::repay` enforces an additional caller restriction not evident in the test (the test invokes it with a freshly generated `payer`, implying at most `payer.require_auth`), the impact would reduce; the test itself demonstrates the direct call succeeds.

### Recommendation
Size `overpayment` from the pool's **measured inbound receipt** (balance delta), not the declared `PoolAction.amount`: snapshot the token balance before processing and cap the refund at `min(declared_excess, measured_inbound)`. Equivalently, debit `cash` (or route through `require_reserves`) for the refund so an unfunded refund cannot exceed what was actually received. Apply the same fix to `ops::recapitalize::apply`'s excess-over-shortfall refund.

### Proof of Concept
The in-repo test already demonstrates it end-to-end: `test_unfunded_repay_overpayment_refund_also_pays_out_of_custody` at `contracts/pool/tests/flows.rs:3589-3611` — a `payer` with no prior balance calls `pool.repay` declaring `amount = custody_before` on a zero-debt market, receives `credited == 0` yet the pool's real token balance drops by `custody_before` while `cash` is unchanged (`assert_unfunded_refund_drained_custody`). A standalone PoC: supply the market via a separate account, call `repay` with a generated address declaring the pool's full balance, and assert `token.balance(payer) == custody_before` with `state.cash` untouched.
