### Title
Deleveraging blocked by the LTV (initial-margin) gate even when the account is above liquidation threshold - (File: contracts/controller/src/risk/validation.rs)

### Summary
`require_post_pool_risk_gates` enforces `ltv_collateral >= total_debt` — the borrow-capacity (initial-margin analogue) requirement — on every strategy flow that ends with debt, including `repay_debt_with_collateral` and `swap_collateral`/`swap_debt`, which are the protocol's own deleveraging paths. An account whose LTV-weighted collateral has fallen below its debt but whose health factor is still ≥ 1 (not liquidatable) cannot reduce its debt via these paths: the call reverts with `InsufficientCollateral` even though the operation strictly reduces exposure and can leave HF unchanged or improved.

### Finding Description
In `contracts/controller/src/risk/validation.rs:41-53`, the post-action gate requires both `totals.ltv_collateral >= totals.total_debt` (the LTV/"initial margin" bound that constrains new borrowing) and `totals.health_factor >= Wad::ONE` (the liquidation/"maintenance margin" bound) whenever the account is not debt-free. This gate runs through `enforce_post_pool_solvency` (`contracts/controller/src/positions/mod.rs:83-91`) and `strategy_finalize`, which `process_repay_debt_with_collateral` invokes unconditionally at the end of every path (`contracts/controller/src/strategies/repay_debt_with_collateral.rs:91`, with the doc comment "every path ends with the standard risk checks").

A user between the two bounds — `ltv_collateral < total_debt` but `health_factor >= 1` — who tries to sell collateral to repay debt is reverted, even when the netting/swap-repay is risk-reducing or HF-neutral. The same applies to `swap_collateral`/`swap_debt` restructurings that lower risk but leave `ltv_collateral < total_debt`. The Zaros analog maps directly: LTV plays the role of initial margin, liquidation threshold (HF ≥ 1) plays the role of maintenance margin, and a position-reducing trade is gated on the stricter bound. The account's own collateral is effectively frozen: `withdraw` is also blocked in this band (correct in isolation, since it only removes collateral), so the user has no way to exit or shrink the position short of repaying with externally held tokens.

### Impact Explanation
Temporary freezing of user funds and forced deterioration toward liquidation. A user in the LTV-insolvent-but-not-liquidatable band cannot use their on-protocol collateral to deleverage. If the market continues to move against them, the account crosses HF < 1 and is liquidated with a bonus penalty — a loss they attempted to avoid. The trapped collateral is only releasable by sourcing the debt asset externally and calling `repay` (which is ungated, `process_repay` at `debt.rs:69-91`), which may be impossible if the user's remaining funds are the collateral locked in the protocol.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable by any unprivileged account through normal use: supply collateral, borrow near the LTV limit, then let the collateral price drop or debt interest accrue until `ltv_collateral < total_debt` while HF ≥ 1. This band is non-trivial because the liquidation threshold is strictly above LTV per listing (`effective_ltv = min(loan_to_value, liquidation_threshold)` and threshold > LTV is enforced in listing validation). From there a single `repay_debt_with_collateral(caller, account_id, collateral, amount, debt, swap, close_position)` call reproduces the revert. It requires no oracle manipulation, no privileged state, and no unusual market configuration — just ordinary price drift, which is precisely when users need to deleverage.

### Recommendation
Distinguish risk-increasing from risk-reducing operations in the post-pool gate, mirroring the Zaros fix (initial margin for growth, maintenance margin for reduction). Concretely, in `require_post_pool_risk_gates` (or at the `strategy_finalize` call sites for `repay_debt_with_collateral`, `swap_debt`, and risk-reducing `swap_collateral`), skip or relax the `ltv_collateral >= total_debt` and `min_borrow_collateral` checks when the operation measurably reduces risk — e.g., when post-action `total_debt` is strictly lower than pre-action and post-action `health_factor` is ≥ the pre-action HF (or ≥ `Wad::ONE`). Keep the `health_factor >= 1` check unconditional so a deleveraging call can never push the account into liquidation.
