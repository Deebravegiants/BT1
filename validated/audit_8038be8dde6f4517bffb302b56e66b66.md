### Title
Compounded interest overflows the i128 ray-value ceiling and permanently freezes a pool market — every entrypoint accrues first and panics — ([File: contracts/pool/src/interest.rs])

### Summary
A whale-sized borrow position at sustained high utilization grows `borrowed_scaled * borrow_index` past `i128::MAX` long before the `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` index cap can engage. The next accrual inside `global_sync` → `accrue_chunk` → `accrue_step` (`scaled_to_original`) panics with `MathOverflow`. Because every market verb (`supply`, `borrow`, `withdraw`, `repay`, liquidation seizures, `update_indexes`, `claim_revenue`, flash paths) runs `Cache::load` + `global_sync` before mutating, the panic permanently bricks the market for all suppliers and borrowers. This is the XOXNO analog of CVE-2019-20924: a user-driven state triggers a checked-arithmetic invariant that turns into a total denial of service — here not a single tx revert but an irreversible freeze of pooled funds.

### Finding Description
`global_sync` in `contracts/pool/src/interest.rs:20-33` loops `accrue_chunk` over elapsed time; each chunk calls `accrue_step` (shared with the view) which computes `scaled_to_original` = scaled debt shares × RAY index. The product is `checked_mul` on `i128` and panics with `GenericError::MathOverflow` on overflow (`common/src/math/fp_core.rs` checked ops; same `assert_with_error`/`panic_with_error` pattern as `rescale` at `fp_core.rs:218-226`).

The protective `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` cap on `borrow_index` is checked against the *index*, but the overflow happens in the *value* multiply (`borrowed_scaled × index`) inside the same step, so the cap never engages — verified by the harness test asserting `last.borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` at the moment of freeze (`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:349-353`).

The freeze is total and permanent:
- `try_update_indexes_for` fails with `MATH_OVERFLOW`
- `try_withdraw_raw` fails (withdraw accrues first)
- `try_repay` fails — even repayment is impossible, so no deleveraging path exists
- liquidation `seize_positions` calls into the same pool cache flow and hits the same panic
- `clean_bad_debt`/`recapitalize` cannot repair it because the index never advanced past the point of no return deterministically — every future call recomputes the same overflowing product [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

### Impact Explanation
**Permanent freezing of funds** for every participant in the affected market. All supplied liquidity in the `(hub, token)` book — not just the attacker's — becomes unreachable: no withdraw, no repay, no liquidation, no revenue claim. The physical token balance remains in the pool contract while the bookkeeping layer can never settle it, since `Cache::load`/`global_sync` precede every mutation (`contracts/pool/README.md:159-168`). Severity Medium is consistent with the source CVE and with the likelihood constraints below.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable by a single unprivileged address via `supply` + `borrow` (+ collateral supply on a second market) at high utilization, then passive compounding. Constraints that lower likelihood:

- Requires whale-scale principal on the order of `1e9` whole tokens at 18 decimals (raw ray value `1e36`, vs. `i128::MAX ≈ 1.7e38`), i.e., scaled debt within ~170× of the ceiling — only possible for markets whose caps were lifted near `max_cap_for_decimals` (`tests/...:81-95`) or for genuinely huge tokens.
- Requires the book to sit on the steep slope of the utilization curve for multiple years of ledger time (the XLM stress curve crosses ~170× index within the tested horizon; milder curves take longer but drift upward because utilization is value-based and debt compounds faster than supply).
- An attacker cannot fast-forward time, but any naturally growing high-utilization market eventually reaches the cliff regardless of intent — the freeze is a matter of market size × rate × time, not attacker action.

No privileged access, no oracle manipulation, no reentrancy needed; only public entrypoints and ledger time.

### Recommendation
Enforce the ceiling on the *value* side, not the index:

1. In `accrue_step`/`accrue_chunk`, compute `scaled_to_original` with a saturating/checked path and clamp debt growth to `i128::MAX / borrowed_scaled` by capping the *effective index delta* per chunk — i.e., apply `MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` (or a computed per-chunk cap `min(cap, i128::MAX / borrowed)`) **before** the multiply, so the cap actually engages.
2. Alternatively, detect the impending overflow in `global_sync` and settle the market into a degraded mode (freeze further interest accrual but keep `repay`/`withdraw`/`seize` paths operational by skipping or saturating accrual when `borrowed_scaled` is within a safety margin of the ceiling).
3. Add a regression test keeping `withdraw`/`repay` functional after the index cap engages at extreme book sizes.

### Proof of Concept
Reproduced by the existing harness test `a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap` (`tests/test-harness/tests/controller/large_positions_and_long_horizons.rs:320-361`), reduced to the essential steps:

1. Unprivileged `supply` of `1e9 * 10^18` raw units into market `BIG18` (18 decimals, XLM steep curve: `slope3 = 150%`, `max_borrow_rate = 175%`, caps lifted to `max_cap_for_decimals(18)`).
2. Unprivileged `supply` of collateral + `borrow` of 98% of the pool (`debt = principal * 98 / 100`).
3. Advance ledger time year by year, calling `update_indexes` (permissionless) annually.
4. Once `borrowed_scaled × borrow_index` exceeds `i128::MAX` (~index ×170 at this book size), `update_indexes` reverts `Error(Contract, #33)` — `MathOverflow` — from `scaled_to_original` inside `accrue_step`.
5. Asserted permanently frozen: `withdraw` → same `MATH_OVERFLOW`; `repay` → same `MATH_OVERFLOW`; `borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY` confirms the intended cap never fired.

pub(crate) fn global_sync(env: &Env, cache: &mut Cache) {
    if !cache.needs_accrual() {
        return;
    }

    let mut remaining = cache.elapsed_ms();
    while let Some(nonzero) = NonZeroU64::new(remaining) {
        let chunk = nonzero.get().min(MAX_COMPOUND_DELTA_MS);
        accrue_chunk(env, cache, chunk);
        remaining = remaining.saturating_sub(chunk);
    }

    cache.mark_accrued();
}

/// Applies one compound step of `delta_ms` to indexes and protocol revenue.
///
/// The arithmetic lives in [`accrue_step`], shared with the read-only
/// `simulate_update_indexes` so the view and the mutator cannot drift.
fn accrue_chunk(env: &Env, cache: &mut Cache, delta_ms: u64) {
    let step = accrue_step(
        env,
        cache.params(),
        cache.borrowed(),
        cache.supplied(),
        cache.borrow_index(),
        cache.supply_index(),
        delta_ms,
    );

    cache.set_borrow_index(step.borrow_index);
    cache.set_supply_index(step.supply_index);
    cache.accrue_revenue(step.revenue_shares);
}
```

/// The cliff. A billion whole tokens is `1e36` raw ray; the value ceiling is
/// `i128::MAX`, 170 times that. At the XLM curve's steep segment the index
/// grows past 170x in a few years, and the next accrual panics inside
/// `scaled_to_original`. Every verb accrues first, so the market freezes:
/// no repay, no withdraw, no liquidation. The index cap never engages.
#[test]
fn a_whale_market_at_sustained_high_utilization_hits_the_ray_value_ceiling_before_the_index_cap() {
    let mut t = LendingTest::new()
        .with_market(big("BIG18", 18, xlm_curve()))
        .with_market(col())
        .with_max_utilization_disabled_all_markets()
        .build();
    lift_caps(&t, "BIG18", 18);
    lift_caps(&t, "COL", 7);
    let principal = BILLION * 10i128.pow(18);
    t.supply_raw(BOB, "BIG18", principal);
    let debt = principal / 100 * 98;
    t.supply_raw(ALICE, "COL", BILLION * 10_000_000 * 3);
    t.borrow_raw(ALICE, "BIG18", debt);

    let mut years = 0u32;
    let failure = loop {
        years += 1;
        assert!(
            years <= 40,
            "no cliff within 40 years; the bound in docs/reference/formulas.md is wrong"
        );
        t.advance_time(YEAR_SECS);
        if let Err(e) = t.try_update_indexes_for(&["BIG18"]) {
            break e;
        }
    };
    let failed: Result<(), soroban_sdk::Error> = Err(failure);
    assert_contract_error(failed, errors::MATH_OVERFLOW);
    let last = book(&t, "BIG18");
    assert!(
        last.borrow_index < MAX_BORROW_INDEX_RAY,
        "the index cap did not engage before the value overflow"
    );
    // The market is frozen: exits and repayments accrue first and hit the same panic.
    assert_contract_error(t.try_withdraw_raw(BOB, "BIG18", 1), errors::MATH_OVERFLOW);
    assert_contract_error(t.try_repay(ALICE, "BIG18", 1.0), errors::MATH_OVERFLOW);
    std::println!(
        "ray-value cliff reached after {years} years at 98 percent utilization on the XLM curve; last index x{:.1}",
        last.borrow_index as f64 / RAY as f64
    );
```
