## Finding

The pause mechanism in Zest's vault contracts reproduces the exact bug class from the Union Finance report: DAO can independently pause `repay` while leaving `accrue` active, forcing borrowers to keep accumulating interest on debt they are blocked from repaying.

### Title
Independently pausing `repay` while `accrue` remains active forces borrowers to accumulate interest on debt they cannot repay - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar` and sibling vault contracts)

### Summary
Each Zest vault (`v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`) stores pause flags as independent booleans in a single struct: `{deposit, redeem, borrow, repay, accrue, flashloan}`, all set together via `set-pause-states`. [1](#0-0)  `system-repay` reverts with `ERR-PAUSED` whenever the `repay` flag is set, independent of the `accrue` flag. [2](#0-1)  Meanwhile `accrue` continues to run on every call (including on `borrow`, `deposit`, `redeem`, and even DAO calls to `set-pause-states` itself), advancing the debt index as long as the `accrue` flag itself is not set. [3](#0-2) 

### Finding Description
The `set-pause-states` function contains special-case logic only for the `accrue` flag: when `accrue` is toggled on, it accrues first to capture pending interest, and when unpaused it jumps `last-update` forward to skip the paused window entirely, so no debt index growth is lost or double counted during that specific pause. [4](#0-3) 

This special handling proves the developers understood the interest-during-pause problem for the `accrue` flag, but they did not extend equivalent protection to the `repay` flag. If DAO sets `{repay: true, accrue: false}` (a single legitimate call to `set-pause-states`, not a compromise, and independent of any borrow/collateral safety decision), the index (`var-get index`) keeps advancing on every subsequent `accrue` call triggered by other users' `deposit`, `borrow`, or `redeem` operations. [5](#0-4)  All borrowers' true debt (`mul-div-up account-scaled-debt borrow-index INDEX-PRECISION`, computed at repayment time in `v0-4-market.clar`) grows in USD-value terms while `system-repay` reverts on every attempt, since it hard-fails when `(get repay states)` is true regardless of whether `accrue` is also paused. [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 

The identity that breaks: `debt_owed(t1) > debt_owed(t0)` for borrowers during the pause window, while `repayable_amount(t) = 0` for the full window, even though the borrower is solvent and willing to pay — an unprivileged principal is denied the ability to service its own debt while that debt keeps growing, exactly mirroring the cited Union Finance `whenNotPaused` bug on `repayBorrow`.

### Impact Explanation
Borrowers cannot reduce or close their debt position during the pause window while interest keeps accruing against their collateral, silently eroding their loan-to-value ratio. If the pause is held long enough (or paired with a price move), positions that were healthy can be pushed into liquidation purely due to protocol-imposed unpayable interest, resulting in a temporary/permanent freezing of the borrower's ability to protect their own collateral/funds — a High-severity "temporary freezing of funds" outcome, matching the original report's impact category.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires only a single legitimate DAO call to `set-pause-states` with `repay: true` and `accrue: false` — a plausible operational action (e.g. pausing repay to investigate an issue) that is not flagged as unsafe anywhere in the code, since the accrue-skip logic gives false confidence that pausing does not disadvantage users.

### Recommendation
Extend the `last-update` skip-forward logic (currently gated only on the `accrue` flag transition) to also trigger whenever `repay` is paused independently of `accrue`, or alternatively force `accrue` to be paused automatically whenever `repay` is paused, ensuring the debt index cannot advance during any period in which borrowers are prevented from repaying.

### Proof of Concept
1. DAO calls `set-pause-states` on `v0-vault-stx.clar` with `{... repay: true, accrue: false, ...}`. [1](#0-0) 
2. Any other user calls `deposit`, `borrow`, or `redeem` on the same vault, each of which calls `accrue` first and advances `index`/`last-update`. [5](#0-4) 
3. A borrower with outstanding debt calls `repay` on `v0-4-market.clar`, which routes to `vault-system-repay` → `system-repay`; the call reverts with `ERR-PAUSED` because `(get repay states)` is true. [8](#0-7) 
4. Over the pause duration, the borrower's `account-scaled-debt` converted at the growing `borrow-index` increases, while they remain unable to call `repay` to reduce it. [9](#0-8)

```text
(define-public (set-pause-states (states {deposit: bool, redeem: bool, borrow: bool, repay: bool, accrue: bool, flashloan: bool}))
  (begin
    (try! (check-dao-auth))
    (let ((current (var-get pause-states))
          (was-paused (get accrue current))
          (now-paused (get accrue states)))
      ;; When pausing accrue, accrue first to capture pending interest
      (if (and (not was-paused) now-paused)
          (begin (try! (accrue)) false)
          false)
      ;; When unpausing accrue, jump last-update to now to skip paused period
      (if (and was-paused (not now-paused))
          (var-set last-update stacks-block-time)
          false)
      (var-set pause-states states)
```
