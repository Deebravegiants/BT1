### Title
Donation to `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault` SPL accounts lets an attacker manipulate `Deposit`/`Withdraw` LP-share math, mirroring the ERC4626 donation-attack root cause - (File: `program/src/processor.rs`, `program/src/math.rs`)

### Summary
Raydium's `Deposit` and `Withdraw` instructions compute the LP-mint/redeem ratio using the *live SPL token balance* of `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault` (via `Calculator::calc_total_without_take_pnl_no_orderbook`), not an internally-tracked reserve that only changes through AMM instructions. Because these vault token accounts are ordinary SPL token accounts, any unprivileged actor can transfer tokens into them directly (a plain SPL `Transfer`, no AMM-program permission required) without minting any LP tokens. This is the exact same root cause as the reported H‑7 issue: a share/value ratio derived from a balance the attacker can inflate ("donate" to) independently of the share-accounting variable (`amm.lp_amount`).

### Finding Description
`process_deposit` and `process_withdraw` both call: [1](#0-0) 
which forwards the raw vault balances into [2](#0-1) 
`amm_pc_vault.amount` / `amm_coin_vault.amount` are read straight from the SPL token accounts pointed to by `amm.coin_vault`/`amm.pc_vault`, and this is exactly the "asset" figure used to derive:

- Deposit's minted LP amount: `mint_lp_amount = deduct_coin_amount * amm.lp_amount / total_coin_without_take_pnl` (floor), see [3](#0-2) 
- Withdraw's redeemed token amount: `coin_amount/pc_amount = withdraw.amount * total_coin(or pc) / amm.lp_amount`, see [4](#0-3) 

Nothing in `Deposit`/`Withdraw` re-derives `total_coin_without_take_pnl` from an internally tracked, mint/burn-driven reserve; it is simply whatever tokens currently sit in the vault account. Since anyone can `Transfer` (or `TransferChecked`) tokens to `amm.coin_vault` / `amm.pc_vault` (public, well-known addresses, no AMM-authority signature needed to *receive* tokens), an attacker can inflate `total_coin_without_take_pnl`/`total_pc_without_take_pnl` at will, independent of `amm.lp_amount`, exactly as a donation to an ERC4626 vault inflates `totalAssets()` independent of `totalSupply()`.

The only protective check is a hard revert when the computed share amount is exactly zero: [5](#0-4) 
This blocks the trivial "victim gets literally 0 LP tokens" case, but does nothing to prevent the attacker from choosing a donation size that leaves the victim's minted LP shares small-but-nonzero, so the victim still transfers their full `deduct_coin_amount`/`deduct_pc_amount` into the vault for a disproportionately small LP claim.

### Impact Explanation
An attacker who holds (or briefly acquires) a meaningful share of `amm.lp_amount` for a given pool can:
1. Observe a pending `Deposit` transaction in the mempool.
2. Front-run it with a plain SPL token `Transfer` donating a large amount directly into `amm_coin_vault`/`amm_pc_vault`, inflating `total_coin_without_take_pnl`/`total_pc_without_take_pnl` without changing `amm.lp_amount`.
3. Let the victim's `Deposit` execute: they contribute their full `deduct_coin_amount`/`deduct_pc_amount`, but their `mint_lp_amount` is computed against the artificially inflated totals, yielding a disproportionately small LP claim.
4. Back-run with a `Withdraw` of their own LP shares, redeeming a proportional share of the now-larger pool (their donation plus a slice of the victim's deposit), recovering their donated funds plus a cut of the victim's contribution.

This is a concrete theft-of-LP-value / insolvent-accounting vector reachable by a single unprivileged actor with attacker-chosen accounts (the donation transfer + a `Deposit`/`Withdraw` instruction), matching the Medium/High bar (unbacked LP dilution, LP fund theft).

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate: it requires the attacker to already hold (or cheaply acquire) some LP position in the target pool and to observe/front-run a victim `Deposit`. Both are realistic on a transparent-mempool chain, and no privileged role or special build is needed — only standard SPL `Transfer` plus the public `Deposit`/`Withdraw` instructions.

### Recommendation
Do not derive the Deposit-mint / Withdraw-redeem ratio from the raw, externally-mutable SPL token vault balances. Instead:
- Maintain and use an internally tracked reserve (updated only by `Deposit`, `Withdraw`, `Swap`, and PnL-take paths) as the denominator for LP math, and periodically reconcile/sweep any "extra" balance (donations) separately (e.g., treat excess balance as protocol PnL rather than feeding it directly into per-transaction share math), or
- Require `mint_lp_amount`/redemption amounts to be bounded by a minimum acceptable ratio check (already partially covered by `other_amount_min`/slippage, but that only protects against price movement of the two sides relative to each other, not against unilateral vault-balance inflation), or
- Explicitly detect and reject deposits when `total_coin_without_take_pnl`/`total_pc_without_take_pnl` diverges materially from the value implied by `amm.lp_amount` and the last known invariant, similar in spirit to virtual-asset/share protections recommended for ERC4626 vaults.

### Proof of Concept
1. Pool `P` exists with vaults `amm_coin_vault`, `amm_pc_vault`, and `amm.lp_amount = L`; attacker holds LP fraction `f` of `L`.
2. Attacker observes victim's pending `Deposit(max_coin_amount = C, max_pc_amount = ...)` in the mempool.
3. Attacker submits an SPL `Transfer` sending `D` tokens directly to `amm_coin_vault` (and/or `amm_pc_vault`), which succeeds unconditionally since receiving tokens requires no AMM-program authorization — see the vault ownership checks in `process_deposit`/`process_withdraw` only validate the vault *address*, not who may fund it: [6](#0-5) .
4. Victim's `Deposit` lands after the donation; `mint_lp_amount` is computed against the inflated `total_coin_without_take_pnl` per [3](#0-2) , granting the victim far fewer LP tokens per unit deposited than expected, while their tokens are transferred in full per [7](#0-6) .
5. Attacker submits `Withdraw` for their `f`-share LP tokens, redeeming a proportional share of the now-inflated `total_coin_without_take_pnl`/`total_pc_without_take_pnl` (which includes the donation and part of the victim's deposit) per [4](#0-3) , recovering the donated value plus a cut of the victim's contribution.

```rust
    pub fn calc_total_without_take_pnl_no_orderbook<'a>(
        pc_amount: u64,
        coin_amount: u64,
        amm: &'a AmmInfo,
    ) -> Result<(u64, u64), AmmError> {
        let total_pc_without_take_pnl = pc_amount
            .checked_sub(amm.state_data.need_take_pnl_pc)
            .ok_or(AmmError::CheckedSubOverflow)?;
        let total_coin_without_take_pnl = coin_amount
            .checked_sub(amm.state_data.need_take_pnl_coin)
            .ok_or(AmmError::CheckedSubOverflow)?;
        Ok((total_pc_without_take_pnl, total_coin_without_take_pnl))
    }
```
