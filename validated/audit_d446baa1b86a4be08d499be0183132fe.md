## Analog Finding

### Title
Vault `deposit` can mint zero shares while still consuming the depositor's collateral, permanently trapping the deposit - ([File: mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdc.clar])

### Summary
The `deposit` entrypoint in every Zest v2 tokenized vault contract (`v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`) computes the shares to mint via `convert-to-shares-preview`, which can legitimately round down to `u0` when the depositor's amount is small relative to `total-assets`/`total-supply`. Unlike `redeem`, which explicitly guards against a zero output (`ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO`), `deposit` has no equivalent check on `inkind` (the computed share amount), only checking that the raw input `amount` is non‑zero.

### Finding Description
`convert-to-shares-preview` performs a rounding-down multiplication/division: [1](#0-0) 

This is the standard share-mint formula used identically in every vault (`sbtc`, `stx`, `ststxbtc`, `usdc`, `usdh`) both under `mainnet/contracts/vault/` and `local-testing/contracts/vault/`: [2](#0-1) 

In `deposit`, `inkind` is computed from this formula and then used both for the slippage check and for minting, but only `amount > u0` is asserted — there is no `inkind > u0` (`ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO`) check: [3](#0-2) 

Contrast this with `redeem`, in the very same contract, which explicitly guards the symmetric case: [4](#0-3) 

The identity that should hold on every deposit is:

```
shares_minted_to_depositor > 0  whenever  amount_deposited > 0
```

Because `min-out` defaults to caller-supplied and can legally be `u0` (or a caller may simply not anticipate this edge case when `total-assets` has grown large relative to `total-supply`, e.g. after significant interest accrual or a large existing depositor base), a `deposit` call with a small `amount` can pass `(asserts! (>= inkind min-out) ERR-SLIPPAGE)` trivially (0 >= 0) while `inkind` is `u0`. The transaction then:
1. Transfers the depositor's real underlying collateral into the vault via `receive-underlying`.
2. Mints `u0` shares to the depositor (`ft-mint? zft u0 recipient` — either a no-op or literally mints nothing).
3. Increases `assets` by the full deposited `amount`.

The depositor has irrevocably transferred value into the vault and received no share token that would let them ever reclaim it. Their collateral silently becomes backing for existing shareholders' shares (the identity `share_value = total_assets / total_supply` increases for everyone else), while the depositor's own claim is zero.

### Impact Explanation
This breaks the deposit-mint identity and results in permanent freezing of the depositor's funds: the deposited underlying asset (USDC, USDH, sBTC, STX, or stSTXbtc, depending on the vault) is irreversibly transferred to the vault contract, but the depositor is left with zero shares and thus no mechanism to redeem any of it back. There is no compensating mechanism (no refund path, no minimum-shares-on-deposit guard) anywhere in the vault contracts examined for `deposit`.

### Likelihood Explanation
This is a normal reachable path — no privileged role or malicious actor is required. It occurs any time `total-assets` (post accrued interest) is sufficiently large relative to `total-supply` that a legitimate small deposit rounds to zero shares under `mul-div-down`, or when a caller (or an integrating dApp) supplies `min-out = u0` for a routine deposit and total-supply has grown from prior deposits/interest. As vault `total-assets` grows over time via interest accrual (`total-assets-preview`), the share price rises and the amount needed to avoid rounding to zero shares increases correspondingly, making this more likely to occur naturally as the vault matures.

### Recommendation
Add an explicit zero-output guard in `deposit`, symmetric to the one already present in `redeem`:
```
(asserts! (> inkind u0) ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO)
```
placed immediately after computing `inkind` and before `receive-underlying`/`ft-mint?`, in every vault contract (`v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`, and their `local-testing` counterparts).

### Proof of Concept
1. Let the vault have `total-supply = 10_000_000` shares and `total-assets = 10_000_000_000` units (share price = 1000 asset-units per share) after interest accrual.
2. A depositor calls `deposit` with `amount = 500` (less than the 1000-unit price per share) and `min-out = u0`.
3. `convert-to-shares-preview` computes `mul-div-down(500, 10_000_000, 10_000_000_000) = 0` (rounds down).
4. `(asserts! (>= inkind min-out) ERR-SLIPPAGE)` passes since `0 >= 0`.
5. `receive-underlying` pulls the depositor's 500 units of collateral into the vault; `ft-mint? zft 0 recipient` mints zero shares; `assets` increases by 500.
6. The depositor now holds `0` vault shares and has no way to reclaim the 500 units they deposited — the funds are permanently lost to them and implicitly redistributed to existing shareholders via the increased `total-assets`. [3](#0-2)

  (let ((ta (total-assets-preview))

            u0

(define-public (deposit (amount uint) (min-out uint) (recipient principal))
    (let (
      (states (var-get pause-states))
      (u (try! (accrue)))
      (account contract-caller)
      (CAP-SUPPLY (var-get cap-supply))
      (current-assets (var-get assets))
      (inkind (convert-to-shares-preview amount)))

    (asserts! (not (get deposit states)) ERR-PAUSED)
    (asserts! (var-get initialized) ERR-INIT)
    (asserts! (not (var-get in-flashloan)) ERR-REENTRANCY)
    (asserts! (> amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)
    (asserts! (>= inkind min-out) ERR-SLIPPAGE)
    (asserts! (<= (+ current-assets amount) CAP-SUPPLY) ERR-SUPPLY-CAP-EXCEEDED)
