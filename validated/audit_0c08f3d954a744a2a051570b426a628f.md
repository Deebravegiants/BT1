### Title
Just-in-time staking allows an unprivileged delegator to capture a share of validator rewards without contributing at-risk stake during the epoch that earned them - (File: `staking-pool/src/internal.rs`)

### Summary
The staking-pool contract distributes epoch rewards pro-rata across all currently held "stake" shares at the moment `internal_ping()` runs, rather than weighting the reward by how long each share was actually staked (and thus actually contributed to the validator's real, on-chain locked stake) during the epoch that generated the reward. This is structurally the same accounting root cause as the referenced Locke.sol H-06 finding: reward accrual is decoupled from genuine token/time-at-risk, allowing an attacker to insert capital right before a reward-crediting event and immediately extract a share of rewards they did not help earn, diluting honest long-term stakers.

### Finding Description
Every state-changing entrypoint (`deposit`, `deposit_and_stake`, `stake`, `stake_all`, `unstake`, `unstake_all`, `withdraw`, `withdraw_all`) begins by calling `internal_ping()` [1](#0-0) , which only distributes rewards once per epoch based on `env::epoch_height()` changing [2](#0-1) . When the epoch has changed, the reward is computed as the delta between the current total account balance (`account_locked_balance + account_balance`) and `self.last_total_balance`, and is added to `self.total_staked_balance` — increasing the price of every existing "stake" share uniformly, regardless of when those shares were minted [3](#0-2) .

Because NEAR's protocol-level stake-weight changes only take effect with an ~2-epoch delay, a `stake()` call made late in epoch `N` (before the epoch boundary) does not affect the actual validator stake that earns rewards during epoch `N`. Yet `internal_stake()` immediately mints new shares at the *current* share price for the attacker [4](#0-3) , and `internal_ping()` does not distinguish between shares that existed throughout the reward-earning period and shares minted moments before the reward is recognized. When the epoch subsequently rolls over and any account triggers `internal_ping()` again, the newly credited reward (earned entirely by the honest stakers' pre-existing, real stake) is split across the *new, larger* total share supply, giving the just-arrived attacker a proportional cut of a reward they contributed nothing toward.

Sequence:
1. Attacker calls `stake()`/`deposit_and_stake()` late in epoch `N`, before the epoch boundary. `internal_ping()` at this point sees no balance delta yet (`total_reward == 0`), so no dilution guard triggers; `internal_stake()` mints shares at the pre-reward price [5](#0-4) [4](#0-3) .
2. Epoch boundary passes; the protocol credits validator rewards earned by the pool's real (pre-attack) stake weight to `account_locked_balance`.
3. Attacker (or anyone) calls any pool method, triggering `internal_ping()`; the reward delta is distributed pro-rata over the now-diluted share pool, crediting the attacker a share of value it did not help generate [3](#0-2) .
4. Attacker immediately calls `unstake()`/`unstake_all()` to lock in the inflated share value, only needing to wait out `NUM_EPOCHS_TO_UNLOCK` before withdrawal, without having borne validator/slashing risk for the reward-earning period.

This mirrors the External Report's root cause: reward accrual state (`ts.rewards`/`total_staked_balance`) is updated based on a snapshot at withdrawal/ping time that does not require the actor's stake to have been genuinely at risk for the period that produced the reward, permitting reward creation "without streaming" (i.e., without genuinely contributing) the underlying value.

### Impact Explanation
This falls under the "High — Share, reward, vesting, refund, whitelist, or balance-accounting divergence that lets an unprivileged user over-credit value... withdraw more than fair entitlement" category. Any unprivileged delegator can repeatedly time deposits around epoch boundaries to skim rewards earned by other stakers' at-risk capital, permanently diluting honest long-term delegators' fair share of validator rewards. The attack requires no special privilege — only a public call to `stake`/`deposit_and_stake` followed by `unstake` through the standard, public entrypoints.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate: epoch boundaries on NEAR are predictable in cadence (though not down to the exact block), so an attacker can reliably approximate timing by staking shortly before an expected epoch switch and monitoring `get_account_staked_balance`/epoch height via view calls, then unstaking on the next ping. The profit per attack is bounded by the attacker's capital relative to total pool size and the pool's per-epoch reward rate, so it scales with capital committed and can be repeated every epoch, similar to the repeatable nature described in the original report.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighting or a minimum bonding period before newly staked shares participate in reward distribution — e.g., track a per-account "stake epoch" and only let shares minted before the start of the current reward-accrual window earn from `internal_ping()`'s distribution, or apply a lockup/vesting delay on newly staked shares equal to the real protocol stake-weight activation delay (~2 epochs) before they are eligible to receive a proportional share of newly credited rewards.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual PoC (based on the existing `staking-pool/src/lib.rs` test harness / `Emulator`, analogous to `test_stake_unstake`):
1. Bob deposits and stakes `1_000_000 NEAR`; `emulator.simulate_stake_call()` establishes real validator stake.
2. Advance epochs so that a reward is pending in `emulator.locked_amount` but not yet reflected via `ping()`.
3. Immediately before calling `ping()` (i.e., before the reward is recognized), have Attacker call `deposit_and_stake()` with an amount comparable to Bob's stake — `internal_ping()` sees `total_reward == 0` at this point, so Attacker's shares are minted at the pre-reward price.
4. Call `ping()` (simulating the epoch turnover reward crediting) — assert that `get_account_staked_balance(attacker)` increases by a proportional share of the reward, despite Attacker having staked only moments earlier and not having contributed to the actual on-chain validator stake weight that earned the reward.
5. Have Attacker call `unstake_all()` immediately to lock in the gained value, demonstrating profit extraction without exposure to the risk/duration that generated the reward — directly analogous to `alice.doStake`/`alice.doWithdraw` in the original H-06 PoC.

    pub(crate) fn internal_stake(&mut self, amount: Balance) {
        assert!(amount > 0, "Staking amount should be positive");

        let account_id = env::predecessor_account_id();
        let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&account_id);

        // Calculate the number of "stake" shares that the account will receive for staking the
        // given amount.
        let num_shares = self.num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down(amount);
        assert!(
            num_shares > 0,
            "The calculated number of \"stake\" shares received for staking should be positive"
        );
        // The amount of tokens the account will be charged from the unstaked balance.
        // Rounded down to avoid overcharging the account to guarantee that the account can always
        // unstake at least the same amount as staked.
        let charge_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_down(num_shares);
        assert!(
            charge_amount > 0,
            "Invariant violation. Calculated staked amount must be positive, because \"stake\" share price should be at least 1"
        );

        assert!(
            account.unstaked >= charge_amount,
            "Not enough unstaked balance to stake"
        );
        account.unstaked -= charge_amount;
        account.stake_shares += num_shares;
        self.internal_save_account(&account_id, &account);

        // The staked amount that will be added to the total to guarantee the "stake" share price
        // never decreases. The difference between `stake_amount` and `charge_amount` is paid
        // from the allocated STAKE_SHARE_PRICE_GUARANTEE_FUND.
        let stake_amount = self.staked_amount_from_num_shares_rounded_up(num_shares);

        self.total_staked_balance += stake_amount;
        self.total_stake_shares += num_shares;

    pub(crate) fn internal_ping(&mut self) -> bool {
        let epoch_height = env::epoch_height();
        if self.last_epoch_height == epoch_height {
            return false;
        }
        self.last_epoch_height = epoch_height;

        // New total amount (both locked and unlocked balances).
        // NOTE: We need to subtract `attached_deposit` in case `ping` called from `deposit` call
        // since the attached deposit gets included in the `account_balance`, and we have not
        // accounted it yet.
        let total_balance =
            env::account_locked_balance() + env::account_balance() - env::attached_deposit();

        assert!(
            total_balance >= self.last_total_balance,
            "The new total balance should not be less than the old total balance"
        );
        let total_reward = total_balance - self.last_total_balance;
        if total_reward > 0 {
            // The validation fee that the contract owner takes.
            let owners_fee = self.reward_fee_fraction.multiply(total_reward);

            // Distributing the remaining reward to the delegators first.
            let remaining_reward = total_reward - owners_fee;
            self.total_staked_balance += remaining_reward;

            // Now buying "stake" shares for the contract owner at the new share price.
            let num_shares = self.num_shares_from_staked_amount_rounded_down(owners_fee);
            if num_shares > 0 {
                // Updating owner's inner account
                let owner_id = self.owner_id.clone();
                let mut account = self.internal_get_account(&owner_id);
                account.stake_shares += num_shares;
                self.internal_save_account(&owner_id, &account);
                // Increasing the total amount of "stake" shares.
                self.total_stake_shares += num_shares;
            }
            // Increasing the total staked balance by the owners fee, no matter whether the owner
            // received any shares or not.
            self.total_staked_balance += owners_fee;
