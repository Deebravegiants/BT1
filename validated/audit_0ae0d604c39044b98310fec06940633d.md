### Title
Unrestricted token receipt permanently strands donations in the pool and controller — (File: contracts/pool/src/lib.rs)

### Summary
The original bug class is a permissionless `receive()` that lets assets enter a contract with no way out. The XOXNO Lending analog is stronger than a Solidity fallback: any Stellar token holder can push tokens directly to the pool or controller via `token.transfer`, and the protocol has no code path that can ever move those tokens back out. Cash is a pure accounting book; only protocol-initiated, measured pulls credit it, and no sweep/skim entrypoint exists on either contract.

### Finding Description
All pool mutators are gated by `#[only_owner]` (the controller): `supply`, `borrow`, `withdraw`, `repay`, `recapitalize`, `flash_loan`, `create_strategy`, `seize_positions`, `net_settle`, `claim_revenue` (contracts/pool/src/lib.rs lines 128–252). None of them compute or release the surplus `token.balance(pool) - Σ market cash`. Inbound flows never read the pre-existing balance as funding: `recapitalize` pulls `amount` from `payer` through `transfer_amount_measured`, books only the measured receipt up to the backing shortfall, and refunds the rest to `payer` (contracts/controller/src/markets.rs:142–163, contracts/pool/src/lib.rs:187–194). `balance_delta_since` in contracts/controller/src/payments.rs:10–20 snapshots balances at call start, so even `flash_position`'s collateral/refund measurements ignore balances that were already sitting at the controller. The design is explicitly acknowledged — "Supply, repayment, and recapitalization credit the tokens actually received; direct donations do not automatically increase booked cash" (docs/reference/architecture.md:57–61) and INV-ACCT-02 "token donations alone do not increase it" (docs/reference/invariants.md:104–109) — but the corollary is unhandled: the donation is neither credited nor recoverable. The test `pool_all_money_paths_preserve_books_and_shared_token_custody` demonstrates the end state: a 7-unit direct transfer sits in `token.balance(pool)` above every market's cash book permanently (tests/test-harness/tests/pool_money_flow_audit.rs:86–96). The same applies to tokens pushed to the controller: `claim_revenue` forwards only what it measures from the pool in that call, leaving pre-existing controller dust untouched with no rescue path (tests/test-harness/tests/controller/outbound_transfer_measurement.rs:124–151).

### Impact Explanation
Any tokens sent directly to the pool or controller — by user error, by a token's rebasing/airdrop mechanics, or deliberately — are permanently frozen. They cannot be credited (no book accepts them), cannot be withdrawn (withdraw is bounded by the cash book and requires supply shares), cannot fund a shortfall (`recapitalize` pulls fresh payer funds rather than consuming the surplus), and cannot be swept (no entrypoint exists; every pool mutator requires the controller, and the controller exposes no stray-funds recovery). This matches the accepted Medium template: assets reachable by an unprivileged push, recoverable only through a contract upgrade.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires an external actor to push tokens to the contract addresses — accidental transfers, misconfigured integrations, or griefing are all realistic since the pool/controller addresses are public (`get_pool_address`, lib.rs:490). No privileged action is needed; the tokens just become unspendable. The protocol itself is not harmed (the surplus even over-collateralizes the books), which is why severity stays Medium rather than higher — the loss falls on the sender and is permanent.

### Recommendation
Add an owner-gated (governance-routed) sweep on the controller that instructs the pool to transfer out `token.balance(pool) - Σ cash` across all markets sharing that token, plus a controller-side sweep for its own stray balances. Alternatively, extend `recapitalize` to consume the unbooked surplus before pulling from `payer`, converting accidental donations into bad-debt coverage.

### Proof of Concept

// Any address; no authorization from the protocol needed.
let pool = ctrl_client.get_pool_address();

// The donation is visible in custody but belongs to no book:
