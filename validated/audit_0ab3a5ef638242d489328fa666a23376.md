## Analysis: Analog Found

The bug class from the report (exchange-rate manipulation via "locked" funds not being reflected in an unchanged `totalSupply`, eventually driving new depositors to receive disproportionate shares until the pool is inflated/frozen) has a structural analog in Raydium's AMM PnL-taking mechanism.

### Title
Unbounded accumulation of `need_take_pnl_coin`/`need_take_pnl_pc` versus static `lp_amount` can shrink the deposit/withdraw exchange-rate denominator to zero, permanently freezing `Deposit`/`Withdraw` - (File: `program/src/processor.rs`)

### Summary
Every `Deposit` and `Withdraw` instruction calls `Processor::calc_take_pnl`, which "skims" accumulated trading profit out of `total_pc_without_take_pnl`/`total_coin_without_take_pnl` into `amm.state_data.need_take_pnl_pc`/`need_take_pnl_coin`, without touching `amm.lp_amount` (the LP "total supply" analog). [1](#0-0) 

Just like the Carapace `ProtectionPool` where locked capital is subtracted from `totalSTokenUnderlying` while `totalSupply` stays fixed, here the "reserved" pnl amounts are subtracted from the pool's accounted coin/pc totals while `amm.lp_amount` remains unchanged until the privileged `withdrawpnl` instruction is called by the `pnl_owner`. [2](#0-1) 

### Finding Description
`total_pc_without_take_pnl`/`total_coin_without_take_pnl` are the denominators used to compute both the deduct amounts for `Deposit` and the mint/withdraw exchange rate against the fixed `amm.lp_amount`: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

`InvariantPool::exchange_token_to_pool` and `InvariantToken::exchange_coin_to_pc`/`exchange_pc_to_coin` divide by these totals directly, without any floor/minimum check: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) 

Every ordinary, unprivileged trading activity (swaps that raise pool `k`) followed by any `Deposit`/`Withdraw` call triggers `calc_take_pnl`, which subtracts a proportional share of the appreciated value from `total_pc_without_take_pnl`/`total_coin_without_take_pnl` and adds it to `need_take_pnl_pc`/`need_take_pnl_coin`: [7](#0-6) 

These "need_take_pnl" amounts stay inside the vault balances (they are not physically withdrawn) until the pool's `pnl_owner` calls `process_withdrawpnl`. As long as the owner does not withdraw (which is entirely at their discretion and outside an ordinary user's control), each further profitable swap cycle followed by a deposit/withdraw call keeps shrinking `total_pc_without_take_pnl`/`total_coin_without_take_pnl` relative to the static `amm.lp_amount`, mirroring the reported pattern where "locked" capital is subtracted from the underlying accounting total while the share-total stays fixed.

As `total_coin_without_take_pnl`/`total_pc_without_take_pnl` shrink toward zero relative to `amm.lp_amount`:
- `mint_lp_amount` computed by `exchange_token_to_pool` grows disproportionately for any new deposit, inflating `amm.lp_amount` via `amm.lp_amount.checked_add(mint_lp_amount).unwrap()` [8](#0-7) , which will `panic`/abort the transaction on overflow once `lp_amount` nears `u64::MAX`, and
- once `total_coin_without_take_pnl` or `total_pc_without_take_pnl` reaches `0`, the `checked_div`/`checked_ceil_div` calls inside `InvariantToken` and `InvariantPool` divide by zero and `.unwrap()` panics, unconditionally reverting **every** future `Deposit` and `Withdraw` transaction.

### Impact Explanation
Once the denominator collapses, both `Deposit` (`process_deposit`) and `Withdraw` (`process_withdraw`) become permanently unusable for that pool — no user can add or remove liquidity anymore, freezing all LP funds already deposited in the pool. This matches the report's "protocol can come to a halt" impact class (permanent freezing of user/LP funds), reachable purely through ordinary, unprivileged instructions (`Deposit`, `Withdraw`, and the four swap instructions) without any privileged signer needing to act maliciously — the owner simply needs to not call `withdrawpnl` (a legitimate and expected operational pattern, not an attack action).

### Likelihood Explanation
Exploitation requires many transactions/cycles of profitable swap activity plus interleaved deposits/withdraws to accumulate a sufficiently large `need_take_pnl_*` gap relative to `lp_amount` — this is not a single-transaction exploit and depends on real economic activity (swap fees generating pnl) accruing over time while the `pnl_owner` withdrawal is delayed or never performed. This makes the practical likelihood moderate rather than trivially/immediately exploitable, but the underlying accounting flaw is deterministic and worsens monotonically with pool usage, matching the "growing over successive cycles" characterization of the original report.

### Recommendation
Reduce `amm.lp_amount` proportionally (or otherwise rebase the LP exchange rate) whenever pnl is skimmed into `need_take_pnl_pc`/`need_take_pnl_coin` in `calc_take_pnl`, or exclude `need_take_pnl_*` from being counted as "backing" only after subtraction consistently on both sides of the ratio so the LP exchange rate cannot degrade toward zero. Additionally, guard `InvariantToken`/`InvariantPool` division paths against zero/near-zero denominators with an explicit error rather than an `unwrap()` panic, and consider forcing/incentivizing periodic `withdrawpnl` settlement to prevent unbounded accumulation.

### Proof of Concept
1. Pool is initialized with `Initialize2`; `amm.lp_amount` set to initial liquidity, `need_take_pnl_pc = need_take_pnl_coin = 0`.
2. Repeat over many transactions:
   a. Attacker (or any user) submits profitable swap sequences via `SwapBaseIn`/`SwapBaseOut` that increase pool value/`k` relative to `target.calc_pnl_x * target.calc_pnl_y` snapshot.
   b. Any subsequent `Deposit` or `Withdraw` call triggers `calc_take_pnl` (processor.rs:167-281), which moves the appreciated value into `need_take_pnl_pc`/`need_take_pnl_coin` (processor.rs:244-262) without changing `amm.lp_amount`.
3. As the vault's `need_take_pnl_*` values grow (owner does not call `withdrawpnl`), `total_pc_without_take_pnl`/`total_coin_without_take_pnl` used in subsequent `Deposit`/`Withdraw` calls (processor.rs:1148-1153, 1719-1724) shrink relative to the static `amm.lp_amount`.
4. Eventually a `Deposit` call computes a `mint_lp_amount` (processor.rs:1244-1250) so large that `amm.lp_amount.checked_add(mint_lp_amount).unwrap()` (processor.rs:1350) panics, or `total_coin_without_take_pnl`/`total_pc_without_take_pnl` hits zero, causing `checked_div`/`checked_ceil_div().unwrap()` inside `InvariantToken`/`InvariantPool` (math.rs:378-477) to panic — permanently reverting every future `Deposit`/`Withdraw` instruction on that pool.

impl InvariantToken {
    /// Exchange rate
    pub fn exchange_coin_to_pc(
        &self,
        token_coin: u64,
        round_direction: RoundDirection,
    ) -> Option<u64> {
        Some(if round_direction == RoundDirection::Floor {
            U128::from(token_coin)
                .checked_mul(self.token_pc.into())
                .unwrap()
                .checked_div(self.token_coin.into())
                .unwrap()
                .as_u64()

                .checked_mul(self.token_pc.into())
